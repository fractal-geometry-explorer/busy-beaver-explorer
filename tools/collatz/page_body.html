Family R15, triaged

98 holdouts that compute a Collatz-like map

For these machines the run from blank tape is, provably or apparently, the orbit of an explicit arithmetic map: numbers multiplied by 3/2, 4/3 or 8/3 with a remainder steering a small counter or word, doubling maps with a moving modulus, Euclid-like games between two blocks. Each machine halts exactly when its orbit meets some special value. This page sorts all 98 by what is left to do, and lets you run 20 of the maps yourself.

None of the 98 is decided here. The sort, by what the next useful step is:

    Each machine's own pages hold the evidence; the short records below are read from them (tools/collatz/triage.json). The sort is a judgement, not a result.

    The maps are transcribed from each machine's folder into tools/collatz/maps.js, and tools/collatz/test_maps.js checks them against the machines: for 17 of them the first map states appear, in order, as exact tapes of the run from blank tape; the other 3 against the sequences quoted in their folders. Exact integers (BigInt), so the numbers can grow to thousands of digits; the run stops at a halt or at the step limit.
