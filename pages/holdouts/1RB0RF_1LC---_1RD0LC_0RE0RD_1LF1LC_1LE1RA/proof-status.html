What is known about 1RB0RF_1LC---_1RD0LC_0RE0RD_1LF1LC_1LE1RA

1RB0RF_1LC---_1RD0LC_0RE0RD_1LF1LC_1LE1RA

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The run is a Collatz-like map on a count m, sampled at K(P, m) = 0^∞ P (10)^m C> 11111 0^∞; m goes to about 3m/2 and the short left-end word P moves according to the parity of m. Lean proves the round-trip rule and the four sample-to-sample transitions for P = empty and P = 1. A halt needs P to climb a chain of 41 specific parities; the measured map runs 10,000 samples (m of 1,762 digits) without one. Heuristically it halts with probability 1, but only after an expected 1012–1013 samples, far beyond any simulation.

The sample map for the other prefixes

For every prefix P met (173 (P, parity) pairs), simulation of three representatives shows the next prefix depends only on P and m mod 2 and the next count is 3⌊m/2⌋ + 4 + δ with δ ∈ {0, 1, 3}. Extrapolated to all m, this is an observation, not a proof.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.053). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.497).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Why it should halt, eventually

Each sample uses one parity bit of m, and the bits behave like coin flips (the map is m → (3m + c)/2). The prefix climbs one rung for the right bit and falls back otherwise; the shortest route from the empty prefix to a halt (prefix 111101, m odd) is 42 samples long. So each visit of the empty prefix (about a third of the samples) succeeds with probability about 2−41: a halt is expected after some 1012–1013 samples, i.e. around 101012 steps.
