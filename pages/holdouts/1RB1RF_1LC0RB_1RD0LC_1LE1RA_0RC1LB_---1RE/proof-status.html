OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state machine halts from blank tape. This page records what is established about it, sorted by what each result rests on. Only a first analysis has been done: the macro rules are not yet known.

The tape is blocks of 1s, mostly 1^3 on the left and 1^3 or 1^4 on the right, separated by one, two or more 0s. Both ends grow like the logarithm of the step count.

Where a halt can be

A closed tape language of windows of 5 cells (tools/py/bbtools/ctl.py, closure checked by the script, not by Lean) contains every configuration reachable from blank tape, and its only halting configurations have F reading a 0 with three 1s just left of the head (1 1 1 F>0).

python3 -m bbtools.ctl 1RB1RF_1LC0RB_1RD0LC_1LE1RA_0RC1LB_---1RE 5

Not decided by n-gram CPS up to width 14

Growth shape: exp/exp

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.099). Right of the start it grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.089).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

The blocks

At the last left records before 109: C>0 0^2 1^3 0^2 1^3 0 1^3 0^2 1^3 0 1^3 0^2 1 0^2 ...; at the right: ... 1^3 0 1^4 0 1^4 0 1^4 0 1^4 ..., with gaps of one, two, four or five 0s. Hundreds of blocks, changing at many places between visits to the ends.

tmrun 1RB1RF_1LC0RB_1RD0LC_1LE1RA_0RC1LB_---1RE 1000000000 records 100000

The ends grow three cells at a time

In snapshots of the leftmost 60 cells taken whenever they change (4,922 snapshots in 109 steps), the left end moved 176 times, every time by exactly 3 cells. At the 312 visits to the right end (the head arriving after being more than 50 cells away) in 109 steps it had grown by 3 cells 294 times. The left end is made of words 1110, 11100 and 1100, with a marker 0000 that travels along it; what they encode is not known.

c/leftsnap.c (./leftsnap 1RB1RF_1LC0RB_1RD0LC_1LE1RA_0RC1LB_---1RE 1000000000 60), c/rvisits.c (./rvisits 1RB1RF_1LC0RB_1RD0LC_1LE1RA_0RC1LB_---1RE 1000000000 50)

Proposed family: F06, block-list counters

The tape is short: 1,302 visited cells at 2.7·108 steps, 1,551 at 109, 1,935 at 4.1·109 and 2,058 at 1.8·1010. Both ends grow like the logarithm of the step count, which is the mark of a counter, not of a bouncer. The tape is runs of 1s of length 3 (some 6 or 9) separated by one, two or five 0s, with stretches of the words 01110 and 10011 of up to 70 copies; about 100 to 130 level-2 tokens, in proportion to the length. Nothing is periodic on a large scale. Among the three families offered it is F06 (a counter); by its shape it would sit better with the counters on a logarithmic tape (F02 or F03).
