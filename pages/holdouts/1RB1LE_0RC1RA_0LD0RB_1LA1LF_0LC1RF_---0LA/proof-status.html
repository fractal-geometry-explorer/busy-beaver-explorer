What is known about 1RB1LE_0RC1RA_0LD0RB_1LA1LF_0LC1RF_---0LA

1RB1LE_0RC1RA_0LD0RB_1LA1LF_0LC1RF_---0LA

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Unlike the rest of its group it grows mostly to the right. Its tape is a word of the tokens 1^5 0, 1^6 0^2, 1^7 0 and a few others, ending in a block 1^(2j+1). j grows by one per overflow, at gaps that double, so some binary counter is hidden in the word; from time to time a restructure rewrites the word and j starts again.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves no halt in the first 107 steps (native_decide); the C simulator finds no halt in the first 1011 steps.

What it does. Each time the head reaches a new cell at the right end, in state C, the tape ends in 1^7 0 1^7 0 1^(2j+1) 0 C>0 for most of an epoch. j grows by one each time and the gap doubles exactly. The last four restructures began at steps 2,963,507, 220,322,024, 466,316,545 and 4,568,654,941; in the current epoch the end block has reached 1^61 (step 66,520,947,462), longer than in any earlier epoch.

Why it is hard. Where the counter is, and the restructure rule, are not known, and a closed tape language from short windows does not confine the halt (state F reading a 0).

The right end in the current epoch

From step 4,568,656,718 to 66,520,947,462 the head reached a new cell at the right end in state C 28 times, two cells each time. The tape then ended in 1^7 0 1^7 0 1^k 0 C>0 with k = 7, 9, …, 61, one step of 2 each time. The gap between successive times doubled exactly at the last 11 of them (ratio 2.0000 to four digits).

Restructures

The end block falls back to a few cells many times in the first 1011 steps, often in bursts of two or three falls within a few thousand steps. The last four bursts began at steps 2,963,507, 220,322,024, 466,316,545 and 4,568,654,941, after end blocks of 27, 43, 35 and 53 cells; the left end of the tape moved shortly before the last three.

A hidden binary counter

A unary block that grows by one unit while the time doubles means that the run counts in binary somewhere else on the tape, presumably in the word on the left, and lengthens the end block at each overflow.
