1RB1LF_0LC0RE_1LA1RD_0LB1LB_1RC1RD_0LD---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 13 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. No halt in the first 101000 steps (accelerated simulator). The long runs trade words at fixed rates and the remainders steer, like a Collatz-like map; no finite table of rules is known.

No halt in the first 101000 steps

An exact simulator with two levels of acceleration runs from blank tape to step 1.0963…·101000 without a halt. It crosses a run of one word at once, and it applies a stretch of the run many times at once only after replaying the stretch with the run lengths as unknowns, which proves the stretch for every cycle it is applied to. It agrees with the macro simulator of the family screen on the whole tape, the step count and all right-end records at 1.0·1012, 1.1·1015 and 3.3·1017 steps.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.497). Right of the start it grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.112).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

What the tape is made of

Mostly the word 110, in a few long runs separated by markers (11110, 1111110, 00 …); the runs exchange length from sweep to sweep.

Proposed family: F01, periodic blocks driven by a bounded engine

The description is small and does not grow: 10 to 23 level-2 tokens at every record from 2.6·108 to 1.8·1010 steps, and 9 to 19 runs in the macro simulator (blocks of 3 cells) at the sampled right-end records up to 1018 steps. For example at the right-end records the tape is (10)^3 001 (010)^3 (100)^3703 00101 0^6 1 0^4 (100)^19078 1010 at step 6,897,736,719 and (10)^3 001 (010)^3 (100)^6716 (10)^3 (010)^26894 10 at step 17,594,309,535. Two or three long runs of one three-cell word and a handful of markers is the F01 shape. The engine is not known: in the macro simulator (blocks of 3 cells) the left end is extended only 3,666 times in the first 3·108 steps and the shape of the markers differs from one extension to the next, so there is no single round to iterate yet.

the README, section “Family screen”

The long runs trade words at fixed rates, and remainders steer

Observed up to 101000 steps: almost all the time is spent in repeats in which two to four long runs of one three-cell word trade words at fixed rates, for example 8 words from one run to another and 2 to a third per cycle. A repeat ends when a run is used up, and the remainder of the division decides which repeat follows, as in a Collatz-like map. 63 kinds of repeat occurred in 18,888 repeats, with rates 8, 16, 32, 64 and (13 times) 256, and new kinds were still appearing late in the run. No finite table of rules is known, so the closed-set test of family F01 has nothing finite to close over yet; the machine may belong with the Collatz-like machines instead.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. It halts exactly when C reads a 0 with two 1s to its left; whether the markers ever produce that is open.
