The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in F reading 1. F is entered only from A1 (writes 1, moves L): 1 A>1. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

Family screen, 13 November 2026

Which family it belongs to

The 22 machines of family F09 (“bouncers over lists of counters, not yet classified”) were screened in one way. The machine runs from blank tape for 2·1010 steps; each time the tape reaches a new cell after a threshold, the tape is compressed twice: first repeats of one word, then repeats of groups of words. If the tape is a few growing blocks and a bounded rest, the number of level-2 tokens stays bounded while the tape grows. Everything in this section is observed in one run. None of it is proved.

Proposed family: F01, periodic blocks driven by a bounded engine (two or three long runs of one word, traded through a few markers).

The description is small and does not grow: 10 to 23 level-2 tokens at every record from 2.6·108 to 1.8·1010 steps, and 9 to 19 runs in the macro simulator (blocks of 3 cells) at the sampled right-end records up to 1018 steps. For example at the right-end records the tape is (10)^3 001 (010)^3 (100)^3703 00101 0^6 1 0^4 (100)^19078 1010 at step 6,897,736,719 and (10)^3 001 (010)^3 (100)^6716 (10)^3 (010)^26894 10 at step 17,594,309,535.

Two or three long runs of one three-cell word and a handful of markers is the F01 shape. The engine is not known: in the macro simulator (blocks of 3 cells) the left end is extended only 3,666 times in the first 3·108 steps and the shape of the markers differs from one extension to the next, so there is no single round to iterate yet.

First look

The shape of the run

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.497). Right of the start it grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.112).

After 109 steps the tape spans 21,829 cells (16,661 left of the start, 114 right), with a density of 1s of 0.654.

What it does

Runs of 110 and markers

Each time the head is at the left end in state B, the tape is a short prefix and then a few long runs of b = 110 separated by markers:

The runs swap length from sweep to sweep while the markers move, appear and vanish. The machine halts exactly when it is in state C on a 0 with two 1s to its left; inside the runs of 110 every 0 has that shape, so the markers decide. No regular round has been isolated yet.
