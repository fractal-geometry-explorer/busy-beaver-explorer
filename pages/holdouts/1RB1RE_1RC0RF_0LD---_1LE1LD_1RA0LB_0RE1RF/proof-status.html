What is known about 1RB1RE_1RC0RF_0LD---_1LE1LD_1RA0LB_0RE1RF

1RB1RE_1RC0RF_0LD---_1LE1LD_1RA0LB_0RE1RF

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 13 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Sweeps back and forth, each sweep rewriting the whole tape into its other form (011 to 100, 10 to 11, a period-12 word to its partner); a period-3 zone at the left end, then a few huge blocks of longer words (12, 15, 30 cells) that are built up and eaten in turn, and a short tail on the right; a halt needs a rightward sweep to meet 0001. Lean proves the sweep rules over the background words and the only local pattern that leads to a halt; no halt in 109 steps of the plain simulator, none in 2.3·1012 steps of an accelerated one (not verified).

The accelerated simulator agrees with the plain one

At 20 arrivals at the left end between 1.5·109 and 1.0·1011 steps (ten of them after a cycle-rule jump from 1.5·1010 to 1.0·1011) the accelerated simulator and the plain C simulator c/at.c give the same state, head position, tape and step count.

A tail cycle, proved for all block sizes

Around 1.5·1010 steps the tail next to the period-30 block repeats every 83 visits of the left end (B + 83, C − 8). One symbolic run of the accelerated simulator with both big counts free proves the rule (B, C) = (538 + 3x0, 89 + 3x1) → (B + 249, C − 24) in 498 events for all x ≥ 0; the plain engine confirms it on numbers. The run to 2.3·1012 steps found and used 3 such rules (two of 498 events, one of 6 events: B + 3 and a block 1^n − 18), all re-checked on numbers. In the phase with the period-15 block (1.3·108 to 6.9·108 steps, 2,400 visits) no tail occurs twice: the tails do not always cycle.

Nine tapes with a short clean zone: none halts

The clean zone (011)^a at the left end loses copies whenever the left block is converted, and in this run it is nearly used up after 2·1012 steps. Experiment: the configuration of the real run at step 3,368,614,121 (B> 0 (011)^8 001101 (101011010110)^7448 ..., just before a conversion) with the 8 replaced by 8, 7, ..., 0, each run for 6·109 plain steps. None of the nine halts. With 8 to 4 the zone comes out shorter ((100)^6 to (100)^2 in its rightward form), with 3 and 2 it is gone or one copy, and with 1 and 0 the conversion writes a new zone of at least 42 copies. Nine samples, not a proof.

Growth shape: sqrt/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.42). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.618).

What the tape looks like

Seen at the left end the tape is a clean zone (011)a (or (101)a), then a block of the period-12 word (101011010110)B that grows by one copy per visit of the left end, then one or two more huge blocks of longer words and a short tail such as (10)a 1 (10)b 1 and (110)3 (10)3, where the irregular events happen. The clean zone does not grow: it shrinks at each conversion of the left block. In the rightward form the soup reads as runs of 1s separated by 0 0.

py/passes.py tape

Blocks and phases; why the closed-set recipe does not apply yet

At arrivals at the left end the tape is 0 (011)^a 101101 (101011010110)^B junk (U)^C tail with B, C huge and U a word of 15, 21, 24 or 30 cells: the shape of the solved machines of this group. But there are phases with three big blocks, conversions of the whole left block, phases with a row of blocks (10)^k, k doubling from block to block (their number grows like the logarithm of the tape), the clean zone (011)^a is a third counter that shrinks at conversions, and the tails do not always cycle. Observed in plain runs to 6·1010 steps and accelerated runs beyond.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. Inside the tape, separators of two 0s are common in the rightward form, so a third 0 next to them is the danger. The run never produced one in 109 steps, but no invariant that forbids it is known.
