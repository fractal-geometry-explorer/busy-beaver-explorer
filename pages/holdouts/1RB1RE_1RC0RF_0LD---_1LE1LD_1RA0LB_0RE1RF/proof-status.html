What is known about 1RB1RE_1RC0RF_0LD---_1LE1LD_1RA0LB_0RE1RF

1RB1RE_1RC0RF_0LD---_1LE1LD_1RA0LB_0RE1RF

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Sweeps back and forth, each sweep rewriting the whole tape into its other form (011 to 100, 10 to 11, a period-12 word to its partner); a growing period-3 zone at the left end, a period-12 zone after it, a soup of short runs on the right; a halt needs a rightward sweep to meet 0001. Lean proves the sweep rules over the background words and the only local pattern that leads to a halt; no halt in 109 steps.

The machine halts only when C reads a 1, and the only way there is E reading 0 0 0 1 (E, A and B each write a 1 and step right, and C meets the 1.

Growth shape: sqrt/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.42). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.618).

What the tape looks like

Seen at the left end the tape has three zones: a clean zone (011)n (or (101)n) that grows by about one copy per long sweep, then a zone of the period-12 word (101011010110)m, also growing, then a soup of short pieces such as (10)a 1 (10)b 1 and (110)3 (10)3, where the irregular events happen. In the rightward form the soup reads as runs of 1s separated by 0 0.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. Inside the tape, separators of two 0s are common in the rightward form, so a third 0 next to them is the danger. The run never produced one in 109 steps, but no invariant that forbids it is known.
