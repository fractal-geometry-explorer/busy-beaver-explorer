OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

The same cascade of counters as its relative 1RB0LE_1LC0RE_0RA0LD_1LA0LF_0LC0RA_0RE---: at the left end a base-3 counter N with 3-cell digits, at the right binary counters with 4-cell digits that obey the stream laws of the F02 two-word sweepers, and between them a middle part that is not decoded. The laws of N and of the binary streams are proved in Lean for every size.

Status as of 16 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. No halt in LONGRUN_STEPS steps of the C simulator; Lean proves no halt in the first 107 steps (native_decide) and the laws of the outer parts of the tape for every size.

Where a halt would come from. Only F1 halts and F is entered only from D reading 1 (1LF): rarely (15 times in 108 steps), always with a 0 left of D.

What is missing. The rules of the middle part between N and the binary counters, and of the rare events.

The base-3 counter N, for every size

Read the cells left of N's low end three at a time from the head outward, 010 = 0, 110 = 1, 100 = 2, blank beyond. A visit in state A adds one; it comes back out in B without carry and in E with a carry; a carry through all digits makes the blank into a new digit 2. Proved from five token rows by induction on the number of digits.

n_inc0, n_inc1, n_inc0_carry, n_inc1_carry, n_overflow, lean/Counter.lean (rows in lean/VisitL.lean)

The binary streams, for every size

The stream laws of the F02 sibling 1RB0LE_1LC0RE_0RA0LD_1LA1LF_0LC0RA_0LB--- hold for this machine: in tokens z = 00, a = 10, c = 01, a visit in state B to an a/z stream is a binary increment, a visit in state A to the counter M (digits z c, c c) adds one and wraps, sending three visits into the stream after it.

vb_even, vb_odd, ve_odd, m_inc, m_wrap, lean/Stream.lean (26 rows in lean/VisitR.lean)

The halting pattern never forms

F is entered only from D reading 1, 15 times in the first 108 steps, always with a 0 left of D, so F reads 0.

The run uses the counter law

In the run, the cells left of -100 are entered only in state A, and all 898,777 of those visits, from step 533,028 to step 62,483,156, are rows of the counter law; then the middle reaches -100 and N's low end moves outward. At 109 steps N fills cells -154 to about -110.
