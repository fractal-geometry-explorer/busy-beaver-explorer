What is known about 1RB1LC_1LA0RE_0LD---_1LB1LD_1RB1RF_0LA0RB

1RB1LC_1LA0RE_0LD---_1LB1LD_1RB1RF_0LA0RB

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Sweeps back and forth over a period-11 background (01010111101 and its rightward form 10101010010) with a soup of (01)^k, 1^3, 1^4 pieces; the clean zone at the left end grows; a halt needs B or F to read 0 just right of 1 1. Lean proves the sweep rules over the background words and the only local pattern that leads to a halt; no halt in 109 steps.

The sweeps cross the background words whole

The only way to a halt

The machine halts only when C reads a 1, and the only ways there are B or F reading a 0 with 1 1 just left of it (B writes 1 or F writes 0, steps left, A reads the first 1 and steps left again, and C meets the second 1.

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.518). What the tape looks like

At the left end there is a clean zone of the period-11 word ((10100101010)91 at the right end of a sweep after 4·106 steps) that grows; the rest of the tape is a soup of (01)k with k = 4, 7, 10, 13, runs 13 0 14 and short copies of the period-11 word.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. In 109 steps B read a 0 with a 1 on its left about 4.2·107 times, and every time the cell before that 1 was a 0; nothing known rules out a second 1 there.
