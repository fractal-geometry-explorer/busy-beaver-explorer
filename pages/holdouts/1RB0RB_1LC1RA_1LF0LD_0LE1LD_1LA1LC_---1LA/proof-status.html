What is known about 1RB0RB_1LC1RA_1LF0LD_0LE1LD_1LA1LC_---1LA

1RB0RB_1LC1RA_1LF0LD_0LE1LD_1LA1LC_---1LA

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 13 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves the halting pattern, the left-going pass over a block and the two crossings of the block of 111110 words for every length. A halt needs a pass that reaches a lone 1 at the left end of the tape (given the observed fact that 0s inside the tape are single). The last pass to reach the left end did so at step 178,267,564 and did not halt; none has come within 50 cells of it since, in a reduced system followed to trigger 1,073,741,824.

The halting pattern

0 0 1 D> 0, the pass on the separator right of a block of length 1 with two 0s on its left, halts three steps later (F reads 0). By the table this is the only way to halt after the first step (hand argument in the README).

halt_pass, lean/Rules.lean

The pass

Moving left from separator to separator, the pass cuts a block of length k+3 into k+1 and 1 and goes on (pass_split); a block of length 1 stops it (pass_stop). For all lengths and every surrounding tape.

lean/Rules.lean, generated by py/gen_rules.py

Crossing the block of words does not depend on its length

The pass crosses n words 111110 in 6n steps, in the same state, turning each into 1110 10; the sweep back turns them into 111110 again. For every n and every surrounding tape.

cross_left, cross_right, lean/Rules.lean

The reduced system

With the growing block cut to 10 words at every trigger (which, by the two crossings, should not change the rest of the tape; checked against the real machine on 20,000 triggers, not proved), the run from trigger 20,000 to 1,073,741,824 has no halt and no arrival at the left end; the deepest pass came within 50 cells of it.

c/reduced.c; README.md

The leftmost zone alone

The leftmost zone run by itself, one arriving pass at a time, from the real configuration of trigger 200, reproduces the real arrival of trigger 9472 (same new cells, same blocks afterwards). Continued, this model halts in its tick 38,816 after two more arrivals. That is not a prediction for the machine: after trigger 9472 the old zone gets a pass only when the new zone lets one through (32 of them by trigger 5,166,316, fewer than 64 by trigger 3·108; the model needs 29,543), and the block between the two zones is being used up, which the model does not describe.

c/zone.c; README.md

Halting needs the left end

At the snapshots taken the 0s inside the tape are single, so the halting pattern can only form at the leftmost block. Observed, not proved.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. It halts if a pass reaches the leftmost block while that block is a lone 1 with blank tape on its left; nothing known forces or forbids that.
