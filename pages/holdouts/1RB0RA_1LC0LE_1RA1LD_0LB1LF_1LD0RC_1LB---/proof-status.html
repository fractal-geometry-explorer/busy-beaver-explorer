1RB0RA_1LC0LE_1RA1LD_0LB1LF_1LD0RC_1LB---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The sweep, the phase and the two clean phase ends are proved in Lean for all values (kernel); blank tape reaches the clean regime at step 58,429 (native_decide) and four clean phases follow by the kernel. The exact cycle accelerator runs 101000 steps without a halt. The phase end for y ≡ 1 (mod 3) and the resets of deeper levels are not yet closed rules.

(x, 3k + y) to (x + 4k, y), by induction on k, with the exact step count pst.

phase, lean/Macro.lean

The clean phase ends

When y comes down to 0 or 2 the front level is reset to (0, x) or (0, x + 2), the counter p gains 2 and the next level (z, w) becomes (z + 2, w − 2). Together: on an empty front level y ↦ 4y/3 (y ≡ 0) and y ↦ 4(y − 2)/3 + 2 (y ≡ 2).

end0, end2, cleanA, cleanB, lean/Macro.lean

heuristic Evidence only

A Collatz-like counter

The residue of y mod 3 at the end of each phase looks irregular; y ≡ 1 starts a secondary bouncer that rebuilds the left part, and the run returns to the clean form with p = 0. Levels further right are reset in turn, each a bit longer than before.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. It halts exactly when D reads a 1 with a 1 to its left (C at 1 1 C>1 or E at 1 1 E>0); the proved rules never do that, but the y ≡ 1 regime and the deep resets are not closed rules yet.
