What is known about 1RB1LD_1RC1RE_0LA1LB_0LD1LC_1RF0RA_---0RC

1RB1LD_1RC1RE_0LA1LB_0LD1LC_1RF0RA_---0RC

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open, and reduced to a Collatz-like map. Lean proves (kernel only, halts_iff in lean/Round.lean) that the machine halts from blank tape exactly when the orbit of (8, 1) under the map (3t, k) → (4t+4, k−1), (3t+1, k) → (4t+4, k+1), (3t+2, k) → (4t+5, k+1) reaches a pair (3t+2, 0). The counter k goes up on two residues of three and down on one; along 1,000,000 rounds of the orbit its least value after round 10 is 7 and it ends at 331,763. Halting would need this upward-drifting walk to come back to 0: almost surely never, but not proved.

The halting configurations

The machine stops exactly when it reads the symbol of a halting transition (F0); the generated statement covers every configuration.

step_none_iff, lean/Blank.lean

No halt in the first 10,000 steps

The list simulator of the shared library, proved to agree with the machine's semantics, runs 10,000 steps from blank tape without a halt, evaluated by Lean's kernel.

blank_runs_kernel, lean/Blank.lean

One round, for every m and k

From E(m,k) = 0^∞ D> 110 (10)^m (110)^k 0^∞ the machine reaches E(m′,k′) with (m′,k′) = next(m,k) for every m ≥ 1 and k, or halts when m = 3t+2 and k = 0. The start of a round (one rule per residue of m), the sweeps (three 10s of the left block become four of the right block, by induction) and the finish are symbolic runs.

No halt in the first 109 steps

holdouts/triage.json

1,000,000 rounds of the orbit

Iterating the map with Python big integers: no halting pair in 1,000,000 rounds; the residues of m mod 3 came 334,119, 332,791 and 333,090 times; k is 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 5, 6, ... at the first rounds, never below 7 after round 10, and 331,763 at the last round (each round is about 16/9 times as long as the one before in machine steps, so this is far beyond any direct simulation).

py/orbit.py

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.494). Right of the start it grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.045).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Probviously nonhalting

If the residues of m mod 3 behave like independent uniform digits, k is a walk with steps +1 (probability 2/3) and −1 (1/3): from a value k it ever returns to 0 with probability 2−k, and then halts only on residue 2. With k in the hundreds of thousands, the chance is nil; the same argument as for Antihydra.
