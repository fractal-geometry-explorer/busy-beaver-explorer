1RB1LE_0LC0RF_1RD0LB_1RE1RD_1RA0RC_1LB---

OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 22 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The tape is a list of numbers in unary (blocks of 4k+3 ones) that each round trip rewrites. The rewrite is now decoded and proved in Lean: in 4-cell letters (s = 0111, c = 1111) a round trip replaces the word by its running XOR, so backwards in time the list evolves by the XOR cellular automaton rule 102. The layer of a few groups at the right end, where a halt would have to come from, is now decoded too: it runs as its own small machine that learns one bit from the bulk per round trip, the same model for all six machines of the two tables, and its only halting pattern (an even group followed by a touching 1) is proved in Lean. Open: an invariant of the reachable tails that excludes that pattern. No halt in 1010 steps.

Where a halt can come from

If the machine halts on its third step from a configuration, that configuration is one of: A with 0 under the head, 1 right, 1 two right; F with 1 left, 0 under the head. So a run can reach a halt only through one of these patterns, and a proof that the machine never halts only has to rule them out. The proof splits on the state and the five cells around the head.

The sweep rules

10 rules for all values of the repeat counts and every tape around them: the leftward sweep turns each 11 of a block into 10 (B) or 01 (C), the rightward sweep turns 001, 010, 100 into 110, 101, 011 (C, D, E), the left end of a block, and the turns at the two ends.

lean/Rules.lean, generated by py/rules.py

The round trip is a running XOR

In 4-cell letters s = 0111, c = 1111: the leftward sweep turns every block s ck into the group 1 (01)2k 000 (block_B, left_sweep, for any list of blocks); the rightward sweep reads the groups in E and A in turn and writes sk+1 for a group read in E, ck+1 for a group read in A, turning the s behind it into c (group_E, group_A, right_sweep, for any list of pairs of groups); and what the rightward sweep writes is the running XOR of the letter word it read (outL_eq_pxor, with its inverse, the XOR of neighbours, in xdiff_pxor). The two ends of the tape are not covered.

lean/Sweep.lean (generated by py/ca.py) and lean/Bulk.lean

The rules at the right end, and the halting pattern

The rightward sweep at the front of the right-end tail, for all repeat counts and every tape around it: an even group 2j read in a first slot writes j−1 ones and turns back (even_E, even_A), a short gap 00 (gap2_E, gap2_A), two touching groups (touch_E, touch_A) and the leftward sweep over an even group (even_block_B). The one halting pattern: an even group read in a first slot followed by a single 1 that touches the next group halts, for every j (halt_even_touch).

The run obeys the XOR rule away from the right end

At every return of the head to the left end in the first 2·108 steps (4,159 round trips), every letter before the right-end layer is the XOR of the same letter one round trip later and its left neighbour: 8,492,322 letters, no exception. The left end gains exactly one cell per round trip and the letter grid moves with it.

py/ca_check.py

The right-end layer is its own machine, fed one bit per round trip

The tail after the last pair of odd groups is rewritten each round trip by a local map G that only needs to know from the bulk whether its first token falls in a first or a second slot. Checked at every round trip of the first 1010 steps of all six machines of the two tables (about 29,000 round trips and 339 to 416 different tails each), no exception; the two tables differ on a single tail. Feeding G both bits at every round trip, a breadth-first search from the start reaches 100,000 tails per table and none contains the halting pattern.

The halting state is never close

F1 is the only halting transition and F is entered only by B1 (0RF): a halt needs B on a 1 with a 1 after it. In the first 109 steps F is entered 117,032,159 times and reads 0 every time.

Regular-language deciders tried (F11)

None of these closes: each reaches a halting configuration in its abstraction, which says nothing about whether the machine halts. n-gram CPS (tools/c/ngramcps.c), equal widths on both sides: a halting local configuration is reached with plain up to width 24, history 1 up to width 24, history 2 up to width 24, history 3 up to width 24, LRU history up to width 7 (larger widths ran out of the 150 s or 60 million configuration budget). n-gram CPS with every cell tagged by its position mod p: fails for p = 2 up to width 20, p = 3 up to width 20, p = 4 up to width 20, p = 6 up to width 20. n-gram CPS where each side also carries the state of a small automaton that has read that side from the far end (every automaton with up to 3 states on both sides, or 4 states on one side), with position mod 1..4 and widths 4, 6, 8: no closed set without a halt in all 120 parameter sets. Run-length windows (the K nearest runs of equal macro cells on each side, run lengths kept exactly below T and mod M above): fails for all 72 parameter sets (cells of 1 to 4 bits, K = 2, 4, 6, T = 2, 4, M = 1, 2, 4). Two-automaton closed tape language by SAT (a deterministic automaton reading each side inward, n states each, CaDiCaL): no solution for n = 2..8; n = 9 not finished in 150 s.

The right-end layer stays thin

The part of the tape at the right end that is not yet in letter form is 13 cells long on average and at most about 100 cells in the first 109 steps, with no growth from the first third of the run to the last; in about 9% of round trips the rightward sweep turns back before the end of the tape. This layer is decoded below.

py/ca_check.py (letters), C dump of the last 400 cells

Growth shape: sqrt/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.496). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.503).

Blocks of 4k+3 ones

At every 7th visit to the right end in the first 3·108 steps, 553,908 of the 554,278 blocks of ones between single 0s have a length 4k+3; the few others are where the head was still working. After a leftward sweep a block of 4k+3 ones has become 2k+1 single 1s (000 (10)^(2k+1)), the groups separated by 000.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The list of numbers is rewritten each round trip by a sequential process with a finite carry (the zigzag of the rightward sweep), but how the list evolves is not understood; a proof would need an invariant of the list (a closed language of lists) that keeps B away from two adjacent 1s.
