Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 9 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Every visit of the right end is one leftward sweep and one rightward refill. Lean proves how the sweep treats each block (for every length), that it can halt only at an odd block behind a single 0, and the whole ordinary visit, which decrements the counter, for any number of blocks. What is missing is an invariant: a description of the tapes that the rare reload visits produce, closed under every kind of visit. Simple local descriptions (which pairs or triples of neighbouring blocks occur) are not closed, so it must carry longer-range information.

Shift rules

For every length n of the run and any tape around it: L D> 1^n R reaches L 0^n D> R; L F> 1^n R reaches L 0^n F> R; L A> (10)^n R reaches L (11)^n A> R. These are the moves the head makes across the long uniform stretches of the tape.

lean/Shifts.lean (written by bbtools.symtm.prove)

The lowest step of the counter

For every length 2y+2 of the last block and x of the block before it, and any tape further left: L 0 1^x 000 1^(2y+2) A> 0^inf reaches L 0 1^(x+3) 00 1^(2y+2) A> 0^inf in 6y+16 steps (the lowest digit of the counter goes from 1 to 0).

dec_last, lean/Rules.lean (from py/rules.py)

The sweep, block by block

For every length: the leftward sweep crosses an odd block behind the gap 00, leaving it in the sparse form 0 1 0 (01)^w (p_pass); it stops and turns right at an odd block behind 000 (p_stop_odd) or at any even block (p_stop_even); at an odd block behind a single 0 it halts (p_halt: state E reads a 1). The pieces of the rightward refill (p_fill_first, p_fill_two, p_fill_mid, p_fill_end) are proved the same way.

lean/Rules.lean (from py/rules.py, written by bbtools.symtm.prove)

The ordinary decrement, for any number of blocks

For any tape L on the left, any a ≥ 1, m ≥ 3 and any list of b1, …, br ≥ 3 (r ≥ 0): L 000 1^(2a+1) 00 1^(2b1+1) … 00 1^(2br+1) 00 1^(2m) A> 0^∞ reaches L 111 00 1^(2a+4) 000 1^(2b1) … 000 1^(2br) 000 1^(2m-4) A> 0^∞: the lowest digit 1 of the counter becomes 0 and the 0s below it become 1s, the blocks change by +3, +3, −1 each and −4. Proved by induction over the list of blocks from the pieces above.

No halt in the first 109 steps

The C simulator tools/c/triage.c runs 109 steps without a halt; at the end 73,592 cells have been visited and 73,306 of them hold a 1.

One visit is one sweep and one refill

Up to 109 steps (36,752 visits of a new cell at the right end) the rightward pass never turns back before it reaches the end: C never reads a 1 straight after A, and B never reads a 0 straight after A inside the tape.

c/visits.c

Which visits the theorems cover

Of the 36,790 visits of the right end up to 109 steps (as tmrun ... at A R lists them), 18,360 are instances of dec_last and 18,244 of dec_general. The other 186 are the reload events: 114 sweeps stop at an even block, 48 at an odd block with short blocks behind it, 16 with the last block too short, 8 at the left end. None reaches the halting case.

py/visits.py

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: fixed/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.523).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

No small local invariant

Reading the tape as tokens (block length up to 8 exactly, longer ones by parity, and the gap after it), the sets of neighbouring pairs and triples seen in the run are not closed: running one visit on words built from them produces new pairs and triples, and repeating this soon reaches words that halt. Closed tape languages from windows of cells, of run-length tokens with parity, and with depth from the end were also tried and allow a halt. So a proof needs an invariant that sees further than a few blocks.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. A halt needs a sweep to reach an odd block behind a single 0 (or the refill to meet 0 1 1). The ordinary decrement never does that; the reload visits would need an exact rule and an invariant closed under all visits.
