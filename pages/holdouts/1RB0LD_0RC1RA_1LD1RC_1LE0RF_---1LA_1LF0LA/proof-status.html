What is known about 1RB0LD_0RC1RA_1LD1RC_1LE0RF_---1LA_1LF0LA

1RB0LD_0RC1RA_1LD1RC_1LE0RF_---1LA_1LF0LA

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. The head sweeps across the whole tape. Most of the tape is one word of 51 cells repeated over and over, the number of copies growing by one every few sweeps; the left end is a run (1 0 1)^a, and between them, and at the right end, sit short zones with no visible order. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves the exact way a halt would happen (D reading 0 with a 0 on its left) and five shift rules; the C simulator finds no halt in the first 1011 steps, and in 3·109 steps the cell left of D, when D reads 0, has always been 1. The irregular zones are not understood.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Bouncer + Chaotic Edge. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

The halting configurations

The machine stops exactly when it reads the symbol of a halting transition (E0); the generated statement covers every configuration.

step_none_iff, lean/Blank.lean

How a halt comes about

E reading 0 is the only halt. E is entered only from D reading 0 (enter_E), which writes 1 and steps left, so the machine halts exactly at 0 D>0 (halt_D). D is entered from A reading 1 and from C reading 0, both moving left (enter_D); this gives 0 0 A>1 and 0 0 C>0 (halt_A, halt_C).

halt_E, halt_D, halt_A, halt_C, enter_E, enter_D, lean/Halt.lean

Shift rules

For every n and every surrounding tape: moving left in front of 0 0 0, D rewrites (011101111011111011111011111110111111101110111011011)^n (a rotation of w) as (110111011011111111100110111101100111110011110110111)^n, 201 steps a copy; this is how the run is written when the head reaches the left end. Moving left, F turns 0^n into 1^n; C crosses 1^n and (1 0)^n to the right; A turns (0 1)^n and B turns (1 0)^n into (1 1)^n, moving right.

dword, ffill, cright, cright10, afill, bfill, lean/Rules.lean

No halt in the first 1011 steps

The C simulator (tools/c/tm.h) runs 1011 steps from blank tape without a halt; the visited tape then spans 310,089 cells, 241,375 of them 1.

c/longrun.c

D never reads 0 next to a 0

In the first 3·109 steps D reads 0 about 139 million times, always with a 1 on its left (1,713 of those at the left end of the tape).

c/ctx.c

The right end is periodic

Recording the tape each time the head reaches a fresh cell at the right end (9,156 times in the first 108 steps) and cutting it after the last two copies of w: from the 173rd time on, the part to the right of the copies of w repeats exactly with period 8. The left end does not: in its 632 visits to a fresh left cell in the first 6·107 steps, the part left of the copies of w took 533 different values.

c/edge.c, py/zones.py

heuristic Evidence only

One long repeated word

When the head reaches the right end, the tape is (1 0 1)^a, a zone of a few dozen irregular cells, w^K with the 51-cell word w = 110111011101101101110111101111101111101111111011111 (in some rotation), and another short irregular zone. K was 73, 113 and 151 at widths 4,000, 6,000 and 8,000, so the repeated part fills most of the tape; a grows slowly and irregularly (12, 13, 21).

tools/c/tmrun.c records

Growth shape

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.398). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.503).

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The run of w and the right end are regular (the right end repeats every 8 sweeps), so the question is about the zone at the left end: a proof would describe its evolution and show that it never produces 0 D>0. Window CTL up to 7 cells and n-gram CPS (widths up to 9, see the README for the larger runs) fail.
