What is known about 1RB0LC_1RC1RA_1RD0RF_0LE---_1LA1LE_0RA1LD

1RB0LC_1RC1RA_1RD0RF_0LE---_1LA1LE_0RA1LD

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The run is a sequence of epochs. Within an epoch a bouncer grows (011)^a (101)^3 (01)^b at the left end at fixed rates; at the end of an epoch the blocks are emptied into an irregular region that holds the rest of the tape and grows from epoch to epoch (about 1,600 compressed words at 4·108 steps, 5,000 at 1.7·109). Measured, not proved. Halting needs D on a 1, entered from C on a 0 or F on a 1; never one step away in the first 3·106 steps.

The halting configurations

Where a halt can come from

If the machine halts on its third step from a configuration, that configuration is one of: B with 0 under the head, 0 right, 1 two right. So a run can reach a halt only through one of these patterns, and a proof that the machine never halts only has to rule them out. The proof splits on the state and the five cells around the head.

halt_route, lean/Route.lean (written by py/route.py)

Regular-language deciders tried (F11)

None of these closes: each reaches a halting configuration in its abstraction, which says nothing about whether the machine halts. n-gram CPS (tools/c/ngramcps.c), equal widths on both sides: a halting local configuration is reached with plain up to width 14, history 1 up to width 13, history 2 up to width 12, history 3 up to width 12, LRU history up to width 5 (larger widths ran out of the 150 s or 60 million configuration budget). n-gram CPS with every cell tagged by its position mod p: fails for p = 2 up to width 12, p = 3 up to width 12, p = 4 up to width 12, p = 6 up to width 12. n-gram CPS where each side also carries the state of a small automaton that has read that side from the far end (every automaton with up to 3 states on both sides, or 4 states on one side), with position mod 1..4 and widths 4, 6, 8: no closed set without a halt in all 120 parameter sets. Run-length windows (the K nearest runs of equal macro cells on each side, run lengths kept exactly below T and mod M above): fails for all 72 parameter sets (cells of 1 to 4 bits, K = 2, 4, 6, T = 2, 4, M = 1, 2, 4). Two-automaton closed tape language by SAT (a deterministic automaton reading each side inward, n states each, CaDiCaL): no solution for n = 2..9; n = 10 not finished in 150 s.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: power/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.554). Right of the start it grows like a power of the step count other than 1/2 (fitted exponent 0.309).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Epochs of steady growth

At the head's visits to the left end (C there) the tape starts C>0 (011)^a (101)^3 (01)^b …, and within an epoch every 400 visits add 200 to a and 600 to b: (1131, 221) at step 403,575,993, (1331, 821), (1531, 1421), …, (4531, 10421) at step 857,064,593. The rest of the tape does not change during the epoch.

tools/c/tmrun.c MACHINE N at C L

Epoch ends feed an irregular region

An epoch ends with a long pass that empties the blocks; afterwards a and b start small again and the irregular region on the right is larger: about 500 compressed words before the epoch that ends between 3.1 and 4.0·108 steps, 1,581 after it; 5,017 after the one that ends between 8.6·108 and 1.7·109. Epochs get longer (they end between 1.1 and 1.6·108, between 3.1 and 4.0·108, between 8.6·108 and 1.7·109, and none from 1.7·109 to 2·109).

Never one step from a halt

The machine halts when D reads a 1; D is entered from C reading a 0 (C0 → 1RD) and from F reading a 1 (F1 → 1LD). In the first 3·106 steps that happens 296,494 times, and D always lands on a 0.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. Inside an epoch the machine is a simple bouncer; what happens at the end of an epoch, where the blocks are poured into the irregular region, is not understood, and that region is where a halt would have to come from.
