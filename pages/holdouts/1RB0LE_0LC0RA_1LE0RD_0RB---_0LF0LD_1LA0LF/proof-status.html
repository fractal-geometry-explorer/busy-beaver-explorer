What is known about 1RB0LE_0LC0RA_1LE0RD_0RB---_0LF0LD_1LA0LF

1RB0LE_0LC0RA_1LE0RD_0RB---_0LF0LD_1LA0LF

Nobody knows whether this six-state machine halts from blank tape. Its tape is single 1s separated by gaps of one, two or three 0s, and it grows to the left. At its visits to the left end a prefix made of the word 0 0 1 grows by one word each time while the time between visits roughly doubles, then shrinks back: the behavior of a counter. This page lists what has been established, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Its events are decoded: a binary counter at the left end (3-cell digits) gains 1 per event and a 2-bit counter M right of a fixed 46-cell word gains 1; this is proved in Lean for every carry length. When M wraps, a slower counter further right changes (not decoded), and now and then the fixed words themselves are rebuilt.

Sweep rules

A, B, C and D cross runs of the words 01, 10, 001 or 100 rightwards in one state, rewriting each copy the same way, for any number of copies and any surrounding tape.

The event rule (non-wrapping events)

From L 0 0 0 (1 0 0)^k ENGINE A> 0 PREFIX [M] R (ENGINE = 00101001010100, PREFIX the 46-cell word 1001010100100101010010001001000100100010101010, M two 4-cell digits 1000 = 0 / 1010 = 1, low first) the machine reaches the same shape with the left counter + 1 (1 0 0 (0 0 0)^k) and M + 1, for M = 0, 1, 2, every k and every tape L, R (8k + 608 or 8k + 616 steps); also when the k ones reach the left end of the tape (the counter gains a digit). Nothing right of M is read.

Visits to the left end

At the visits of E to the left end on blank the tape is E>0 0 1 (0 0 1)^k 0 1 … followed by single 1s with gaps of one to three 0s. k grows by 1 from one visit to the next while the time between visits roughly doubles, and drops back now and then (to 8 at step 4,121,436; to 2 at step 109,450,476, after reaching 18).

tmrun 1RB0LE_0LC0RA_1LE0RD_0RB---_0LF0LD_1LA0LF 1000000000 at E L

Where it can halt

A closed tape language with windows of 6 cells (found and checked by bbtools.ctl, not yet in Lean) allows a halt only with D reading a 1 at the left end of the tape, followed by 100100, 100101, 101001 or 101010.

python3 -m bbtools.ctl 1RB0LE_0LC0RA_1LE0RD_0RB---_0LF0LD_1LA0LF 6

heuristic Evidence only

A counter

A word per visit and twice the time per word is the signature of a binary counter whose digits are the gaps; the drops of k are its carries reaching further into the tape.

open Open

The rule

How the gaps encode a number, and what a visit does to it, are not known.

The wrap of M and beyond

When M wraps, a slower counter right of M (4-cell words 0101/0100 and others) changes; how, and how the PREFIX is rebuilt, is not decoded. A halt (D reading 1) can only come in those steps, never inside a non-wrapping event.
