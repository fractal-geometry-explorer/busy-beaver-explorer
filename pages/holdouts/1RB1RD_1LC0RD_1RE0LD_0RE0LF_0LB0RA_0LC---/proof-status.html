1RB1RD_1LC0RD_1RE0LD_0RE0LF_0LB0RA_0LC---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. It has a twin, which differs only in what A does on a 1. This page lists what is established, sorted by what each result rests on; the counter at its left end is understood, the work zone that drives it is not.

Status as of 9 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. It halts only when F reads a 1, which has not happened in 2·1010 steps. The left end is a binary counter whose increment is proved in Lean; the work zone that drives it is not understood.

Shift rules

Three shift rules for all n and any surrounding tape: E crosses (110)^n leaving (010)^n, D crosses (011)^n leaving (001)^n, and B crosses (100)^n leaving (101)^n.

lean/Rules.lean, holdouts/1RB1RD_1LC0RD_1RE0LD_0RE0LF_0LB0RA_0LC---/py/gen_rules.py

The increment of the left counter

For every n and any tape around it, L 1000 (1010)^n C>1 1 R reaches L 1010 1 (0001)^n 0 D> R in 12n+8 steps: C walks left over n one-digits 1010, turns the zero-digit 1000 into a one, and B walks back turning each one-digit into a zero-digit. A1 is not used, so the same theorem holds for both twins.

Where the twins differ

In the first 109 steps A reads a 1 only 11 times, always with 0s on both sides; F reads only 0s, followed by a 0.

holdouts/1RB1RD_1LC0RD_1RE0LD_0RE0LF_0LB0RA_0LC---/c/ctx.c

A binary counter at the left end

Whenever the head is at least 8 cells right of the counter, the tape from the left end reads 1010101101010110, then 4-cell digits 1010 (one) and 1000 (zero), then 11. Read as a binary number (least significant digit on the right) this counter goes up at almost every change. From step 6.3·106 to 109 the decoded value changes 529,577 times; 529,571 changes are increases, 4 are overflows where the counter gains a digit (18 → 21 digits), and 2 are not classified. At 109 steps it has 20 digits.

Not decided by n-gram CPS up to width 14

The n-gram Closed Position Set decider (tools/c/ngramcps.c) with equal widths 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 and 14, plain and with the fixed-length (1) and LRU history augmentations, either reaches a halting local configuration (UNKNOWN) or stops at 3·107 local configurations: it does not prove non-halting at these settings.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/fixed

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.072).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Tape structure

Late in the run the tape is made of long stretches of (0 1)^n, (0^3 1)^n and (1^2 0)^n; both ends grow, the right one by single cells at irregular times (e.g. 2a: cell 175 at step 4.7·108). The stretches (0 1)^n and (0^3 1)^n are the digits 1010 and 1000 of the left binary counter read with another phase (see below).

A work zone between the counter and a growing (110)^n block

Right of the counter the head works in a zone of 20 to 40 cells of words 10, 1000, 110, turning thousands of times between two increments of the counter; at the right end a block (110)^n grows by one word at each right record (for 2a, n = 12, 13, 14 at the right records at 4.7·108, 5.8·108 and 9.6·108 steps). What the work zone computes between two increments is not known.
