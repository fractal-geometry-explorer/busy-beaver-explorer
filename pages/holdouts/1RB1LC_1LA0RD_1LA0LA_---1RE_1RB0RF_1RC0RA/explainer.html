The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in D reading 0. D is entered only from B1 (writes 0, moves R): B>1 0. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

What it does

Defects drifting in a sea

Most of the tape is the block 1^5 0; the rightward sweep makes each into 110 110, the leftward sweep turns it back. Defect blocks (13, 4, 7, 21 ones) travel through the sea at their own speeds.

Can it halt?

Where a halt would come from

It halts from B>1 0; B reads a 1 about 103 million times in 109 steps, always with a 1 on its right. A halt would have to come from a defect interaction.

Where a halt can come from

If the machine halts on its third step from a configuration, that configuration is one of: A with 0 under the head, 1 right, 0 two right; E with 0 under the head, 1 right, 0 two right. So a run can reach a halt only through one of these patterns, and a proof that the machine never halts only has to rule them out. The proof splits on the state and the five cells around the head.
