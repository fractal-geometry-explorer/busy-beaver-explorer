What is known about 1RB1LC_1LA0RD_1LA0LA_---1RE_1RB0RF_1RC0RA

1RB1LC_1LA0RD_1LA0LA_---1RE_1RB0RF_1RC0RA

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. At a left turn the tape is mostly blocks 1^5 0 (374 of 461 blocks at 3·106 steps) with defects (blocks of 13, 4, 7, 21); at a right turn the sea reads (110)^k. The sweeps convert between the two forms by shift rules proved in Lean. The defects drift: different stretches of the tape move by 24, 36, 39 or 42 cells per round trip, so defects meet and interact.

Lean proves no halt in the first 107 steps (10,000 by the kernel alone); the C simulator finds none in 109.

How it works

The tape is a sea of the block 1^5 0, rewritten by each sweep into 110 110 and back. Defects in the sea (blocks of other lengths) drift at different speeds, so they meet and interact.

This is the kind of machine whose behaviour is a gas of particles on a periodic background. Halting needs B on a 1 with a 0 to its right, which has not happened in 109 steps.

The halting configurations

The machine stops exactly when it reads the symbol of a halting transition (D0); the generated statement covers every configuration.

step_none_iff, lean/Blank.lean

Where a halt can come from

If the machine halts on its third step from a configuration, that configuration is one of: A with 0 under the head, 1 right, 0 two right; E with 0 under the head, 1 right, 0 two right. So a run can reach a halt only through one of these patterns, and a proof that the machine never halts only has to rule them out. The proof splits on the state and the five cells around the head.

halt_route, lean/Route.lean (written by py/route.py)

The sweep rules

Shift rules of the two sweeps, for every repeat count and any tape around: going right D turns each block 111101 into 110110 and E each 111 into 101; going left A turns each 101101 back into 110111 and each 10 into 11.

Growth shape: sqrt/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.515). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.495).

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. A description of the defects (which words, how fast each moves, what a collision makes) would turn the run into a particle system; it has not been worked out.
