What is known about 1RB1RF_1LC1RE_0LD0LC_1RD0RB_1RA0RB_1RE---

1RB1RF_1LC1RE_0LD0LC_1RD0RB_1RA0RB_1RE---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. Its tape is a word of blocks of 1s on the left and a binary counter on the right; the head rewrites the word by a fixed map and counts. This page lists what has been established, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 12 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean (kernel) proves the six regular macro steps and the two main shapes of the anomaly for all parameter values, and no halt in the first 10,000 steps (107 with native_decide). Lean also shows that the pattern of the word and of the gaps is not an invariant by itself: a short Q configuration of the same form halts after 190 steps. Non-halting therefore depends on sizes: every collision cascade must end before the word runs out. In the simulated run it is always won, by at least 1.0 bits. Update, 12 November. Lean now also proves this machine's own form of the anomaly with a one-unit counter, 12 collision rules, and a partial macro map (a function from one word-and-counter configuration to the next, proved to be followed by the machine wherever it is defined). The map is far from complete: of the 270 collisions in the simulated run, 43 follow a proved rule. For the sister machine 1RB0RF_1LC1RE_0LD0LC_1RD0RB_1RA0RB_0LC--- the map is complete; here the collisions have many more cases, and they keep a misaligned gap alive instead of clearing it.

The six regular Q-steps

From W 0 1^(3a) B>0 R the machine reaches the next Q configuration after a step count linear in the parameters, in each of the six cases (last block 2j+5, 3, or a 1 after 2i+1; counter low part a+2, or one unit then t gaps of 2): shift rules generated by bbtools.symtm.prove.

q_big_wide … q_one_unit, lean/Rules.lean

Q-step = φ twice, counter plus one

The same six steps restated on Q configurations qc W a P T: the word goes to phi (phi W) (phi defined on lists) and the counter's low part and gaps update as a binary counter.

qstep_big_wide … qstep_one_unit, lean/Macro.lean

The anomaly, exactly

When the word has run out the machine rewrites it in one sweep, for every value of the parameters and every tape beyond: from 1^(2c+2) 0 1^(3(a+1)) B>0 R (blank to the left) it reaches 1^2 (0 1)^c 0 1^3 B>0 0^(3a) 1 R, one cell further left, in 3a+8c+17 steps; from the word 1^(2c+2) 0 1 with a+2 units it reaches the word 2 1…1 3 with a gap of 3a+1 in 3a+8c+46 steps. The new gap is the misaligned one (not of the form 3p+2).

anom_one, anom_two and anomaly_one, anomaly_two, lean/Transients.lean

The word-and-gap pattern is not an invariant

From the Q configuration 1^2 0 1^3 0 1^3 B>0 (a valid word 2 3, a one-unit counter, no gaps, blank tape) the machine halts at step 190. Its word is so short that it runs out a second time before the misaligned gap made by the first anomaly is cleared; the second anomaly leaves gaps of 1 and 0 in a row, and the collision on them halts. So no proof can use only the shape of the word and of the gaps (even/odd blocks, gaps ≡ 2 mod 3): it has to carry the sizes.

qc_short_halts, lean/Transients.lean

Pieces of the irregular steps

Five rules that hold for all six machines of the family and for every value of the parameters: the head rewrites the end of the word as φ applied twice and comes back, without reading the tape on either side (3 shapes of the word's end); and the way a regular step with a one-unit low part ends (next gap at least 3 cells, or blank tape).

wp_big, wp_three, wp_one, aph_unit, aph_unit_blank, lean/Collide.lean

The anomaly with a one-unit counter, for this machine

Word a block 2c+2 and a single 1, counter low part one unit followed by t gaps of length 2 and a gap of at least 4 cells or blank tape (2 rules, every t): the new word is 2 1…1 3, the low part becomes t+2 units and the next gap loses 4. This step passes an F event, so it differs between the six machines. These rules are statements about tapes; they are not yet part of the macro map below.

anomx_*, lean/Collide.lean (generated by py/gen_collide.py in 1RB0RF_1LC1RE_0LD0LC_1RD0RB_1RA0RB_0LC---)

Some collisions

A collision starts with the counter's low part one unit, then t gaps of length 2, then a misaligned gap of 0 or 1 cells. Misaligned gap 0, any number U of gaps of 2 behind it, next gap at least 5 cells or blank tape (12 rules): for t even a block 3 (U = 0) or 5 is appended and the low part becomes max(U, 1) units; for t odd three single 1s are appended and the low part becomes U+1 units. The next gap loses 5 cells in the first case and 4 in the others. Proved for every value of the parameters and any tape to the left.

coll_*, lean/Collide.lean; restated as zcoll_* in lean/CollideQ.lean

A partial macro map

A function astep that sends a word-and-counter configuration (word, low part, list of gap lengths) to the next one. It is defined for the regular steps, for the anomaly when the low part is wide enough, and for the collisions above; elsewhere it answers ‘no rule’. Lean proves that wherever it is defined the machine really goes from the first configuration to the second, in at least 17 steps. It does not cover the anomaly with a one-unit counter, and the very first configuration of the run from blank tape (step 27) is one, so this map cannot yet follow the run from the start: it is a building block, not a bound.

astep, astep_sound, lean/Full.lean (generated by py/gen_full.py in 1RB0RF_1LC1RE_0LD0LC_1RD0RB_1RA0RB_0LC---); notation in lean/Abstract.lean

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/fixed

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.038).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

How the other collisions differ

Every collision with misaligned gap 1 rewrites the word further left, so it is not of the form ‘append blocks to the word’; none of these is proved. Found by simulating each case for a few sizes; not proved.

Gap alignment

In every run the gaps after the first misaligned gap have length ≡ 2 (mod 3), at least 2. The halting patterns of the collisions that we found need a gap of length 0 there.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. What is missing, in order: (1) the remaining collision cases as Lean rules. Unlike in the 0LC machine, a collision here does not clear the misaligned gap: it shortens the next gap by 3 to 7 cells, which is misaligned in turn, so gaps of every small length occur and each needs its own case; in several cases the head also goes back into the word. A finer description (the configurations with an empty low part that follow an F event) would probably replace this case list by a few rules. (2) the one-unit anomaly and those rules inside the macro map, so that it follows the run from step 27. (3) the size argument: every cascade of collisions ends before the word runs out. For the 0LC machine, where (1) and (2) are done, (3) is the open part and looks like a Collatz-type question; the same is to be expected here.
