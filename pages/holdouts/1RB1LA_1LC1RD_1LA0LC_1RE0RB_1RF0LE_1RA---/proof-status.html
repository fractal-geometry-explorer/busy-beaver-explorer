1RB1LA_1LC1RD_1LA0LC_1RE0RB_1RF0LE_1RA---

OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. It belongs to a family of four holdouts that share their first four states, and works as two binary counters, one counting down in the blocks left of the head and one counting up in the gaps right of it. This page lists what is established, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 16 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean now proves the counter itself: read the tape as two binary numbers, E in the odd blocks left of the head and N in the gaps right of it, and every countdown of E, for every size of E and N, runs as the counter says (run3, run5). Lean also proves that the blank-tape run enters this counter after 256 steps and runs its first countdown. The pass rules (including the halting one) are proved too.

Coq now reduces the machine to the round map F of busycoq pull request 40 and proves that a halt needs an unmarked round (X odd, every 1 bit of X at an even position). Whether F ever reaches one is open: no invariant keeps the marker. F was iterated 160,000 rounds with no halt and never fewer than 8 marker bits.

The pass rules

Six rules on symbolic tapes, for all values of their parameters and any surrounding tape: a pass on an even top block (L 1 A>0 1^(2k+4) 0^(g+4) 1 R), on a block of 2, on an odd block crossing j small gaps, on a block of 3, a gap of length one closing, and pass_odd_halt: an odd pass whose D-run meets a gap one cell too short reaches F reading a 1, the only halting transition. Proofs by rfl steps and crossing lemmas, written by tools/py/bbtools/symtm.py.

lean/Rules.lean, holdouts/1RB1LA_1LC1RD_1LA0LC_1RE0RB_1RB0RF_0RB---/py/gen_rules.py

The counter, proved for every size

Write E in p bits on the left (from the head outward, a 0 bit is 1 1 and a 1 bit is 1 0, above a floor that starts with a 1) and N in n bits on the right (a 0 bit is 0^5, a 1 bit is 0^4 1, followed by any tape X). In mode 3 (top block 1^3) the machine takes (E, N) to (E − k, N + k) for every k ≤ E with N + k < 2n, in at least 26·k steps (run3); in mode 5 (top block 1^5) it takes (E, N) to (E − k, N + 2k) in at least 56·k steps (run5). Proofs: three pass-pair rules written by tools/py/bbtools/symtm.py, the binary decrement and increment on bit lists, and induction on k.

lean/Counter.lean, generated by holdouts/1RB1LA_1LC1RD_1LA0LC_1RE0RB_1RB0RF_0RB---/py/gen_counter.py

The blank-tape run enters the counter

After 256 steps from blank tape the machine is in mode 5 with E = 35 (6 bits) and N = 1 (init_counter), and it then counts E down to 0, reaching N = 71 after at least 1960 further steps (first_countdown).

A halt needs an unmarked round (Coq, on busycoq's definitions)

M1c_needs_unmarked: if halts TM93.tm' c0 (busycoq's halts and c0; TM93.tm' is this table), then some iterate of the round map F from (L, X) = (8, 145) is a round with X odd and div2 X unmarked: no 1 bit at an even position, so every 1 bit of X sits at an even position and X written in base 4 uses only the digits 0 and 1. F and the equivalence of the machine with it (F_spec, eqv_F_gen) are from busycoq pull request 40 by Andrew McKnight (open, not merged; the file is pinned at commit 069dc1e). Added here: F_marker (X odd and div2 X marked: F(L, X) is defined and odd, because F stops at the first even gap of div2 X, and a gap is even exactly when the 1 bit closing it is at an even position), F_None_even (F halts only on even X) and halt_needs_unmarked. The same file proves the statement for 1RB1LA_1LC1RD_1LA0LC_1RE0RB_1RF1RC_1RA---. Print Assumptions: closed under the global context. Coq 8.20.1 on busycoq's BB6 branch (commit 33209bc).

No halt before 7.28·104298 steps

The round-2 accelerator py/jump2.py (with c/ps.c, in the folder of 1a), run with the gap encoding of this machine (GB=4 GP=5) and the step constants K3=26,4,10 K5=56,4,10, simulates directly from pass start to pass start until the tape is a counter configuration of lean/Counter.lean, then jumps a whole countdown at once. Each jump is an instance of run3/run5, so its end tape is proved; only the Python reading of the tape, the step count of a jump (the fitted formula) and the direct simulation between jumps are unproved. Every jump of at most 108 steps was re-run by direct simulation and matched token for token. In 28 minutes on one core it made 1,664 jumps and reached step 7.28·104298 (E up to 14,274 bits) with no halt.

Defects stay single

In the 28.5 million passes that this run simulated directly (all the transition cascades between its jumps), the right part never held more than one gap of irregular size at a time, never more than 1 block of two or more 1s, and the left part never more than two even blocks.

The marker survives 160,000 rounds

F iterated from (8, 145) for 160,000 rounds with exact integers: no halt, X odd in every round, and after the fifth round div2 X always has at least 8 one bits at even positions (an unmarked round needs all of them gone at once). L, the length of the left counter, reaches 1.36 million digits. The longest run of rounds whose top gap is odd is 28.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/fixed

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.047).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

The phase transition

When E reaches 0 the floor block 1^(2s) under it is popped into the top block, the next pass pushes s single 1s, and a new countdown starts with E = 2s − 1 (s one bits; floor 1^2) in the other mode (3 and 5 alternate); N stays on the right, but the cut leaves a gap of irregular size in front of it. Irregular gaps then act in a cascade of short sub-phases: a gap one too long (for 1a/1b; the analogous residue mod 5 for 1c/1d) collapses into a block 1^2, a gap one too short followed by a single 1 is cut down and passes its defect on to the next gap; sometimes the whole of N is pulled to the left and N restarts at 0. The floor grows by 2 whenever E passes a power of two, so it is about twice the bit length of the largest E so far. Seen at every transition of the runs above; not proved, and no closed formula for the new (E, N) as a function of the old one is known.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. Coq reduces the question to the round map F: the machine halts exactly when F returns None, and that needs a round with X odd and div2 X unmarked (all 1 bits of X at even positions). Each round rewrites the low bits of X, so the marker is not obviously preserved; no invariant proving that it survives every round is known. In 160,000 rounds it never came close (at least 8 marker bits).
