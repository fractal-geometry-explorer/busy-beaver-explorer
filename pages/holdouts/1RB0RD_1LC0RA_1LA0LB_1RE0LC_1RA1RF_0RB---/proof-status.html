1RB0RD_1LC0RA_1LA0LB_1RE0LC_1RA1RF_0RB---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. With w = 1010110 and a = 10, the tape at a left extension during an active phase is 0 0 w^n ... a^k 1: each bounce adds one w at the left and removes one a from the right-end block. From step 302,136,397 to 2,126,686,397 the left counter goes 648 → 10648 while the right block goes 10159 → 159. When the block is used up the machine stops extending to the left for a long time (steps 2.17·109 to 3.85·109) and builds a new right block (6363 pairs) out of the middle of the tape, which is a hierarchy of repeated patterns ((a^2 w a^10 w)^m, (a^8 w a^40 w)^m, then a few large blocks). The sweeps are proved in Lean; the phase rule is observed, not proved.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: power/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like a power of the step count other than 1/2 (fitted exponent 0.659). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.486).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Draining phases

At left extensions from step 302,136,397 the tape ends ... w^n 1 a^k w with (n, k) = (648, 10159), (1048, 9759), ..., (10648, 159): n + k stays 10807 and one bounce moves one unit. A later phase starts at step 3,847,811,442 with a block of 6363 and drains the same way (n + k = 16925).
