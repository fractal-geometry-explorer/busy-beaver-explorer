What is known about 1RB0LC_1RC1RA_1RD0RF_0LE---_1LA1LE_0RA1RF

1RB0LC_1RC1RA_1RD0RF_0LE---_1LA1LE_0RA1RF

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves three passes over the regular parts of the tape and the halting situation (kernel), and no halt in the first 107 steps (native_decide); the C simulator finds no halt in 109 steps.

What it does. The head sweeps the whole tape. At the left-end turns the tape is 0 0, a few copies of 110, then a run qa of the 24-cell word q = 010110101101010110101101, a run sb of s = 101010110110110, runs of 10 whose lengths roughly double (5, 16, 38, 78, 157 at step 7,087,597), a run of 101110110110, and an irregular right end. The q-run gains a copy every few round trips while the material to its right is consumed; when it runs out the regions are rebuilt and q starts again from a few copies.

Where a halt would come from. Only D reading a 1 halts, and D is entered only from C reading a 0; so C>0 1 halts (halt_C). In the run D always reads a 0.

Every way to a halt

Two steps before any halt the machine is in B>0 0 1; no other configuration halts within three steps.

halt_route, lean/Route.lean (written by py/route.py)

Three passes

A moving right rewrites each copy of q (in the rotation 011010110101011010110101) as 100111001111100111001111, 32 steps a copy; C moving left rewrites the result back to q, 68 steps a copy; A moving right turns each 011 into 100. All for any number of copies.

pass_right_q, pass_left_q, pass_right_c, lean/Rules.lean

The halting situation

C>0 1 halts after two steps; D is entered only from C reading a 0.

halt_C, lean/Rules.lean

Growth shape: sqrt/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.539). Growth and rebuilding

At the left-end turns the q-run grows from 3 copies (561,704 steps) to 111 (4,263,440) while the s-run shrinks from 50 copies to 7; by 4.7·106 both are gone. Then q starts again (10 copies at 5.19·106), an s-run of 36 copies reappears at 5.9·106 and stays at 35 or 36 up to 1.2·107 steps while q grows to 109 and the region of 101110110110 further right shrinks.
