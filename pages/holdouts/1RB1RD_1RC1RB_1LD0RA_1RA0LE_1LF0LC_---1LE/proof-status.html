What is known about 1RB1RD_1RC1RB_1LD0RA_1RA0LE_1LF0LC_---1LE

1RB1RD_1RC1RB_1LD0RA_1RA0LE_1LF0LC_---1LE

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The tape is a list of counters, runs of the word 011 separated by short words. The last few counters, at the right end, trade amounts with each other and with a block that grows at the end; the older counters to the left are left almost unchanged. The transfer rule is not written down yet. An exact run-length simulation finds no halt in 1015 steps.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: fixed/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.603).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Active counters at the right end

The list keeps about 50–77 tokens; at each moment one of the last counters shrinks at a steady rate (about 1,500 per 106 visits) while the end block grows; the first counters (011410, 011255, ... at 106 visits) barely change.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The halting transition F0 is reached as D1 → 0LE, E0 → 1LF; a closed tape language from windows of 4 cells does not confine it to an edge, and the transfer rule of the counters is not known.
