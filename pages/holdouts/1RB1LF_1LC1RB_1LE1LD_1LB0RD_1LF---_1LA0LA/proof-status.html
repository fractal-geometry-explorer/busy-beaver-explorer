What is known about 1RB1LF_1LC1RB_1LE1LD_1LB0RD_1LF---_1LA0LA

1RB1LF_1LC1RB_1LE1LD_1LB0RD_1LF---_1LA0LA

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape. Seen in pairs of cells, each visit of the growing left end applies one recursive map to the tape; that map runs a counter in base 4 whose levels are long blocks of the word 111010. The counter itself can never halt; what the head does on the rare occasions it reaches the right end is not understood.

Status as of 12 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The map from one wall visit to the next is known exactly as a recursion on words of pairs, and the four phases of the counter are proved in Lean for every block length and every tape to the right. Direct simulation runs 2·1010 steps without a halt. But there are words of pairs on which a visit halts (proved in Lean), so everything depends on the words the right-end events leave behind.

The pair moves

With X = 10, Y = 11, O = 00: F moving left swaps X and Y and turns round on a blank pair (reflect, flipX0/1, flipY0/1); Y B> X X becomes <F Y Y Y (turn0/1); Y B> X Yd X becomes X Od-1 Y Y Y B> (digit1, digit). For every count and every surrounding tape.

Where a halt can come from

A closed tape language from windows of 6 cells (closed: yes) covers the run; the only places it lets the halting transition E1 fire are E reading a 1 with the abstract left side in a window of 0s, followed by at least six 1s. With windows of 4 and 8 the same picture.

An exact accelerated simulator

py/blocksim.py keeps the tape as runs of 111010 and crosses each run with a shift rule; at every visit of the left wall up to 2·106 steps its tape equals the C simulator's (py/bscheck.py).

The visit map is a recursion on words

V(S) = G(S) with G(Ya X X U) = Xa Y Y Y U, G(Ya X Yk X U) = Xa Y Gk(Y Y U), G(Ya) = Xa Y Y: checked against the machine on 20,000 random words and on every covered wall visit of the run from blank tape (the recursion does not cover the right-end events).

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The counter cannot halt; a right-end event could leave a halting word, and no rule for these events is known.

README

The right-end events

When the last level of the counter overflows the head reaches the blank right end in D; what follows (parity changes, blank stretches inside the tape, resets of the counter) has no known rule, and it decides whether a halting word ever appears.
