What is known about 1RB1LC_1LA1RE_0RD0LA_---1LB_1LD0RF_0RE1RB

1RB1LC_1LA1RE_0RD0LA_---1LB_1LD0RF_0RE1RB

OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Its run is a binary counter on the left, whose overflows come at doubling times, and a word of binary digits on the right through which each overflow moves a marker one block; when the marker reaches the end of the tape the word is rebuilt longer and the counter starts again.

Status as of 17 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves no halt in the first 1.2·1070 steps: a certificate written by the accelerated simulator is accepted by a checker whose soundness is checked by Lean's kernel (the check itself is one native_decide evaluation). The C simulator finds no halt in the first 1011 steps.

What it does. At each overflow of its left counter the tape is C>0 (1 0)^z W. Every shuttle of the head adds one to the z-digit counter L on the left and one to a binary counter R whose bits sit in fixed slots of W; when L overflows (z grows by one) R has gained 2z, and W changes by exactly one binary carry starting at slot z. Late in an epoch that carry moves a marker bit one block right per overflow, with gaps obeying D' = 2D + 2 exactly. When R overflows the word is rebuilt (steps 17,682,600, 5,745,422,543, 49,800,461,566).

Why it is hard. The counter laws are checked by Lean only for the digit layouts the run actually meets, not for all words, and the rebuilds are not understood. A halt needs F to read two 0s in a row, which on the right sweep means reaching the end of the tape in F: Lean proves a sweep entering the last block in E does that exactly when the block has length 1 mod 3. In 1.6·1045 steps the end is reached 83 times, never in F; why the rebuilt words keep it so is not proved.

A sound checker for long runs

A certificate is a list of plain runs and batches of counter ticks. For a batch the checker cuts the tape into the two counters by a given layout (and confirms the cut by rebuilding the tape), runs the left and right parts of one tick on windows holding only the low digits, for every carry length, and requires each to end with its digits incremented. Proved for any certificate: if the checker accepts it with step count N, the machine runs N steps from blank tape. The window runs fail rather than look outside their window, so their results hold for any surrounding tape; the step count of d ticks is in closed form.

checkCert_sound, lean/Cert.lean; wrun_sound, side_sound, tick, batch, lean/Tick.lean

The right sweep and the end of the tape

E> (111)^n reaches (011)^n E> on any tape; a last block 1^(3n+1) entered in E ends in D on a 0, where the machine halts, and blocks 1^(3n+2), 1^(3n+3) turn back in A or D on a 1.

sweep, edge_halt, lean/Edge.lean (generated by py/gen_edge.py); edge_stops, edge_B_runs, edge_E_runs, lean/EdgeHalt.lean

The overflow configurations of the run

All 144 moments in the first 1011 steps where C is at the left end on 0 1 0 have the shape (1 0)^z W with W of odd blocks; z rises by one from each to the next except at 16 restructures where it falls back to 1 or 2, the last three at steps 17,682,600, 5,745,422,543 and 49,800,461,566.

tmrun ... records, read in pairs of cells

The overflow law: one binary carry per overflow

For consecutive overflow events (z, W) and (z + 1, W') of the run, W' differs from W by one binary carry (digits p1 < … < pk, 1 → 0 at all but the last, 0 → 1 at the last) at 55 of the 106 pairs up to 2·109 steps (96 of 162 up to 5·1010); the other pairs are restructures and their aftermath. Re-running one event (step 17,857,444) with every z from 0 to 16 shows the carry always starts at a fixed slot for z (slots 2, 5, 8, 11, 18, 21, 24, …, 3 digits apart with a few separators): W holds a binary number R and an overflow at length z adds 2z.

py/overflows.py check, py/overflows.py slots

The marker and the doubling gaps

Late in an epoch W = P 111110 (110)^b 111 in digits (the marker is the single 1 bit of R's high part, cells 1^11 0); each overflow gives P 110 111110 (110)^(b-1) 111, and the gap D between overflows becomes exactly 2D + 2: 26 such moves and 23 consecutive pairs, all as predicted, in the first 5·1010 steps.

py/overflows.py check on tmrun ... at C L

Restructures are set off by R

From the event at step 9,008,202, re-run with z = 5..13, R overflows before L and the next event is a restructure after 3,441,111 to 3,453,981 steps, about the same for every z; what it writes depends on z (the digits of L are copied into the new word).

The accelerated simulator agrees with the direct run

py/accel.py learns the digits of the two counters and the step cost of a tick by carry lengths, and applies runs of ticks arithmetically; it gives the 126 overflow events of the first 2·1010 steps with exactly the step counts, z and W of tools/c/tmrun.c.

The right end of the tape in 1.6·1045 steps

In the accelerated run to 1.6·1045 steps (842 events, 23 rebuilds, the last at 1.47·1042, W grown to 782 digits) the head reaches blank tape at the right 83 times, in E (24) or B (59), never in F: three times an epoch, ending ...011011011, ...110110101, ...101101101.

heuristic Evidence only

Random words of the same shape halt

Started from C>0 (1 0)^z W with random short words W, the machine often halts within a hundred steps (in state D reading the 0 next to the last cell), so non-halting is a property of the words the run writes, not of the shape.

experiments with a direct simulator started from given tapes

Two counters on one clock

Between overflows the head shuttles around a point near the start of W; the turning points left of it follow the ruler sequence of a binary counter (L, digits as cell pairs 0 0/1 1) and those right of it the ruler sequence of a second one (R, the slots of W). The overflow law is what this predicts.

turning points of the head between steps 17,857,444 and 18,032,242

open Open

The restructures, and the question itself

Not known: the tick of the two counters for all words (Lean checks it only on the layouts of the run, so the overflow law for all z is not a theorem), the rule of a rebuild as a function of (z, L, W), why the rebuilt words are never entered by the right sweep in a way that reaches the end of the tape in F, and whether the machine halts.
