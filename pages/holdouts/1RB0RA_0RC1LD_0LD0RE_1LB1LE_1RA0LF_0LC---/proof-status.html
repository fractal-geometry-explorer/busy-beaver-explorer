1RB0RA_0RC1LD_0LD0RE_1LB1LE_1RA0LF_0LC---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Read in four-cell words (d = 0100, c = 0111), the tape is a list of alternating runs. Near the left the run lengths are nearly all 1 mod 3, further right nearly all multiples of 3, and between them a stretch repeats the lengths 10, 4, 13, 10, 4, 13. Three leftward crossings of runs are proved in Lean. The way the lengths change is not worked out, so halting (F reading a 1) is not ruled out.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: power/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.474). Right of the start it grows like a power of the step count other than 1/2 (fitted exponent 0.665).

Proposed family: F06, block-list counters

With repeats of single words only, the number of pieces is proportional to the tape (21,095 level-1 tokens for 183,249 cells at 1.6·1010 steps). Most of them are one block of long period: [(0111)^10 (0100)^4 (0111)^13 (0100)^10 (0111)^4 (0100)^13]^N with N = 264 at the right-end record at 4.1·109 steps and N = 595 at 1.6·1010 (a period of 216 cells). With such groups counted once, the description is constant: 588, 564 and 588 level-2 tokens at the left-end records at 109, 4.1·109 and 1.6·1010 steps, and 837 at each of the three right-end records. Right of the block the tape is a frozen list of runs of growing lengths (16, 7, 22, 28, 22, 22, 16, 25, … up to 149) and then a list of units (1110)^a (1000)^3 111 (0100)^b (0111)^3 01 000 with (a, b) = (18, 15), (66, 36), (51, 21), (57, 9), … at the left-end record at 1.6·1010 steps; the right-end record at 4.1·109 steps shows the same units with b one smaller. The table is that of 1RB0LF_1RC0RB_0RD1LE_0LE0RA_1LC1LA_0LD--- with the states renamed (related.json), from another start. The two runs are different and nothing is claimed about one from the other. A list of units that changes slowly while a periodic block grows behind it is read here as a list of counters: F06. The round rule is not known. Because the description outside the block is constant over a factor of 16 in steps, it is also worth one try of the F01 closure with the 216-cell word as the block.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The machine halts only when F reads a 1. F is entered from E on a 1 (writing 0, moving left). The sweeps handle runs in a way that keeps the mod-3 pattern in the samples, but this is not proved, and an exception could put F on a 1. CTL (windows of 2 to 5 cells) and NGramCPS (widths 2 to 8, history, LRU) fail.
