OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

Open. The run is a sequence of cycles, from one block of n ones to the next: n = 2, 42, 160, …, 71,144,918 so far (14 cycles, no halt to 2.8·1016 steps). Proved in Lean for every size: the ones become copies of 01, an inner counter eats them from the right (two copies and two ones become three copies, the ones are refilled from the copies), and at the end the copies become ones again. Between those, a cycle may pass through E-phases, in which the same kind of counter runs through a stretch of zeros and spawns sub-counters; that part is not decoded.

The inner counter, exactly

After the sweep the tape is 1 (01)^N 0 0, N = n+1, head in F at the right end, and the inner counter starts: X(p, q, r) = 0^inf 1 (01)^p 1 B> 1 (01)^q (11)^r 0^inf, a head block at the right end that eats the copies of 01 on its left. A round takes X(p+2, q, r+1) to X(p, q+3, r) (two copies and two ones become three copies); with no ones left, X(p+1, q, 0) restarts as X(p, 1, q+1). When the copies on the left are used up (p = 0 or 1) the head runs to the left end: A> 0 x (01)^m (11)^s with (x, m, s) = g(N, 1, 0), g a recursion on p. Proved for every N: from C(2x+2) the machine reaches that configuration (fphase); this part of a cycle cannot halt.

fphase, xrun, g, lean/Counter.lean; the rules y_run, x_start, x_round, x_restart, x_end0, x_end1, x_end00 in lean/Rules.lean

Rounds of the head block in other surroundings

With zeros on its left the head block eats two zeros per round: L 0 0 0 1 B> 1 (01)^q 1 1 R reaches L 0 1 B> 1 (01)^(q+2) R (z_round); with blank tape on its left the tape grows: b_round. And 0^inf 1 (01)^m 0 A> reaches C(2m+4) (to_c).

What happens at the left end

From A> 0 x (01)^m 1^k (k even): if x = 0 and m is even the inner counter starts again with N = m+k+3; if x = 0 and m is odd an E-phase starts from 1 (01)^(m+2) 0^(k+2) 01 E>; if x = 1 the cycle ends, 1 (01)^(m+k) 0 A> and then C(2(m+k)+4). After an E-phase (k odd or even, x = 1 or none): k even ends the cycle, k odd restarts the counter with N = m+k−1. Checked on all 35 such events of the run to 2.8·1016 steps, and g on all 25 counter starts.

Does it halt from blank tape?

The inner counter and the outer parts of a cycle are proved and cannot halt; the transitions at the left end are checked on the run. What is left is the E-phase: the head block running through a stretch of zeros, where each run-out of ones plants a new block of copies of 01 inside the zeros, on which a counter of the same kind runs, and so on. It is a recursive counter of the same family, and it is where a halt (D reads 1) could be.
