What is known about 1RB0LF_1RC1RB_1LD0RA_1LA0LE_0LC1LC_1LE---

1RB0LF_1RC1RB_1LD0RA_1LA0LE_0LC1LC_1LE---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The head sweeps the whole tape. Its background is 0111 (after a sweep to the right) or the period-8 word 01001111 (after a sweep to the left), and the sweeps over it in both directions are proved shift rules. A handful of short defects split the background into long domains, and how the defects move and grow has not been made into a rule. Lean proves no halt in the first 10,000 steps by the kernel alone and in the first 107 with native_decide, and proves the shift rules of the sweeps (lean/Rules.lean); the C simulator finds no halt in the first 109 steps.

The halting configurations

The machine stops exactly when it reads the symbol of a halting transition (F1); the generated statement covers every configuration.

step_none_iff, lean/Blank.lean

Where a halt can come from

If the machine halts on its third step from a configuration, that configuration is one of: D with 1 two left, 1 left, 0 under the head. So a run can reach a halt only through one of these patterns, and a proof that the machine never halts only has to rule them out. The proof splits on the state and the five cells around the head.

halt_route, lean/Route.lean (written by py/route.py)

No halt in the first 10,000 steps

The list simulator of the shared library, proved to agree with the machine's semantics, runs 10,000 steps from blank tape without a halt, evaluated by Lean's kernel.

Shift rules of the sweeps

The sweeps over the background: rightward L B> (01001111)^n R and L 1 C> (10011110)^n R (the left-sweep form becomes (0111)^2 per copy), leftward L (11010011)^n D>1 R and L (10100111)^n C>0 R (back to the period-8 form), for every n and all tapes L, R.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: power/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.495). Right of the start it grows like a power of the step count other than 1/2 (fitted exponent 0.288).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

A few long domains

At step 394,721,780 (width 19,836) the tape is (1110)^3460, (0111)^200, (0111)^1206, (0111)^74 and short defects such as 1^8 0 1^3 0 1^6 or 1^17; at step 1,484,459,896 (width 39,113) the domains are 1676, 6498, 10, 552, 1018 copies long. The number of defects stays small (four to six); their motion was not reduced to a rule.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The machine halts on F1, reached as 1 A>1 (A reads 1, moves left onto a 1). The sweeps over the background never produce it; a halt would have to come from a defect, and the defects have no known rule.
