Does this machine ever halt?

1RB0RA_1LC1RA_0LD1LD_0LE0LC_1LA0LF_---1LC

OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows. The tape is a row of units, each a pair of counters and a block of 1s. A unit with blank cells to its left pours one counter into the other; when it is done it turns into a wall and hands its block of 1s to the next unit as blank cells, and the same happens there, level below level. Lean proves each of these moves for all sizes. The joints between units come in hundreds of small cases that nobody has written down, and the halting question hides there. No halt in 5.1·1036 steps.

The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in F reading 0. F is entered only from E1 (writes 0, moves L): 0 E>1. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

What the tape is made of

Units: two counters and a block of 1s

The tape is a row of units. A unit is (10)^x 00 (10)^y 110 0 1^w 0: a counter x written as x pairs 10, a counter y written the same way, and a block of w ones. Only one unit is running at any time; the others wait.

The running unit needs blank cells to its left. In one cycle (the head sweeps the unit twice) it moves one pair out of y, puts three pairs into x and two 1s into w, and grows six cells into the blank:

So with 6k blank cells it does k cycles: (x, y+k, w) → (x+3k, y, w+2k), in k(8x+4y+16) + 14k2 steps. The head never looks past the w-block and never further left than the six cells, which is why the rule holds anywhere on the tape. At the very left of the tape the blank is endless and the unit runs until y is 0.

Emergent behaviour

Walls and gaps, level below level

A unit with y = 0 cannot cycle. It turns: its x pairs become a solid wall of 2x+10 ones, and the head erases the w-block on its way right. Those erased cells, about w of them, are now blank cells in front of the next unit, which starts to run its own phase in them. If that unit reaches y = 0 before its gap is used up, it turns too, and the unit after it runs. The tape becomes a stack: wall, gap, wall, gap, …, running unit.

When a gap is used up the running unit freezes where it is and the head walks back to the wall on its left. The wall turns back into pairs and becomes a unit again: (10)^(x+1) 11 000 becomes the unit with x = 1, y = x and w = 2 (the return). So what a unit collected in x during one phase is what it has to pour during the next. That unit then runs on in whatever is left of its own gap.

There is one more move: a unit that has 1s to its left instead of blank cells (the wall itself) runs a slower cycle that takes two 1s from the wall and gives itself one pair: (x, y+1, w) → (x+1, y, w+2). A unit can eat into the wall of the level above it this way.

The left end

Main phases: y → 3y + 4, until it is not

At the left end of the tape the phase always completes. A main phase starts as the unit (1, y, w), ends as (3y+1, 0, w+2y), turns into a wall of 6y+12 ones, and the head goes off to the right. If the wall is untouched when the head comes back, the return makes it the unit (1, 3y+4, 2): the next main phase.

Then, on the trip after the 17th, the unit right of the wall went on against the wall with the slower cycle 200,334,327 times, and the 18th main phase started with 200,334,327 pairs fewer. After that the pattern holds again up to the 37th phase, as far as the simulator goes. Nothing says when it breaks next: the levels below feed back into the top one.

What is not understood

Where units meet

The four moves above are exact and proved. What is missing is what happens at the joints: how the head enters a unit after a turn, in which half of its cycle a unit freezes when fewer than six blank cells are left, and when it goes on against the wall instead. These depend on a few cells: how much of the gap is left (0 to 5 cells), whether w is 2 or 3, whether x is 0.

Sorting every left turn of the head by the runs of cells around it (with every long run counted as “long”) gives 387 different pictures in 1.5 million turns up to 1028 steps, and the same 387 already at 1020. So the machine very likely is a finite set of rules on the list of units, but nobody has written them down, and without them there is no way to argue about all of the run at once.

Where it could halt

Where it could halt

The only missing transition is F reading 0. F is entered only from E reading 1, and E only from D reading 0. Going left, C and D alternate: C leaves its cell alone, D turns a 1 into a 0. The machine halts when such a sweep finds 0 1 0 just left of a cell C passed. For example, C arriving at the right end of 10 10 1^m halts when m is odd and passes when m is even.

So halting is a matter of parity at the joints of the units. In 5.1·1036 steps the parity was always right. A proof would need the rules of the joints and an invariant that keeps it right.

Status

What is proved

Lean (kernel): the cycle with six blank cells, the cycle against a wall of 1s, k cycles of either at once with the exact step count, the turn and the return, each for every size and every tape around it (lean/Gap.lean, lean/Levels.lean); the cycle and phase at the left end (lean/Rules.lean, lean/Phase.lean). Lean (native_decide): blank tape reaches the first three main phases (steps 173, 1,225, 5,625) and each reaches its wall (lean/Reach.lean); no halt in 107 steps (lean/Blank.lean). Computed: no halt in 5.1·1036 steps (c/macro.c, tested against the plain simulator). Heuristic: the main phase table, the account of the 18th phase, the 387 kinds of joint. Open: the rules of the joints and the halting question.

Where units meet

How a unit is entered after a turn, how it freezes when its blank cells run out and when it goes on against the wall depend on a few cells at the joints. Classing every left turn of the head by the cell runs around it gives 387 classes in 1.5 million events up to 1028 steps, the same 387 as up to 1020. They have not been written down as rules.

Open
Does it halt from blank tape?

The only halting transition is F reading 0: a left sweep (C keeps a cell, D erases a 1, alternately) that finds 0 1 0 just left of a cell C passed, for example C arriving on the right end of (10)^2 1^m with m odd. Whether that can happen is a question about parities at the joints of the units, for which there is no macro machine yet.
