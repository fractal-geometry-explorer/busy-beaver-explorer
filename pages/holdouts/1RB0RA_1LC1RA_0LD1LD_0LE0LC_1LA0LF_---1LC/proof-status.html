What is known about 1RB0RA_1LC1RA_0LD1LD_0LE0LC_1LA0LF_---1LC

1RB0RA_1LC1RA_0LD1LD_0LE0LC_1LA0LF_---1LC

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 12 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves, for every size and with any tape around them, the four moves of a unit (10)^x 00 (10)^y 110 0 1^w 0: its cycle with six blank cells to the left, its slower cycle against a wall of 1s, its turn into a wall when y is 0, and the return that makes a wall a unit again. What happens where two units meet is known only from simulation. An exact run-length simulator with phase jumps runs 5.1·1036 steps without a halt.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Chaotic Fractal Type 1. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

The cycle and the phase at the left end

P x y w R is A>0 (10)^x 00 (10)^y 110 0 1^w 0 R with blank tape to the left. One cycle: P x (y+1) w R reaches P (x+3) y (w+2) R in 8x + 4(y+1) + 26 steps, for every x, y, w and every tape R. A whole phase: P x y w R reaches P (x+3y) 0 (w+2y) R in pcost x y steps.

cyc_ee, cyc_eo, cyc_oe, cyc_oo, lean/Rules.lean; cycle, phase, lean/Phase.lean

The phase at any depth: six blank cells per cycle

With U x y w = (10)^x 00 (10)^y 110 0 1^w 0: 0^(6k) A>0 U x (y+k) w reaches A>0 U (x+3k) y (w+2k) in k(8x+4y+16) + 14k2 steps, for every x, y, w, k, whatever is left of the 6k zeros and right of the unit.

g_cycle, lean/Gap.lean (py/gen_gap.py); cycle_gap, phase_gap, lean/Levels.lean

The phase against a wall of 1s: two 1s per cycle

With the head on the last of 2k+2 ones, U (x+1) (y+k+1) w right of it: after (k+1)(8x+4y+24) + 6(k+1)2 steps the ones are gone, the unit is U (x+k+2) y (w+2k+2) and the head is on the cell that was left of the ones, whatever it holds.

g_q, lean/Gap.lean; cycle_ones, phase_ones, lean/Levels.lean

The turn and the return

Turn: 0^9 A>0 U x 0 w R reaches, in 14x + 60 steps, a wall of 2x+10 ones with the head on the last of them, followed by 1 0 1^(w+4) 0 R. Return: 0^4 A>0 (10)^(x+1) 11 000 R reaches A>0 U 1 x 2 R in 4x + 16 steps. At the left end of the tape: a main phase P 1 y w R reaches the wall of 6y+12 ones, and A>0 (10)^(x+1) 11 000 R reaches the main phase P 1 x 2 R.

g_turn, g_ret, lean/Gap.lean; turn, ret, main_to_turn, main_from_ret, lean/Levels.lean

No halt in the first 5.1·1036 steps

c/macro.c keeps the tape as runs of 0, 1 and 10, crosses a run in one move and jumps over k cycles of a unit with the two phase rules proved in Lean. It runs 5,135,031,925,650,943,510,945,361,186,015,609,585 steps (37 main phases, 495 million moves, 8 seconds) without a halt; 64-bit counters stop it there. Tests: the whole tape equals the plain simulator's at 20 stops up to step 2,971,093; the main phase starts equal the plain simulator's up to 3.3·106 steps and agree with and without jumps up to 1015 steps (once, by hand, up to 1.3·1018).

The main phases: y → 3y + 4, with an exception

The phases at the left end that start with x = 1 have y = 4, 14, 46, 142, 430, …: from the second on y → 3y + 4, up to the 37th (y = 567,663,852,794,517,601, step 6.2·1035), except from the 17th (y = 229,582,510) to the 18th (y = 488,413,207 = 3y + 4 − 200,334,327). There the unit right of the wall went on against the wall with the slower cycle and took 200,334,327 pairs from it. So y → 3y + 4 is not a law of the machine.

Where units meet

How a unit is entered after a turn, how it freezes when its blank cells run out and when it goes on against the wall depend on a few cells at the joints. Classing every left turn of the head by the cell runs around it gives 387 classes in 1.5 million events up to 1028 steps, the same 387 as up to 1020. They have not been written down as rules.

Does it halt from blank tape?

The only halting transition is F reading 0: a left sweep (C keeps a cell, D erases a 1, alternately) that finds 0 1 0 just left of a cell C passed, for example C arriving on the right end of (10)^2 1^m with m odd. Whether that can happen is a question about parities at the joints of the units, for which there is no macro machine yet.
