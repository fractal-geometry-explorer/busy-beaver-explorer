The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in F reading 0. F is entered only from E0 (writes 1, moves R): E>0 0. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

The shape of the run

Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.51).

After 109 steps the tape spans 20,511 cells (4,829 left of the start, 10,281 right), with a density of 1s of 0.698.

What it does

A bouncer and its excursions

Most of the time the head bounces between the right end and a wall inside the tape; the wall creeps. Occasionally the head goes into the left part in a zigzag, and the left edge jumps out by 30, 60 or 90 cells.

Can it halt?

Where a halt would come from

It halts from E>0 0; E reads a 0 about 29 million times in 109 steps, always with a 1 on its right.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The bouncing phase looks provable with shift rules (a wall moving by a fixed amount per bounce); what ends it and what the zigzag phase does to the left part are not understood.
