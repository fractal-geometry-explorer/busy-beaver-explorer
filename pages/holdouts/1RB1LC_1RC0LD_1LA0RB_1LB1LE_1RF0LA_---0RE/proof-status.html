What is known about 1RB1LC_1RC0LD_1LA0RB_1LB1LE_1RF0LA_---0RE

1RB1LC_1RC0LD_1LA0RB_1LB1LE_1RF0LA_---0RE

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open, but it is now a question about numbers. Lean proves (kernel only) that the machine halts from blank tape if and only if the orbit of 22 under an explicit map reaches a halt: halts_iff in lean/Orbit.lean. The map is N → 3N + 24 + 6j − 20·2j, and it halts exactly when the remainder mj = N + 10 − 10·2j + 2j is 0. 106 rounds of the map do not halt; no residue class rules a halt out, and the chance of a halt later is heuristically below 10−200000.

The halting configurations

The machine stops exactly when it reads the symbol of a halting transition (F0); the generated statement covers every configuration.

No halt in the first 10,000 steps

The list simulator of the shared library, proved to agree with the machine's semantics, runs 10,000 steps from blank tape without a halt, evaluated by Lean's kernel.

blank_runs_kernel, lean/Blank.lean

One-pass rules

Nine rules, each for all values of its counts: the first pass and the zigzag of a doubling, its last pass, the three ways a doubling phase can run out of 1s, the transfer of two 1s into a pair 10, the end of a round and the halting case.

lean/Rules.lean, generated by py/gen_rules.py with bbtools.symtm

A doubling, for every size

From state D at the left end of (10)^(K+1) 1^(c+2K+8) the machine reaches state D at the left end of (10)^(2K+6) 1^c (the zigzag chained by induction).

double, lean/Orbit.lean

The end of a round

From (10)^K 1^m with m even and 2 ≤ m < 2K+6 the machine reaches 10 1^(2K+3m+2); from (10)^K with no 1s (K ≥ 1) it halts.

finish, halt_zero, phase_spec, lean/Orbit.lean

Halts if and only if the orbit of 22 hits a halt

From blank tape the machine reaches D> 10 1^22 after 208 steps, and it halts from blank tape if and only if some orbit i = none, where orbit iterates next N = phase 1 N from 22.

halts_iff, lean/Orbit.lean (axioms: propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound)

The closed form of the map, checked against the machine

With j the number of doublings (the least j ≥ 1 with 20·2j > N + 12 + 2j), the remainder is m = N + 10 − 10·2j + 2j and N' = 3N + 24 + 6j − 20·2j. The C program runs the machine from every even N from 8 to 3000 and agrees with this formula every time, including the halts at N = 8, 26, 64, 142, 300, 618, 1256, 2534 (the values N = 10·2j − 2j − 10).

c/check_map.c, py/orbit.py

106 rounds without a halt

Iterating the map from 22 with exact integers: no halt in the first 106 rounds; N then has 666,845 bits. The closest approach of m to 0, relative to its range 10·2j, is 3.8·10−6 (round 736,098), about what uniform sampling predicts for a million rounds.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.538). Right of the start it grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.084).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Probviously nonhalting

m is spread over a range of about 10·2j values (even ones only), and its residues mod small numbers look uniform along the orbit, so round i halts with chance about 1/(5·2j); j grows by about 0.67 per round. After the rounds already checked (j is then about 667,000), the total chance of a halt is below 10−200000.
