What is known about 1RB0RF_0LC1RE_---1LD_1LE0LB_0RF0LD_1RD1RA

1RB0RF_0LC1RE_---1LD_1LE0LB_0RF0LD_1RD1RA

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves the odd branch of the phase map for every odd e: C(2i+1) → C(3i+5), and the first, halving stage of the even branch for every even e ≥ 4: C(2i+4) reaches E> 110 (10)i 1100 (10)i+4. The rest of the even branch was tabulated by simulation for e ≤ 300: e ≡ 0 (mod 4) goes to (3e+6)/2, e ≡ 6 (mod 8) to (3e+12)/2, e ≡ 2 (mod 8) recursively, and e = 2k − 6 halts. Whether the blank orbit ever hits a halting value is the open question.

The odd branch of the map

For every i: C(2i+1) → C(3i+5), i.e. e ↦ (3e+7)/2 for odd e, through P(a,b) = 0∞ 111 (0111)a 01 E> 0 (01)b: C(2i+1) → P(i,2), P(a+1,b) → P(a,b+3) in 6b+10 steps, P(0,b) → C(b+3).

odd_step, rounds, enter_pack, finish_P, lean/Macro.lean; rules enter, pack, round, finish in lean/Rules.lean

The even branch: the halving stage

For every i, C(2i+4) reaches 0∞ E> 1 1 0 (10)i 1 1 0 0 (10)i+4: the 01-run is packed into 0111 words (even_pack), and then each 0111 becomes one 10 on each side of a marker 1100 (half0, half1, half, by induction halving). The next pass packs these 10s in pairs, so the continuation depends on the parity of i, which is the split e ≡ 0 / 2 (mod 4). Two later building blocks are proved as rules: E going left turns a 0111 word into three more 10s of the right-hand run (absorb_1011) and takes in a single 10 (absorb_10); chaining them into the whole even map is not done.

even_start, halving, lean/Even.lean; rules in lean/Rules.lean

Where it can halt

Only C reading 0 halts; C is entered only from B reading 0 (0LC), so the machine halts exactly on 0 B> 0. Read off the table.

the table; step_none_iff, lean/Blank.lean

The even branch, tabulated

By simulation from C(e) for e = 1..300 (c/phasemap.c): odd e gives (3e+7)/2 every time (now proved); e ≡ 0 (mod 4), e ≥ 8, gives (3e+6)/2; e ≡ 6 (mod 8) gives (3e+12)/2; e ≡ 2 (mod 8) has no single formula (a cascade that halves the 01-run, e.g. 18 → 41, 74 → 101, 90 → 167); the machine halts from C(e) exactly for e = 2, 10, 26, 58, 122, 250 in that range, and also for 506 and 1018: e = 2k − 6.

Why it is hard

Like a Collatz map: the orbit grows by about 3/2 per phase, and it halts only if it lands on 2k − 6 (or on another halting value of the even cascade). A random-looking orbit hits such a sparse set with small probability, so it probably never halts, but nothing proves that.

README

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.04). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.552).

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. Lean proves the odd branch of the map; the even branch (and the halting values 2k − 6) are from simulation, and whether the blank orbit, now past C(70796), ever reaches a halting value is a Collatz-like question.
