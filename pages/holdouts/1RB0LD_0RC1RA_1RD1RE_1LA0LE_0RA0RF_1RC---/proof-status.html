1RB0LD_0RC1RA_1RD1RE_1LA0LE_0RA0RF_1RC---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves the trip rule and the seven rules that end an epoch in the common cases, for every size (lean/Rules.lean). Followed with these rules, the run from blank tape has not halted in 1039,417 steps: 37,692 epochs, all but one of which are instances of the proved rules. Whether it ever halts depends on the residues modulo 3 of numbers that grow by about 10/3 per epoch, a Collatz-like question.

The trip and the start

1^3 (01)^(m+1) C> at the right end (blank to the right) becomes (01)^(m+6) C> in 6m + 45 steps, whatever lies further left; by induction, 1^(3k) (01)^(m+1) C> becomes (01)^(m+1+5k) C> in k(6m + 30) + 15k² steps. From blank tape the machine reaches (01)^9 C> in 89 steps.

trip, trips, start, lean/Rules.lean

The epoch ends, in the common cases

With level 1 = 1^r (01)^M, r = 0 or 2: zero_blank, zero_dec, zero_merge (r = 0: level 1 becomes 1^(2M+6) (01)^3 or 1^(2M+7) (01)^3, taking a 01 or the ones of level 2), two_blank, two_trip, two_dec, two_merge (r = 2: level 2 takes one step and level 1 becomes 1^(2M-4) (01)^3 or 1^(2M-8) (01)^3). Each holds for every M, the sizes of the other levels and the rest of the tape, with the exact step count. Not covered: a carry from level 2 into level 3, and level-2 runs of 01 shorter than 4.

The rule at every level

A level k ≥ 2 changes only when the level below it ends an epoch with two ones left. It then takes one step: a trip, 1^(n+3) (01)^m to 1^n (01)^(m+7); or, with no ones, a rebuild to 1^(2m+5) (01)^6 (or 1^(2m+4+p) (01)^6), taking from level k+1; or, with two ones, a carry into level k+1 and a rebuild to 1^(2m-6) (01)^6 after a trip there, 1^(2m-10) (01)^6 otherwise. A level reduced to a single one is the same tape as one more 01 on the level above (a pop). Observed in every simulated case with m ≥ 5; smaller m behaves differently and is not tabulated.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The machine halts only when state F reads a 1; no proved rule does that, and plain simulation of all 8,064 event configurations with up to two small extra levels found no halt. A proof of non-halting would need rules for every case (the carries and small levels too) closing over all stacks that can arise; the order in which the cases come is set by sizes modulo 3.
