What is known about 1RB1RD_1LC0RF_1LD0LB_1RE0RB_---1RA_0LA1RC

1RB1RD_1LC0RF_1LD0LB_1RE0RB_---1RA_0LA1RC

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The tape is a list of levels, each a run of ones 1X followed by 1111110 00 and a run of words (1111110)Y. On the active level each cycle removes one word and adds 15 ones; when the words run out the head rebuilds the ones into a new run of words, about one per 7 ones. Lean proves that the machine can only halt at the right end of the tape, where the active level works.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Quadratic Mess. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

A closed tape language from windows of 4 cells covers the run from blank tape; in it the halting transition E0 can only fire with blank tape from the head on: the machine halts iff E steps onto the blank region right of the tape (after D0 → 1RE on the last cell's neighbour).

heuristic Evidence only

The cycle of the active level

Every 8 visits of the right end, X + 15 and Y − 1 on the last level, until Y = 0; read off direct runs to 1.2·1010 steps.

Rebuilds at about 7 ones per word

When a level is used up the ones are rebuilt into words; sizes grow by about 15/7 per round.

Growth shape: fixed/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.495).

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The level cycle and the rebuild rule are read off the run, not proved; a halt needs D at the right end, which depends on the remainders of the rebuilds.
