What is known about 1RB1LE_1LC1RC_1LA1RD_1LD0RB_0LC0LF_0LB---

1RB1LE_1LC1RC_1LA1RD_1LD0RB_0LC0LF_0LB---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 13 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The machine's cycle is understood (a block of ones multiplied by about 3/2, with a remainder mod 6 written into a short tail); the two sub-rounds are proved in Lean for all counts, the cycle map is computed, and the run has been followed to 1013 steps without a halt. Which tails could lead to the halting pattern is not known.

The cycle map

From an S-state B>0 0 0 1^X 0 T the next S-state has X' = (3X + c)/2, with c and the new tail depending on X mod 6 and on T (table on the explainer); checked by direct simulation from constructed tapes, and along the run from blank tape to step 3.6·108.

py/smap.py, c/sstate.c

No halt in 1013 steps

The accelerated simulator of the shared tools (cycle rules proved by symbolic simulation) runs 1013 steps without a halt; the S-state at step 2,710,634,500,213 has X = 2,125,526.

tools/py/bbtools/cycles.py

Rounds between end events, by remainder class

Sample the run whenever the head is in state A reading 1 with only 0s to its left. Read rightwards from the head, the tape is then A U^m B: one long periodic block between two short context words. For each class (A, U, B, m modulo 1 or 2 (the smallest modulus that works for the context)) the next sample is simulated for six values of m and extended as an affine map of m (with a cubic step count). Along the direct run from blank tape to 3·109 steps (38 events) the class map predicts the next event in all 26 rounds it applies to. This is an extrapolation checked by computation, not a proof.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.494). Right of the start it grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.071).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

How it could halt

The machine halts exactly in state C on a 0 with three 1s to its left. None of the S-states tried (all X mod 6, all tails of at most three blocks of at most 9 ones) halts before the next S-state, so halting would need a tail not seen yet.

100,179 rounds of the class map without a halt

Iterated with exact integers from the event at step 32,285 of the run (A = (empty), U = 110, m = 81, B = 13 0 16): 100,179 rounds without meeting a halting class; m then has 29,307 bits (about 1017,645 steps of the machine). 204 distinct contexts occur, the longest of 4,246 cells. The iteration stops there, at a context too large for the six simulations (a second long block has formed); this is a limit of the tool, not an event of the machine.

No halting class among 530 contexts

Breadth first from the starting context, with every remainder at every context (whether or not the numbers can really produce that sequence of remainders, and only for m large compared with the contexts): 530 contexts expanded, 530 found, to depth 184.
