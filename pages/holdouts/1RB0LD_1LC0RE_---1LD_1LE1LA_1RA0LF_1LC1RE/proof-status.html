What is known about 1RB0LD_1LC0RE_---1LD_1LE1LA_1RA0LF_1LC1RE

1RB0LD_1LC0RE_---1LD_1LE1LA_1RA0LF_1LC1RE

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves the head's fast moves through the background and the halting situations (kernel), and no halt in the first 107 steps (native_decide); the C simulator finds no halt in 109 steps.

What it does. The tape is the alternating pattern 1010… with defects: places where two equal cells meet (11, 111, 1111, 00). About 16% of neighbouring pairs are 11 and about 4% are 00, and these fractions hardly change as the tape grows. The head does not sweep cleanly: it crosses the tape in zig-zags of tens to hundreds of cells, moving the defects around, and no regular word or counter shows up.

Where a halt would come from. Only C reading a 0 halts. C is entered from B and from F reading a 0 (each writes a 1 and steps left), so 0 B>0 and 0 F>0 halt (halt_B, halt_F). In the run, whenever B or F reads a 0 the cell to its left is a 1.

No halt in the first 10,000 steps

The list simulator of the shared library, proved to agree with the machine's semantics, runs 10,000 steps from blank tape without a halt, evaluated by Lean's kernel.

blank_runs_kernel, lean/Blank.lean

Every way to a halt

Two steps before any halt the machine is in 0 0 E>1 (so 0 B>0 never happens: B is entered from A, which leaves a 1 on its left); no other configuration halts within three steps.

halt_route, lean/Route.lean (written by py/route.py)

Fast moves

Moving right, A turns each 0101 of the background into 1101 and B turns each 1010 into 1011, eight steps a copy, for any number of copies.

fill_A, fill_B, lean/Rules.lean

The halting situations

0 B>0 and 0 F>0 halt after two steps; these are the only ways into C reading a 0.

halt_B, halt_F, lean/Rules.lean

Growth shape: sqrt/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.506). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.493).
