OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 13 November 2026.

In short

The question is open, but the run is now understood up to its rebuilds. Between rebuilds the machine is a binary counter that applies one fixed operation to a list of blocks once per tick, and the list is the binary expansion of the time it has left (checked against the machine, not proved). Rebuilds are simulated cell by cell. With this the run passes 3,792 rebuilds, more than 109,553 steps, without a halt (before: 109 steps).

Same skeleton as a sibling. The machine 1RB0RE_0RC0RF_1LD1RA_1RE0LD_0LB1RC_1LE--- follows the same model with blocks of 3a + 2 cells instead of 2a + 1. There, tapes of the machine's own shape halt; here none was found, and the rebuilds have no rule yet.

Doubling rounds

No halt in 109 steps. Between consecutive moments the head is in C at the right end on a pure block tape the last block grows by 5; from step 3·105 on each round takes twice the time of the one before plus a small constant c (c = 5 or 9 during a walk, between −55 and 33 when the list is rebuilt).

tools/c/tmrun.c (at C R)

Between rebuilds: a binary counter that applies one list operation per tick

The same model as for the sibling 1RB0RE_0RC0RF_1LD1RA_1RE0LD_0LB1RC_1LE---, in another coding. At the moments the head is in C on a 0 just right of a 1, the tape is a list of blocks of 1s separated by single 0s (the interior), then a block of one 1, a last block l, and to the right of the head a zone of single 1s. Call it regular if the first block is even, the other interior blocks are odd, l is a positive multiple of 5, and the gaps of the zone are 0 mod 5 (the first, counted from the head) and 4 mod 5 (the others). From one such moment to the next (a sub-round) the interior takes one step W: (..., x, 2a + 1) becomes (..., x + 2, 1, ..., 1) with a blocks of 1. The last block and the zone are a binary counter: with segments s1 = g1, si = gi + 1, a last block l > 5 becomes a new first segment l − 5 and l = 5; with l = 5 the n leading segments equal to 5 are removed, the next segment loses 5 cells (blank tape supplies them if there is none) and l = 5n + 10. So l plus the segments is 5(m + 1) for 2m sub-rounds (one round), then m grows by 1. Checked against the machine one sub-round at a time: of the 400,000 sub-rounds after step 1,399 (16 million steps) the 399,965 regular ones all follow this; the other 35 are the transient of the first rebuild. Observed and checked, not proved.

holdouts/1RB0RF_1RC1LB_1LD1RE_1LB0LD_1RF0RC_1RA---/py/model.py (check 400000)

The list is the binary expansion of the time it has left

With digits ai = (bi − 1)/2, W removes the last digit a, adds 1 to the digit before it and appends a zeros. A tail (..., q, a) becomes (..., q + a + 1) after exactly 2a steps, so an interior with digits a1, ..., ak is a single block after exactly T = Σi 2ai + ... + ak + k − i steps of W. (This is arithmetic on the rule W, not on the machine.) Checked on the machine: the model predicts the single block at sub-round 7,964 of that run and it is there; and after the first rebuild the lists at six ends of rounds, up to step 1.07·1010, are those of a direct run of the machine (2 1^7 3 1^3 7 1^10 7 3 1^4 | 1 | 120 at step 335,864,993, ..., 2 1^6 3 15 1^10 7 3 1^4 | 1 | 145 at step 10,737,738,872).

heuristic Evidence only

3,792 rebuilds without a halt

A run that takes the regular sub-rounds by arithmetic (any number at once, with the two rules above) and gives everything else to the machine itself, cell by cell: the transient after each single block. It passes 3,792 rebuilds; by then the counter has made more than 231,736 sub-rounds, so the machine more than 109,553 steps. A halt could only be found by the direct part and none was. The arithmetic part rests on the two rules above for all sizes, which are checked, not proved. The run stops there for a practical reason. Some transients contain a second kind of regular stretch (observed, not modelled): the block before the last one is 2l/5 + 1 instead of 1, the list counts the sub-rounds upwards in binary instead of downwards, and a gap of the zone of 5q + 4 cells is used up in about 2q+1 sub-rounds. The 1,504th transient has one with q = 24, which the run simulates cell by cell (about 109 steps); after rebuild 3,792 comes one that is too long for that.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. Between rebuilds the machine cannot halt if the model is right; what the transient of a rebuild does has no rule yet, and a second kind of regular stretch inside the transients, in which the list counts upwards, is not modelled. The sibling 1RB0RE_0RC0RF_1LD1RA_1RE0LD_0LB1RC_1LE---, which follows the same model, has tapes of its own shape that halt, so the shape alone is unlikely to decide this machine either.
