The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in D reading 1. D is entered only from C0 (writes 1, moves L): 1 C>0. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

The records list every step at which the head reaches a cell no earlier step reached, with the side, the width so far and the whole tape: the first place to look for a repeating macro structure.

Macro structure

Two binary counters on one tape

Run the machine for a while and the tape sorts itself into two numbers written in binary. On the far left, after a single 1 that acts as a sentinel, is a counter U whose digits are blocks of 5 cells: 00000 is a 0 and 00001 a 1, most significant digit on the left. Then come a short gap 00, the head in state D, and a fixed header such as 0110101. Right of the header is a second counter V whose digits are pairs, least significant first: 01 is a 1 and 11 a 0.

The tick configuration is 1 U 00 D>0110101 V. Every tick adds 1 to U and takes 1 from V. Most of the time nothing else happens.

The rule

One tick: U + 1, V − 1

The head walks left through the trailing 1-digits of U, turning them into 0-digits (the carries), writes a 1-digit, then walks right to V and turns its trailing 0-pairs into 1-pairs and its lowest 1-pair into a 0-pair (the borrows). Written with j carries and i + 1 borrows:

beforeaftersteps
L 00000 (00001)^j 00 D>0110101 01 RL 00001 (00000)^j 00 D>0110101 11 R10 * j + 38
L 00000 (00001)^j 00 D>0110101 (11)^(i+1) 01 RL 00001 (00000)^j 00 D>0110101 (01)^(i+1) 11 R8 * i + 10 * j + 46

These two shapes are proved in Lean for every j and i (lean/Rules.lean), for every header the run has used (0110101, 0110111). Because the cost of a tick depends only on the numbers of carries and borrows, a long run of ticks has an exact cost: the sum of the trailing zeros of the numbers U+1, ..., U+k and V−k+1, ..., V, which has a closed form. The accelerated simulator uses it to skip billions of ticks at once.

When V runs out

The rebuild as a function of the counter

When V reaches 0 the tape is clean, 0^inf F U G S>H (11)^n 1 0^inf, and the machine's state is (H, n, N): header, length of V, and the up-counter read as one number together with its top word F. The rebuild then runs to the next clean tick configuration (H', n', N', V'), and the phase adds V' to the counter: the next state is (H', n', N' + V').

Here the counter's top word F is either 1 or 1000x. In the six rebuilds computed, those with top word 1 left only a two-digit counter N' (2 or 3), so the next counter, 2n' + N' − δ, is written almost entirely by the rebuild.

Where it could stop

Halting, reduced to the rebuild map

Ticks never halt, so a halt can only come from a rebuild. The exhaustive runs of the rebuild for small counters list the ones that halt; whether the run from blank tape ever feeds one of them is the open question, now a question about the macro map on (H, n, N) rather than about the raw machine.

Kernel
lean/Rules.lean (4 theorems)
The tick rules of the two counters

For each of the headers 0110101, 0110111 the run uses, two theorems: with V odd, L 00000 (00001)^j 00 D>0110101 01 R becomes L 00001 (00000)^j 00 D>0110101 11 R in 10 * j + 38 steps; with V having i + 1 trailing zeros, L 00000 (00001)^j 00 D>0110101 (11)^(i+1) 01 R becomes L 00001 (00000)^j 00 D>0110101 (01)^(i+1) 11 R in 8 * i + 10 * j + 46 steps. That is U + 1 and V − 1 for every number j of carries and i + 1 of borrows and every tape L, R beyond (proved along the symbolic run of tools/py/bbtools/symtm.py).

The macro map and its simulator

At each moment V reaches 0 the tape is the clean configuration 0^inf F U 00 D> H (11)^n 1 0^inf (F the counter's top word), so the state is (H, n, N) with N the up-counter read as one number. py/rebuild.py writes that tape, runs the rebuild (tick runs of its sub-phases jumped by the proved tick rules, the rest cell by cell in C) to the next clean tick configuration (H', n', N', V'), and then adds the V' ticks of the phase by arithmetic: next state (H', n', N' + V'), steps by the closed formula of the tick costs. Tested: its first 4 states and step counts, up to about 1054 steps, equal those of py/counters.py (itself tested against tools/c/tmrun.c).

Heuristic
py/rebuild_stats.py 1RB1LC_0RC0R... run
What the rebuild map looks like in the run

Only 6 rebuilds fit in the 10-minute run (the seventh, with V of 2,176 digits, is run cell by cell and did not finish). Header and top word go round the cycle 0110111/1000, 0110111/1, 0110101/1000, 0110101/1. Every rebuild with top word 1 (3 of 3) left only a two-digit counter N' (2 or 3), so the next counter is 2n' + N' − δ, written almost entirely by the rebuild. The two with header 0110111 and top word 1000x kept the counter and V gained one digit; the one with 0110101/1000 read 33 digits. Six rebuilds are too few to call any of this a rule.

Open
Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The macro map on (H, n, N) is computed exactly; its rebuilds have not been described for all N, so neither a non-halting invariant nor a halting run is known. The stopping walk found for 1RB1LC_0RC0RB_1LD0LA_1LE---_1LF0LA_1LA0LB (round 3) does not describe this machine's rebuilds: of the 584 rebuilds with N < 29 (both headers, n − len(N) + 1 in {0, 1}) on which the walk stops below the top digit, none gives the predicted (header, n', N'). No rebuild rule is proved in Lean for this machine (there is no lean/Rebuild.lean).
