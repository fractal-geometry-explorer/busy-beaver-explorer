Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

The tape holds two binary counters, U up and V down. When V runs out the machine rebuilds V from the bottom digits of U; the rebuild is now computed exactly as a map on the counters.

Status as of 9 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves the ticks; the exact macro map finds no halt in the first 10656 steps.

Where a halt would come from. Only from a rebuild after V reaches 0; which rebuilds halt is listed for small counters.

The tick rules of the two counters

For each of the headers 0110101, 0110111 the run uses, two theorems: with V odd, L 00000 (00001)^j 00 D>0110101 01 R becomes L 00001 (00000)^j 00 D>0110101 11 R in 10 * j + 38 steps; with V having i + 1 trailing zeros, L 00000 (00001)^j 00 D>0110101 (11)^(i+1) 01 R becomes L 00001 (00000)^j 00 D>0110101 (01)^(i+1) 11 R in 8 * i + 10 * j + 46 steps. That is U + 1 and V − 1 for every number j of carries and i + 1 of borrows and every tape L, R beyond (proved along the symbolic run of tools/py/bbtools/symtm.py).

The macro map and its simulator

At each moment V reaches 0 the tape is the clean configuration 0^inf F U 00 D> H (11)^n 1 0^inf (F the counter's top word), so the state is (H, n, N) with N the up-counter read as one number. py/rebuild.py writes that tape, runs the rebuild (tick runs of its sub-phases jumped by the proved tick rules, the rest cell by cell in C) to the next clean tick configuration (H', n', N', V'), and then adds the V' ticks of the phase by arithmetic: next state (H', n', N' + V'), steps by the closed formula of the tick costs. Tested: its first 4 states and step counts, up to about 1054 steps, equal those of py/counters.py (itself tested against tools/c/tmrun.c).

What the rebuild map looks like in the run

Only 6 rebuilds fit in the 10-minute run (the seventh, with V of 2,176 digits, is run cell by cell and did not finish). Header and top word go round the cycle 0110111/1000, 0110111/1, 0110101/1000, 0110101/1. Every rebuild with top word 1 (3 of 3) left only a two-digit counter N' (2 or 3), so the next counter is 2n' + N' − δ, written almost entirely by the rebuild. The two with header 0110111 and top word 1000x kept the counter and V gained one digit; the one with 0110101/1000 read 33 digits. Six rebuilds are too few to call any of this a rule.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The macro map on (H, n, N) is computed exactly; its rebuilds have not been described for all N, so neither a non-halting invariant nor a halting run is known. The stopping walk found for 1RB1LC_0RC0RB_1LD0LA_1LE---_1LF0LA_1LA0LB (round 3) does not describe this machine's rebuilds: of the 584 rebuilds with N < 29 (both headers, n − len(N) + 1 in {0, 1}) on which the walk stops below the top digit, none gives the predicted (header, n', N'). No rebuild rule is proved in Lean for this machine (there is no lean/Rebuild.lean).
