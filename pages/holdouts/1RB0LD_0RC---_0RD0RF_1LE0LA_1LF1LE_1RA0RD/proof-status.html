What is known about 1RB0LD_0RC---_0RD0RF_1LE0LA_1LF1LE_1RA0RD

1RB0LD_0RC---_0RD0RF_1LE0LA_1LF1LE_1RA0RD

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves no halt in the first 107 steps and proves the shift rules of the bouncer part (the sweeps and the turn at the right end) for every length. The run, followed exactly by a segment simulator with cycle jumps, has not halted in 2.5·1072 steps.

What it does. The head bounces between a wall near the left and the right end of the tape, adding two cells at the right end per round trip. Between them lies a long run of one 8-cell word. Behind the wall the tape is a long run of an 18-cell word. In phase A the wall slowly eats the 18-cell run and the 8-cell run grows; in phase B the wall marches right, turning the 8-cell run back into 18-cell words. Each such epoch multiplies the lengths by about 9/4.

Why it is hard. At every turn from phase A to B the wall works on the debris left of it: counters made of a 36-cell word, which lose 0, 1 or 2 copies per epoch in an irregular pattern. Now and then (at about 1.7·1014, 5·1021 and 2.6·1072 steps) the whole 18-cell run is turned into a new counter of 36-cell words (345,724 copies, then 1,710,343,625, then about 3.9·1034) and the bouncer starts again from small sizes. Whether the machine halts depends on how these nested counters run out, which follows the residues of numbers that grow by 9/4 per epoch.

The sweeps and the turn at the right end

With P = 10011010 and Q = 11110001: a leftward sweep turns P^n 1 0 E>0 into 1 0 E>0 Q^n in 36n steps; a rightward sweep turns 0 D> Q^n into P^n 0 D> in 32n steps; at the right end 0 D>1 then blank becomes 1 0 E>0 1 in 5 steps. Together: from 0 D> Q^n 1 the head comes back to the wall as 1 0 E>0 Q^n 1, two cells wider, in 68n + 5 steps, for every n. The leftward sweep carries a word on both sides of the head, so the file proves cross_l2, a two-sided form of the library's shift rule.

No halt in the first 2.5·1072 steps

py/segsim.py keeps the tape as cells and repeats of words and crosses long repeats with shift rules it finds and checks on one copy; py/accel.py jumps many periods of a regime whose counts change by constant amounts (checked over three periods, step counts summed exactly). The tape agrees cell for cell with the plain C simulator after 109 and 1010 steps (py/run.py check). Run for 30 minutes, it reaches 2.5·1072 steps without a halt.

No halt in the first 4·109 steps (plain simulator)

The C simulator, with every long excursion of the head logged (c/sweeps.c), shows the bouncer: in phase A the left turning point drifts left by 72 cells per 36 round trips, in phase B it moves right by exactly 18 cells per round trip. Phase B lasted 86, 203, 463, 1,051 and 2,359 round trips in the epochs ending near 3.8·106, 1.9·107, 9.3·107, 4.7·108 and 2.4·109 steps (ratios 2.36, 2.28, 2.27, 2.24).

heuristic Evidence only

Epochs grow by about 9/4

Phase A: per round trip the 18-cell run loses 2 cells and the 8-cell run gains 4 (2 from the wall, 2 from the right end), so x copies of the 18-cell word last 9x round trips and leave about 4.5x copies of the 8-cell word. Phase B: per round trip one 18-cell word is made from two 8-cell words, so y copies give about y/2 new 18-cell words. One epoch maps x to about 9x/4. Read off the simulator, not proved.

py/run.py epochs

Nested counters, used up irregularly

Left of the 18-cell run the tape holds runs of the 36-cell word 101001100110010011101001101010100110 (10 copies at first). When phase A ends the wall works on the nearest such run and removes 0, 1 or 2 copies, in no visible pattern. Three times so far the whole 18-cell run was turned into such a counter and the bouncer restarted from small sizes: at 1.7·1014 steps (345,724 copies, plus a new small counter of 5) and at 4.96·1021 steps (1,710,343,625 copies, plus 38) and at 2.57·1072 steps (38,924,542,121,409,275,697,275,846,796,041,586 copies, followed by one of 732,592,001,087,343,578). Between restarts the epochs replay the same sizes.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The bouncer part is proved; the wall's work at the end of each phase A (on the counters and the debris between them) is the open part. It changes the counters by amounts that depend on residues of the epoch sizes, which is the kind of question (like the Collatz-type BB(6) cryptids) that has no known method.
