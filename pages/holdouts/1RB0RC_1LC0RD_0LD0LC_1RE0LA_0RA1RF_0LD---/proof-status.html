What is known about 1RB0RC_1LC0RD_0LD0LC_1RE0LA_0RA1RF_0LD---

1RB0RC_1LC0RD_0LD0LC_1RE0LA_0RA1RF_0LD---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

It is one of five machines that share states A to E (1RB0RC_1LC0RD_0LD0LC_1RE0LA_0RA?RF_F---) and differ only in what E writes on a 1 and in state F. All five run the same machinery, a nested base-5 odometer built from blocks of the word 1000; they differ only in how a rare collision is resolved, the one place where state F is used.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The run is understood as a nested odometer whose regular rounds are proved in Lean, but its irregular events (blocks running empty) are only observed, not proved to come from a closed list; nothing like a proof of non-halting is in sight.

Where a halt would have to come from. The machine halts only in F reading a 1, and F is entered only from E reading a 1, right after D wrote a 1 on a 0: so a halt needs D>0 1 1 somewhere. In the run F is entered only 3 times in the first 2·109 steps (first at step 49,249), always when two g4 gadgets meet across an empty block (or at a gadget made by such an event), and it always reads a 0.

No closed set of shapes. The irregular gadgets form a closed alphabet, but the tape language they span contains halting tapes: the trap 101, and a tape of regular gadgets with every block of length ≥ 2 that halts (both proved in Lean). So the decision has to come from relations between block lengths, which no shape-level (CTL-style) argument captures.

Pushed to 2.77·1017 steps. An accelerated simulator, validated round by round against the plain machine up to 2·1010 steps, finds no halt and no trap in the first 2.77·1017 steps.

Conversion sweeps

Moving right, A, B, D, E turn the words 0100, 1000, 0001, 0010 into 1010 or 0101; moving left, C turns every two 10s into one 1000 (shifted), and turns a separating 1 into a 0. These are what a sweep does to a block: by ↔ a2y with a = 10, b = 1000.

a_cross, b_cross, d_cross, e_cross, c_halve, c_sep, lean/Rules.lean

Regular odometer rounds

With the head in D at the left end, the tape bk G1 by1 G2 … (gadgets g0 = 1001, g1 = 1001100, g2 = 100110, g3 = 10011, g4 = 10010, s = 0) goes to the next such configuration by one round: k gains one b and the first gadget advances: g0→g1 takes a b from the block after it, g1→g2→g3 change nothing else, g4→g0 moves a b from the block after to the block before, s→g0 takes one from the block before, and g3→g4 carries into the next gadget. Proved for every kind of first gadget and every kind of gadget receiving a carry, for all block lengths ≥ 3.

round_g0, round_g1, round_g2, round_g4, carry_g0 … carry_s, lean/Rules.lean

Resets

When a carry runs through all gadgets (all are g3) to the last one, the head sweeps to the right end and back: every gadget becomes s, every block but the first gains one b, and a new level b g1 appears at the left. Proved for one and two levels.

reset1, reset2, lean/Rules.lean

The collision, where F is used

bk g4 g4 by g2 … (two g4 with an empty block between): the round enters F once, on a 0, and the pair merges into one g4 and the block on the left gains three b.

collide, lean/Rules.lean

The trap: 101 halts

With the head in D on bk+1 1 0 1 1, whatever follows, the machine halts after 4k+15 steps. The stretch 101 1 is the transient gadget 10 (left by the collision g4 0 g2) meeting the transient gadget 1 (left by another collision) across an empty block.

trap, lean/Rules.lean; trap_halts, lean/Escape.lean

Gadget shapes alone cannot prove non-halting

From the head in D on b6 g4 b5 g3 b2 s b2 g0 (b = 1000; only the regular gadgets, every block of length ≥ 2) the machine halts, after 3,275 steps. So the set of tapes "regular gadgets with any block lengths" contains halting configurations, and an invariant that closes must say something about the block lengths, not only about which gadgets occur where. Without s: b6 g1 b5 g0 b2 g1 b5 g0, after 375,448 steps (simulation).

witness_halts, lean/Escape.lean; py/witness_search.py

The closed-set search over shapes

py/shapes.py runs every round symbolically (blocks either empty or of any length ≥ 1, split whenever the run needs it) over prefixes of up to three gadgets. The alphabet of gadgets closes: the regular ones with 1, 10, 100, 1001101, 10011010, 10011011, 100110110, 1001101100, and every irregular gadget seen in 2·1010 steps of the run splits into these. But the search does not close. 33 prefixes halt, all through the trap above. 8,054 of 29,307 prefixes still need a longer prefix: carry chains through g3, and runs of empty blocks the head walks through.

120,470 regular rounds and 47 irregular ones. The transient gadgets 1, 10, 100, … do occur, sometimes next to another gadget across an empty block (g3 0 1, g4 0 10), but never as 10 0 1, the trap.

An accelerated simulator, checked round by round

py/accsim.py works on the list of (stretch, block length) at each return of the head to the left end. Each round is a symbolic symtm run on a prefix of the list, with the blocks symbolic. It is cached by shape and reused with exact step counts, and recomputed with exact values when a result depends on small blocks. Up to 19,999,506,782 steps it agrees with the plain machine on the tape and the step count of every one of 120,517 rounds.

Why it is hard

The odometer itself is regular, but the block lengths it drains come from earlier resets, and when a block runs empty the gadgets on both sides merge in ways that depend on their phases mod 5. The sequence of these events, and whether one ever produces D>0 1 1, looks irregular; CTL with 4 to 8 states finds no closed invariant.

open Open

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. A proof of non-halting needs an invariant that relates block lengths (for example, that the gadget 10 never meets the gadget 1 across an empty block). Shapes alone do not suffice: witness_halts gives a tape of regular gadgets from which the machine halts.
