Does this machine ever halt?

1RB0RC_1LC0RD_0LD0LC_1RE0LA_0RA0RF_1RD---

Nobody knows. This machine is a nested counter: the tape is a row of blocks, and between them small gadgets count to five and carry to the right, like the wheels of an odometer that eat the blocks they sit between. This page runs the real machine in your browser and explains the counter; the rounds of the counter are proved in Lean.

The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in F reading 1. F is entered only from E1 (writes 0, moves R): E>1 1. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

The run at a glance

Space-time diagram and records

The diagram stacks the tape at many moments. Sampling at each new cell shows the shape of the run as the visited region grows; sampling evenly in time shows where the head spends its time. The records list every step at which the head reaches a cell no earlier step reached, with the side, the width so far and the whole tape: the first place to look for a repeating macro structure.

The picture

Blocks that change form at every sweep

The tape is made of two words, a = 10 and b = 1000. Moving right, the head turns every b it crosses into aa; moving left, it turns aa back into b. So a block is a2y or by depending on which way the head last went through it. The blocks are separated by short gadgets: g0 = 1001, g1 = 1001100, g2 = 100110, g3 = 10011, g4 = 10010, or a bare 0 (s).

The rule

An odometer of five-state wheels

Look at the tape each time the head comes back to the left end in state D. It reads bk G1 by1 G2 by2 … Gn. One round later the first block has one more b and the first gadget has advanced one step in the cycle g0 → g1 → g2 → g3 → g4 → g0:

step of the wheelalso
g0 → g1the block after it loses one b
g1 → g2, g2 → g3nothing
g3 → g4carry: the next gadget advances
g4 → g0one b moves from the block after to the block before
s → g0the block before loses one b

So gadget i+1 turns once for every five turns of gadget i, exactly like an odometer, and each full turn of a wheel drains two b from the block on its right. Every one of these rounds is a theorem in Lean, for all block lengths.

When a carry runs off the last gadget, the head sweeps across the whole tape and back (a reset): all gadgets become s, and a new wheel is born at the left end. The full sweeps are the rare moments when the head reaches the right end of the tape.

Why it is hard

Wheels that run out of road

A wheel drains the block on its right, and a block can run empty. Then the two gadgets on either side meet and something irregular happens: they merge into a short-lived gadget (100, 1, 10, …), or blocks merge, or a new wheel appears. These events are rare (a few dozen in 109 steps against tens of thousands of regular rounds) but they decide the shape of everything that follows.

State F, the only one that can halt, is used only in one such event: two g4 wheels meeting across an empty block. The five machines of the family differ exactly there: in this one the pair becomes s, an empty block and g0, and the block on the left gains four b. F always reads a 0 there; a halt would need the pattern D>0 1 1, which no event seen so far produces.

Why shapes are not enough

A closed alphabet, but no closed set

One way to prove that a machine never halts is to find a set of tape shapes that contains the start, maps into itself, and never allows a halt. Here the shapes would be rows of gadgets with blocks of any length. The gadgets do close: the irregular events only ever produce eight more of them (1, 10, 100, 1001101, 10011010, 10011011, 100110110, 1001101100), so the alphabet is finite.

The set itself does not close. When the transient gadget 10 meets the transient gadget 1 across an empty block, the stretch 101 appears, and with the head arriving on it in D the machine halts (trap_halts, proved in Lean). Worse, b6 g4 b5 g4 b2 g0 b2 g0, a tape of regular gadgets with no empty block, also leads to a halt (witness_halts). Which tapes are safe depends on the block lengths, on whether two blocks empty at the same moment. That is arithmetic the shapes do not see.

The shape of the run

Right of the start it stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half).

Conversion sweeps

Moving right, A, B, D, E turn the words 0100, 1000, 0001, 0010 into 1010 or 0101; moving left, C turns every two 10s into one 1000 (shifted), and turns a separating 1 into a 0. These are what a sweep does to a block: by ↔ a2y with a = 10, b = 1000.

Kernel
round_g0, round_g1, round_g2, round_g4, carry_g0 … carry_s, lean/Rules.lean
Regular odometer rounds

With the head in D at the left end, the tape bk G1 by1 G2 … (gadgets g0 = 1001, g1 = 1001100, g2 = 100110, g3 = 10011, g4 = 10010, s = 0) goes to the next such configuration by one round: k gains one b and the first gadget advances: g0→g1 takes a b from the block after it, g1→g2→g3 change nothing else, g4→g0 moves a b from the block after to the block before, s→g0 takes one from the block before, and g3→g4 carries into the next gadget. Proved for every kind of first gadget and every kind of gadget receiving a carry, for all block lengths ≥ 3.

Kernel
reset1, reset2, lean/Rules.lean
Resets

When a carry runs through all gadgets (all are g3) to the last one, the head sweeps to the right end and back: every gadget becomes s, every block but the first gains one b, and a new level b g1 appears at the left. Proved for one and two levels.

Kernel
trap, lean/Rules.lean; trap_halts, lean/Escape.lean
The trap: 101 halts

With the head in D on bk+1 1 0 1 1, whatever follows, the machine halts after 4k+15 steps. The stretch 101 1 is the transient gadget 10 (left by the collision g4 0 g2) meeting the transient gadget 1 (left by another collision) across an empty block.

Kernel
witness_halts, lean/Escape.lean; py/witness_search.py
Gadget shapes alone cannot prove non-halting

From the head in D on b6 g4 b5 g4 b2 g0 b2 g0 (b = 1000; only the regular gadgets, every block of length ≥ 2) the machine halts, after 3,268 steps. So the set of tapes "regular gadgets with any block lengths" contains halting configurations, and an invariant that closes must say something about the block lengths, not only about which gadgets occur where. Without s: the same tape. Here even 10 0 g0 is fatal: g3 ba 10 g0 bz ... halts when the next carry arrives.

Computed
py/shapes.py
The closed-set search over shapes

py/shapes.py runs every round symbolically (blocks either empty or of any length ≥ 1, split whenever the run needs it) over prefixes of up to three gadgets. The alphabet of gadgets closes: the regular ones with 1, 10, 100, 1001101, 10011010, 10011011, 100110110, 1001101100, and every irregular gadget seen in 2·1010 steps of the run splits into these. But the search does not close. 288 prefixes halt, all through the trap above. 11,950 of 43,243 prefixes still need a longer prefix: carry chains through g3, and runs of empty blocks the head walks through.

An accelerated simulator, checked round by round

py/accsim.py works on the list of (stretch, block length) at each return of the head to the left end. Each round is a symbolic symtm run on a prefix of the list, with the blocks symbolic. It is cached by shape and reused with exact step counts, and recomputed with exact values when a result depends on small blocks. Up to 19,999,824,447 steps it agrees with the plain machine on the tape and the step count of every one of 129,119 rounds.

The accelerated run reaches step 116,909,558,295,867,781 (326,103,591 rounds, 40 minutes, one core) without a halt. There it has 13 gadgets and a first block of 70,480,416. Heuristic
Why it is hard

The odometer itself is regular, but the block lengths it drains come from earlier resets, and when a block runs empty the gadgets on both sides merge in ways that depend on their phases mod 5. The sequence of these events, and whether one ever produces D>0 1 1, looks irregular; CTL with 4 to 8 states finds no closed invariant.

Open
Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. A proof of non-halting needs an invariant that relates block lengths (for example, that the gadget 10 never meets the gadget 1 across an empty block). Shapes alone do not suffice: witness_halts gives a tape of regular gadgets from which the machine halts.
