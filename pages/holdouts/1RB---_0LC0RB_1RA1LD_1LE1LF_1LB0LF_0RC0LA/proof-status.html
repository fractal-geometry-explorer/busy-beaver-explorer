1RB---_0LC0RB_1RA1LD_1LE1LF_1LB0LF_0RC0LA

This six-state machine is a holdout of the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The machine moves numbers between binary counters, in nested loops that get deeper each epoch; it halts only if state A lands on a 1. No halt in the first 1011 steps.

The transfer engine, every visit, every size

An engine visit is A on the 0 of d in L' i e x i d R. The theorems prove the whole visit for every size: x = b becomes a, or x = a becomes b with a borrow over m digits a ending at a digit b, z (0000) or v (0100), which drops one step (b→a, z→b, v→z); and the right counter gets 1 with a carry over k ones (digits b/c), or across the separator S1 = e c g b e g, or across S1 and S2 = e c g c (12k+50 steps for x = b over k carries; 12j+12k+10m+240 for a borrow over m digits and a carry across S1). 12 theorems, 3 to 6 variables each.

The engine covers the whole transfer phase

From step 9,410,541 to step 120,015,313 the head visits the engine (at cell −129) 1,023,987 times; all but 21 visits are instances of the theorems (the borrows that reach the left end read the blank cells as z digits). The 21 others are the overflows of the right counter.

The right counter shrinks at each overflow

The right counter has 19 digits (3 + 10 + 6, in three pieces). It first overflows at visit 499,692 (so it started at 24,596), and then after 218, 217, …, 16, 8, 8, 4, 2, 1 more visits: each overflow costs it a digit, and the phase ends at visit 1,023,987, when it has none left. The first overflow is the epoch at step 63,383,081.

Growth shape: exp/exp

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.08). Transfers between counters, nested

Read off the run: the engine moves 1 from a down-counting counter to an up-counting one at each visit; separators let carries through; each epoch adds a counter to the middle of the tape.

The next epoch runs the same engine one level deeper

In the reference machine, at step 3·108, the tape reads L1 i b e L2 i e i e x y d R (L1 13 digits, L2 6 digits): the two-digit counter x y counts down every visit, L2 every fourth, and the right counter, now with a one-digit piece in front (b/c e g …), counts up. Not yet proved.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. It halts only if A reads a 1; the run is a growing nest of counter transfers whose epoch-to-epoch rule is not decoded.
