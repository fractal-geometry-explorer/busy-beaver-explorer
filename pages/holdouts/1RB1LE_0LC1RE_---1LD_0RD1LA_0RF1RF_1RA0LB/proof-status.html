1RB1LE_0LC1RE_---1LD_0RD1LA_0RF1RF_1RA0LB

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape. It builds a ladder of ever longer runs of one block of 1s at the right end of the tape and, every now and then, wipes the ladder into a plain repeated word and starts a new one. A halt is only possible at the far left end, which it has not visited since the first few hundred steps.

Status as of 12 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The tape between visits of the right end is a ladder of runs of the block 190 whose lengths grow geometrically (ratio about 8/3) from left to right, separated by short words of 1s that change at every sweep; at step 27,167,335 one sweep rewrites every 190 of the left part into 17010, that part freezes, and a new ladder starts to its right. An exact accelerated simulator (checked step for step against the C simulator) runs 2.4·108 steps without a halt. A closed tape language confines a halt to the left end with 00011001 under and right of the head; the exact rule of the ladder and of its resets is not yet written down.

An exact accelerated simulator

py/blocksim.py, py/bscheck.py

A geometric ladder of 190 blocks, and a reset

Written as lengths of runs of 1s (single 0s dropped), the tape at the right-end visits is a short head followed by 9*n1 w1 9*n2 w2 ... 9*nk 7 1, with digit words wi such as 3 1 3 1, 7 11, 13 1, 21; the counts grow in ratio about 8/3 (e.g. 2, 6, 19, 53, 144, 384, 1024 at visit 11,201) and the last grows by 1 every 8 visits. At step 27,167,335 one sweep rewrites the left part block for block (each 190 becomes 17010, the same length; the digit words become 13010, 111010, …); that part then stays frozen and a new ladder builds up at its right.

The visit map as a recursion on words

V(X U) = Y Y U, V(Y X U) = X X Y U, V(Y Y X X U) = X Y4 U, V(Y Y Y S) = X X Y V(S), V(Y Y X Y Y S) = X Y4 V(S): equal to the machine on 19,312 random words and on the 724 visits they cover among the first 1,012 of the run. The words starting Y Y X Y X follow further rules not yet written down.

py/vmodel.py

The halting pattern

The machine halts exactly when B reads a 0 with a 0 on its left; inside the tape all 0s are single, so only at the blank left edge. The head reached the left end at steps 1,781 and 27,158,888 in the first 3·109 steps, arriving in E and D.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. It depends on the state in which the rare visits that cross the whole tape arrive at the left edge; the rule for those visits and for the reset they cause is not known.
