1RB1LE_0LC1RE_---1LD_0RD1LA_0RF1RF_1RA0LB

Nobody knows whether it halts. The machine builds a ladder of ever longer runs of one block of 1s at the right end of the tape, and every now and then flattens the ladder into a plain repeated pattern and starts again. A halt is only possible at the far left end, in one precise local pattern, and the head has not been back there since the beginning.

The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in C reading 0. C is entered only from B0 (writes 0, moves L): 0 B>0. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

First look

The shape of the run

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.527).

After 109 steps the tape spans 41,692 cells (5 left of the start, 31,830 right), with a density of 1s of 0.725.

How a visit works

A recursion on words of pairs

Read the tape from the head leftwards, two cells at a time: X is the pair 0 1, Y is 1 1. Each visit of the right end rewrites the word and makes it one pair longer:

V(X U)          = Y Y U                bounce
V(Y X U)        = X X Y U
V(Y Y X X U)    = X Y^4 U
V(Y Y Y S)      = X X Y V(S)           three Y's pass the visit on
V(Y Y X Y Y S)  = X Y^4 V(S)           so does Y Y X Y Y

The first three are turns: the head goes back to the right end. The last two are carries: the visit goes on to the left. These five rules cover about seven visits in ten and agree with the machine wherever they apply; the remaining words (those starting Y Y X Y X) follow more rules of the same kind that are not written down yet. Whole visits for three families of words are Lean theorems. The block 19 0 of the ladder is the word Y4 X.

The machine halts exactly when state B reads a 0 that has another 0 on its left, and inside the tape every 0 stands alone: so a halt needs B at the blank left edge, which only a visit carried across the whole tape can reach. That has happened once since the first 2,000 steps (the reset), and the head arrived in D.

Structure

A ladder of blocks

Each time the head reaches the right end of the tape in state A, write the tape as the lengths of its runs of 1s (the 0s between them are single). Most of it is the block 1111111110, nine 1s and a 0, repeated. The repeats come in rungs separated by short words such as 3 1 3 1 or 13 1, and each rung is about 8/3 times as long as the one before it:

... 15 9 3 1 13 1 9×2 21 9×6 21 9×19 3 1 11 9×53 3 1 3 1 9×144 3 15 9×384 3 7 1 9×1024 7 1  A>

At each sweep the short words between the rungs change, like the digits of a counter, and the last rung grows by one block every eighth sweep. The rungs further left are copies made more rarely, which is why their lengths shrink geometrically.

Rare event

The ladder is flattened

At step 27,167,335 something different happens: a single sweep rewrites the whole left part block for block: every 1111111110 becomes 1111111010 (one 1 turned into a 0), and the short words become similar blocks. That part then never changes again, and a fresh ladder starts growing to its right. The second machine-made ladder looks like the first, so another flattening may come later; when and why it happens is still to be worked out.

Where it could stop

Only at the far left

The machine stops only in state C reading a 0. A closed tape language, a list of every 8-cell window that can ever appear around the head, shows that this can only happen at the left end of the tape (blank to the left) with 0 0 0 1 1 0 0 1 under and right of the head. The tape now starts with 110 0 1 0 13 0 1 0 ..., and the head has not extended the tape to the left since the first steps. Whether a future flattening could bring that pattern to the left end is the open question.

py/runs.py
A geometric ladder of 190 blocks, and a reset

Written as lengths of runs of 1s (single 0s dropped), the tape at the right-end visits is a short head followed by 9*n1 w1 9*n2 w2 ... 9*nk 7 1, with digit words wi such as 3 1 3 1, 7 11, 13 1, 21; the counts grow in ratio about 8/3 (e.g. 2, 6, 19, 53, 144, 384, 1024 at visit 11,201) and the last grows by 1 every 8 visits. At step 27,167,335 one sweep rewrites the left part block for block (each 190 becomes 17010, the same length; the digit words become 13010, 111010, …); that part then stays frozen and a new ladder builds up at its right.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. It depends on the state in which the rare visits that cross the whole tape arrive at the left edge; the rule for those visits and for the reset they cause is not known.
