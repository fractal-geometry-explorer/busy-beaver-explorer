What is known about 1RB1LA_1LC0RE_---0LD_1LA0LF_1RA1RE_1LA0LB

1RB1LA_1LC0RE_---0LD_1LA0LF_1RA1RE_1LA0LB

OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 12 November 2026.

In short

Open, Collatz-like, reduced in Lean to an explicit map. It is a Lean theorem (kernel-checked) that the machine halts from blank tape if and only if the orbit of one configuration under an explicit map G on two lists of block lengths halts. The tape is a list of blocks of 1s with the head between two of them; a long block next to an empty one is dismantled two 1s at a time, half going to each neighbour, down to 2 or 3 ones by its parity. The machine halts exactly when the blocks left of the head are used up, which needs every one of them to be 111 at the same moment; for a lone block that means 3·(2m − 1) ones. The orbit has been followed for 109 applications of the accelerated map (1.5·1024 steps of the machine) without a halt.

Shift rules of the sweeps

The head crosses blocks of 1s: L (1)^n A>1 R to the left and L E> (1)^n R to the right, one step a cell, for every n and all tapes L, R.

lean/Rules.lean (generated by py/rules.py)

The map G on two lists of block lengths

At the moments the head is in state B on a 0 with a block of 1s directly on its left, the tape is … 0 1g2 0 1g1 B> 0 1b0 000 1b1 000 …: blocks separated by one 0 on the left and by 000 on the right. Call it v gs bs with the two lists of block lengths. G is an explicit computable map from one such moment to the next, with five cases by the length k of the block next to the head (k = 1, 2, 3, k ≥ 4, and no block: halt). The theorem G_sem says, for every such configuration x and with nothing assumed: if G x is a configuration y, the machine goes from x to y in at least one step; if G x is undefined, the machine halts from x. The proofs compose the two proved block crossings with single steps.

G, v_one, v_two, v_three, v_big, G_sem, lean/Map.lean

Halts iff the orbit of G halts

The machine halts from blank tape if and only if the orbit of v [1] [] (the tape 1 B> 0 after the first step) under G reaches a configuration where G is undefined. Those are exactly the configurations with no block (or an empty block) left of the head (G_none_iff).

halts_iff, G_none_iff, lean/Map.lean

The transfer loop, and the fast map GA

When the block next to the head has K ≥ 4 ones and the first block on the right is empty, two applications of G take two 1s from the block and give one to the next block on each side, and the situation repeats: the block is dismantled down to 2 (K even) or 3 (K odd) ones, half of it going left and half right (loop, proved by induction for every K). GA is G with this loop done in one application; halts_iff_fast is the same equivalence for GA: the machine halts from blank tape iff the orbit of v [1] [] under GA halts.

round, loop, GA, GA_sem, halts_iff_fast, lean/Map.lean

A lone block of 3·(2m − 1) ones halts

If the left list is a single block of 3·(2m+1 − 1) ones (3, 9, 21, 45, 93, …) and the first block on the right is empty, the orbit under GA halts 2m + 2 applications later: the loop turns a lone block of 2j + 5 ones into a lone block of j + 1, and a lone block of 3 is erased, which leaves nothing left of the head.

No halt in the first 1.5·1024 steps

The C program c/vsa.c follows GA with exact step counts (128-bit integers): no halt in the first 109 applications of GA, which stand for 1016 applications of G and 1,508,121,318,047,435,715,005,702 steps of the machine; the tape then holds 988,764,137 ones. The maps and their step counts are checked against a direct simulation cell by cell for the first 20,000 configurations (py/vmap.py check).

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: power/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like a power of the step count other than 1/2 (fitted exponent 0.27). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.357).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

A halt is neither excluded nor likely soon

The left list is down to a single block about once in 180 applications of GA, and that block is then of the size of the tape; the machine halts there only if the block has exactly 3·(2m − 1) ones (or in one of a few similar exact coincidences of small blocks). If these blocks behaved like random numbers this would give an expected number of halts of the order of 0.01 each time the tape doubles, for ever. In the first 108 applications of GA a lone block had fewer than 16 ones only 16 times, and never 3 or 9 ones.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. By halts_iff the question is exactly whether the orbit of G ever uses up the list of blocks left of the head, which needs all of them to be 111 at once: a Collatz-like question about one explicit orbit of block lists.
