What is known about 1RB0LF_1LC0RC_1RA1LD_0RB0LE_1LC0LB_---1LA

1RB0LF_1LC0RC_1RA1LD_0RB0LE_1LC0LB_---1LA

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The machine is a base-4 counter of walls in a sea of 110, incremented from the right end. Lean proves one sweep for every carry chain (any length), the two kinds of merger, the walk of T after an overflow and steps of the top's cycle; an exact digit simulator, checked against the direct run to 2·109 steps, ran to 8.6·1015 steps without a halt or an unknown event. A closed set of digit configurations is not yet assembled.

The sweeps' shift rules

Rightward, A (with 01 behind it) crosses (110110)^n unchanged, 16 steps per 6 cells; leftward, D, C and E cross the 110 sea in its three phases (110, 101, 011), 3 steps a triple.

a_right, d_left, c_left, e_left, lean/Rules.lean

The shape of a halt

F on a 0 halts; F is entered only from A on a 1 (writing 0, moving left), and A finds a 0 on its left only when it came from F on a 1. So it halts iff F reads a 1 with 1 0 to its left: 0 1 F> 1 reaches F on a 0 in 2 steps (the converse by reading the table, not formalized).

halt_shape, halt_after_shape, lean/Rules.lean

One sweep adds one, for a carry chain of any length

At the sample point (A on the last 1 at the right end) the tape is a list of digits (110)^r W. With the first non-W3 digit (the stop) in shape W0, W1 or W2 and any list ts of W3 digits after it (the carry chain, any length, any runs), one sweep turns the stop into the next shape (W0→W1 with its run 2a+1→2a, W1→W2 with 2a+2→2a, W2→W3) and every W3 of the chain into W0. Proved by induction on the list (seqtm).

sweep_w0, sweep_w1, sweep_w2, lean/Sweeps.lean, lean/Seq.lean

Mergers, the walk of T and the top

A W1 with left run 0 that receives a carry fuses with the W0 before it (one digit fewer, merge_w0_w1) or turns a W2 before it into W1 and itself into W0 (merge_w2_w1); after an overflow a T walks left through the U's (sweep_u_t); two steps of the top's cycle (prefix_w1, prefix_10_w0). Each for any carry chain.

The digit model is exact

py/digits.py maps one sample to the next by running the machine symbolically (symtm) on a window: the carry chain, the stop and as many digits before it as the run touches, the rest of the tape an unknown context, long runs as variables. Compared with the direct run at every sample point: the same tapes and the same step counts at all 30,218 sample points up to 2·109 steps.

No halt in 8.6·1015 steps

From the sample at 2·109 steps the digit model ran 6.8·107 sweeps more, to 8.6·1015 steps: no halt and no window it could not read back as digits; 22 overflows; 303 window rules in all (72 up to the length of the carry chain). The first run behind the top was used up near 6·109 steps and the run behind it took its place.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.075). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.506).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Why it probably never halts

Every event seen (sweeps, mergers, overflows, the T walk, the top's cycle, the first run being used up) is one of a small catalogue of local rewrites of digits, none of which passes the halting shape 0 1 F> 1; the catalogue stopped growing except for longer carry chains. A proof needs that catalogue closed under the rules.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. It would halt only through 0 1 F> 1, which no rule found produces. What is missing is a set of digit configurations (a bigram language over walls and short runs) shown closed under one sweep, the overflow rule for chains of any length, and the assembly in Lean.
