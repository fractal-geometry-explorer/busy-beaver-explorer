What is known about 1RB0LE_1RC0RF_0RD0RB_1RE0RC_1LA0LA_1RA---

1RB0LE_1RC0RF_0RD0RB_1RE0RC_1LA0LA_1RA---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 13 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The run is a sequence of cycles. Cycle n starts at P 1^3 0^K 1 E>0 with a gap of K ≈ 3·2n zeros. Sweeps then fill the gap from the right, four 0s per sweep; one pass to the left end and one back rewrite the prefix P and open the next gap, K' = 2K − c. Lean proves the fill sweep and any number of them at once (kernel), and that the run from blank tape follows them. A C simulator that jumps over the fill sweeps runs 28 cycles, 1.06·1017 steps, with no halt; it agrees exactly with direct simulation through cycle 12. The prefix holds smaller gaps that grow by factors near 1.36 to 1.5 per cycle, an irregular hierarchy with no visible closed form.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Bell-like. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

No halt in 28 cycles, 1.06·1017 steps

c/cyc17.c simulates directly but rewrites the tape for all fill sweeps but the last two at once. Its cycle starts (step, K, whole prefix) agree with plain simulation through cycle 12 (25,164,855 steps). It ran to cycle 28 with no halt: 106,309,693,328,363,073 steps, K = 798,641,935, 9.99·109 steps simulated directly, before it ran out of its 1.5·109-cell tape.

c/cyc17.c

Rounds between end events, by remainder class

Sample the run whenever the head is in state A reading 0 with only 0s to its left. Read rightwards from the head, the tape is then A U^m B: one long periodic block between two short context words. For each class (A, U, B, m modulo 1 or 2 (the smallest modulus that works for the context)) the next sample is simulated for six values of m and extended as an affine map of m (with a cubic step count). Along the direct run from blank tape to 3·109 steps (24 events) the class map predicts the next event in all 15 rounds it applies to. This is an extrapolation checked by computation, not a proof.

Shape of the cycles

K goes 9, 15, 27, 51, 99, 197, 389, 773, …, 798641935: K' = 2K − c, where c is a few tens at first and grows with the prefix. The prefix (37,199 cells at cycle 28) alternates between two head forms. Its tail holds gaps of 0s, at cycle 28 0^313 1^3 0^711 1^3 0^36103; the largest grows by about 1.48 per cycle and the next ones by 1.36 to 1.5. New gaps appear and old ones merge. It halts only on B>1 1 (B1 goes to F, F1 halts), which would have to happen in one of the passes over the prefix.

No halting class among 785 contexts

Breadth first from the starting context, with every remainder at every context (whether or not the numbers can really produce that sequence of remainders, and only for m large compared with the contexts): 785 contexts expanded, 785 found, to depth 23. None of them halts; the search is not complete (the set of contexts keeps growing), and it says nothing about a halt that needs a context as long as the block itself. 355 contexts longer than 300 cells were not expanded.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The fill phase is understood and proved. The passes that rewrite the prefix are not: they act like a hierarchy of counters whose gaps grow at incommensurate rates, and no invariant is known that keeps B>1 1 away.
