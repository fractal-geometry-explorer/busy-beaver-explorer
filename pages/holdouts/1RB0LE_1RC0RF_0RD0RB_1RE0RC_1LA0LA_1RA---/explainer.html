Does this machine ever halt?

1RB0LE_1RC0RF_0RD0RB_1RE0RC_1LA0LA_1RA---

Nobody knows yet. The machine works in cycles. Each cycle fills a long gap of 0s four cells at a time, then opens a new gap about twice as long and rewrites a short prefix that holds smaller gaps. Lean proves the filling. A simulator that jumps over it ran 28 cycles, 1017 steps, without a halt.

The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in F reading 1. F is entered only from B1 (writes 0, moves R): B>1 1. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

Remainder classes

Does any remainder class halt?

A generic test used on the whole family: sample the run whenever the head is in state A reading 0 with only 0s to its left. Read rightwards from the head, the tape is then A Um B, one long periodic block between two short context words A and B. The round to the next sample is computed class by class: for a class (A, U, B, m modulo 1 or 2 (the smallest modulus that works for the context)) six values of m are simulated, and when they agree the round is taken to be an affine map of m. Against the direct run to 3·109 steps the class map is right in all 15 rounds it applies to. It is an observed law, not a proved one.

Can any class halt? Breadth first from the starting context, with every remainder at every context (whether or not the numbers can really produce that sequence of remainders, and only for m large compared with the contexts): 785 contexts expanded, 785 found, to depth 23. None of them halts; the search is not complete (the set of contexts keeps growing), and it says nothing about a halt that needs a context as long as the block itself. 355 contexts longer than 300 cells were not expanded.

First look

The shape of the run

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.508).

After 109 steps the tape spans 83,723 cells (23 left of the start, 65,615 right), with a density of 1s of 0.414.

Structure

Cycles: a prefix and a gap

Watch the tape at the moments the head is in state E on a new cell at the right end. At the start of each cycle it reads P 1^3 0^K 1 E>0: a short prefix P, three 1s and a gap of K zeros. The first gaps are 9, 15, 27, 51, 99, 197, 389 and 773: each is nearly twice the one before.

Cycle 5, at step 3,194: 1^2 0^3 1^3 0^3 1^3 0^3 1 0 1^3 0 1^3 0 1^3 0 1^3 0^99 1 E>0.

The basic move

Filling the gap

Within a cycle the right end reads 0^z 1 0 1 1 1 0 (10)^m 1 E>0. One sweep runs left to the gap and back. It takes four 0s off the gap and adds four 10 pairs, z → z − 4 and m → m + 4, in 4m + 40 steps. The prefix is not touched. Lean proves this for all z, m and every prefix (fill, fills), and checks that the run from blank tape is in this form at step 3,224.

So a gap of K costs about K/4 sweeps and K2/2 steps, and each cycle takes about four times as many steps as the one before.

The emergent behaviour

Two passes and a hierarchy of gaps

When fewer than four 0s are left, one pass runs to the left end of the tape and one runs back. They rewrite the prefix and turn the (10)^m run into the next gap, K' = 2K − c. The prefix also holds gaps. They grow more slowly, by about 1.36 to 1.5 per cycle, and new ones appear while old ones merge. At cycle 28 (K = 798,641,935) the prefix is 37,199 cells long and ends 0^313 1^3 0^711 1^3 0^36103.

The danger

Where it could halt

The only halting transition is F reading 1, and F is entered only from B reading 1. So the machine halts exactly when B meets 1 1. The fill sweeps never do this. It would have to happen during a pass over the prefix, whose contents keep changing in an irregular way.

Status

What is proved

Lean, kernel: the fill sweep and k sweeps at once, with exact step counts, and the run from blank tape reaching them (lean/Macro.lean).

Lean, native_decide: no halt in the first 107 steps (lean/Blank.lean).

Computed: 28 cycles and 1.06·1017 steps without a halt (c/cyc17.c, which agrees with plain simulation through cycle 12).

Open: the passes over the prefix, and so the question.

README.md
Shape of the cycles

K goes 9, 15, 27, 51, 99, 197, 389, 773, …, 798641935: K' = 2K − c, where c is a few tens at first and grows with the prefix. The prefix (37,199 cells at cycle 28) alternates between two head forms. Its tail holds gaps of 0s, at cycle 28 0^313 1^3 0^711 1^3 0^36103; the largest grows by about 1.48 per cycle and the next ones by 1.36 to 1.5. New gaps appear and old ones merge. It halts only on B>1 1 (B1 goes to F, F1 halts), which would have to happen in one of the passes over the prefix.

Open
Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The fill phase is understood and proved. The passes that rewrite the prefix are not: they act like a hierarchy of counters whose gaps grow at incommensurate rates, and no invariant is known that keeps B>1 1 away.
