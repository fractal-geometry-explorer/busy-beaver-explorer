Does this machine ever halt?

1RB---_0RC0RE_1RD1RF_1LE0LB_1RC0LD_1RC1RA

Nobody knows yet. A counter grows by a factor of about 8/3 each phase, and its residues mod 3 edit a word of blocks on the tape. A halting word exists, but the nearest one needs 108 particular residues in a row, so no simulation will see it; whether the real sequence ever produces one is the open question.

The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in A reading 1. A is entered only from F1 (writes 1, moves R): F>1 1. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

The run at a glance

The diagram stacks the tape at many moments. Sampling at each new cell shows the shape of the run as the visited region grows; sampling evenly in time shows where the head spends its time. The records list every step at which the head reaches a cell no earlier step reached, with the side, the width so far and the whole tape: the first place to look for a repeating macro structure.

Macro structure

Phases: a counter and a word

Stop the machine every time the head, in state C, is at the right end of the tape with the tape reading

P 1^n 0 C>0

a word P (ending in 0), a block of n 1s and the fixed end 0. During a phase the head goes back and forth between the counter and the right end: each round takes three 1s from the counter and writes four pairs 01 at the right (Lean, for all counts). When fewer than three 1s are left, the residue n mod 3 decides what happens: the head goes on into P and edits its last blocks, and on the way back the pairs become the next counter. So n becomes 8·(n div 3) + d, where d and the next word depend only on P and n mod 3.

In the words, a residue-0 phase absorbs the last block of P into the counter (proved in Lean for the machines with factor 8/3), while the other residues push new blocks or edit the last ones, often through a smaller phase of the same kind run on a block of P. So P is a stack of nested counters.

Open question

Does any word halt?

The word map was measured by simulation (each pair of a word and a residue is run from three sample counts, which must agree). Exploring the word dynamics breadth first for every sequence of residues (600 words, the longest 43 cells): no transition halts, and every one returns to a phase start.

From the first phase start (step 110, P = 1111110, n = 12), computed exactly: no halt in 2,000 phases (n then has 2,835 bits); 20 distinct words, the longest 17 cells, and the word keeps returning to empty.

So, unlike Antihydra-type machines, there is no known halting word and no probabilistic argument for a halt: the machine is plausibly nonhalting. A proof would need a description of every word the dynamics can reach, closed under all residues, together with the phase ends proved in Lean for each kind of word. That was not done here.

Related machines. 1RB---_0RC0RE_1RD1RF_1LE0LB_1RC0LD_1RC1RA and 1RB0LC_1RC1RE_1LA0LD_0RB0RA_1RB1RF_1RD--- are the same table with the states relabelled (no mirror), started in different states: their phase rules, word maps and Lean files are the same up to the name of the phase state (C and B), and only their orbits differ. 1RB---_1RC1RE_1LD0LE_1RF0LC_0RD0RA_0RB1RD has the same kind of phase, with factor 10/3 instead of 8/3.

Remainder classes

Some remainder classes halt, 108 rounds away

A generic test used on the whole family: sample the run whenever the head is in state A reading 0 with only 0s to its right. Read leftwards from the head, the tape is then A Um B, one long periodic block between two short context words A and B. The round to the next sample is computed class by class: for a class (A, U, B, m mod 3) six values of m are simulated, and when they agree the round is taken to be an affine map of m. Against the direct run to 3·109 steps the class map is right in all 7 rounds it applies to. It is an observed law, not a proved one.

The orbit. Iterated with exact integers from the event at step 2,763 of the run (A = 0, U = 1, m = 107, B = (empty)): 161,449 rounds without meeting a halting class; m then has 228,464 bits (about 10137,548 steps of the machine). 113 distinct contexts occur, the longest of 34 cells.

Can any class halt? Breadth first from the starting context, with every remainder at every context (whether or not the numbers can really produce that sequence of remainders, and only for m large compared with the contexts): 5,000 contexts expanded, 5,440 found, to depth 44. None of them halts; the search is not complete (the set of contexts keeps growing), and it says nothing about a halt that needs a context as long as the block itself.

A halt is reachable. The same search restricted to contexts of at most 26 cells runs to the end: 3,089 contexts (488 longer ones are reached but not expanded), and 2 of their classes halt. The nearest is 108 rounds from the starting context: after the remainders 12 22 1 2 12 2 13 22 1 22 12 22 1 2 12 22 1 22 13 22 1 22 1 22 12 22 13 22 1 2 13 22 1 22 12 22 1 2 12 22 1 22 13 22 1 22 1 22 12 22 1 2 12 22 1 22 13 22 1 22 1 2 (mod 3) the tail is 01011011, and with m ≡ 1 (mod 3) the machine halts before the next event (all six simulated values of m halt). So, granted the class map, a halt is possible, but only through one prescribed sequence of 108 remainders or a longer one; with remainders like fair dice that is one chance in 3108 per try. The orbit computed has not come near it.

That makes the machine a cryptid: whether it halts is a question about one orbit of a Collatz-like map, and nothing known decides it.

First look

The shape of the run

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.505).

After 109 steps the tape spans 76,311 cells (13 left of the start, 59,647 right), with a density of 1s of 0.609.
