1RB0LF_0RC1RD_0LD0RA_1RC0RE_1RF---_1LA0LB

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves (kernel) that a halt needs the word A>0 1 1 1 (halt_needs_A0111), A stepping onto the front of a train, and proves the sweep that eats one word of a train (sweep_train, for all values of its parameters). No halt in the first 1011 steps (C); in the run, A meets 0 1 1 0 thousands of times but never 0 1 1 1.

No halt in the first 10,000 steps

The list simulator of the shared library, proved to agree with the machine's semantics, runs 10,000 steps from blank tape without a halt, evaluated by Lean's kernel.

blank_runs_kernel, lean/Blank.lean

A halt needs A>0 1 1 1

Three steps before a halt the head is in state A on a 0 followed by 1 1 1: E (halting on a 1) is entered only from D1, D only from B1, and B from A0 or from F1 (which writes the 0 that D would read).
