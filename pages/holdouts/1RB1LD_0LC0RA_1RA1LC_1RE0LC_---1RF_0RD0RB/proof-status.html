What is known about 1RB1LD_0LC0RA_1RA1LC_1RE0LC_---1RF_0RD0RB

1RB1LD_0LC0RA_1RA1LC_1RE0LC_---1RF_0RD0RB

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. After step 43,506 the left end of the tape never moves again. The tape is an irregular region of ones-runs separated by single 0s, then a tail (10)^n. Each tick the head zigzags left across the tail and runs back, adding a cell. The two sweeps are proved in Lean. Unlike a counter, the irregular region grows in proportion to the number of ticks, and a tick can rewrite it at any depth. No structure that would decide halting has been found.

The halting configurations

The machine stops exactly when it reads the symbol of a halting transition (E0); the generated statement covers every configuration.

step_none_iff, lean/Blank.lean

Where a halt can come from

If the machine halts on its third step from a configuration, that configuration is one of: F with 0 under the head, 0 right, 0 two right. So a run can reach a halt only through one of these patterns, and a proof that the machine never halts only has to rule them out. The proof splits on the state and the five cells around the head.

halt_route, lean/Route.lean (written by py/route.py)

No halt in the first 10,000 steps

The list simulator of the shared library, proved to agree with the machine's semantics, runs 10,000 steps from blank tape without a halt, evaluated by Lean's kernel.

blank_runs_kernel, lean/Blank.lean

The sweeps across the tail

Going left, the head zigzags across (10)^n in states C and A, two cells every four steps (zig). Going right in state B, it runs across in one step a cell, rewriting each 10 as 01 (run_right). Both hold for every n and every tape around.

zig, run_right, lean/Rules.lean

Both parts grow linearly in the ticks

In the first 109 steps the left end is last extended at step 43,506. At steps 2.8·106, 2.5·107 and 108, the irregular region has 1,528, 4,060 and 7,904 cells and the tail has 374, 1,006 and 1,931 copies of 10. The tape grows by one cell per tick, so both parts grow like the square root of the time.

What the irregular region is made of

At step 108 the region is a sequence of ones-runs separated by single 0s. Of its 1,191 runs, 679 have length 6, 246 length 2, 193 length 10, and the rest are rare defects (lengths 0, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 14).

holdouts/1RB1LD_1LC0RE_1LA0LD_0LB0LE_0RB0LF_1RE---/c/turns.c

Rewrites at every depth

Over the 11,392 ticks in the first 108 steps, about half change only the last 20 cells of the region. The rest reach back with a slowly decaying, heavy-tailed distribution: 563 reach 20–39 cells, 109 reach 80–99, and still 30 reach 720–739. This is not the geometric fall-off of a counter.

Regular-language deciders tried (F11)

None of these closes: each reaches a halting configuration in its abstraction, which says nothing about whether the machine halts. n-gram CPS (tools/c/ngramcps.c), equal widths on both sides: a halting local configuration is reached with plain up to width 24, history 1 up to width 24, history 2 up to width 24, history 3 up to width 24, LRU history up to width 22 (larger widths ran out of the 150 s or 60 million configuration budget). n-gram CPS with every cell tagged by its position mod p: fails for p = 2 up to width 20, p = 3 up to width 20, p = 4 up to width 20, p = 6 up to width 20. n-gram CPS where each side also carries the state of a small automaton that has read that side from the far end (every automaton with up to 3 states on both sides, or 4 states on one side), with position mod 1..4 and widths 4, 6, 8: no closed set without a halt in all 120 parameter sets. Run-length windows (the K nearest runs of equal macro cells on each side, run lengths kept exactly below T and mod M above): fails for all 72 parameter sets (cells of 1 to 4 bits, K = 2, 4, 6, T = 2, 4, M = 1, 2, 4). Two-automaton closed tape language by SAT (a deterministic automaton reading each side inward, n states each, CaDiCaL): no solution for n = 2..9; n = 10 not finished in 150 s.

sweeps of 13 November 2026 (family F11); the tagged and run-length deciders are not in the repository yet

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: fixed/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.474).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Why it is hard

The machine halts only when D reads 0 with a 0 to its right (E reading 0 is its only undefined transition). The irregular region looks like the output of a process with long-range signals rather than a counter, and no rule for it has been found. It might hide a simpler structure at a larger scale.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The open part is the dynamics of the irregular region.
