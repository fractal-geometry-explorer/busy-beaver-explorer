What is known about 1RB1RD_1LC0LE_1RA0LB_1RA1RF_0RA0RC_1RE---

1RB1RD_1LC0LE_1RA0LB_1RA1RF_0RA0RC_1RE---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 13 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The machine iterates a Collatz-like map: a block of m ones becomes, one phase later, a block of about 8m/3 ones, with a short prefix that acts as a second, nested counter. The cycle inside a phase is proved in Lean; the phase map is fitted case by case and agrees with direct simulation through 1.67·1010 steps. Iterated for 3,000 phases (about 102557 steps) it never meets a case that halts. Update, 13 November 2026: a search of all tails of at most 26 cells with the generic class map finds halting classes; the nearest needs 107 prescribed residues mod 3 in a row from the start of the run (observed, not proved). The machine is a cryptid.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Bell-like. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

The phase map, checked against the simulator

From the phase start Q 0 1^m (01)^5 1 D>0 with m = 3k + r, the next phase start is Q' 0 1^(8k+c) (01)^5 1 D>0 with Q' and c fixed by (Q, r), and a step count 12k² + Bk + C. Fitted on four block sizes per case; the predicted start steps of phases 1 to 9 (3,291 ... 16,707,143,908) equal those of the direct C simulation.

py/phasemap.py

Rounds between end events, by remainder class

Sample the run whenever the head is in state D reading 1 with only 0s to its right. Read leftwards from the head, the tape is then A U^m B: one long periodic block between two short context words. For each class (A, U, B, m mod 3) the next sample is simulated for six values of m and extended as an affine map of m (with a cubic step count). Along the direct run from blank tape to 3·109 steps (10 events) the class map predicts the next event in all 7 rounds it applies to. This is an extrapolation checked by computation, not a proof.

heuristic Evidence only

3,000 phases without a halting case

The fitted map, iterated from blank tape for 3,000 phases (to about 102557 steps), meets 68 (prefix, remainder) cases, none of which halts; the 120 prefixes nearest the empty one (breadth first) have no halting case either.

python3 py/phasemap.py 3000, python3 py/phasemap.py cases 120

Growth shape: fixed/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.505).

No halting class among 5,000 contexts

Breadth first from the starting context, with every remainder at every context (whether or not the numbers can really produce that sequence of remainders, and only for m large compared with the contexts): 5,000 contexts expanded, 5,440 found, to depth 43. None of them halts; the search is not complete (the set of contexts keeps growing), and it says nothing about a halt that needs a context as long as the block itself.

A halting class is reachable, 107 rounds away

The same search restricted to contexts of at most 26 cells runs to the end: 10,061 contexts (1,649 longer ones are reached but not expanded), and 5 of their classes halt. The nearest is 107 rounds from the starting context: after the remainders 1 22 1 2 12 2 13 22 1 22 12 22 1 2 12 22 1 22 13 22 1 22 1 22 12 22 13 22 1 2 13 22 1 22 12 22 1 2 12 22 1 22 13 22 1 22 1 22 12 22 1 2 12 22 1 22 13 22 1 22 1 2 (mod 3) the tail is 01011011, and with m ≡ 1 (mod 3) the machine halts before the next event (all six simulated values of m halt). So, granted the class map, a halt is possible, but only through one prescribed sequence of 107 remainders or a longer one; with remainders like fair dice that is one chance in 3107 per try. The orbit computed has not come near it.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. Granted the class map (observed, not proved), it halts exactly if the orbit ever reaches a halting class; the nearest known one needs 107 prescribed remainders mod 3 in a row. A Collatz-like question about one orbit: a cryptid.
