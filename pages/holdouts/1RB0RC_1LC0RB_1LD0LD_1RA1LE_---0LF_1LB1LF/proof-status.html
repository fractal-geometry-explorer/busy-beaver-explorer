OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The run is understood at the level of words: at the left turn the tape is blocks of ones separated by single 0s; between round trips the whole tape except a chaotic zone of about 450 to 610 cells at the right end slides 16 cells left unchanged, and the left end gains one copy of the word 1^5 0 1^6 0 1^4 0 1 0 per round trip. The sweep rules are proved in Lean; the right-end engine is not understood, and the machine can halt only inside it or at a turn.

Lean proves no halt in the first 107 steps (10,000 by the kernel alone); the C simulator finds none in 109.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Bouncer + Chaotic Edge. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

How it works

At the moment the head turns at the left end, the tape is a row of blocks of ones separated by single 0s. The rightward sweep rewrites each block into runs of three ones separated by runs of 0s; the leftward sweep rebuilds blocks, with short zigzags. Both sweeps are lists of shift rules proved in lean/Rules.lean.

Over a round trip almost nothing changes: the tape between the first 67 cells and the last 450–610 is the same word as before, slid 16 cells to the left, and the left end gains one copy of 1^5 0 1^6 0 1^4 0 1 0. All the activity is in a small chaotic engine at the right end, which moves right with the tape's edge and leaves a frozen, non-periodic trail. Whether that engine can ever write the one pattern that halts (D reading a 1 with a 0 on its left) is the open question.

The sweep rules

Five-to-thirteen-step shift rules of the two sweeps, for every repeat count and any tape around: B erases a block of ones going right (b_erase), F keeps one going left (f_ones), and B crosses the words 1101111 and 101111 of the right sweep.

Growth shape: sqrt/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.505). Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. A proof would have to control the right-end engine: show that the cells it leaves behind never change again and that it never writes 0 1 where D arrives. Its width looks bounded but creeps up slowly; no invariant for it is known.
