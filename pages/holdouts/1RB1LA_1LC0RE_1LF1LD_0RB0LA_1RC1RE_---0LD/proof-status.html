Open: a cryptidIts halting is thought to come down to a Collatz-like question that no known method decides (the community's label, not a result).

This is Space Needle, a six-state machine found by mxdys in January 2025 and listed by the bbchallenge community as a cryptid: its halting from blank tape is equivalent to an open number-theory question. This page records what is established here, sorted by what each result rests on.

The tape is a few long blocks of 1s. From one visit of the left edge to the next, the machine applies mxdys's map to the lengths (b, c) of the two blocks of 0^∞ A>0 1^b 0 0 1^c 0^∞. Lean proves the map, for all b and c, and proves that the machine halts exactly when Katelyn Doucette's one-variable sequence 6, 10, 17, 41, 101, 251, ... reaches a power of two.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open, and equivalent to a question about a simple sequence. Lean proves, checked by the kernel alone, that the machine halts from blank tape if and only if some term of the sequence D0 = 6, Dk+1 = Dk + v + 3a, where Dk = 2v(2a+1), is a power of two (halts_iff_pow2).

None of the first 300,000 terms is (Lean, native_decide; the first 20,000 by the kernel), nor of the first 3·106 (C). The terms grow by a factor of about 1.92 per step and their number of factors 2 stays small (at most 24 so far), so a power of two would be an extraordinary coincidence: the machine is probviously non-halting. A proof would need control of the 2-adic valuations of a sequence defined by additions, which is beyond current methods.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Cubic Mess. Its 3 November 2026 edition lists the machine as a cryptid: its halting is thought to come down to a Collatz-like question that no known method decides. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

The halting configurations

The machine stops exactly when F reads a 0.

step_none_iff, lean/Blank.lean

Shift rules

Fifteen rules on symbolic tapes, for all values of their parameters and any surrounding tape: one bounce of the bouncer (L 0 1^(m+3) C>0 R to L 1 0 1^(m+1) C>0 1 R), one zig-zag, the ends of the phases, the halt of (1, c), and blank tape to (3, 1) in 45 steps. Written by tools/py/bbtools/symtm.py as chains of rfl steps and the crossing lemmas.

lean/Rules.lean, py/gen_rules.py

mxdys's macro rules

Write X(b, c) for 0^∞ A>0 1^b 0 0 1^c 0^∞. For every b and c ≥ 1: X(0, c) reaches X(c + 2, 1); X(2k + 2, c) reaches X(5k + c + 7, 1); X(2k + 3, c) reaches X(k, k + c + 4); X(1, c) halts 13 steps later. Proved by three inductions (the bouncer, the zig-zag, the shift of a block) over the shift rules.

even_rule, odd_rule, zero_rule, halt_rule, lean/Macro.lean

The reduction to mxdys's map

Let g be the map above on pairs (b, c), and orbit n its n-th iterate from (3, 1). The machine halts from blank tape if and only if some orbit n has b = 1.

halts_iff, lean/Macro.lean

The reduction to Doucette's sequence

Let D0 = 6 and Dk+1 = F(Dk) with F(D) = D + v + 3a for D = 2v(2a + 1). The configurations X(b, 1) of the orbit are exactly b = Dk − 3; between two of them come v odd steps, each halving b + 3, and one even step. So the machine halts from blank tape if and only if some Dk is a power of two.

The macro rules against the machine

A direct run of 3·1010 steps (c/verify.c) meets the shape X(b, c) exactly at the configurations the orbit of g predicts, in order, and at no others: 19 of them, from X(3, 1) at step 45 to X(7483, 1) at step 10,608,569,291 and X(3740, 3745) at step 10,636,589,390.

No power of two in the first 3·106 terms

Multi-precision C (c/doucette.c, checked against Python for the first 105 terms: bit length and largest v agree). The 3·106th term has about 2.8·106 bits; the largest v seen is 24, the mean v is 0.998. The community reports 108 terms with at most 25 factors of 2.

c/doucette.c

heuristic Evidence only

Probviously non-halting

If the low bits of Dk behave like random bits, v is geometric with mean 1, D grows by an average factor of about 1.920 per term (0.9412 bits, LegionMammal978's estimate; 0.9414 measured), and the chance that a term of n bits is a power of two is about 2−n. Summed over the terms after the ones checked, the chance of a halt is far below 10−10000.
