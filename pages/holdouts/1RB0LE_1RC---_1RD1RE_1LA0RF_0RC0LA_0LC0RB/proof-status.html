1RB0LE_1RC---_1RD1RE_1LA0RF_0RC0LA_0LC0RB

This six-state, two-symbol Turing machine was one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. It does not halt from blank tape: Lean proves it with the kernel alone, from a table of phase rules proved for all sizes and a linear invariant on the phase forms. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 9 November 2026.

In short

It never halts. Lean proves nonhalting (kernel only; axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound). From blank tape the machine reaches a phase form whose counts satisfy an invariant Good, a set of linear inequalities, and every move of the branch table (one cycle iterate and one exit rule, 20 cases) leads, after at least one step, to a form that satisfies it again.

What it does. The run alternates between two kinds of phase. In a right phase the head never visits the left end: the tape is (1110)a (1111110)k 111 001 (10)m, and each visit to the right end moves one copy from the run of 10 to the run of 1111110, keeping k + m fixed. In a round-trip phase the tape seen from the left end is 0… (1000)a (10)b wc with the 14-cell word w = 00000010000010 (two copies of the old 7-cell word rewritten); c falls while a and b grow, and when c reaches 0 the next right phase starts. Each phase is longer than the previous one (round-trip phases from 0.8 to 2.0·106 and from 6.4 to 20.8·106 steps), which is why the tape grows like the square root of time on average but in bursts.

Where a halt would come from. Only B reading a 1 halts. B is entered from A reading a 0 and from F reading a 1, both stepping right; so A>0 1 and F>1 1 halt (halt_A, halt_F). In the run B always reads a 0.

The branch table, for all sizes

Besides the cycles and odd_phase, Lean proves for all values of the parameters: the entry of an even phase, EF (a+7) (q+5) → T3 5 14 a 8 7 (q+2); the five exits of its 5-visit cycle by the number d < 5 of 0010 left (d = 0, 3, 4: two runs of 10 merge into T2; d = 1, 2: three runs, the middle one frozen, form U3); the 4-visit cycle of U3; the exits of the two- and three-run round trips into right phases RG and RG3, for both parities of the 14-cell words left; the cycles of those right phases; their ends when the run of 1111011 has even length (into O, or into T2); and the exit of the 4-visit cycle with no 14-cell word left (sexit0, into the right phase RF). lean/Ends.lean adds the other parities: RG and RG3 ending with an odd run of 1111011 start a 5-visit round trip T3 directly (gendo', g3endo'), and RF ending with an even run of 1111110 gives O a 0 c (rende'), which is an S1 after one pass (ozero, t3_s1). With these every form has a rule for each residue of its counts. Every rule is a symbolic run checked by the kernel, followed by a re-bracketing proof. Each rule states its exact step count.

The invariant

Good gives, for each of ten forms (O a b c with b large, O a 0 c, S1, RF, EF, T3, T2, U3, RG, RG3), 3 to 16 linear inequalities with integer coefficients: the growing counts keep their ratios in a polyhedral cone that every move maps strictly inside the next one; the bounded counts stay in range and T3, U3, RG3 keep exact relations (for example x = 2a + 4 in T3); and a positive linear size, which every move multiplies by at least 1.1, stays above a threshold. These give every offset the rules need, and c ≥ 2⌊d/5⌋ + 3 in T3, so the 5-visit cycle never runs out of words. good_step proves each of the 20 cases with one move lemma and omega; the end of table_from_blank satisfies Good (decide).

Growth shape: sqrt/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.465). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.529).

How the invariant was found

Not part of the proof. The forms' counts at the form entries along the replay, divided by their sum, give a cloud of points per form. A fixpoint of linear programs over the 20 moves (homogeneous parts, margin 10-3) enlarges their convex hulls until each move maps every cone inside the next; a second linear program finds the size function. Along the replay every form entry from the 39th on satisfies Good.
