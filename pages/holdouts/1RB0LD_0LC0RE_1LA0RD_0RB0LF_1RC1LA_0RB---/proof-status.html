OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Mirrored and with its states renamed, this machine has the engine of 1RB0LE_1LC0RE_0RA0LD_1LA0LF_0LC0RA_0RE--- and the F02 two-word sweepers: a base-3 counter N with 3-cell digits at the right end (the wall of 110s of the first look is made of its top digits), binary counters with 4-cell digits on the left, and an undecoded middle between them. The laws of N and of the binary streams are proved in Lean for every size.

Status as of 16 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. No halt in LONGRUN_STEPS steps of the C simulator; Lean proves no halt in the first 107 steps (native_decide) and the laws of the outer parts of the tape for every size.

Where a halt would come from. Only F1 halts and F is entered only from D reading 1 (0LF), millions of times (3,571,420 in 108 steps), always with a 0 left of D. The rare transition is E1 (66 times in 108 steps), the counterpart of the rare D1 events of its relative.

What is missing. The rules of the middle part, and an invariant explaining why D never reads 1 next to a 1.

The base-3 counter N, for every size

Read the cells right of N's low end three at a time from the head outward, 010 = 0, 110 = 1, 100 = 2, blank beyond. A visit in state C adds one; it comes back out in A without carry and in D with a carry; a carry through all digits makes the blank into a new digit 2.

n_inc0, n_inc1, n_inc0_carry, n_inc1_carry, n_overflow, lean/Counter.lean (rows in lean/VisitR.lean)

The run uses the counter law

In the run, the cells right of 126 are entered only in state C, and all 142,058 of those visits, from step 689,712 to step 43,905,102, are rows of the counter law; then the middle reaches 126 and N's low end moves outward. At 109 steps N fills cells 149 to 191 (15 digits).
