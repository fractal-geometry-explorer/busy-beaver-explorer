What is known about 1RB0RF_1LC1RE_0LD0LC_1RD0RB_1RA0RB_0LC---

1RB0RF_1LC1RE_0LD0LC_1RD0RB_1RA0RB_0LC---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. Its tape is a word of blocks of 1s on the left and a binary counter on the right; the head rewrites the word by a fixed map and counts. This page lists what has been established, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 12 November 2026.

In short

The question is open, but it is now a question about a small explicit map. Lean (kernel) proves every kind of macro step the run takes: the six regular steps, the anomalies and all 26 cases of the collision. They are assembled into one function, the macro map, which sends a word-and-counter configuration to the next one, and Lean proves: if the macro map can be applied for ever starting from the configuration reached at step 27, the machine never halts. The map goes through 108 macro steps without failing (Lean, compiled evaluation; this is more than 1.7·109 machine steps) and through 300 anomalies and collisions in the accelerated simulation (about 21248 machine steps). A proof that it never fails would need to control the low binary digits of the counter at every anomaly; they behave like random bits, and we found no way to do that.

The six regular Q-steps

From W 0 1^(3a) B>0 R the machine reaches the next Q configuration after a step count linear in the parameters, in each of the six cases (last block 2j+5, 3, or a 1 after 2i+1; counter low part a+2, or one unit then t gaps of 2): shift rules generated by bbtools.symtm.prove.

q_big_wide … q_one_unit, lean/Rules.lean

Q-step = φ twice, counter plus one

The same six steps restated on Q configurations qc W a P T: the word goes to phi (phi W) (phi defined on lists) and the counter's low part and gaps update as a binary counter.

qstep_big_wide … qstep_one_unit, lean/Macro.lean

The anomaly, exactly

When the word has run out the machine rewrites it in one sweep, for every value of the parameters and every tape beyond: from 1^(2c+2) 0 1^(3(a+1)) B>0 R (blank to the left) it reaches 1^2 (0 1)^c 0 1^3 B>0 0^(3a) 1 R, one cell further left, in 3a+8c+17 steps; from the word 1^(2c+2) 0 1 with a+2 units it reaches the word 2 1…1 3 with a gap of 3a+1 in 3a+8c+46 steps; with one unit and a first gap 3(p+1)+2 it reaches the regular Q configuration with word 2 1…1 5 and first gap 3p+2 in 8c+57 steps. The new gap is the misaligned one (not of the form 3p+2).

anom_one, anom_two, anom_unit and anomaly_one, anomaly_two, anomaly_unit, lean/Transients.lean

The word-and-gap pattern is not an invariant

From the Q configuration 1^2 0 1^3 0 1^3 B>0 (a valid word 2 3, a one-unit counter, no gaps, blank tape) the machine halts at step 190. Its word is so short that it runs out a second time before the misaligned gap made by the first anomaly is cleared; the second anomaly leaves gaps of 1 and 0 in a row, and the collision on them halts. So no proof can use only the shape of the word and of the gaps (even/odd blocks, gaps ≡ 2 mod 3): it has to carry the sizes.

qc_short_halts, lean/Transients.lean

The collision, in all its cases

A collision is the step taken when the counter's low part is one unit, followed by t gaps of length 2 and then a gap g of length 0 or 1. Lean proves what it does for every t, both values of g, any number U of gaps of length 2 behind it, and a next gap of at least 5 cells or blank tape (26 rules): the word takes its usual double step, its last block grows by 2, and the counter's low part is turned into about 3t/2 new blocks at the end of the word; the counter restarts from one unit (or a few, when U ≥ 2) and the next gap is shortened by 3, 4 or 5 cells. The rules do not depend on the word: they are proved for any tape to the left.

coll_e0_0_gap … coll_o1_u2_blank, lean/Collide.lean (generated by py/gen_collide.py); restated as zcoll_* in lean/CollideQ.lean; the table is coll in lean/Full.lean

The anomaly with a one-unit counter

When the word is a block and a single 1 and the counter's low part is one unit followed by t gaps of length 2 and a gap of at least 3 cells (or blank tape), the new word is 2 1…1 5, the low part becomes t+1 units and the gap loses 3 cells, for every value of the parameters (4 rules). Together with the two anomalies above this covers every anomaly of the simulated run.

anom_unit0_gap, anom_unit0_blank, anom_units_gap, anom_units_blank, lean/Collide.lean; qa_* in lean/CollideQ.lean

The macro map, and that the machine follows it

astep is a function on configurations (W, a, G) (the word's blocks, the counter's low part, the list of gap lengths, standing for the tape W 0 1^(3a) B>0 0^g1 1 0^g2 1 …). It returns the next such configuration: a regular step, an anomaly or a collision, or nothing when none of the proved rules applies. Lean proves that whenever it returns a configuration, the machine really goes from the first tape to the second, in at least 17 steps.

astep, astep_sound, lean/Full.lean; configurations qr in lean/Abstract.lean

Non-halting reduced to the macro map

The run from blank tape reaches the configuration W = 2 1, a = 1, no gaps at step 27. Lean proves: if the macro map can be applied any number of times starting there, the machine never halts; and if it can be applied k times, the machine does not halt in its first 27 + 17k steps. So the halting problem of this machine is now the question whether a short explicit function on lists of numbers ever returns nothing.

start_reached, not_halts_of_arun, runs_of_arun, lean/Full.lean

The race

Over 99 epochs (anomaly to anomaly) the collision cascade ends at most 232 steps after the anomaly, while the epoch lasts up to 21222 steps; the smallest margin is 4.7 bits (1 epoch below 10 bits).

The macro map agrees with the machine on 300 transients

py/amodel.py is the macro map in Python. Along the accelerated run every anomaly and collision (89 + 11 + 8 anomalies of the three kinds, 192 collisions) was also run cell by cell; the map gives the same configuration each time, and it never fails.

When the race is lost

After an anomaly from a one-block word, with the new word B bits long (see the explainer) and the counter's low part a units, the first collision comes after exactly 2a − 2 regular steps if a ≤ B − 1; if a ≥ B the word runs out first (checked for B = 2…8, a = 1…9 with the macro map). In the simulated run a is at most 18 while B grows from 7 to over 1200; the smallest B − a after the first three anomalies is 6, and the counter is never longer than B bits.

python3 py/amodel.py race, python3 py/check_amodel.py 300

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/fixed

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.037).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Why it probably never halts

The low part a at an anomaly is one more than the number of trailing zeros of the counter's value at that moment. In the simulated run these values look like random bits: a = 1 about half the time, 2 a quarter of the time, and so on. If they were random, the chance of a ≥ B at an anomaly would be about 2−B, and since B grows by at least 5 at each anomaly the chances add up to less than 2−16 from the fourth anomaly on. This is a heuristic, not a proof: the bits are determined, not random.

observed over 108 anomalies; not proved

Gap alignment

In every run the gaps behind the first misaligned gap have length ≡ 2 (mod 3), at least 2, so every collision meets a next gap of at least 5 cells, a run of gaps of 2, or blank tape: the cases the macro map covers.

observed over all simulated transients; not proved

open Open

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. By the Lean reduction it is enough to show that the macro map never fails on the run. It can fail only if a misaligned gap is still there when the word runs out (the race is lost) and the gaps then line up badly. Winning every race needs, at each anomaly, that the counter's value is not divisible by a power of two about as large as the value itself (a ≤ B − 1). That value is the sum of the previous epochs' contributions, shifted and truncated by the collisions; we found no invariant that controls its low digits, and the situation resembles the Collatz-like problems where such digit questions are out of reach. A size bound alone (cascade length against word length) is not preserved from one epoch to the next: the counter may be as long as the word, and only its last digits decide.
