What is known about 1RB1LD_1RC0RC_1LD1RB_1LF0LE_0LA1LE_---1LB

1RB1LD_1RC0RC_1LD1RB_1LF0LE_0LA1LE_---1LB

OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 17 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves that, read in pairs, the tape at each turn is a binary word and one round trip of the head is one XOR sweep of it: find the rightmost 00, flip the letter left of it, replace every letter right of it by the XOR of itself and its right neighbour, append 11 (mstep_ok). A halt needs a 00 only at the very left end. The block of 111110 words enters the sweep only through its length mod 3 (zone_step), so Lean drops it: the machine halts from blank tape iff the zone orbit halts (halts_iff_zone), and compiled evaluation runs that orbit through the first 1.7·1015 steps without a halt. Since the last arrival the tape holds a new zone, a stranded block of 112,628 letters and the old zone at the left end. By the Lean lemmas the old zone moves only when a sweep gets through the new zone, and for as long as the block lasts it then moves exactly as it would alone: run alone it reaches the left end again, without halting, at its 17,532nd tick. In 3·1010 sweeps from zone step 3·107 only 23 sweeps got through (about 7 per factor of 10). Run a whole period at a time (lean/Window.lean), the cascade gives no halt in the first 1.5·1039 steps (compiled evaluation, lean/WindowRun.lean), and the C engine runs it for 3·277 sweeps without a halt or a reset.

The halting pattern

0 0 1 E> 0, the pass on the separator right of a block of length 1 with two 0s on its left, halts three steps later (F reads 0). By the table this is the only way to halt (hand argument in the README).

halt_pass, lean/Rules.lean

The pass

Moving left from separator to separator, the pass cuts a block of length k+3 into k+1 and 1 and goes on (pass_split); a block of length 1 stops it (pass_stop). For all lengths and every surrounding tape.

lean/Rules.lean, generated by py/gen_rules.py

Crossing the block of words does not depend on its length

The pass crosses n words 111110 in 6n steps, in the same state, turning each into 1110 10; the sweep back turns them into 111110 again. For every n and every surrounding tape.

cross_left, cross_right, lean/Rules.lean

The pass machine

This machine’s table is that of 1RB0RB_1LC1RA_1LF0LD_0LE1LD_1RA1LC_---1LA with the states renamed, so that machine’s exact pass machine carries over with the states renamed: a left pass over the pairs, a right pass, and the two ways of running off the left end, proved for all lists. Blank tape reaches the first turn configuration after 3 steps, and the machine halts from blank tape if and only if the orbit of the pass machine halts.

mstep_sound, init_RT, lean/Sweep.lean; halts_iff, lean/Reduce.lean

One round trip is one XOR sweep

At a turn at the right end, read in pairs, the tape is a binary word. When it has a 00, one macro step is exactly one sweep: the rightmost 00 is found, the letter left of it is flipped, every letter right of it becomes the XOR of itself and its right neighbour, and 11 is appended (4d + 12 steps, d the depth of the 00). A 00 only at the very left end halts; no 00 at all is a reset at the left end.

mstep_ok, mstep_halt, lean/Bits.lean; the sweep swr in lean/Xor.lean

The leftmost-zone reduction

For every length of the block of words the sweep does the same thing to the rest of the word; the length enters only mod 3, through one letter (zone_step). A sweep that finds its 00 early leaves everything further left untouched (swr_append_ok), and a sweep through a stretch without 00 acts beyond it only through the stretch’s last letter (swr_split).

lean/Xor.lean (the same file for the four machines with this block)

Halting is a question about the zone orbit

The zone orbit keeps only the count of the block of words and the rest of the word; it uses the sweep at the right end and the pass machine at the left end. The machine halts from blank tape if and only if the zone orbit halts.

halts_iff_zone, lean/Zone.lean

Halting needs the left end

Every halt of the macro step happens at the left end of the word: a sweep halts exactly when the only 00 of the word is at its very left end, and the other halting cases of the pass machine are passes that run off the left end.

mstep_halt, lean/Bits.lean; scanF / scan, apass in lean/Sweep.lean

The cascade a period at a time

A cell is back to its bit after a list of events or after two copies of it, so the events that enter cell m during the first 3·2d sweeps can be built cell by cell (one or two passes each, d the number of cells that needed two). Run through the rest of the word, they give every event that leaves it in that window.

wfold_run, window_quiet, window_of_check, lean/Window.lean

Two zones

Since the last arrival the tape holds a new zone, a stranded block of 112,628 letters and the old zone at the left end. By the Lean lemmas the old zone moves only when a sweep gets through the new zone, and for as long as the block lasts it then moves exactly as it would alone: run alone it reaches the left end again, without halting, at its 17,532nd tick. In 3·1010 sweeps from zone step 3·107 only 23 sweeps got through (about 7 per factor of 10). The old zone moving as if alone while the block lasts is a hand argument from the Lean lemmas (the cells that can differ spread from the new zone by at most one per sweep that reaches the block), checked against the zone orbit at all 23 passes of a run of 3·1010 sweeps.

Windows up to 3·277 sweeps

Cut after 161 cells, the window is 3·277 sweeps: nothing leaves the word. The 79 events that reach the old zone in it are all background, so the old zone has so far moved exactly as alone. The background events come about 1.35 per doubling of the window, the front of the disturbance moves about 3.3 cells per doubling (cell 255 at d = 77). If both speeds hold, the old zone gets its 17,532nd background event, after which alone it lets a sweep through as a reset, at d ≈ 13,000, while the front is near cell 43,000, far from the old zone.

heuristic Evidence only

The leftmost zone alone

The leftmost zone run by itself, one arriving pass at a time, from the real configuration of trigger 100, reproduces all the real arrivals at the left end up to trigger 112,740 (same triggers, same new cells). Continued, this model halts in its tick 21,540,826 after 36 more arrivals. That is not a prediction for the machine: after trigger 112,740 the old zone gets a pass only when the new zone lets one through, ever more rarely, and the block between the two zones is being used up, which the model does not describe.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. It halts if a pass reaches the leftmost block while that block is a lone 1 with blank tape on its left; nothing known forces or forbids that.
