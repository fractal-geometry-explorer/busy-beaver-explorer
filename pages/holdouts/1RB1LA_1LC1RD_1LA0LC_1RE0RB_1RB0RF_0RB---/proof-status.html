What is known about 1RB1LA_1LC1RD_1LA0LC_1RE0RB_1RB0RF_0RB---

1RB1LA_1LC1RD_1LA0LC_1RE0RB_1RB0RF_0RB---

OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. It belongs to a family of four holdouts that share their first four states, and works as two binary counters, one counting down in the blocks left of the head and one counting up in the gaps right of it. This page lists what is established, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean now proves the counter itself: read the tape as two binary numbers, E in the odd blocks left of the head and N in the gaps right of it, and every countdown of E, for every size of E and N, runs as the counter says (run3, run5). Lean also proves that the blank-tape run enters this counter after 160 steps and runs its first countdown. The pass rules (including the halting one) are proved too.

What is not proved is what happens between countdowns. When E runs out the tape is reorganised; the reorganisation leaves defects on the right (gaps of an irregular size, blocks of two 1s), and the machine halts only if such defects meet in one particular way. No invariant excluding this is known. An accelerated simulation, whose jumps are instances of the Lean countdowns, reaches 8.47·101907 steps with no halt and with never more than one defect at a time.

The pass rules

Six rules on symbolic tapes, for all values of their parameters and any surrounding tape: a pass on an even top block (L 1 A>0 1^(2k+4) 0^(g+2) 1 R), on a block of 2, on an odd block crossing j small gaps, on a block of 3, a gap of length one closing, and pass_odd_halt: an odd pass whose D-run meets a gap one cell too short reaches F reading a 1, the only halting transition. Proofs by rfl steps and crossing lemmas, written by tools/py/bbtools/symtm.py.

The counter, proved for every size

Write E in p bits on the left (from the head outward, a 0 bit is 1 1 and a 1 bit is 1 0, above a floor that starts with a 1) and N in n bits on the right (a 0 bit is 0^3, a 1 bit is 0^2 1, followed by any tape X). In mode 3 (top block 1^3) the machine takes (E, N) to (E − k, N + k) for every k ≤ E with N + k < 2n, in at least 22·k steps (run3); in mode 5 (top block 1^5) it takes (E, N) to (E − k, N + 2k) in at least 48·k steps (run5). Proofs: three pass-pair rules written by tools/py/bbtools/symtm.py, the binary decrement and increment on bit lists, and induction on k.

The counter rule on 4.7 million cycles

On the regular shape (head A on 0, 1^5 or 1^3 to its right, then gaps 0^(3q+2) 1) every one of 4,687,473 consecutive cycles of 1a between steps 1,500 and 3·108 takes (E, F) to (E − 1, F + 1) as the two-counter model says, and takes 48 + 4v + 6j steps (v, j read off the top block and the leading gaps). 1a and 1b have the same run here: in their first 109 steps F is entered 6 times, always on a 0 followed by a 0.

No halt before 8.47·101907 steps

The round-2 accelerator py/jump2.py (with c/ps.c, in the folder of 1a) simulates directly from pass start to pass start until the tape is a counter configuration of lean/Counter.lean (top block 3 or 5, E and N readable with at least 12 bits), then jumps a whole countdown at once: k cycles with k up to E (or down to the next power of two) and within the room N has before its first defect. Each jump is an instance of run3/run5, so its end tape is proved; only the Python reading of the tape, the exact step count (fitted formula a + 4ν(E) + 6ν(N+1) per pair) and the direct simulation between jumps are unproved. Jumps of at most 108 steps were each re-run by direct simulation and matched token for token. In 25 minutes on one core it made 245 jumps and reached step 8.47·101907 (E up to 6,452 bits) with no halt.

Defects stay single

In the 42.1 million passes that the round-2 run of 1a simulated directly (all the transition cascades between its 245 jumps), the right part never held more than one defect, never more than one 1^2 block, the left part never more than two even blocks, and the fatal precursor (a gap one cell short directly followed by a block of two or more) never occurred.

Growth shape: exp/fixed

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.036).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

The phase transition

When E reaches 0 the floor block 1^(2s) under it is popped into the top block, the next pass pushes s single 1s, and a new countdown starts with E = 2s − 1 (s one bits; floor 1^2) in the other mode (3 and 5 alternate); N stays on the right, but the cut leaves a gap of irregular size in front of it. Irregular gaps then act in a cascade of short sub-phases: a gap one too long (for 1a/1b; the analogous residue mod 5 for 1c/1d) collapses into a block 1^2, a gap one too short followed by a single 1 is cut down and passes its defect on to the next gap; sometimes the whole of N is pulled to the left and N restarts at 0. The floor grows by 2 whenever E passes a power of two, so it is about twice the bit length of the largest E so far. Seen at every transition of the runs above; not proved, and no closed formula for the new (E, N) as a function of the old one is known.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. By pass_odd_halt the machine halts exactly when an odd pass, after the gaps of the smallest size, meets a gap of length one followed by a block of two or more 1s. In the counter language this needs two defects to meet: an irregular gap cut down to length one directly in front of a 1^2 block left by another defect. During a countdown there are no defects (proved: the countdown is run3/run5), so it can only happen inside a transition cascade. In the runs above there was never more than one defect at a time; no invariant proving that is known, and the transitions have not been reduced to a number-theoretic map.
