What is known about 1RB0RE_0RC---_1LD1LE_1LE1LD_1RF0LC_1RA1RF

1RB0RE_0RC---_1LD1LE_1LE1LD_1RF0LC_1RA1RF

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 13 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The machine acts on a list of blocks of 1s: Lean proves every rule by which the head meets a block or a separator, and that it halts exactly on F> 0 0 1. A leftward pass computes a running parity, and the run is organised in epochs that grow the tape by a factor about 1.75. In 1010 steps F met two 0s in a row only at the right end of the tape, with blank tape beyond (observed). Whether that always holds is not settled.

The leftward sweep is a running parity

With the head in D (even) or C (odd) on the last cell of a 4-cell word 111x: D on u = 1110 leaves 0101 and goes to C; D on v = 1111 leaves 1111 and stays D; C on u leaves 1111 and goes to D; C on v leaves 0101 and stays C. So the word written behind the head is the running XOR (from the right) of the u's, as 1111 (even) or 0101 (odd). Plus the runs (1111)n for D, E and F.

d_u0 ... c_v1, d_run, e_run, f_run, lean/Rules.lean (from py/rules.py)

The machine on blocks of 1s

Going right, F crosses a block; at a separator followed by a block of one cell it fills the separator, erases the cell and fills the next separator (the blocks on both sides gain a cell); at a separator followed by a longer block it turns, moving two cells from that block to the one on its left; three 0s before a 1 are filled; at four 0s it writes 1011 and turns. Going left, D crosses a block and fills the separator after it; E erases every second cell of the next block and, if the block's length is odd, the pass goes on in D, otherwise the head turns. Each statement holds for any tape around the pattern.

f_block, f_single, f_turn, f_three_zeros, f_end, d_block, d_sep, e_pairs, e_last, c_last, c_sep, e_turn, turn_two, lean/Blocks.lean (from py/blocks_rules.py)

Where it can halt

Only B reading 1 halts; B is entered only from A reading 0, and A only from F reading 0. So the machine halts exactly on F> 0 0 1: F on that pattern halts within two steps (halt_F), and two steps before any halt the machine is in F on 0 0 1 (halt_route). An earlier version of this page gave the pattern as F> 1 0 1, which was wrong.

halt_F, lean/Blocks.lean; halt_route, lean/Route.lean (from py/route.py)

Every turn of F is at a block of odd length, except at the right end (1010 steps)

In the first 1010 steps F turned 139,833 times. The block it turned at had odd length every time (3 cells 36,463 times, 5 cells 46,436, 7 cells 19,254, 9 cells 20,280, and longer), except 22 turns at a block of exactly two cells, each of which was the last block of the tape. A turn at a block of two with another block after it would leave 0 0 1 (turn_two). F met two 0s in a row 42 times, each time at the right end with only blank cells beyond. No halt.

heuristic Evidence only

Two phases in an epoch

Followed turn by turn through one epoch (steps 4,104 to 7,890): first the head bounces between the left end and a turning place that moves right, eating the blocks beyond it two cells at a time; at each right turn the zone is a list of blocks 14k+3, at each left turn blocks 14k+1 separated by odd numbers of one-cell blocks. When the turning place reaches the right end the head crosses back to the left end in short bounces, leaving blocks of 3 and pairs 1, 5 (the words 1110 and 1011) for the next epoch to eat. Observed on that epoch, not proved.

py/blocks.py

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.501). Right of the start it grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.064).

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. Halting needs F> 0 0 1. In the run so far two 0s in a row appear in front of F only at the right end of the tape, with blank tape beyond. A proof would need an invariant that keeps every block at which F turns odd, and a description of what the right end does once per epoch.
