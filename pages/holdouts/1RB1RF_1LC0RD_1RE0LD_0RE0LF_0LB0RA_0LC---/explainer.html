The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in F reading 1. F is entered only from A1 (writes 1, moves R): A>1 1; D1 (writes 0, moves L): 1 D>1. So a halt is the moment one of these patterns appears, with the head on the cell after >.

The twins

Two machines that part at step 132

This machine and its twin, 1RB1RD_1LC0RD_... (2a) and 1RB1RF_1LC0RD_... (2b), differ only in what state A does when it reads a 1: 2a goes to D, 2b to F. A reads a 1 very rarely (11 times in the first 109 steps of 2a, 17 times for 2b), the first time at step 132. After that the two runs differ, but they are built the same way.

What it does

A binary counter fed by a busy work zone

The left end of the tape is a binary counter. After a short fixed prefix (10) it is a row of 4-cell digits: 1010 means 1 and 1000 means 0, least significant on the right, ended by 11. To add one, the head arrives in state C on that 11, runs left over the trailing ones, changes the first zero into a one, and runs back in state B changing the ones it passed into zeros. Lean proves this move for any number of trailing ones; in simulation the counter goes up at almost every change (149,848 increases in 109 steps).

Right of the counter is a work zone of a few dozen cells where the head zigzags thousands of times between two increments, and at the right end a block of 110 words that grows by one word now and then. The stretches (0 1)^n and (0^3 1)^n seen on the tape are just the counter's ones and zeros, read with a different phase.

Why it is open

What the work zone does is unknown

The counter is understood; the work zone is not. It decides how many steps pass between increments and when the 110 block grows, and it is where F would have to read a 1 for the machine to stop. A proof either way needs the macro rule of the work zone: which configurations it returns to when it hands a carry to the counter, as a function of the counter's digits. That has not been found.

First look

The shape of the run

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.046).

After 109 steps the tape spans 150 cells (44 left of the start, 99 right), with a density of 1s of 0.347.

Computed
c/leftsnap.c, py/cntchk.py in holdouts/1RB1RD_1LC0RD_1RE0LD_0RE0LF_0LB0RA_0LC---
A binary counter at the left end

Whenever the head is at least 8 cells right of the counter, the tape from the left end reads 10, then 4-cell digits 1010 (one) and 1000 (zero), then 11. Read as a binary number (least significant digit on the right) this counter goes up at almost every change. From step 1.5·107 to 109 the decoded value changes 149,850 times; 149,848 are increases and 2 are changes of width (19 → 24 → 26 digits as the script reads them; the counter has 26 digits at the end, with value 19,634,864, about 29% of the way to overflow).

Heuristic
Tape structure

Late in the run the tape is made of long stretches of (0 1)^n, (0^3 1)^n and (1^2 0)^n; both ends grow, the right one by single cells at irregular times (e.g. 2a: cell 175 at step 4.7·108). The stretches (0 1)^n and (0^3 1)^n are the digits 1010 and 1000 of the left binary counter read with another phase (see below).

Heuristic
tools/c/tmrun.c snapshots
A work zone between the counter and a growing (110)^n block

Right of the counter the head works in a zone of 20 to 40 cells of words 10, 1000, 110, turning thousands of times between two increments of the counter; at the right end a block (110)^n grows by one word at each right record (for 2a, n = 12, 13, 14 at the right records at 4.7·108, 5.8·108 and 9.6·108 steps). What the work zone computes between two increments is not known.
