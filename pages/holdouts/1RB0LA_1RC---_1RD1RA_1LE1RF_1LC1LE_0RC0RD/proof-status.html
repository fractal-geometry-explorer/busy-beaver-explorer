What is known about 1RB0LA_1RC---_1RD1RA_1LE1RF_1LC1LE_0RC0RD

1RB0LA_1RC---_1RD1RA_1LE1RF_1LC1LE_0RC0RD

Nobody knows whether this six-state machine halts from blank tape. Its run looks like the separator counters of this group: a block of 1s at the right end grows by 4 cells per visit of the head while the time per visit doubles, and the blocks to its left change slowly. This page lists what has been established, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 12 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The clock is proved in Lean for every size (tick): what remains is the word map P, one excursion of the head into the blocks left of the counter, iterated 2j times by tick j. The machine can only halt inside an excursion (B reading a 1 in a long block, from a closed tape language computed outside Lean); no invariant of the words that excludes it is known.

Sweep rules

A, B, C, D and F cross runs of the words 001, 100 or 11 rightwards in one state, rewriting each copy the same way, for any number of copies and any surrounding tape.

a_right_001, b_right_001, c_right_100, d_right_11, d_right_100, f_right_11, f_right_001, lean/Rules.lean

The clock: tick j is 2^j excursions

On the mirror image (L and R swapped), write J j W for the tape 1^(5+4j) 0 W from the head. If W goes to W' by 2j excursions (PChain: each one takes L 1 E> W to L F> 1 W' for every L) of total cost E, then 0^4 D> J j W reaches D> J (j+1) W' in exactly E + 51·2j − 4 steps, for every j and every tape beyond the four 0s. The block is a binary counter with digits 1000/0000; the proof is an induction on j (cnt) over three rules proved for all sizes (sweep, increment, finish). The statement is conditional on the excursions returning.

tick, tick_left_end, cnt, lean/MirrorClock.lean; rule_sweep, rule_inc, rule_fin, lean/MirrorRules.lean

The mirror image runs like the machine

Every run of the mirror machine is, mirrored, a run of the machine (of_mirror, from a one-step lemma for any pair of mirrored tables), so the tick holds for the machine itself at the right end of its tape.

steps_mir, of_mirror, tick_right_end, tick, lean/MirrorBridge.lean

The clock carries the run from blank tape

The run of the mirror image from blank tape is at D> J 0 W at step 9,321; the 15 excursions of the next four ticks are each proved for every tape to the left, and the tick theorem (levels 0 to 3; 56, 132, 276 and 532 steps) takes the run to D> J 4 W' at step 10,317. So the hypothesis of the tick is met by the real run: the theorem is not vacuous.

at_start, pstep0 … pstep14, tick0 … tick3, run_ticks, lean/MirrorInstance.lean

Events

At the visits of F to the right end on blank the tape is blocks of 1s split by single 0s, 1^2 0 1^x … 0 1^N F>0, with x in {1, 5, 9, 13} except a few blocks near the start of a phase; within a phase N grows by 4 per event and the time between events doubles. Phases end with a rebuild: at step 2,114,623 (N 61 to 6) and at step 2,736,109,137 (N 106 to 2); after the second a block of 41 stands before N.

Where it can halt

A closed tape language with windows of 6 cells (found and checked by bbtools.ctl, not yet in Lean) allows a halt only with B reading a 1 and six 1s on its left: inside a long block of 1s.

The word map

What one excursion does to the blocks left of the counter as arithmetic, what a rebuild (an excursion that does not return to the counter) does, and whether an excursion can reach the halting context B on 1. An invariant of the words closed under P and under the rebuilds would decide the machine.
