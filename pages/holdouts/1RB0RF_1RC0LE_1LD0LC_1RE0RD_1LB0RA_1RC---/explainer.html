The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in F reading 1. F is entered only from A1 (writes 0, moves R): A>1 1. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

What it does

A calm sea and a restless rest

Each time the head steps off the left end, the tape begins with a short fixed piece and then a sea: the two cells 10 repeated N times. After the sea comes an irregular part: runs of 01 or 10 of many lengths, with a single extra 0 or 1 between them, and a few longer words.

The sea gains exactly 3 copies per left turn. The head rarely comes to the left end (1,241 times in 109 steps); most of the time it zigzags inside the irregular part and rewrites it. That part keeps growing, about 12.5 cells per left turn, so it is not a small machine of fixed size sitting between two regular stretches, and the method that decided two sibling machines today cannot take it as it is.

Can it halt?

One pattern, and fragile neighbours

The machine stops only when state F reads a 1, and F is entered only when state A reads a 1 and moves right. So it halts exactly when A stands on a 1 with another 1 to its right (A>1 1); Lean proves this, and that the machine runs forever if the pattern never appears.

In the run from blank tape A reads a 1 about 390,000 times in 109 steps, never with a 1 on its right. But the margin is thin: take a tape of the real run, flip one cell, and let the machine go on. In 83 of 600 such trials it halts; with two cells flipped, in 148 of 600. The real tapes avoid the halt by their exact structure, not by their general shape.

What is missing: the rules by which the irregular part is rewritten, and a property of the real tapes that those rules preserve and that keeps A>1 1 away. A hint: between single 0s the runs of 01 mostly come in multiples of three.

Where a halt can come from

If the machine halts on its third step from a configuration, that configuration is one of: E with 1 under the head, 1 right, 1 two right. So a run can reach a halt only through one of these patterns, and a proof that the machine never halts only has to rule them out. The proof splits on the state and the five cells around the head.

The sweep rules

Shift rules of the zigzags, for every repeat count and any tape around: going left C carries 10 and turns each 10 into 01, and each 101 into 001; going right D turns each 010101 into 110100; going left B, carrying 01, turns each 1011010 into 0100101.

Open
Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The irregular part is where the work and any halt happen; it grows without bound and its rules are not known beyond the four sweep rules. Since neighbouring tapes halt, the next step is to find what the real tapes have in common (for example the run lengths divisible by 3).
