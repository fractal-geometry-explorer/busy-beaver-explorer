What is known about 1RB1LF_1RC1RB_1RD0RB_0LE0LF_---1LD_0LE1LA

1RB1LF_1RC1RB_1RD0RB_0LE0LF_---1LD_0LE1LA

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

Open, with the way to halt pinned down and the run followed exactly to 1018 steps. The tape is a list of blocks (01)^x (1)^y; Lean proves (kernel) that a merge at the left end halts the machine when the right block has at least two pairs more than the left one, and that a run-length block machine follows the Turing machine exactly from blank tape. Its C twin finds no halt in 1018 steps: at all 49 merges at the left end with a left block of 50 pairs or more the right block was at most 0.35 times the left one (0.6 at the tiny early ones).

The merge zigzag

L (01)^(a+1) A>0 1 (11)^n 0 (01)^(k+1) R becomes L (01)^a A>0 1 (11)^(n+2) 0 (01)^k R in 4n+10 steps, for every a, n, k and every tape L, R; iterated j times by induction.

zig in lean/Rules.lean, zig_iter in lean/Merge.lean

A short left block halts the machine

At the left end, 0^∞ 1 1 1 (01)^a A>0 1 (11)^n 0 (01)^(a+k+2) R halts, for every a, n, k and R: the zigzag uses up the left block and E reads the blank cell at the left end.

left_merge_halts in lean/Merge.lean

An exact abstract machine for the whole run

The tape cut into blocks of two cells, each side of the head run-length coded: one step of this block machine runs the Turing machine on the block the head faces (two 48-entry window tables, each entry checked by rfl) and crosses a whole run of equal blocks at once when the head leaves a block in the state it entered with. Every block-machine step is a run of the Turing machine, a block-machine halt is a halt, and the empty block configuration is the blank tape.

holdouts/triage.json

The sweep law

Between two visits of the left end, every block (01)^x becomes (01)^(x+1) and every block (1)^y becomes (1)^(y-2): 289,603 checks in the first 1010 steps, no exception.

scratch analysis of the C run (py/analyze.py)

The merge law

When a block (1)^y between (01)^a and (01)^b (1)^c runs out, the zigzag leaves (01)^(a-b) (1)^(4b+c); seen at every merge of the first 1010 steps.

py/analyze.py

No halt in the first 1018 steps

The block machine in C (c/msim.c, the same window tables and run crossing; it agrees with Lean's evaluation of mrun on the step counts after 1,000, 4,635,164 and 18,000,000 block steps) runs 1018 steps in about 8 minutes without a halt.

c/msim.c, py/leftmerges.py

49 merges at the left end, none close to halting

In the first 1018 steps there are 49 merges at the left end; the right block was at most 0.6 times the left one at the tiny early merges (3 to 13 pairs), at most 0.346 once the left block had 50 pairs, and between 0.22 and 0.34 once it had 100 (halting needs about 1). At every merge inside the tape the left block was at least 1.67 times the right one, and at least 2.89 times when the right block had 15 pairs or more.

c/msim.c, py/leftmerges.py

Each left-end merge protects the next one

With X0, X1, X2 the first three blocks of pairs at a left-end merge, the next left-end merge is safe if X0 − X1 > X2, because X0 − X1 never decreases between two left-end merges (an argument from the sweep and merge laws; checked at 11,085,706 consecutive left-end visits to 1015 steps, one exception at step 2,871 with blocks of at most 10 pairs). At every left-end merge with X0 ≥ 100 up to 2·1017 steps (47 merges), (X0 − X1)/X2 was at least 5.8.

Growth shape: sqrt/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.505). tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

The left block stays about 3 times longer

At the 23 merges at the left end in the first 1010 steps the left block was 2.9 to 4.3 times the right one (last: 30,587 against 9,616), and a longer run of the left end alone found a 24th at about 1.2·1010 steps with 45,881 against 12,860 (ratio 3.6); at all 1,497 merges with a right block of at least 15 pairs the ratio was at least 2.9.

py/analyze.py

A roughly self-similar cascade

From one left-end merge to the next the left block grows by a factor of 1.3 to 1.5 (often exactly 3/2 up to rounding: 571,835, 857,753, 1,286,629) and follows X0' = X0 + X1 + m2 (±1). Each block of pairs is longer than all later ones together at every left-end visit (smallest ratio 1.65 to 1014 steps), but neither this nor any simple inequality on the blocks survives both sweeps and merges, so it is not an inductive invariant.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The machine halts if a merge at the left end ever meets a right block not shorter than the left one (by 2). The block machine of lean/Macro.lean is exact, so a halt would show up in it; a proof of non-halting needs an invariant of the whole cascade of merges, which is not known.
