Does this machine ever halt?

1RB0LD_0RC---_0LD0RF_1LE1LD_0LA0RD_0RB1RA

Nobody knows. The head shuttles between the ends, rewriting runs of 100 / 1100 into one run of 1100 and back; a trip costs about the width squared, for a few cells of growth, so the width grows like the cube root of the time.

The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in B reading 1. B is entered only from A0 (writes 1, moves R): A>0 1; F0 (writes 0, moves R): F>0 1. So a halt is the moment one of these patterns appears, with the head on the cell after >.

The run at a glance

Space-time diagram and records

The diagram stacks the tape at many moments. Sampling at each new cell shows the shape of the run as the visited region grows; sampling evenly in time shows where the head spends its time. The records list every step at which the head reaches a cell no earlier step reached, with the side, the width so far and the whole tape: the first place to look for a repeating macro structure.

Growth law

Why the width grows like t1/3

The head goes back and forth across the whole tape. After step 5·108 there are 298 side switches (extensions of the tape on the other side from the last one), on average 1,676,606 steps apart, which is about 0.196·w2 for the final width w = 2924; each adds about 2.0 cells to the width. A constant gain per w2 steps gives w3 ~ t. The fitted exponent of the width after step 107 is 0.326.

After 109 steps the tape spans 2,925 cells (1,793 left of the start, 591 right), with a density of 1s of 0.496.

Heuristic
the transition table
How it could halt

The halting transition is B reading 1. B is entered from A reading 0 and from F reading 0, both moving right. So a halt is exactly A>0 1 or F>0 1.

Open
Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The run structure holds at the switches printed; what the disordered stretch does, and whether it can produce the halting pattern, are open.
