OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Sweeps back and forth, rewriting period-5 words (00101, 10110 and their phases) and runs of 1s on every sweep; the tape is a soup of these pieces; a halt needs a leftward sweep to meet 0 0 1 just before a 1. Lean proves the sweep rules over the background words and the only local pattern that leads to a halt; no halt in 109 steps.

The sweeps cross the background words whole

12 shift rules: for every n and every surrounding tape, a sweep in the stated state crosses n copies of a background word and leaves n copies of its partner, in the stated number of steps (for example L F> (00101)^n R ⟶ L (10101)^n F> R).

The only way to a halt

The machine halts only when D reads a 0, and the only way there is E reading a 1 with 0 0 1 just left of it (E, B and C step left and D meets the second 0.

Growth shape: sqrt/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.451). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.512).

What the tape looks like

Seen at the left end (after a leftward sweep) the tape is a soup of runs 14 to 19, pieces 0 0 1 0 1 0 (01)3 and blocks (00101)k; seen at the right end (after a rightward sweep) it is blocks (10110)k, (01101)k, (10101)k, (101)k. Unlike the other machines of this group there is no long clean zone: the whole tape stays irregular.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The shallow near miss (D on a 1 with 1 0 1 on its left) happens tens of millions of times, so the safety comes from the order of pieces, not from a rare event. No invariant is known.
