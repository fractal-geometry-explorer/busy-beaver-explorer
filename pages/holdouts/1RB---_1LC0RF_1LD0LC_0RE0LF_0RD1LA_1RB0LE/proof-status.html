1RB---_1LC0RF_1LD0LC_0RE0LF_0RD1LA_1RB0LE

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The head bounces between a block that grows at the left end and a wall of exactly 1,573 cells at the right end that it rewrites every sweep like a counter; the counter would need something like 348 sweeps to fill the wall, and what happens then is the question.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: fixed/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.494). Right of the start it stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Proposed family: F06, block-list counters

During the present epoch the description of the tape does not grow: 146 to 176 level-2 tokens at the four left-end records from 2.6·108 to 1.6·1010 steps, and in the macro simulator (blocks of 5 cells) exactly 314 runs at each of 80 sampled left-end records from 3·107 to 1016 steps. Only the block (11010)^n grows. The wall is a counter with digits of 25 cells. After its first 44 cells, each of the first 40 digits has the form x110 x110 x011 0011 0x11 0x11 0 at every one of 2,819 sweeps (steps 2·107 to 3·108): five free cells per digit. The first digit returns to the same contents every 8 sweeps, the first two digits together every 64 sweeps and the first three every 512 sweeps, taking 8, 64 and 512 different values (py/digits.py). The deepest digit changed by a sweep is digit 2 on 1,336 sweeps, digit 3 on 782, digit 4 on 326, digit 5 on 124, and so on down to digit 11 once. The counter has overflowed before. At the left-end record at step 1,428,412 the tape was (11010)^244 followed by about 240 cells that end in 16 eight-cell words. No new cell was added at the left end for the next three million steps, and at the next left-end record (step 4,487,735) the block was gone, a 25-cell word 0101100110111001100110111 stood 45 times in a row (new digits, all equal) and the tape ended in 30 eight-cell words. So the machine is a counter that is rebuilt longer when it overflows, with the block as its clock, and the next overflow is far beyond simulation. That is the shape of the counter families (F06), not of a bounded engine (F01): the wall is bounded only within an epoch.
