OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The tape is a list of blocks (10)^n separated by single 1s. In the right phase each sweep (head at the right end in state B) takes one 10 off the first block and appends a block (10)^3 1 at the right end; this rule is proved in Lean for every length. When the first block is used up, the left phase starts: the head bounces between the left end and a marker, the first block grows by 4 copies per two bounces while the block after the marker shrinks by 2, the marker moves through the list and rebuilds it (the left phase rules are also proved). Then a new right phase starts with a much longer first block (3,728 copies at 6.8·108 steps, used up at 1.234·109). No closed map yet.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Chaotic Bell Class 3. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

Right-phase sweep

From the head at the right end in state B, one sweep turns (10)^(a+2) 1 [(10)^(b+2) 1] ((10)^3 1)^(n+1) into the same with one 10 less in the first block and one more block of three, in 4a+24n+53 steps (4a+4b+24n+73 with the middle block).

sweep_tail, sweep_mid, lean/Rules.lean

Left-phase bounces

From the head at the left end in state C: (01)^(x+2) 0 (01)^(y+3) 00110 goes to the marker and back in 4x+22 steps, the first block three copies longer and the marker now a 1; the next bounce (4x+26 steps) adds one more copy and restores the marker 0.

The phases in the run

In the direct run the right phases end (first block 0) at 8.0·104, 1.96·107 and 1.234·109 steps; the left phases between them last until 8.3·106 and 6.5·108 steps, and the third lasts past 3·109. From 1.234·109 to 2·109 steps the head reaches only the left end (47,163 times in state A, 23,587 in C), and the tail of 4655 blocks of three is left as it is.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.553). Right of the start it grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.04).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Growing phases, no sign of a halt

Each right phase is longer than the last (its first block is built from the whole list in the left phase), and the list behind it is periodic in large stretches ([19,35]×15, [541,917]×11, [5,13]×13), the signature of a deterministic hierarchical counter rather than a random walk.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The two phases are understood one sweep at a time, but the rebuilding of the list in the left phase has not been reduced to a map.
