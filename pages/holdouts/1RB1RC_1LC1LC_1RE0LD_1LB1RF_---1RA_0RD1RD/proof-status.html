What is known about 1RB1RC_1LC1LC_1RE0LD_1LB1RF_---1RA_0RD1RD

1RB1RC_1LC1LC_1RE0LD_1LB1RF_---1RA_0RD1RD

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open, and reduced to a map. Lean's kernel proves that the machine halts from blank tape iff the orbit of an explicit map on two counts reaches u = v or u = v + 1, and that the first 10,000 rounds do not; the map iterated for 106 rounds does not get there, and a probability argument says it never will.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Quadratic Mess. Its 3 November 2026 edition lists the machine as a cryptid: its halting is thought to come down to a Collatz-like question that no known method decides. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

Every round, in closed form

For every u ≥ 3 and v, from the round start P u v = C> 0 11 0 (11)^u 0 (11)^v (or Q u v, with one more 11 0) the machine reaches the next round start given by the map next: P u v → P(3u+2, v−u−1) if u < v, → Q(3v+3, u−v−2) if u ≥ v+2; Q u v → Q(3u+3, v−u−1) or P(3v+5, u−v−2); and it halts if u = v or u = v + 1. Built from 31 symbolic rules and an induction on the number of bounces.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.058). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.5).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Why it is expected never to halt

The ratio u/(u+v) moves like a chaotic orbit of an expanding map, so a round lands within one of the diagonal with chance about 1/(u+v), which shrinks geometrically; the total chance of a halt after the computed rounds is about 2−900000. No congruence excludes the diagonal (the residues reachable mod m include diagonal ones for every m tried).

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known: it halts iff the subtract-and-triple orbit ever brings the two counts within one of each other. A cryptid in the bbchallenge sense.
