The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in F reading 0. F is entered only from D0 (writes 0, moves R): D>0 0. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

E1 L E0 L C

The tape

Domains of 01, then a comb

The left part is domains of the pairs 01, separated by short defects (10)^4 111; after a rightward sweep a domain reads (0111)^m. The right part is a comb of period 10 and a short tail. Two end turns:

The leftward sweeps over all three backgrounds are proved shift rules (lean/Rules.lean).

What is seen

Uneven domains

The domains of 01 are few and very uneven (4480 next to 52 above; at step 1,041,927,495 they are 3312, 168, 11072, 176, 50 and the comb has 891 copies). The tail is a repetition of (01)^6 (10)^4 111. No rule for the change of the domain lengths has been found.

What halting would need

One pattern

F0 is the only halting transition, reached as D>0 0. It cannot happen inside a domain or the comb; whether a defect or the tail ever produces it is open.

First look

The shape of the run

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.583). Right of the start it grows like a power of the step count other than 1/2 (fitted exponent 0.301).

After 109 steps the tape spans 38,776 cells (27,339 left of the start, 2,108 right), with a density of 1s of 0.682.

Kernel
lean/Rules.lean (generated by py/rules.py)
Shift rules of the sweeps

The leftward sweeps: L (0111)^n E>1 R (each 0111 becomes 1010), L (01)^n E>1 R and L (10)^n C>1 R (the pairs change phase), and L (0100110111)^n E>0 R over the period-10 comb, for every n and all tapes L, R.

Open
Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The machine halts on F0, reached as D>0 0 (D reads 0, moves right onto a 0). The sweeps never produce it inside a domain or the comb; whether a defect or the tail ever does is open.
