What is known about 1RB1RB_0LC0LD_0LE1LD_1LB0RE_1RA0RF_1RD---

1RB1RB_0LC0LD_0LE1LD_1LB0RE_1RA0RF_1RD---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 9 November 2026.

In short

The question is open, but the run is now known in detail: it goes through epochs whose sizes follow T' = 2T + 5 exactly, with X = 6T + 17 copies of the word baccaaa at each epoch start, 72T + 290 visits of the right end per epoch and (from T = 91 on) exactly 16416T² + 151769T + 288000 steps. Three of the five phases of every epoch are proved in Lean for all counts; the other two, where an inner counter runs deep inside the tape, are exact cycles of 144 and 288 visits checked by symbolic computation but not yet in Lean.

Other proofs and outside reports

An outside proof is waiting for review: it never halts

Shift rules

For every length n of the run and any tape around it: L (10)^n B> 1 R reaches L B> 1 (10)^n R; L A> (01)^n R reaches L (01)^n A> R; L (01)^n D> 0 R reaches L D> 0 (01)^n R. These are the moves the head makes across the long uniform stretches of the tape.

First phase of an epoch, for every count

With a = 10, b = 100, c = 1000 and the head in state D on the first blank cell: L (baccaaa)^(n+1) ba 1 00 (aaaaca)^m aaa (caa)^(m+2) c 1 D> reaches L (baccaaa)^n ba 1 00 (aaaaca)^(m+1) aaa (caa)^(m+3) c 1 D> in 74m + 167 steps, for all n, m and every tape L on the left; iterated, P1 n 0 reaches P1 0 n.

No halt in the first 109 steps

The C simulator tools/c/triage.c runs 109 steps without a halt; at the end 97,518 cells have been visited and 40,211 of them hold a 1.

holdouts/triage.json

Halting needs D on 1 1 1

Read off the table: F is entered only by E reading 1 (moving right), and E only by C reading 0 (moving left, so F then reads the 0 that C wrote) or by D reading 1 (moving right). So a halt happens exactly when D reads a 1 that is followed by two more 1s. Three 1s in a row do appear next to the head (171,801,958 times in the first 109 steps), which is why simple local invariants fail.

the transition table

Phases 3 and 4 are exact cycles of 144 and 288 visits

With U = (c)^7 (bac)^5 acacbacb (ac)^3 (bac)^5 aca: in phase 3, 144 visits add one U, 12 copies of acacaaac and 72 of acaaac and use up 144 of aac; in phase 4, 288 visits add one U, 12 acaaacac and 144 acaaa and use up 72 acaaac. Checked by exact symbolic simulation (bbtools.symtm with shift rules) from a tape of the run with every long count a variable and the far left an unknown context: the rule holds for every value of the counts. Not in Lean: written by symtm, the proofs would run to hundreds of megabytes.

py/cycles.py

No halt in the first 6·1010 steps; nine epochs

c/epochs.c (plain simulation, 6·1010 steps, no halt) finds the epoch starts at steps 638,914, 2,791,601, 11,869,478, 49,048,755, 199,088,630, 801,808,537, 3,217,627,644, 12,890,601,695 and 51,601,711,810, with T = 7, 19, 43, 91, 187, 379, 763, 1531, 3067 (T' = 2T + 5, so T = 3·2k − 5) and X = 6T + 17 each time. Visits of the right end per epoch: exactly 72T + 290; steps per epoch: exactly 16416T² + 151769T + 288000 for T = 91 to 1531.

c/epochs.c, README

What an epoch leaves behind

Each epoch freezes a block (acacaaac)^(T−10) (ac)^36 (bac)^(6T−3) cc … U^((T−43)/6) (c)^13 (U a fixed word of 168 cells) in front of the moving part, and adds one copy of U to the block frozen by the epoch before; read from the tapes of the nine epoch starts.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: fixed/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.499).

The whole question

To prove non-halting along these lines: the phase 3 and 4 cycles in Lean (with the untouched part of the tape as a context in each visit, or with a verified symbolic executor), the short transitions between the phases as symbolic runs in T, and the induction over epochs (two kinds, as T alternates between 1 and 7 modulo 9). Not done.
