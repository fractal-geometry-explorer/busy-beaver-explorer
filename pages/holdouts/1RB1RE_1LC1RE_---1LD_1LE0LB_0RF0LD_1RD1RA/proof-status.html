What is known about 1RB1RE_1LC1RE_---1LD_1LE0LB_0RF0LD_1RD1RA

1RB1RE_1LC1RE_---1LD_1LE0LB_0RF0LD_1RD1RA

OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 12 November 2026.

In short

The question is open, and Collatz-like. At every visit of the left end the tape is a list of blocks ax0 110 ax1 110 … (a = 01). A round halves blocks from the left as long as a carry survives and pours the halves into the blocks on the right: a single block x becomes (3x+5)/2 when odd and [x/2−1, 0, x] when even, an odd first block x0 becomes (x0−3)/2. Lean proves that a first block of 1 halts the machine, so it halts if and only if a run of odd rounds starts with x0+3 a power of two (as far as the computed round map is right: it is checked against the machine, not proved). The map has been iterated for 1,000,000 rounds from blank tape, more than 1094,000 steps, without a halt.

Drain trips

After the halving sweep the head drains the b's (b = 0111) one per trip: bm+1 ap … aq becomes bm ap+1 … aq+2 (a = 01), for every value and any list of middle blocks, with exact step counts for one and two blocks.

drain1, drain2D, drainS, drainSD, lean/Rules.lean

A first block of 1 halts the machine

From the left-end configuration E> 0 1 a s … with first block 1 the machine stops after 21 steps (separator 110) or 29 (separator 110110), whatever the rest of the tape.

The round map in closed form (large blocks)

A round is a sweep that halves blocks from the left while a carry survives (the first block passes it on when odd, later blocks when even), followed by one drain trip per b. For lists whose halved blocks are not small this gives the next list in closed form, e.g. [x] → [(3x+5)/2] (x odd) or [x/2−1, 0, x] (x even); [x0, x1, …, xk] → [x0/2+x1+2, x2, …, xk+x0] (x0 even, k ≥ 2); an odd first block always goes to (x0−3)/2. Equal to the plain machine on every one of 4,037 random lists in its domain.

closed in py/roundmap.py, py/orbit.py test 6000

The exact round map for every list

For a fixed parity the round is affine in every block above 16, so the map cuts large blocks down, runs the small tape and its neighbours on the plain machine and extends linearly. It agrees with the plain machine on 6,000 random lists of up to 6 blocks (values up to 70) and with all 1,669 multi-block left-end tapes of the independent accelerated run to 10400 steps. Checked, not proved.

nxt in py/roundmap.py, py/orbit.py

Which rounds halt

On the plain machine, among all 2,899 lists of up to three small blocks and 20,000 random lists of 2 to 6 blocks below 10, the round halts exactly when the first block is 1 and another block follows, or the tape is the single empty block. In terms of the map: the machine halts iff a run of odd first blocks starts with x0+3 a power of two, or a single block [4] occurs.

py/direct.py, py/roundmap.py

No halt in 1,000,000 rounds

The round map iterated from the first left-end tape of the blank-tape run ([0, 0, 2] at step 50) does not halt in 1,000,000 rounds and never leaves the family of lists; at the end the tape holds a number of a's with 314,797 bits, so the machine has made more than 1094,000 steps. This rests on the computed round map.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.075). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.505).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Halting would need an exact power of two

Halting means x0+3 is a power of two at the start of a run of odd rounds. If those x0 behave like random numbers the chance is about 2−n for n bits; past the rounds already followed they have tens of thousands of bits.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. Deciding it means controlling whether x0+3 is ever a power of two along an exponentially growing orbit: a Collatz-like question. The reduction to the round map is computed, not yet proved in Lean (the sweep with its carry and the small-block cases are missing).
