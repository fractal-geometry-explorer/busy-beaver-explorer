What is known about 1RB1LA_0LC0RC_1LE1RD_1RE1RC_1LF0LA_---1LE

1RB1LA_0LC0RC_1LE1RD_1RE1RC_1LF0LA_---1LE

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open, and now provably equivalent to a question about one number sequence. The tape is always A>0 1^p 0 1^q at the sample point. Lean proves the whole map, with exact step counts: odd p: (p, q) → ((p−3)/2, q + (p+5)/2); even p: (p, q) → (3p/2 + q, 1); p = 0: (0, q) → (2, q−1); p = 1 halts. Lean proves that the machine halts from blank tape iff the orbit of (0, 2) under this map reaches p = 1, iff some sample K(p, 1) has p + 3 a power of two, and that a halt, if any, comes after more than 22073 steps (kernel; 280181 by compiled evaluation). Heuristically it never halts.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Quadratic Mess. Its 3 November 2026 edition lists the machine as a cryptid: its halting is thought to come down to a Collatz-like question that no known method decides. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

The odd case of the map

K(2k+3, q) reaches K(k, q+k+4) in exactly 2k²+10k+13 steps, for all k, q (k passes of inner_odd by induction, then end_odd); K(0, q+1) → K(2, q) in 7 steps; K(1, q) halts; blank tape reaches K(0, 2) after 7 steps.

K_odd, K_zero, K_one_halts, blank_K, lean/Macro.lean; rules in lean/Rules.lean

The even case of the map

K(2k+2, q) reaches K(3k+3+q, 1) in exactly q(4k+8)+6k²+22k+15 steps, for all k, q: q passes of inner_even move the block to the right end (even_iter), then an inner induction at the blank (end_loop: each round, end_even and two more inner_even passes, moves two ones of the block out and adds three ones on the left), then end_fin walks back to the left end.

K_even, end_loop, even_iter, lean/Macro.lean; rules in lean/Rules.lean

The reduction: halting is a question about one orbit

With F the map above (F(1, q) = (1, q)) and orbit n = Fn(0, 2): blank tape reaches K(orbit n) after exactly time n steps for every n (reach), and the machine halts from blank tape iff some orbit n has p = 1 (halts_iff), iff some orbit n has q = 1 and p + 3 a power of two (halts_iff_pow2).

halts_iff, halts_iff_pow2, reach, lean/Reduction.lean

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.05). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.53).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Why it should not halt

An odd p is halved again and again ((p−3)/2, i.e. p + 3 is halved) until it is even; it reaches 1 only if p + 3 was a power of two (this equivalence is proved). The values of p at the start of these chains grow geometrically, and the chance that a number of size X is 3 less than a power of two is about 1/X, so the expected number of future hits is far below one.

Open: by the proved reduction, equivalent to whether the orbit of (0, 2) under F ever produces a sample K(p, 1) with p + 3 a power of two.
