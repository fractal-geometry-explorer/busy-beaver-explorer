Does this machine ever halt?

1RB0RF_0LC---_1LD1LC_1RE0LA_1RA1RD_0RD1RF

Nobody knows yet. Sweeps back and forth, each sweep rewriting the whole tape into its other form (011 to 100, 10 to 11, a period-12 word to its partner); a growing period-3 zone at the left end, a period-12 zone after it, a soup of short runs on the right; a halt needs a rightward sweep to meet 0001. The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in B reading 1. B is entered only from A0 (writes 1, moves R): A>0 1. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

What it does

Sweeps that rewrite a periodic tape

The head sweeps right and left, turning wherever the local pattern sends it back, and every sweep rewrites what it crosses. Rightward (D and E, with F and A) the tape is put into a rightward form: D turns each 011 into 100 and each 01 into 11, E turns 110 into 001 and 10 into 11, and a period-12 word such as 101101011010 becomes 110011100111. Leftward (C with D, A, B making small zigzags) the tape goes back to its leftward form.

Seen at the left end the tape has three zones: a clean zone (011)n (or (101)n) that grows by about one copy per long sweep, then a zone of the period-12 word (101011010110)m, also growing, then a soup of short pieces such as (10)a 1 (10)b 1 and (110)3 (10)3, where the irregular events happen. In the rightward form the soup reads as runs of 1s separated by 0 0.

The sweeps

What one sweep does to the words it crosses

A shift rule says that a sweep crosses any number n of copies of a word in one state and leaves n copies of another word behind. These are proved in Lean for every n and every tape around them (lean/Rules.lean); S> is the head in state S on the next cell, L and R the rest of the tape.

Halting

The one pattern that halts, and why it has not appeared

The machine halts only when B reads a 1, and Lean proves that the only way there is D reading 0 0 0 1 (D, E and A each write a 1 and step right, and B meets the 1; halt_needs_pattern, lean/Halt.lean). In the first 109 steps D read 0 0 0 6,026 times, every one on blank cells past the right end of the tape, never inside it.

Inside the tape, separators of two 0s are common in the rightward form, so a third 0 next to them is the danger. The run never produced one in 109 steps, but no invariant that forbids it is known.

Next step. Write the soup as a sequence of blocks (the runs between 00 separators) and find the block rewriting done by one round trip; then look for an invariant on blocks that keeps separators at most two 0s wide.

Twin. 1RB1RE_1RC0RF_0LD---_1LE1LD_1RA0LB_0RE1RF is this table with the states renamed (A→B, B→C, C→D, D→E, E→A, F→F); it starts in the state this machine calls E. The two runs from blank tape are different (no common configuration at the left end in their first 300,000 steps), but they use the same sweeps, words and halting pattern, and every rule here holds there after renaming.
