OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 26 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Each lap copies the tape 5 cells further left, and all change happens in an irregular zone at the right end where the lap turns. Both sweeps are proved in Lean as transducers (the right sweep over any accepted token list, the left sweep over blocks of every length from two of its three states); no halt in the first 2·109 steps (C) and 107 (Lean, native_decide). The wall-carry signature that suggested the busycoq #115 method is an artefact of the copying.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Bouncer + Chaotic Edge. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

Two shift rules

Rightward, C zigzags across 1 0 1 words, rewriting each into 1 1 0 in 7 steps (sweep_right); leftward, A crosses 1 1 0 words, rewriting each into 1 0 0 (sweep_left_A); for every n and every surrounding tape.

lean/Passes.lean (generated by py/gen_rules.py)

The right sweep is a transducer on tokens

Write the tape as tokens x = 1 0, y = 1 1 0, u = 1 0 0, v = 1 1 0 0. Going right, the head is between tokens in one of four scanner states (a machine state with a fixed word on its left). Ten crossings are proved for every tape on both sides (rule_sound), and pass_sound proves, by induction on the list, that the machine crosses any token list for which every token has a rule, leaving blocks 1^(2k) 0 behind.

lean/RightPass.lean (generated by py/gen_sweeps.py)

The left sweep counts mod 3

Going left, the head reads the blocks 1^(2k) 0 back and writes tokens. From two of its three states (L0, D; L2, B) the crossing of a block is proved for every k, and the next state depends only on k mod 3 (six theorems, for every k and every tape on both sides). The third state (L1, A) first makes an 11-cell trip right over the tokens it has just written; its crossings are not proved yet.

heuristic Evidence only

Each lap copies the tape

Read from its own left end, the tape after a lap equals the tape before it except within the last 5 to 469 cells before the turning point (median 32, over 2,614 laps to 2·108 steps). The left end moves 5 cells left per lap; the turning point stays between cells -659 and +228 of the start (2·109 steps), so the body left of it is an inert record and all change happens in the zone at the right end.

c/copycheck.c, c/laps.c

The turn is a nested excursion

When the right sweep, in state E, meets u, the head turns left into the blocks it has just written. In 3·108 steps 1,913 such excursions came back right after 10 to 200 cells (the sweep goes on in state C) and 3,097 ran to the left end and ended the lap. Which one happens depends on the blocks to the left, not on the tokens to the right.

c/excursions.c

The wall-carry signature is an artefact

The 26 November screen for the busycoq #115-#119 method flagged values {2, 5, 8} behind the wall word 7 7 1 1 2. Each occurs exactly once per lap (2,910 times each in 3·107 steps): they are the steps of one leftward zigzag through a 1^8 block in the copied body, not a carry returned from the far end.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. A halt needs the leftward E/F zigzag to meet two 0s in the wrong phase, which the proved crossings never do; the open part is which token words the zone at the right end can hold, and the excursions that decide where each lap turns.
