What is known about 1RB0LF_1RC0RB_0RD1LE_0LE0RA_1LC1LA_0LD---

1RB0LF_1RC0RB_0RD1LE_0LE0RA_1LC1LA_0LD---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. The head sweeps across the whole tape, which is written in four-cell words. Most of it is a long list of runs (0100)^a (0111)^b; the list survives from one sweep to the next, while a counter at the left end and an active zone change. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves the exact way a halt would happen (1 1 E>1), that the other way into A is harmless, and how the sweeps cross the four-cell words; the C simulator finds no halt in the first 1011 steps. How the list of counters evolves is not worked out.

The halting configurations

The machine stops exactly when it reads the symbol of a halting transition (F1); the generated statement covers every configuration.

step_none_iff, lean/Blank.lean

How a halt comes about

F reading 1 is the only halt. F is entered only from A reading 1 (enter_F), which writes 0 and steps left, so the machine halts exactly at 1 A>1. A is entered from D reading 1 and from E reading 1 (enter_A); the D way is harmless (via_D_safe), so a halt needs 1 1 E>1 (halt_E), reached from 1 1 1 C>1 or 1 1 1 D>0.

halt_F, halt_A, halt_E, halt_C, halt_D, enter_F, enter_A, enter_E, lean/Halt.lean

The way in through D is harmless

A is entered from D reading 1, moving right; D writes 0 there, so when A reads 1 and steps back left, F reads that 0 and goes on. For every surrounding tape.

enter_A, via_D_safe, lean/Halt.lean

Crossing the words

For every n and every surrounding tape, moving right: D turns (1000)^n into (0110)^n, A turns (0001)^n into (1100)^n, C turns (0100)^n into (0011)^n, B turns (0010)^n into (1001)^n and erases 1^n. Moving left: E carries a 0 across (0001)^n, D across (0010)^n, and E and C fill (0 1)^n and (1 0)^n into 1s.

dword, aword, cword, bword, berase, eshift, dshift, efill, cfill, lean/Rules.lean

A list of counters

When the head reaches the left end the tape reads C>0 (1000)^k ... or E>0 (0001)^k ... followed by a long list of runs (0100)^a1 (0111)^b1 (0100)^a2 (0111)^b2 ... (or the same in the shifted words 0001 / 1101), with values such as 13, 25, 58, 46, 25, 34, ... The same values reappear in the same order sweep after sweep; the list changes only next to the left counter and in an active zone near the right end.

Growth shape

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.562). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.487).

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. A proof would describe how a sweep pair acts on the list of counters (where values are consumed and new ones are written) and show that three 1s in a row never meet E or C or D in the halting way. Window CTL up to 7 cells and n-gram CPS (widths up to 9, see the README for the larger runs) fail.
