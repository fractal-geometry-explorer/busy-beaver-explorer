What is known about 1RB1RD_0LC1RA_0RA1LC_1RA1LE_1RF0LD_---0RE

1RB1RD_0LC1RA_0RA1LC_1RA1LE_1RF0LD_---0RE

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. Its tape is always two blocks side by side, an alternating block (10)^x and a solid block 1^y, and the machine shuttles cells from one to the other by doubling. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves no halt in the first 10,000 steps by the kernel alone and in the first 107 with native_decide; an exact macro simulator (blocks of 2 cells, runs crossed at once) finds no halt in the first 1015 steps. Lean proves both phase rules of the doubling (below) for all sizes.

What Lean proves. The whole phase map, every case for all sizes (E(x, z) = E>0 (10)^x 1^z, A(x, y) = (10)^x 1^y A>0 and the bounce frame Q(m, j, t) = (10)^m 1 D>0 0 (1010)^j 1^t), and halts_iff: the machine halts from blank tape if and only if the map, iterated from A(0, 0) (the blank tape), reaches E(x, 0) or E(x, 1). By native_decide the first 108 phases (about 4.1·1021 machine steps) do not. Lean also proves that the even phases lead only to even phases or to an all-ones tape, and checks, for every all-ones tape of length n < 105, that its run halts before the next even phase or all-ones tape exactly when n is 1 or 12·2k − 2k − 9 (3, 13, 35, 81, 175, …). lean/Fatal.lean proves that list for every n, by the kernel (ones_fatal_all, ones_safe_all). What is not proved: that the run never lands on a fatal length.

Where a halt would come from. The halting transition F0 fires only when state E stands on the 0 at the left end with at most one 1 after the alternating block. Probing every two-block tape with x, y < 150 shows that a parity rule keeps that from happening, except right after the tape has become all ones, 1^n: from 1^n A>0 the machine halts exactly for n = 1, 3, 6, 13, 35, 81, 175, 365, … (n = 12·2k − 2k − 9 and two small values, checked up to n = 400).

Why it has not halted. All-ones moments are rare: eleven in the first 109 steps, at n = 5, 10, 14, 30, 61, 89, 169, 222, 382, 542 and 781, none of them in the halting list, and only five more by step 1015 (n = 9705, 59501, 77694, 271974, 311881). A halt needs an all-ones moment to land exactly on a number of that sparse list, so the machine very probably never halts, but nothing here proves it.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Cubic Mess. Its 3 November 2026 edition lists the machine as a cryptid: its halting is thought to come down to a Collatz-like question that no known method decides. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

No halt in the first 10,000 steps

The list simulator of the shared library, proved to agree with the machine's semantics, runs 10,000 steps from blank tape without a halt, evaluated by Lean's kernel.

blank_runs_kernel, lean/Blank.lean

The two phase rules, for all sizes

Lean proves, with rfl steps, the crossing lemmas and induction on the number of bounces: E(x,z) reaches E(2x+4, z−2x−6) whenever x ≥ 1 and z ≥ 2x + 7 (ephase), and A(x,y) reaches A(x−y/2, 2y+2) whenever y ≥ 4 is even and 2x ≥ y + 2 (aphase). Both phases are made of the same bounce, (10)^(m+1) 1 D>0 0 (1010)^j 1^(r+3) to (10)^m 1 D>0 0 (1010)^(j+1) 1^(r+1) in 8j + 10 steps, plus a start and an end piece generated by py/gen_rules.py. The parity analysis is proved separately, on the whole phase map (even_next in lean/Phases.lean).

lean/Rules.lean, lean/EPhase.lean

The whole phase map

Write E(x, z) = E>0 (10)^x 1^z, A(x, y) = (10)^x 1^y A>0 and the bounce frame Q(m, j, t) = (10)^m 1 D>0 0 (1010)^j 1^t. next sends each of them to the next one the machine reaches (bounces taken all at once), with 21 cases; next_sound proves every case for all parameters (one symbolic run of lean/Pieces.lean each, generated by py/pieces.py, and induction for the bounces). next fails only at E(x, 0) and E(x, 1), which halt (e_halts).

lean/Phases.lean, lean/Pieces.lean

Halts if and only if the map reaches E(x, 0) or E(x, 1)

halts_iff: the machine halts from blank tape if and only if some iterate of next from A(0, 0) (the blank tape) is E(x, z) with z ≤ 1.

lean/Phases.lean

The parity classes

even_next: the even phases (E(x, z) with z even and z ≥ 2, A(x, y) with x ≥ 1 and y even, Q(m, j, t) with t even) lead only to even phases or to an all-ones tape. So from an even phase the machine cannot halt before the tape is next all ones (even_safe).

lean/Phases.lean

The fatal all-ones lengths, for every n

ones_fatal_all: for every n with n = 1 or n = 12·2k − 2k − 9 for some k, the machine halts from the all-ones tape A(0, n). ones_safe_all: for every other n, any iterate of next from A(0, n) that halts comes after another all-ones tape. The proof: from E(x, z) with z odd the run is a chain of doublings E(x, z) → E(2x+4, z−2x−6) that halts exactly when z = zHalt(x, k) (e_fate, strong induction on z), with zHalt(x, k) closed-form (zHalt_closed).

fatal_list, by native_decide: for every n < 105, the run from the all-ones tape A(0, n) reaches a halting phase before an even phase or another all-ones tape exactly when n is 1 or 12·2k − 2k − 9 (3, 13, 35, 81, 175, …) (ones_fatal, ones_safe). The two phase rules, along the run

Write E(x,z) for the tape E>0 (10)^x 1^z (blank around) and A(x,y) for (10)^x 1^y A>0. Of the 2,085 such moments in the first 109 steps, every left phase with z ≥ 2x + 8 went E(x,z) → E(2x+4, z−2x−6) (507 times) and every right phase with y even and 2x ≥ y + 2 went A(x,y) → A(x−y/2, 2y+2) (534 times).

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: power/power

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like a power of the step count other than 1/2 (fitted exponent 0.336). Right of the start it grows like a power of the step count other than 1/2 (fitted exponent 0.336).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Step counts of the phases

Probing from those tapes, E(x,z) → E(2x+4, z−2x−6) takes 4x² + 24x + 30 steps and A(x,y) → A(x−y/2, 2y+2) takes y² + 2y + 6 steps (checked for small x, y; the step counts of the Lean pieces add up to the same formulas). Doubling costs the square of the block, so a tape of width w takes about w³ steps to grow: the width grows like t1/3.

py/phases.py

Halting needs an all-ones tape from a sparse list

From every two-block tape E(x,z) and A(x,y) with x, z, y < 150 the next two-block tape was computed. Call E(x,z) with z even and z ≥ 2, and A(x,y) with x ≥ 1 and y even, even: every even tape leads to an even tape or to an all-ones tape 1^n A>0, never to a halt. The machine halts only from E(x,0) or E(x,1). From 1^n A>0 it halts exactly when n is 1, 3, 6, 13, 35, 81, 175 or 365 (n ≤ 400), the list n = 12·2k − 2k − 9 plus 1 and 6. Not proved for all sizes.
