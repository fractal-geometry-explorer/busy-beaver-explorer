What is known about 1RB0LC_1RC0RA_1LD1LA_1RE1LD_---1RF_0RD1RA

1RB0LC_1RC0RA_1LD1LA_1RE1LD_---1RF_0RD1RA

OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Seen at the moments the head, in state B, reaches a new cell on the right, the tape is mostly a row of digits: blocks 1^(4m+1) 0, one more than a multiple of four ones each followed by a single 0, read as the numbers m. In the run of 2·1010 steps 74 of the 103 such moments have exactly this form (the others hold a block of 4m+2 ones, which appears at the visits to the left end and lingers). Most digits are 0. Near the right end a big block grows by four ones at every second new right cell, and the time between new right cells doubles; meanwhile a digit 1 walks left through the 0s, one place per record. It is the same kind of machine as 1RB---_1RC1RF_1LD1RE_1LB1LD_0RB0RC_1RA0LF.

At each new cell on the right in state B the tape is mostly a list of digits 1^(4m+1) 0, most of them 0, then a big block of ones, then 0 1 0 1 B>0. The new cells come in pairs a few steps apart and then a long pause, during which the head runs to the left end, rewrites the list (in the middle of a period the digits look scrambled and the big block is cut into pieces) and comes back with the list restored and the big block four 1s longer. The pause doubles from one period to the next.

Status as of 9 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The machine halts exactly from F> 0 0 0 (both directions in Lean). The run is a sequence of periods: in each the head goes to the left end and back, and at the right end the big block grows by four 1s (a 21-step rule proved in Lean). The periods double: 459,930, 919,858, ..., 58,870,786 steps, each exactly twice the previous minus 2, and later 3,759,931,386 and 7,519,862,778 steps, with occasional larger corrections when the digits near the left end change. F has met 0 0 1 only at steps 257, 11,457, 16,839,809 and 2,371,684,619, the only times up to that step (5 times in 1010 steps by the ctxcount census), each time just after a long block was written at the very left end of the tape; the halt needs a 0 where those near misses had the 1.

The halting context

From F> 0 0 0 the machine reaches its halting transition (proved step by step, then step_none_iff). The halting transition is E reading a 0. E is entered only from D reading a 0 (D writes 1 and moves right), and D is entered from C or D moving left (so the cell D's 1 would hand to E is the 1 they just wrote) or from F reading a 0 and moving right. So the only way to halt is F> 0 0 0, and F itself comes only from E reading a 1. The converse is proved: a configuration halts two steps later if and only if it is F> 0 0 0 (halt_iff_2), so a halting run from blank passes through it (halts_blank_pred_2).

The clock test

At the visits of the right end in state C on a 0 the tape is W 14n+1 0 1 0 1 1 C>0. From the visit at step 16,962,494 (n = 8), the word W at each later visit of the phase is the word at the visit before after 2k applications of one base map, up to 214 applications (the visit at step 252,438,652). This is the clock of the machines of holdouts/1RB0RA_0LC0RE_1LE1LD_0LE---_1LF0LC_1RA1LE, found by running the machine; its tick is not proved (the head works through the block like a counter, which is not the sweep–trip–merge template of gen_clock.py).

Growth shape: exp/exp

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.051).
