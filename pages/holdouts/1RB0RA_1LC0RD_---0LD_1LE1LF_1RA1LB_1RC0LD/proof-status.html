What is known about 1RB0RA_1LC0RD_---0LD_1LE1LF_1RA1LB_1RC0LD

1RB0RA_1LC0RD_---0LD_1LE1LF_1RA1LB_1RC0LD

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. A fixed fraction (77%) of the tape is a uniform block whose sweeps are proved in Lean; the rest is a binary word rewritten every round trip with no known rule. No halt in 1010 steps.

The halting configurations

The machine stops exactly when it reads the symbol of a halting transition (C0); the generated statement covers every configuration.

Where a halt can come from

If the machine halts on its third step from a configuration, that configuration is one of: E with 0 two left, 0 left, 1 under the head. So a run can reach a halt only through one of these patterns, and a proof that the machine never halts only has to rule them out. The proof splits on the state and the five cells around the head.

halt_route, lean/Route.lean (written by py/route.py)

The sweep rules

6 rules for all values of the repeat counts and every tape around them: the left end with the uniform block, (001)^(n+2) crossed in 5n+10 steps; the rightward sweep over 010 (A); the leftward sweep over 011, 101, 110 (D, F); A erasing a block of ones.

The halting state is never close

C0 is the only halting transition and C is entered only by B0 (1LC). When B comes from A (A0 = 1RB) the cell C reads is the 1 A just wrote, so a halt needs B to come from E1 (1LB) onto two 0s: the pattern 0 0 E>1. In the first 109 steps C is entered 100,516,612 times and reads 1 every time.

Growth shape: sqrt/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.5). A uniform block of 77%

At visits to the left end the tape starts with (001)^n, and this block is 77.0–77.2% of the tape at every 150th visit up to 1.8·109 steps (4,281 of 5,560 cells at step 2·107, 40,812 of 52,972 at 1.8·109). The tape grows by about 36 cells per round trip, 33 of them at the left end.

py/sweeps.py

The binary zone

Right of the block, at the left-end visits, the tape is single 1s separated by one or three 0s (24,768 of 24,769 runs of 1s have length 1; the runs of 0s: 16,366 of length 1, 8,368 of length 3, 35 others) in the first 3·108 steps: a binary word, 01 and 0001, of about 23% of the tape, rewritten every round trip.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The uniform block is harmless (its sweeps are proved); everything depends on the binary zone, whose rewriting has no known description, and on the interface between the two.
