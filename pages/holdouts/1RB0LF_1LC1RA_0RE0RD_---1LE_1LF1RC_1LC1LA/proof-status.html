What is known about 1RB0LF_1LC1RA_0RE0RD_---1LE_1LF1RC_1LC1LA

1RB0LF_1LC1RA_0RE0RD_---1LE_1LF1RC_1LC1LA

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

Open, Collatz-like, reduced in Lean to a number question. It is a Lean theorem (kernel-checked, no native_decide) that the machine halts from blank tape if and only if the orbit of the block 144 under an explicit arithmetic map F hits a halting value: a block 1t with t = 2j+1 − 2j − 7 (3, 17, 47, 109, 235, …), or a restart state of the same form. The orbit misses them for its first 10,000 resets (t reaches about 3,900 bits), and a random-orbit heuristic makes a hit very unlikely; whether it ever hits is open.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Quadratic Mess. Its 3 November 2026 edition lists the machine as a cryptid: its halting is thought to come down to a Collatz-like question that no known method decides. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

kernel Proved in Lean, kernel-checked

The halting configurations

The machine stops exactly when it reads the symbol of a halting transition (D0); the generated statement covers every configuration.

step_none_iff, lean/Blank.lean

No halt in the first 10,000 steps

The list simulator of the shared library, proved to agree with the machine's semantics, runs 10,000 steps from blank tape without a halt, evaluated by Lean's kernel.

blank_runs_kernel, lean/Blank.lean

The sweep rule

From (01)n+3 (11)m+1 with the head in B at the right end, the machine reaches (01)n+1 12m+8 in 8m + 18 steps, for all n, m (and a second form of the same sweep in state A). Found and proved by bbtools.cycles.

rule0, rule1, lean/Rules.lean

The leaf rules of the reset

Thirteen rules for all parameter values, found and proved by bbtools.symtm.prove: the sweep (10)x+212y+1 A> → (10)x12y+7 A>, the even reset 12k+6 A> → (10)k+117 A>, the start of the odd reset 12k+7 A> → C> 0 12k+14, the start, one step and the four possible ends of a doubling round, the restart, and the halting configuration.

lean/Leaves.lean (generated by py/gen_reset.py), restated in lean/Reset.lean

The reset map

An explicit computable map F on states z t (the block 1t with A after it) and rs d (the rounds of the odd reset starting over): F s = some s' means the machine goes from s to s' in at least one step, F s = none means it halts from s. The inductions over the sweeps, over the steps of a round and over the rounds are proved by hand; the six blocks t ≤ 5 by evaluation.

F, F_sem, chain_sem, lean/Reset.lean

Halts iff the orbit of 44 hits H

The machine halts from blank tape iff the orbit of z 44 under F reaches a state in H, where H is z t with t + 2s + 11 = 2s+3 for some s (t = 2j+1 − 2j − 7) and rs d with d + s + 4 = 2s+2. Blank tape is z 0, and F takes it to z 19 and then z 44.

halts_iff, halts_iff_44, F_none_iff, halts_iff_H, lean/Reset.lean

No halt in the first 300 resets

The orbit of z 44 has no halting state among its first 300 resets, evaluated by the kernel.

orbit_300, lean/Orbit.lean

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: power/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like a power of the step count other than 1/2 (fitted exponent 0.146). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.509).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

The orbit probably never hits H

In each doubling band (Hj-1, Hj] only one odd value halts, the bands double in width and t grows by a factor of about 1.3 per reset, so the expected number of future hits is far below 1.
