Does this machine ever halt?

1RB0LE_1RC0LB_1RD1RF_1RE0RB_1LA0RD_0RE---

Nobody knows yet. This machine keeps its tape as blocks (10)n glued by a separator 01100, with one flaw somewhere: a single 0 or 1 where a separator should be. Every time the tape grows by two cells a signal runs left to the flaw and back, rewriting each block it crosses. Lean proves those sweeps exactly; a simulator built from them ran 3·1013 steps without a halt.

The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in F reading 1. F is entered only from C1 (writes 1, moves R): C>1 1. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

The shape of the run

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.494).

After 109 steps the tape spans 88,351 cells (6,365 left of the start, 50,204 right), with a density of 1s of 0.488.

Structure

Blocks, separators and one defect

Look at the tape each time the head is in state E on a new cell at the right end, with a 1 just left of it: an E-record. It is always made of blocks (10)n (write <n>), each followed by a separator: usually S = 01100, but at one place (sometimes a few) a single 0 or 1. The last of those is the defect; the blocks right of it, all separated by S, form the zone.

The record at step 431: <2> S <2> S <2> S 1 <5> 1 E>0, a defect 1 and a zone of one block. At step 2·108: about 1,970 blocks <2>, then 137 blocks <14>, then <62> <303> <853> <2150> <1118>, all separated by S.

The basic move

One sweep: left to the defect and back

From an E-record the head writes a 1 and a signal (state A on a 1) runs left. Crossing a block <n> costs 2n steps and shifts the block one cell; crossing a separator S costs 21 steps and leaves (10)2 1 behind: every block right of an S is carried back as a block two larger. At the defect the signal turns:

  • defect 1: it becomes 0 and the first zone block y grows by 3;
  • defect 0 after a block x ≥ 2: x loses 2, the defect becomes 1, y grows by 5.

Then D runs right, turning each (10)w+2 1 back into S <w>, and at the blank end writes the 1 of the next E-record. Net effect: the last block z pays 2, the tape is 2 cells longer. In Lean these are record_d1 and record_d0, for zones of any length, cost 4z + 4y + 40 + Σ(4w + 26) steps.

The emergent behaviour

Events and a counter of blocks 4k − 2

The generic rules alternate the defect between 1 and 0, feed the first zone block (+3, +5) and drain the last (−2), and eat the block before the defect 2 at a time. When something runs out an event happens: the last block reaching 0 or 1 makes the S before it the new defect and freezes the old one; a block before a 0-defect below 2 merges blocks and moves the defect left; at the left edge two-blocks <2> S are added. About one sweep in five is an event.

Over long runs the blocks settle on the values 2, 14, 62, 254, 1022, …, 4k − 2: a block grows n → 4n + 6 as the defect passes it. The tape is a counter whose digits are runs of equal blocks, read from the right, while the left edge grows in bursts of <2> blocks. After 2.96·1013 steps there are 244,161 blocks and the right end reads … S <1048574> S <1048574> S <491400>.

The danger

Where it could halt

The only halting transition is F reading 1, reached from D>1 0 1 1. Inside a sweep this is a turn at the defect where the block just right of it is 0 or 1 on the way back. In a zone of two or more blocks that block is y + 2 ≥ 2, so the generic rules cannot halt; in a zone of one block it is z, which in the runs only returns as 2 after an event and then grows. None of the 891 window rules the simulator met halts.

Status

What is proved

Lean, kernel (lean/Gadgets.lean, lean/Macro.lean): the cell gadgets; the leftward and rightward passes over a zone of any length (apass, dpass); the four generic sweep rules with exact step counts; the run from blank tape reaching the record at step 431 and following record1_d1 to step 457.

Lean, native_decide (lean/Blank.lean): no halt in the first 107 steps.

Computed: the macro simulator (symbolic window rules) checked tape for tape against the machine at every E-record of the first 2·108 steps and at samples to 4·109; then run to 2.96·1013 steps with no halt.

Open: the events are not in Lean, and no closed set of configurations excluding a halting turn is known.

Heuristic
README.md
Why it does not halt soon

The machine can only stop with D>1 0 1 1 on the tape, which within a sweep means a turn at the defect with the first right block Y ≤ 1. In a zone of two or more blocks Y = y + 2 ≥ 2, so the generic rules never halt; in a zone of one block Y = z, and in the runs z only comes back as 2 after an event and then grows. No reachable window rule halts. The block sizes settle into values 4k − 2 (2, 14, 62, 254, …), each made by the n → 4n + 6 growth of a block as the defect passes, so the tape behaves like a counter whose digits are blocks; nothing in it is seen to drift toward a halting turn.

Open
Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. A proof would need a closed set of macro configurations covering the events (the end of the zone running out, the defect reaching a frozen separator or the left edge), with the generic rules already in Lean.
