Open: a cryptidIts halting is thought to come down to a Collatz-like question that no known method decides (the community's label, not a result).

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

Open, probably halting; reduced in Lean to an exact map. The run is a sequence of phases. In a phase, a block of x ones grows to ⌊3x/2⌋+7 while a counter c of copies of 01 counts down by 2. When c runs out the machine halts if x is in one residue class out of four, and otherwise starts a new phase with c about x. The step, the resets and halts_iff (halts iff the orbit of (4, 4, 3) under the map meets a halt) are proved in Lean for all x; phases 0, 1 and 2 are evaluated exactly and do not halt. The end of phase 3, after about 10203 steps, depends on the residue mod 4 of a number with 10101 digits. Heuristically each phase halts with chance 1/4.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Bell-like. Its 3 November 2026 edition lists the machine as a cryptid: its halting is thought to come down to a Collatz-like question that no known method decides. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

The step: St(x, c+2) reaches St(⌊3x/2⌋+7, c)

St(x, c) is the head in state A on the first cell of 1^x (01)^2 1^6 (01)^c 1^t 0 1^2, t = 3 or 6. For every x ≥ 1, every c and every rest of the tape: S(x, c+2) reaches S(⌊3x/2⌋+7, c). Each round (a 0 walking through the left block) takes two ones off the left block and adds three to the middle one, W(x+2, b+1) → W(x, b+4); at x = 2 or 1 the middle block becomes the new left block and two copies of 01 rebuild the marker.

step_rule, lean/Step.lean; the rounds and ends in lean/Rules.lean

The reset, as an exact map

When c reaches 0 or 1 (the last step ends on the tail 1^t 0 1^2), the middle block becomes a block 1^B behind 1^4 0 or 1^5 0, with B = 3⌊x/2⌋+7 (+t if c = 0). Depending on the parity of B this either halts (state F reads a 0) or is converted, maybe with a short restart, into a new phase: S3(4, (B−5)/2), S3(6, (B−8)/2) or S3(5, (B−5)/2) after c = 0; S6(4, (B−3)/2), S6(6, (B−6)/2) or S6(5, (B−3)/2) after c = 1, t = 3; and after c = 1, t = 6 a short phase that ends at S3(⌊3y/2⌋+4, 1), y = B or B+1. With the step this is a map next on (x, c, t), and St(x, c) leads to next(x, c, t) for every x ≥ 1 and c, by 34 more symbolic rules and induction.

The reset table by residue classes

Read off next: from St(x, 2) (one step to c = 0, then the reset) it halts exactly for x ≡ 1, 6 (mod 8) if t = 3 and x ≡ 2, 5 (mod 8) if t = 6; from S3(x, 1) for x ≡ 2 (mod 4); from S6(x, 1) (through the short phase) for x ≡ 0, 9, 11, 14 (mod 16). heuristic Evidence only

Phase 3 is out of reach

Phase 3 ends at S3(y, 1) with y the result of (c−1)/2 applications of x ↦ ⌊3x/2⌋+7 to 6, and halts iff y ≡ 2 (mod 4). y has about 10101 digits and cannot be computed; no shortcut for its residue is known.

Probably halts, after an astronomical time

Every reset halts for exactly one residue class in four, and the residue of x after many steps of ⌊3x/2⌋+7 behaves like a random number. So each phase from phase 3 on halts with chance about 1/4, independently, and the machine halts with probability 1 in this model, most likely within a few phases. Each phase is exponentially longer than the one before (c′ is about x, and x grows like 1.5c), so a halt, if it comes, comes after a tower of exponentials of steps. This is the «probviously halting» situation: no proof either way is in sight.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Exactly when the orbit of (4, 4, 3) under next reaches a halt (proved). The first chance is the end of phase 3, where the question is whether the ((c−1)/2)-th iterate of x ↦ ⌊3x/2⌋+7 from 6, for a known c ≈ 2.3·10101, is 2 mod 4.
