What is known about 1RB1LF_1LC0RD_0LD0LC_1RE0LA_0RA0RC_0RE---

1RB1LF_1LC0RD_0LD0LC_1RE0LA_0RA0RC_0RE---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 13 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves the passes of the base-5 counter (each phase of the first gadget, and a carry into the second); the round ends follow a radix-5 pattern seen in simulation, but the step from one round end to the next, where blocks run out and are rebuilt, is not derived, so halting is not reduced to a map.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Skelet 1-like Class 1. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

The sweeps

Sixteen shift rules over (10)^n, (01)^n, (1000)^n and the rotations of these words, for all n and any tapes around (one copy by rfl, all copies by TM.cross_r / TM.cross_l).

lean/Rules.lean

The counter passes

With b = 1000 and the gadgets G0 = 10010, G1 = 1001, G2 = 1001100, G3 = 100110, G4 = 10011: one pass of the head from the left end ticks the first gadget, G0→G1 and G1→G2 moving one b from the block after it to the block before it, G2→G3, G3→G4 moving nothing, and the left block gains a b; G4→G0 carries into the next gadget, which ticks the same way (or, if it is a fresh 0, becomes G1). Nine theorems, for all block lengths ≥ 3.

Right-end events to 2.9·1010 steps

A C run to 2.9·1010 steps, sampled each time the tape grows on the right (24 times): the tape is a list of blocks a^x (a = 10) separated by 1^2 0. In the last samples block j from the right loses 18·5j−1 copies of a between events.

README.md

Round ends to 4.8·1013 steps

At each round end (the head at the right end of the tape) the tape is ax1 1 ax2 1 … axk 1 aL with a = 10; L is 311 since step 4.2·109. From one round end to the next the block j places left of L loses 18·5j−1 a's while it can pay; blocks that cannot pay are rebuilt or merged, and new leftmost blocks have length 12·5m−4 (37,496, 187,496, 937,496, 4,687,496). 31 round ends found.

No halt in 2.4·1014 steps

An exact accelerated simulator (the macro machine of bbtools.macro with 4-cell blocks, plus jumps over repeated periods of passes) runs 2.4·1014 steps in 15 minutes without a halt. It agrees with the plain C simulator on the whole tape (a hash) at 107, 109, 5·109 and 1010 steps; the jumps themselves are not proved.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.525). Right of the start it grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.057).

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. A halt needs 1 A> 1; the counter passes never produce it, so it would have to come from a block running out or from the re-encoding at a round end, which are not derived yet.
