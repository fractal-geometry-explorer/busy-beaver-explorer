What is known about 1RB1LC_0LC---_1RE1LD_1RD0LA_1RF0RC_0RA1RC

1RB1LC_0LC---_1RE1LD_1RD0LA_1RF0RC_0RA1RC

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. At every moment the visited part grows on the right, the tape is (10)^x 1^4 (01)^n1 1^3 (01)^n2 1^3 … (01)^nk 1 0 1, and between two such moments the j-th counter from the right loses exactly 2j, for the counters up to some point from the right; beyond it the list is rebuilt. This is measured, not proved. Halting needs A on a 0 with a 1 to its right, which never happens in the first 109 steps.

Shift rules of the sweeps

Lean proves, for every count and every tape around, the four shift rules by which the sweeps cross the blocks: C crosses (10)^n leftward and (01)^n rightward (turning it into (10)^n), A crosses (01)^n leftward, E crosses (10)^n rightward (turning it into (01)^n).

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: power/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.511). Right of the start it grows like a power of the step count other than 1/2 (fitted exponent 0.15).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

The tape is a list of counters

Each time the visited part grows on the right (in A, then B), the tape is (10)^x 1^4 (01)^n1 1^3 (01)^n2 1^3 … 1^3 (01)^nk 1 0 1 B>0, e.g. at step 233,350,399: x = 2461 and the counters 2693 3139 1283 339 5487 1363 2621 313 2778 377.

The rate law: the j-th counter from the right loses 2j

Between two right extensions, counter j (counted from the right, j = 1, 2, …) decreases by exactly 2j for every j up to some point; the counters beyond it are rebuilt. In 21 of the 74 transitions from step 8,225 to 2.5·109 all k counters pay, and then always a new counter appears at the front and x = 2k+3 − 3 (61, 125, …, 8189, 16381). In the other 53 the rebuild starts at some counter; in 28 of them it is the first counter (from the right) smaller than its rate 2j, or its right neighbour; what decides it in general is not yet understood.

the right-extension samples of tools/c/tmrun.c (scratch script)

Never one step from a halt

The machine halts when B reads a 1, and B is entered only from A reading a 0 (A0 → 1RB). In the first 3·106 steps A reads a 0 8,918 times, always with a 0 to its right.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The rate law is measured, not proved, and the rebuilding of the counters that cannot pay is not yet written as a map. A proof would need both, and an invariant of the counters that keeps a 1 away from the right of A when A reads 0.
