What is known about 1RB---_1RC0LE_0LD0RF_1LB0RE_0RC0LE_1RD1RA

1RB---_1RC0LE_0LD0RF_1LB0RE_0RC0LE_1RD1RA

OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

The head works at one place, cell 75, in state D (an event). A 2-bit phase next to it counts events mod 4; three of every four events add 1 to a binary counter on the left, and one adds 1 to a base-3 counter on the right. So the left side holds 3n and the right side n, for n the number of phase cycles. These rules are proved in Lean for every size. Overflows of the left counter send the head to the left end and change the layout.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The events follow seven rules proved in Lean for every size (97.9% of the 92 million events of the first 3·109 steps; 99.8% with 176 rarer rules, also proved); none halts (each ends at the next event). Halting needs C on 1 1 1 (from the table); C has even seen 1 1 only 9 times in 3·109 steps, 7 before the first event and 2 during reorganizations (steps 2,425,727 and 153,303,845).

The halt from the halting context

From C> 1 1 1 the machine reaches its halting transition (proved step by step, then step_none_iff). The halting transition is A reading a 1. After the first step A is entered only from F reading a 1 (F writes 1 and moves right), and F only from C reading a 1. Lean (halt_ctx) proves only that from this context the machine halts; that every halt passes through it is read off the table above, not proved in Lean.

halt_ctx, lean/Rules.lean

Shift rules

12 shift rules over the words of the tape (for example (10)^n B> 1 becomes B> 1 (10)^n in 4n steps, D> (100)^n becomes (101)^n D> in 7n steps), proved for every n.

lean/Rules.lean (generated by lean/rules.py)

The counter rules, for every size

Seven event rules: phase 0→1 and 2→3 add 1 to the left counter (any number n of carried ones, 12n+8 steps); 3→0 changes nothing else (4 steps); 1→2 adds 1 to the left counter and 1 to the base-3 counter (k carried 2s, 12k+12n+32 steps), in all four cases of the digits met.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. Halting needs C to meet 1 1 1, and the only places it meets 1 1 are right after the turns at the left end, where the third cell has always been a 0. Whether it always will depends on the irregular middle of the tape, whose rule is not known.
