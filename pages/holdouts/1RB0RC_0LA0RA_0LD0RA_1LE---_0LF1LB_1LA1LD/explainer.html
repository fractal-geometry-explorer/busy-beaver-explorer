The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in D reading 1. D is entered only from C0 (writes 0, moves L): 1 C>0; F1 (writes 1, moves L): 1 F>1. So a halt is the moment one of these patterns appears, with the head on the cell after >.

What it does

A sea with defects

At a left turn the tape is 0 1 x1 1 x2 1 …: a sea of 01 pairs, the 0s of some pairs replaced by 1s (defects). In the picture below each row is the tape at a left turn and a cell is black where two neighbouring cells are equal, that is, at the defects; the regular sea is grey.

Each round trip maps the defect string to a new one: the rightward sweep complements it, the leftward sweep rewrites it with a small finite memory. It is one step of a cellular-automaton-like rule, applied to a string that gets a few cells longer each time.

For example r_sea: from A> 01 01 01 the head reaches 10 10 10 A> in 6 steps, whatever lies around. l_sea: from 10 10 A> 110110 it reaches A> 110110 10 10 in 24 steps.

Can it halt?

A question about the defect rule

The machine halts from 1 C>0 or 1 F>1 (D then reads a 1). Each happens about 71 million times in the first 109 steps, always with a 0 on the left. Those moments are inside the leftward sweep’s handling of defects, so a halt would be a particular defect configuration. No invariant of the defect strings that rules it out is known.

Where a halt can come from

If the machine halts on its third step from a configuration, that configuration is one of: E with 1 two left, 1 left, 0 under the head. So a run can reach a halt only through one of these patterns, and a proof that the machine never halts only has to rule them out. The proof splits on the state and the five cells around the head.

The sweep rules

The sweeps over the sea, for every length and any tape around: A> (01)^n becomes (10)^n A> and a defect pair 11 becomes 00 (rightward, 2 steps a pair); (10)^n A> 110110 becomes A> 110110 (10)^n (leftward, the head carries 110110, 12 steps a pair), and the same seen from state F.

Heuristic
py/observe.py turns (README)
A step of a cellular-automaton-like rule

At every left turn the cells of one parity are all 1 and the others carry the defect bits; the whole tape changes from one round trip to the next (no frozen part). The defect string is not an XOR (linear) function of a window of the previous one: the best such fit with up to 3 neighbours fails on 13% of cells.

Open
Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The defect dynamics is a deterministic rule on bit strings, but neither linear nor obviously simple; a proof would need the full transducer of the leftward sweep (it has a few states: the carried word changes at defects) and an invariant of the defect strings that excludes the halting patterns.
