1RB0RC_0LA0RA_0LD0RA_1LE---_0LF1LB_1LA1LD

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. At a left turn the tape is 0 1 x1 1 x2 1 …: a sea of 01 in which some 0s (the defects xi = 1) are 1s. The rightward sweep complements every defect bit (pair 01 to 10, 11 to 00); the leftward sweep carries the word 110110 across the sea, 12 steps a pair, and the defects it meets change it. A round trip is thus a finite-state transducer on the defect string, a step of a cellular-automaton-like rule; the defects form triangles and drift. Both sweep rules over the sea are proved in Lean.

Lean proves no halt in the first 107 steps (10,000 by the kernel alone); the C simulator finds none in 109.

How it works

The tape is a sea of the word 01, with defects: at a left turn the tape reads 0 1 x1 1 x2 1 x3 …, every second cell a 1, the others 0 except at defects. The rightward sweep is the simplest possible: it turns every pair 01 into 10 and every 11 into 00, complementing the defect bits. The leftward sweep is a zigzag that carries the word 110110 leftward 2 cells per 12 steps; when it meets a defect the carried word changes for a while and the new defects are written.

So each round trip applies a finite-state transducer to the string of defect bits, like one time step of a one-dimensional cellular automaton. Drawn turn after turn, the defects form triangles and diagonal streaks, a structure reminiscent of additive automata but not linear. Whether the halting pattern can ever be produced is a question about this rule.

Where a halt can come from

If the machine halts on its third step from a configuration, that configuration is one of: E with 1 two left, 1 left, 0 under the head. So a run can reach a halt only through one of these patterns, and a proof that the machine never halts only has to rule them out. The proof splits on the state and the five cells around the head.

A step of a cellular-automaton-like rule

At every left turn the cells of one parity are all 1 and the others carry the defect bits; the whole tape changes from one round trip to the next (no frozen part). The defect string is not an XOR (linear) function of a window of the previous one: the best such fit with up to 3 neighbours fails on 13% of cells.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The defect dynamics is a deterministic rule on bit strings, but neither linear nor obviously simple; a proof would need the full transducer of the leftward sweep (it has a few states: the carried word changes at defects) and an invariant of the defect strings that excludes the halting patterns.
