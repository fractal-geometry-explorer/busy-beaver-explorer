What is known about 1RB1RD_1LC0RD_1RF1RB_1LE0RA_0LB0LE_---0RC

1RB1RD_1LC0RD_1RF1RB_1LE0RA_0LB0LE_---0RC

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 12 November 2026.

In short

The question is open; it is now a question about one explicit map. The complete round map on lists of blocks is written down (py/roundmap.py) and agrees with direct simulation on more than 15,000 random block lists; it is computed, not yet a Lean theorem as a whole. Lean's kernel proves the even rounds (E n → E (5n/2 + 1)), the odd sweep (six 1s off the block, one onto the next), the top-level ends, and that the machine reaches E 26041. Iterating the map from blank tape gives no halt in 1,000,000 rounds (the block then has about 205,000 digits); a halt needs a number on the orbit to be a base-6 repunit or the like, which is astronomically unlikely but not excluded.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Bell-like. Its 3 November 2026 edition lists the machine as a cryptid: its halting is thought to come down to a Collatz-like question that no known method decides. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

The odd sweep and the top-level ends

With the phase state P(i, x, R) = w^i C> 0 1 0 1^x 0 R, w = 10101: for odd x ≥ 7 one sweep takes six 1s off the block, writes a w and adds one to the next block (or makes a new block), for any tape on both sides. At top level, x = 2 with nothing after ends the round in 5i + 6 ones, x = 3 in one block 5i + 6 + y, x = 5 in blocks 5i + 6 and y + 1, and x = 1 with nothing after reaches the halting transition.

The complete round map

A round is a recursion on phase states: odd blocks are divided by 6 by the odd sweep; an even block first runs a nested round on the blocks to its right and merges the result (with nothing to its right this is x → x − 2, so n ↦ 5n/2 + 1); the remainders 0, 1, 3, 5 end the round, differently at top level and nested (table in the README). A round halts only at top level with remainder 1 and nothing after, or nested with remainder 5 and nothing after. Compared with direct simulation on more than 15,000 random lists of up to six blocks (blocks up to 3000) with no mismatch; for one block it is the earlier table (n = 6^j·m + (6^j − 1)/5: repunits halt, even m gives n + 3m/2 + 1 + j, m ≡ 3 gives n + 3 + j, m = 6q + 5 gives (n − q + 1 + j, q + 1)), checked for every n < 200,000 against the map.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.062). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.46).

Why a halt is unlikely

A round halts only when a digit recursion ends exactly: the block, after stripping trailing base-6 digits 1, must be 0 (a repunit), or in a nested round the quotients after digits 5 must come down to 0. For numbers of D base-6 digits that has chance about 6−D; second blocks are quotients of the first by a small power of 6, so they are long too. Summed over the rounds after the computed ones this is astronomically small. Small tapes do halt: (a, 5), (a, 41), (a, 257) for even a ≥ 4, and E 7, E 43, E 259.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The machine halts exactly when the orbit of (2) under the round map reaches a halting round (given the computed map). What is missing: the nested rules as Lean theorems and the reduction stated in Lean; and an argument that the orbit avoids the halting numbers, which is a Collatz-like question.
