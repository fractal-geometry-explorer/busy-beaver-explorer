A 6-state Busy Beaver machine · Status as of 8 November 2026.

Does this machine ever halt?

1RB0LC_1RC0RA_1LA0LD_1LE0RB_1RD1LF_0LA---

Nobody knows. Started on a blank tape, this machine writes a list of numbers, each a run of 01s or 10s. In one regime, each sweep of the head takes one unit from the first number and adds a word at the right end. In the other, each sweep takes one unit from the first number and rewrites a few cells at the first place where the list is not regular. Both are proved in Lean for every size. What is not proved is the handful of special sweeps between them, and which tapes arise in the long run.

The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in F reading 1. F is entered only from E1 (writes 1, moves L): 1 E>1. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

Where it could stop

The one way to halt

The only halting entry is F reading a 1. F is entered only from E reading a 1 (E writes 1 and moves left), so the machine halts exactly when 1 E>1 occurs. In the first 108 steps E reads a 1 687,974 times; every time the cell to the left was a 0, and in all but 38 cases the cells around it read 101[0]110.

Macro structure

The right-end regime: one unit per trip

Stop the simulator whenever the head reaches a new cell at the right end in state A. At step 39,094 the tape reads

(10)^107 1 (10)^6 1 (10)^31 (1101010)^20 A>0

and 1,101 steps later

(10)^106 1 (10)^6 1 (10)^31 (1101010)^21 A>0.

The head went left over the whole tape and back: going left it rewrites every block (10)^(k+2) 1 into 1 (10)^k 0 1 1 0 0, at the left end it adds one cell, and going right it restores each block as 1 (10)^(k+2), one cell further left. The net effect is that the first run of 10s is one shorter and the right end has one more word 1101010. Lean proves this for every first counter, every tail and every list of blocks in between (trip), by putting six generated pieces together with an induction on the list; trip_iter repeats it until the first counter is down to (10)^2.

The left end

Left-end phases: a local rewriting system

When the first counter is used up the right end stops growing, and for millions of steps the head turns near the left end instead. Stop the simulator whenever the head reaches a new leftmost cell in state A, and read the tape from the head. It is a list of units (01)^b 0 0110, then a run (01)^a, then a short frontier word, then a rest that the next trip will not touch. At step 22,147,896, for example, the tape starts

A> (01)^312 0 0110 (01)^113 0 0110 (01)^363 0 0101 0 0110 (01)^35 0 0110 …

Here the units have 312 and 113, the run has 363, and the frontier word is 00101. In one round trip the head runs right over the units, turns at the frontier, runs back and adds a cell at the left. The first unit loses one 01 and (01)^a w is rewritten, nothing else:

  • (01)^a 1 becomes (01)^(a+2) 0,
  • (01)^a 00101 becomes (01)^(a+4) 1,
  • (01)^a 001001 becomes (01)^(a+5),
  • (01)^a 0001 becomes (01)^a 00110011,
  • (01)^a 0000 becomes (01)^a 00110010,
  • (01)^a 001000 becomes (01)^(a+4) 00.

With no unit in front, each right side has one 01 fewer and nothing shrinks. After a maximal (01)^a the next cells are 1 or 00, so these six words, a new unit 00110, and 00111 (never seen) are all the cases. Lean proves each rule for every a, every list of units with b ≥ 2, and every rest (lean/Frontier.lean). The old picture of a pump and a cursor is this system at work. A cursor (01)^c (10)^d 0110 is the frontier 1 followed by (01)^(d-1) 0 0110. Two trips move c up by 6 and d down by 1, and when d is used up the words 0001 and 001001 turn the cursor into a new unit and start the next block as the cursor. With no unit in front, the same two trips are the pump.

Up to 3·107 steps, 41 trips from the left end are not covered. In 32 the first unit is used up and a later unit takes its place. In 7 a small frontier right after a unit starts a new unit. 2 are early trips with a small unit. Then the right-end regime starts again with a new first counter and a slightly changed list:

  • steps 35,911 to 263,899: a = 108, 2 blocks;
  • steps 7,677,126 to 21,453,756: a = 714, 125 blocks (8, then 3, 11 fifty-seven times, then 16, 123, 35, 123, 35, 123, 35, 123, 35, 235), tail 0 to 714;
  • steps 36,507,005 to 48,506,720: a = 295, 123 blocks, tail 717 to 1012;
  • from step 250,823,121: a = 7299, 121 blocks, tail from 1015, still running at 3·108 steps.

The first two of these are entered in Lean (reach1, reach2) and run to their end with trip_iter. What is left is the boundary trips and an invariant on which tapes arise. A simple sufficient conjecture: no tape at a left-A record contains 1111, and from every tape without 1111 the next left-A record is reached. The second half holds for all tapes of at most 30 cells, and the first for the first 3·108 steps.

The shape of the run

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.534). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.413).

The right-end regime, for every list of blocks

Write T a ks m for the tape (10)^(a+2) 1 (10)^(k_n+2) … 1 (10)^(k_1+2) (1101010)^(m+1) A>0, blank on both sides, with ks = [k_1, …, k_n] any list. One round trip of the head takes T (a+1) ks m to T a ks (m+1): the first counter loses one 10, the tail gains one 1101010, every block is crossed twice and comes back unchanged. Iterated: T (a+j) ks m reaches T a ks (m+j).

The left-end phase: one round trip is a local rewrite

Read the tape from a new leftmost cell (state A, blank to the left) as units (01)^b 0 0110, then (01)^a, then a frontier word w, then any rest S. If the first unit has b = x+2 and every other unit has b ≥ 2, one round trip takes the first unit to (01)^(x+1) 0 0110, leaves the other units alone, and rewrites (01)^(a+3) w as follows. 1 becomes (01)^(a+5) 0. 00101 becomes (01)^(a+7) 1. 001001 becomes (01)^(a+8). 0001 becomes (01)^(a+3) 00110011. 0000 becomes (01)^(a+3) 00110010. 001000 becomes (01)^(a+7) 00. With no unit in front, the same holds with one 01 fewer on the right. The cases a = 0, 1, 2 are separate rules. The proof uses tripGen: if the head turns at In and leaves 1 1 Out, the whole trip follows by induction on the list of units.

py/coverage.py 3e7
Almost every trip from the left end is a proved rule

Up to 3·107 steps there are 5,490 left-A records (the head on a new leftmost cell in state A). From 2,105 of them the trip is a whole-trip rule with no unit in front. From 2,509 it is a trip_tu*/trip_su* theorem. 835 lie inside a right-end regime. Computed
c/leftrec.c (modes run and all)
No 1111 at the left-A records, and no halt from tapes without it

At all 17,518 left-A records in the first 3·108 steps the tape has no factor 1111. Conversely, for every tape of at most 30 cells without 1111, the head started in A on its first cell (blank elsewhere) reaches the next left-A record without halting (387,559,437 tapes, at most 138 steps each). Every one-trip halt from a tape of at most 16 cells needs a 1111. The set of tapes without 1111 is not closed under a trip, though: about 4% of those trips create one.

Open
Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. Two things are missing. (i) Rules for the boundary trips: the first unit used up (b ≤ 1), the seven small frontiers right after a unit, units with b ≤ 1 outside the right-end regime, the word 00111 (never seen), and the switches into and out of the right-end regime. (ii) An invariant on the tapes that arise, strong enough that every trip is a proved rule or a boundary case. A sufficient conjecture: every left-A record has no factor 1111, and from every tape without 1111 the head reaches the next left-A record. Both halves are checked as described above, but neither is proved. The regime map (first counters 13, 108, 714, 295, 7299, …) comes from this rewriting acting on a list that keeps changing, and it looks open-ended.
