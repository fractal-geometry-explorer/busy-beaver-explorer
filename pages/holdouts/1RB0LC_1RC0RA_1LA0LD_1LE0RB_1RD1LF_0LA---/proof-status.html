OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. Its tape is a list of counters written as runs of 01 or 10. It has two regimes. Both are proved in Lean one round trip at a time for every size. What is not proved is how the run passes from one regime to the next. This page lists what has been established, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 13 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves the following by the kernel. First, the round trip of the right-end regime, for every list of blocks. Second, 41 rules for the left-end phase, each covering one round trip for every size, every list of units and every rest of the tape. Lean also shows (by the kernel for the first 35,911 steps, with native_decide for the first 7.7·106) that the run enters the right-end regime twice. Up to 3·107 steps, 5,449 of the 5,490 round trips that start at the left end are instances of these theorems. The other 41 are boundary cases, and none of them is proved. No halt in the first 109 steps.

The right-end regime, for every list of blocks

Write T a ks m for the tape (10)^(a+2) 1 (10)^(k_n+2) … 1 (10)^(k_1+2) (1101010)^(m+1) A>0, blank on both sides, with ks = [k_1, …, k_n] any list. One round trip of the head takes T (a+1) ks m to T a ks (m+1): the first counter loses one 10, the tail gains one 1101010, every block is crossed twice and comes back unchanged. Iterated: T (a+j) ks m reaches T a ks (m+j).

lean/Rules.lean (six pieces, generated by py/gen_rules.py); trip, trip_iter, lean/Trip.lean

The left-end phase: one round trip is a local rewrite

Read the tape from a new leftmost cell (state A, blank to the left) as units (01)^b 0 0110, then (01)^a, then a frontier word w, then any rest S. If the first unit has b = x+2 and every other unit has b ≥ 2, one round trip takes the first unit to (01)^(x+1) 0 0110, leaves the other units alone, and rewrites (01)^(a+3) w as follows. 1 becomes (01)^(a+5) 0. 00101 becomes (01)^(a+7) 1. 001001 becomes (01)^(a+8). 0001 becomes (01)^(a+3) 00110011. 0000 becomes (01)^(a+3) 00110010. 001000 becomes (01)^(a+7) 00. With no unit in front, the same holds with one 01 fewer on the right. The cases a = 0, 1, 2 are separate rules. The proof uses tripGen: if the head turns at In and leaves 1 1 Out, the whole trip follows by induction on the list of units.

lean/FrontierRules.lean (41 rules, generated by py/gen_frontier.py); tripGen and the 17 trip_tu*, trip_su* theorems, lean/Frontier.lean

The cursor iterated

Write F1 x bs c d for A> (01)^x 0 0110, then units bs, then the cursor (01)^c (10)^d 0110, then any rest. Write F2 for the same with the cursor (01)^c 0 (01)^d 0 0110. Then F1 (x+2) bs (c+2) (d+2) reaches F2 (x+1) bs (c+4) (d+1), and F2 (x+2) bs (c+2) (d+2) reaches F1 (x+1) bs (c+6) (d+1). Iterated, F1 (x+2j+1) bs (c+2) (d+2j+1) reaches F1 (x+1) bs (c+6j+2) (d+1).

tripF1, tripF2, cursor_iter, lean/Cursor.lean (pieces in lean/CursorRules.lean)

holdouts/triage.json

E never reads 1 with a 1 to its left (so far)

The only halting entry is F reading a 1, and F is entered only from E>1 (write 1, move left): the machine halts exactly when 1 E>1 occurs. In the first 108 steps F is entered 687,974 times, always onto a 0, and in all but 38 of them the window around the head is 101[0]110.

c/haltwatch.c, first 108 steps

Almost every trip from the left end is a proved rule

Up to 3·107 steps there are 5,490 left-A records (the head on a new leftmost cell in state A). From 2,105 of them the trip is a whole-trip rule with no unit in front. From 2,509 it is a trip_tu*/trip_su* theorem. 835 lie inside a right-end regime. The remaining 41 are not covered: 32 have the first unit used up (b ≤ 1), 7 have a small frontier right after a unit (the rewrite starts a new unit), and 2 early ones have a small unit.

py/coverage.py 3e7

No 1111 at the left-A records, and no halt from tapes without it

At all 17,518 left-A records in the first 3·108 steps the tape has no factor 1111. Conversely, for every tape of at most 30 cells without 1111, the head started in A on its first cell (blank elsewhere) reaches the next left-A record without halting (387,559,437 tapes, at most 138 steps each). Every one-trip halt from a tape of at most 16 cells needs a 1111. The set of tapes without 1111 is not closed under a trip, though: about 4% of those trips create one.

Growth shape: sqrt/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.534). The regimes alternate

The right-end regime runs at steps 2,889–7,790 (a = 13), 35,911–263,899 (a = 108), 7,677,126–21,453,756 (a = 714), 36,507,005–48,506,720 (a = 295) and from 250,823,121 (a = 7299, still running at 3·108). In between, the head turns near the left end (the rewriting phase above). The tail survives these phases (m = 714, 717, 1012, 1015, …).

py/phases.py

No halt in the first 8.29×1010 steps of an accelerated run

Observed, not proved: the accelerated simulator rsim.py ran 8.29×1010 steps from blank tape without reaching a halting transition. Its tape, state and head position were equal to those of a plain C simulator at 6 checkpoints, the last at 1.04×1010 steps. Of these steps, the part after the last checkpoint rests on the simulator alone, including 481 trip-level jumps: a jump applies many identical round trips at once after seeing the same tape shape recur with constant count changes, which is validated against direct simulation but not proved.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. Two things are missing. (i) Rules for the boundary trips: the first unit used up (b ≤ 1), the seven small frontiers right after a unit, units with b ≤ 1 outside the right-end regime, the word 00111 (never seen), and the switches into and out of the right-end regime. (ii) An invariant on the tapes that arise, strong enough that every trip is a proved rule or a boundary case. A sufficient conjecture: every left-A record has no factor 1111, and from every tape without 1111 the head reaches the next left-A record. Both halves are checked as described above, but neither is proved. The regime map (first counters 13, 108, 714, 295, 7299, …) comes from this rewriting acting on a list that keeps changing, and it looks open-ended.
