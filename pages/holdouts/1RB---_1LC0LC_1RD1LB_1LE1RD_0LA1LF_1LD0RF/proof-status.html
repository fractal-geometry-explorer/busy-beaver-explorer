What is known about 1RB---_1LC0LC_1RD1LB_1LE1RD_0LA1LF_1LD0RF

1RB---_1LC0LC_1RD1LB_1LE1RD_0LA1LF_1LD0RF

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

Open, with strong evidence that it never halts. The tape is a list of blocks of 1s; the head alternates between a pass to the right that erases every other block and a sweep to the left that rebuilds them. Lean proves these block rules for all block lengths, and that the sweep meeting a gap of exactly four 0s halts; reading the table backwards, that is the only way to the halt. In 2·1010 steps every gap met there is odd. The counter built from these rules (a chain of units, each a quarter as fast as the one before) is described from the run but not yet proved.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Shift-overflow structural counter. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

Block rules of the two passes

Pass to the right (1 D> 1 1^a): a next block of b ≥ 3 cells is erased, leaving b − 2 zeros, and the pass restarts on the block after it, now 4 longer (r_step, r_step_3, r_step_4, r_step1); a next block of 2 is merged (r_merge); a next block of 1 turns the head (r_bounce). Sweep to the left (C and B on alternate cells): every other 1 is erased (l_sweep), a single 0 is filled (l_gap1), a gap of two or more turns the head (l_turn, b_turn7); the wall is crossed n words at a time (wall_l). For all values of the parameters.

No halt in the first 2.48×1015 steps (exact macro run)

A macro simulator (blocks of 6 cells, long runs of equal blocks crossed in one go; exact: every macro step is a run of the machine, counted step for step) ran from blank tape to step 2,482,693,369,154,826 without a halt (the run stopped there because the tape no longer compresses into few enough runs of blocks). At this block size it gives the same state, head position and tape as the direct simulator after 2,000,009,883 steps. This is a bounded run and says nothing about later steps.

The rules against the run, 2·1010 steps

Of the 7.1·108 passes to the right, all but 168 start under one of the proved rules, and the block erased is always odd (never 2 or 4 cells). B stands on the leftmost 1 of a block 123 times, with 1, 3, 5, 7, 11, 17, 55 or more than 59 zeros on its left, never 4. After step 193,799,086 the head keeps a fixed parity in each of the states A, B, C, E (C and A on even cells, B on odd ones) and comes no closer than 182 cells to the right end of the tape.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: fixed/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.498). Right of the start it stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

A chain of base-4 units

Read at the left end, the tape is a fixed prefix, then units: a wall (110101)^n followed by a digit with four phases. A unit advances one phase when the unit before it has gone through its four, and its wall gains two words per cycle. At 3·109 steps the walls have 9989, 2486, 614, 113, 27 and 4 words. New units appear at the far end of the chain, in front of a last stretch of about 250 cells that has not changed since step 7.4·108.

open Open

Does it halt from blank tape?

Missing: the rules of a whole round through a chain of units of any length (digit phases, the carry between units, the birth of a new top unit), and an invariant closed under them that keeps every gap met by B odd. The run to 2·1010 steps (and an outside accelerated run to 1017, not checked here) shows no halt.
