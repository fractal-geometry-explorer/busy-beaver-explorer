What is known about 1RB0LE_0RC1RC_0RD1LA_1LD0LA_1LF1LC_---0LA

1RB0LE_0RC1RC_0RD1LA_1LD0LA_1LF1LC_---0LA

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 12 November 2026.

In short

The question is open; the structure is partly known. At the moments when the head is in state C at the right end on a 0, the tape is X(a, b) = (10)^a 1^b C> 0 or Y(a, b) = (10)^a 1^b 0 C> 0. For a large enough, X(a, b) goes to X or Y (by the parity of b) with b′ = f(b) about 3.5b and a′ = a − d(b), with f and d irregular but independent of a (checked for b < 70); the run uses this until a cannot pay, and then the large b is turned back into a (for example X(127, 424) → X(318, 369)). The functions f, d and the turnover are not yet worked out.

Third pass. No halt in 1018 steps (macro simulation). Two inner loops of a round are identified; no pushdown certificate with a DFA of at most 6 states exists on either side.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Quadratic Mess. Its 3 November 2026 edition lists the machine as a cryptid: its halting is thought to come down to a Collatz-like question that no known method decides. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

The b-map

For a large enough: X(a, b) → X(a−d, f(b)) for even b and Y(a−d, f(b)) for odd b, with (f, d) = (10, 4), (8, 3), (22, 8), (32, 13), (16, 4), (44, 17), (46, 18), … for b = 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, … and step counts linear in a; Y behaves the same with f larger by 2 to 4. Checked for b < 70.

The b-map, to b < 400, and the X/Y relation

For X(a, b) = (10)^a 1^b C> 0 with a large, the next event is X(a−d(b), f(b)) for even b and Y(a−d(b), f(b)) for odd b, the same for two values of a; from Y(a, b) = (10)^a 1^b 0 C> 0 it is exactly Y(a−d(b)−2, f(b)+4) for even b and X(a−d(b), f(b)+2) for odd b. So one function pair (f, d) describes both kinds. Checked for every b < 400.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.478). Right of the start it grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.091).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

f is digit-driven in base 3

For even b, f(b) changes by +12, −6 as the last ternary digit of b/2 goes 0→1→2, with carries contributing amounts that depend on the higher digits: f looks like a 3-regular sequence (computed by a finite automaton reading the ternary digits), which would give it a closed form.
