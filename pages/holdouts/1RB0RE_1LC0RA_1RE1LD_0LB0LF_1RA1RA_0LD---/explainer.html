The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in F reading 1. F is entered only from D1 (writes 0, moves L): 1 D>1. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

Structure

Digits, a walker and a doubling clock

Stop the machine at the end of each round: the head in state A on the first blank cell right of everything written. The tape then reads

1 1 1 0 0   u1 u2 … uk   X   (1 0)N 1

a leader, a list of six-cell units 1 x y 1 0 0, a stretch X that stays fixed for a long time, and a tail of N single 1s. Write each unit as a digit by its middle bits xy: 00 = 1, 01 = 0, 10 = 2, 11 = 3. The tail is the low end of a binary counter: one round is that counter running up to its top, about 2N steps, and the tail is one 1 longer afterwards.

At the end of each round exactly one local change has been made in the digits, at a walker that moves left:

phasechangec
10 → 1 at the walker+16
1that 1 → 2, walker one digit left+4
1 → 2an old 1 → 3+18
20 3 → 1 0+14
2that 1 → 3, walker one digit left−10

and the new round lasts exactly T′ = 2T + c, with T the length of the round before. In the second epoch, which starts at step 40,509,722 with the digits 0000100000, the walker turns the last five 0s into 2s, meets the old 1, and carries a 3 to the front: 20 rounds, ending at step 6,201,936,302, every one of them to the step. The third epoch, from step 12.4·109, follows the same law.

Rebuilds

After the leader changes (at the end of the carry, or when the phase-1 walker reaches it), the next round is different: the tail of single 1s and the fixed stretch X are turned into a new, longer list of digits and a new X, and the tail starts again short. This happened at about 2.3·104, 4.05·107 1.2·1010 and 3.48·1011 steps; the lists after them have 4, 12, 13 and 14 digits. In the round ending at step 348,360,095,421 the phase-1 walker, with no digit left, changes the leader 11100 to 10100 and leaves the digits 222222000; the round length is 2T + 16, as py/epoch.py gives it. The round after is a rebuild (step 348,489,525,650), as after the leader change of epoch 2.

The rule of a rebuild is not known, so the run can be followed by arithmetic only to the end of an epoch. A halt (F reading a 1) can come only from a rebuild: inside an epoch the rounds repeat the same five local changes.

First look

The shape of the run

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.097).

After 109 steps the tape spans 182 cells (24 left of the start, 155 right), with a density of 1s of 0.571.

The walker law: every round of epochs 2 and 3

Write the six-cell units 1 x y 1 0 0 as digits by xy: 00 = 1, 01 = 0, 10 = 2, 11 = 3. In an epoch a walker moves left through the digits. Phase 1 (building 2s): a 0 becomes 1 (c = +16), then that 1 becomes 2 and the walker moves left (c = +4); if the walker finds a 1 that was already there, it becomes 3 (c = +18) and phase 2 starts. Phase 2 (a carry): the digit left of the 3 goes 0 → 1 and the 3 becomes 0 (c = +14), then that 1 becomes 3 and the carry moves left (c = −10); at the front the leader takes the place of the digit (11100 → 10100, c = +14) and the next round is a rebuild. When the phase-1 walker reaches the front (no digit left), the leader also becomes 10100 with the digits unchanged (c = +16, seen once, in epoch 3), and the next round is a rebuild. Each round lasts T′ = 2T + c. From the start of epoch 2 (step 40,509,722, T = 2,926) the law gives all 20 rounds of the epoch to the step (the last ends at 6,201,936,302); from step 12,398,695,221 it gives every round of epoch 3 seen, including 96,327,513,809 and 180,338,374,341, which were predicted before the simulator reached them.

Where a halt can be (CTL)

A closed tape language of windows of up to 6 cells closes, but its abstract configurations still allow F to read a 1 in several contexts, so it does not confine the halt.

The rebuilds, and the question

Rebuilds at about 2.3·104, 4.05·107 1.2·1010 and 3.48·1011 steps turn the tail of single 1s and the fixed stretch into a new, longer list of digits (12, 13, then 14 digits) and a new fixed stretch. Their rule, which decides the next epoch, is not known; nor whether the machine halts.
