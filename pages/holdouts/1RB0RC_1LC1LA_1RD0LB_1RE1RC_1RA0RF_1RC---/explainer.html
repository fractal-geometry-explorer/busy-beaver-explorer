Does this machine ever halt?

1RB0RC_1LC1LA_1RD0LB_1RE1RC_1RA0RF_1RC---

Nobody knows. The head sweeps from end to end of a tape that is almost all one repeating pattern, carrying a growing crowd of small defects that drift, change kind and are born at the ends. This page shows the pattern, the proved sweep rules, and why a halt would need something no run has shown.

The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in F reading 1. F is entered only from E1 (writes 0, moves R): E>1 1. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

The run at a glance

Space-time diagram and records

The diagram stacks the tape at many moments. Sampling at each new cell shows the shape of the run as the visited region grows; sampling evenly in time shows where the head spends its time. The records list every step at which the head reaches a cell no earlier step reached, with the side, the width so far and the whole tape: the first place to look for a repeating macro structure.

The structure

A periodic background with drifting defects

The head runs back and forth over the whole tape. A visit to the left end usually adds 3 new cells there and a visit to the right end 5; so the tape grows by about 8 cells per round trip and a round trip costs about as many steps as the tape is wide: hence the square-root growth on both sides.

The tape has two forms. Just after a leftward sweep it is almost entirely the word 001 repeated; just after a rightward sweep almost entirely 111110. Each sweep translates one form into the other, word by word: the rightward sweep in state C turns 001001 into 110111, the leftward one in state B turns 111011 back into 001001 (the same 6-cell period read with another phase). Lean proves both for every length.

What is not background are defects: in the 001 form, gaps between two 1s of length 1 (01, call it a) or 4 (00001, b) instead of 2. They are sparse (a few hundred on tens of thousands of cells), they drift across the tape at a few fixed speeds measured in background periods per round trip, a block of bs moves as a rigid body, as turn into bs at a front that advances one defect per round trip, and a lone a that reaches the left end is absorbed there. The two ends keep creating new defects: a sweep over pure background does not give back pure background at the ends (the end rules below leave 0 001 001 and 0 0001 001).

Every so often the head lingers at the right end and grows it by hundreds of cells in one visit (once 1,725), then the sweeps resume.

Halting

What a halt would need

The only halting transition is F on a 1, and F is entered only from E on a 1 (writing 0, moving right). So the machine halts exactly when E reads a 1 whose right neighbour is also 1. In the rightward sweep over the background, E reads the 1 of a 001 and F then reads the first 0 of the next 001; a halt needs a gap of length 0 (two adjacent 1s) where a 001 should start, at that phase. Gaps of length 0 have never been seen.

In the first 109 steps F is entered 53,310,917 times; it read 0 every time. No halt in 1010 steps.

Two other holdouts share this machine's transition table: 1RB0RF_1RC0RD_1LD1LB_1RE0LC_1RA1RD_1RD--- is it with the states renamed, and 1RB1RE_1LC0RA_1LD1LB_1LE0LF_1LA0LB_1LB--- is its mirror image with the states renamed. On blank tape they run like this machine started in state E and (mirrored) in state B, instead of A. The shared table does not make the halting questions the same. The two machines start from different configurations, and their runs from blank tape never reach a common configuration (checked for the first 200,000 steps, up to the renaming). So one could halt and the other not; each is its own open question.

Heuristic
py/sweeps.py (mode 0)
Background and defects

At the first new cell of every visit to the left end, in the first 108 steps, the tape is a run of 001 words broken by isolated defects: a gap of length 1 (01, call it a) or 4 (00001, b) between consecutive 1s instead of 2. No other gap length occurs away from the two ends. The number of defects grows about linearly with the number of sweeps (about 20 after 50 visits to the left end, 130 after 500, 320 after 1,050).

Heuristic
py/sweeps.py
How the defects move

Measured in background periods (3 cells) per round trip, the defects drift at one of two speeds, and a whole frozen block of b defects moves rigidly; a defects turn into b at a front that moves one defect per round trip; a lone a runs to the left end, where it is absorbed and the next defect changes kind. The ends create new defects: a sweep over pure background does not reproduce pure background at the ends.

Heuristic
direct simulation
Right-end episodes

A visit usually adds 3 cells at the left end and 5 at the right; now and then the head stays near the right end for a long time and adds many cells there (11, 17, 27, ... and once 1,725 cells in one visit, near step 2.9·107).

Computed
direct simulation
The halting state is never close

F1 is the only halting transition and F is entered only by E1 (0RF), so a halt needs E on a 1 with another 1 right after it. In the first 109 steps F is entered 53,310,917 times and reads 0 every time.

Open
Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. A proof would have to control the defects: their kinds, speeds and what happens when they meet each other or an end. No closed language of the simple kinds found by the generic searches (windows of up to 11 cells, with run-length parity) contains the run.
