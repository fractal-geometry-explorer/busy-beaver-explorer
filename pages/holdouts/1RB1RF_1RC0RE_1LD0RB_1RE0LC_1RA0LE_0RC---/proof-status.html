OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 15 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The tape is a queue of blocks (01)^k of sizes 4j − 2 with single-cell cursors travelling through it; all local rules are proved in Lean. The form of the tape does not exclude a halt: a tape of that form that halts is proved in Lean, and in the first 1018 steps the run from blank tape showed the dangerous shape (three cursor cells 1 side by side) at exactly one visit of the right end, where it would have had to coincide with the birth of a cursor. No closed invariant is known.

The local rules of the queue

With a = 01, b = 00110 and the head in state B at the right end. Feeding: L a^(k+1) c a^m b a^(n+2) 0 B> becomes L a^k c' a^(m+4) b a^n 0 B> (the cursor c moves one a left and flips, the block after it gets 4, the end block pays 2; without a separator the end block gets 2). Crossing a separator in two visits: b 0 → 0 a 1 1 a b → 0 a^6 b, and b 1 a^m → 0 0 a^(m+5) → 1 a b a^m. Birth: when the end block is empty the last separator becomes a new cursor and the old one stays where it is. All for all sizes and every tape to the left.

feed0, feed1, feedb0, feedb1, hop0a, hop0b, hop1a, hop1b, birth0, birth1, birth2 in lean/Rules.lean (generated by py/rules.py); feed2, feeds in lean/Phase.lean

A tape of the block form that halts

The tape 1 a b a 0 a 1 1 1 0 a^22 0 B> (blocks, one separator, cursor cells) halts after 213 steps: three cursor cells 1 side by side, with the next cursor arriving as a 0 directly right of them, is the halting shape. So “blocks, separators and cursors” is not an invariant that excludes halting. This is not a statement about the run from blank tape.

Growth shape: fixed/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.559).

Tape structure

With a = 01 and b = 00110, the tape at each visit of the right end is 1 a (b a^2)^q followed by a list of blocks a^k separated by b, with single cursor cells in between (q = 127 at 1011 steps, 16,418,839 at 1018: a visit with no cursor on the tape inserts one b a^2 at the left). The block sizes that persist are 14, 62, 254, 1022, 4094, …, that is 4j − 2, each four times the previous plus 6.

README.md; py/qsim.py prints the tape

A queue of blocks of size 4^j - 2

A cursor that walks through a block of k copies leaves 4k (plus 6 at a separator) behind it, which gives the persistent sizes 2, 14, 62, 254, 1022, ... The numbers of equal blocks persist from one size to the next (1264, 127 and 258 copies sit at sizes 254, 1022, 16382 at 2×1015 steps and at 16382, 65534, 1048574 at 1018): a self-similar counter. Observed; the global schedule of the cursors is not understood.

No halt in the first 1018 steps of an accelerated run

py/qsim.py is an exact simulator with macro rules for a^n, b and (b a^n)^q, plus jumps: when the same tape shape recurs with constant changes of the counts for 4 periods, many periods are applied at once and the next one is checked. Without jumps it agrees with a cell-by-cell simulator at every visit of the right end tested (6,000 visits, 5.3×107 steps); with jumps it agrees with the run without jumps at every visit up to 3×1010 steps. It reaches 1018 steps in 160 s without a halt (3,304,842 visits simulated one by one, 127,033 jumps). The jumps are validated, not proved. (An earlier accelerated run, rsim.py, reached 4.61×1014 steps.)

py/qsim.py, py/qsim_check.py

How close the run from blank tape came

The halting shape needs (1) a birth while a cursor is half way across a separator (0 a 1 1 a b), (2) a later cursor reaching that place, which gives 1 1 1 for one visit, and (3) a birth at exactly that visit. In the first 1018 steps there are 124 births; (1) happened once (birth 22, step 7.08×108), (2) followed at step 1.057×1011, the only visit among 3,304,842 simulated one by one with three cursor cells 1 side by side; (3) did not happen: the end block held 14,076 copies, not 0. With of the order 109 visits and 124 births in 1018 steps, a random visit is a birth with chance about 10−7; the real schedule is determined, not random, and not understood.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. A proof of non-halting has to show that a birth never falls on the visit at which three cursor cells 1 stand together (or that the next cursor then arrives as a 1); a proof of halting has to find when it does. Both need the global schedule of the cursors: the counter of equal blocks as an exact recurrence. The sibling 1RB1RF_0RC0RE_0RD1RA_1LE0LA_1LB0LD_0RD--- has an outside proof in review that it halts.
