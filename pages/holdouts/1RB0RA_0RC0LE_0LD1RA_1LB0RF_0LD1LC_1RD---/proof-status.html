OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 22 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The two basic counter increments (lowest digit 0, and lowest digits 1 then 0) are proved in Lean for all block lengths (kernel). The general increment rule matches all 2,282 clean increments of the first 3·107 steps, and the C simulator runs 2·1011 steps without a halt. Carries into the higher separators, the reservoir refills and the 1100 region are not yet rules.

One counter, two starts (21 November): this machine is the table of 1RB0LF_0LC0RE_0RA1LD_1LB0LD_0RA1RC_1LA--- started in state D, proved in Lean, and that folder’s exact block macro machine reproduces its run; the reset map and the tail operators found there hold here too, so it is open for the same reason.

No halt in the first 7.04·1012 steps (accelerated run, blocks of 4)

The run-length simulator tools/c/rleaccel.cpp (blocks of 4 cells, runs of a block crossed in one step, and cycle jumps, each used only after its one-cycle rule has been proved symbolically for all lengths above the minimums it needs; tested cell for cell against direct runs) runs 41,400,000 macro steps from blank tape without a halt, in 10 seconds. That is more than 7.04·1012 machine steps. It found no rule that repeats forever, so it does not decide the machine.

The increment rule, checked

Low 1-digits become 0, the next 0 becomes 1, its block and the lowest block gain two 10s each, the reservoir loses two. A direct simulation of 3·107 steps finds 2,282 increments that match and none that disagree; 141 carries run into higher separators and 17 reservoir refills fall outside the rule.

No halt in the first 3.14×10134 steps (region-memo accelerator)

The region-memo accelerator tools/py/bbtools/regaccel.py ran the mirrored table (so that the counter is at the left end), with blocks of 4 cells, from blank tape for 602 seconds and reached step 3.14×10134 without a halt. It keeps the tape run-length coded and, for each stretch of tape from the left end, a macro (the run until the head first leaves that stretch) computed by a symbolic run of the machine with the long runs as variables; a macro is used only when the linear inequalities its symbolic run assumed hold, otherwise the stretch is run again by the same recursion. So the run is exact, step count included. Check: 8 configurations of this run (every time the head faced blank tape on the right, up to 2×109 steps) agree cell for cell with a direct run (tools/py/regcheck.py). This is a bounded run and says nothing about later steps.

The reset map on the whole run

Seen in the mirror, a countdown block X is used up and the next block becomes the countdown; the next reset has X′ = ⌊3X/2⌋ + 1 (X even) or ⌊3X/2⌋ + 3 (X odd): 7, 13, 22, 34, 52, 79, …, 24271 at every one of the 19 transitions of the first 3·109 steps; 33 in this machine’s macro run (2·107 records, 2.5·1014 steps, no halt) and 34 in the twin’s (to 3.4·1014 steps).

The tail is not safe by itself

Steered runs of the real machine, with the countdown X replaced at each reset and the tail kept, halt when the phase lengths are irregular: 21 of 200 runs with X uniform in [36, 400] over 30 resets (20 of 100 for the twin), 25 of 100 with X in [2000, 4000], 4 of 40 with noise in [−3, 3] on the true X. Constant X, a fixed growth ratio, and 400 single changes to the true orbit never halted. So the safety of the carries depends on the 3/2 orbit's phase lengths, not only on the head part. Every steered halt is at a sweep that turns in the rough part of the tail. The true run turns there at 0.7% of its records, against 14.4% in the steered runs, and its 1,864 rough turns so far would give about 0.2 halts at the steered rate.

c/perturb.c, c/branch.c (in the folder of 1RB0LF_0LC0RE_0RA1LD_1LB0LD_0RA1RC_1LA---)

What the tail sees

The rest of the counter (the tail) is acted on by four operators fixed by the parity of the growing block: E0, E1 alternating inside each phase (2,965 of 2,965 increments of the first 6,000 records) and E1 O0 O1 or E1 at a reset (13 of 14 resets). Whether the machine halts is whether this word of operators, spelled out by the 3/2 orbit, ever drives the tail into the halting pattern.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. It halts exactly when C or E reads a 0 that has a 1 on its left and (10)^k 1 1 on its right. The increments never do this; the refills, long carries and the 1100 region are not closed rules yet. The reset map is proved for the head of the tape (reset_map), so a halt can only happen inside a carry into the tail.
