What is known about 1RB1RA_0LC0RA_1LF0LD_1LE0RC_1LB1LD_1RB---

1RB1RA_0LC0RA_1LF0LD_1LE0RC_1LB1LD_1RB---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 13 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The mechanism is now known and partly proved. The head sweeps right in state A (every 0 moves one cell right) and left two cells at a time in states E and D: a pair of cells 01 becomes 11, a pair 11 becomes 10. On a tape of blocks of ones of odd length this only moves the content two cells to the right per round trip; the right end eats it and the left end adds a new word 1 3 1 5 5 (block lengths) every six round trips. Everything else comes from defects, places where the left sweep lands on a 0: the head turns round there and bounces between the defect and the right end, which rebuilds the tape to the right of the defect in a longer pattern.

Lean proves (kernel) that a halt can only come from one pattern, state E on a 0 with 1 0 0 to its left, and that the machine never halts if that pattern never appears. The C simulator sees no halt in 6.1·1010 steps, and the pattern never: in that run E met two 0s in a row inside the tape 285 times, after step 3.6·107 always in the same harmless surrounding. 3,600 real tapes with one or two cells changed do not halt either, but small tapes of blocks do (111011 halts after 655 steps), so the shape of the tape alone does not prevent a halt. No closed set of configurations was found: the tape is nested to unbounded depth, so the closed-set method that found closed sets for two sibling machines does not apply as it stands, and four automatic searches for a regular invariant failed.

How it works

Going right (state A) every 0 moves one cell right. Going left (states E, D) the tape is rewritten in pairs of cells: 01 becomes 11, 11 becomes 10. On blocks of ones of odd length the two sweeps together only move the content two cells to the right; the right end consumes it, the left end adds the word 1 3 1 5 5 every six round trips.

When the left sweep lands on a 0 (an even block or two 0s: a defect) the head turns round inside the tape and bounces between the defect and the right end; the defect moves left and leaves a longer pattern behind. Defects nest, which gives zones of growing pattern and the irregular resets.

A halt needs the left sweep to land on the last 0 of 1 0 0 0 (proved in Lean). That never happened in 6.1·1010 steps, nor on 3,600 real tapes with a cell or two changed; small tapes of blocks do reach it, so the shape alone does not protect the run. A proof that it cannot happen is missing.

Where a halt can come from

If the machine halts on its third step from a configuration, that configuration is one of: B with 1 two left, 0 left, 0 under the head. So a run can reach a halt only through one of these patterns, and a proof that the machine never halts only has to rule them out. The proof splits on the state and the five cells around the head.

The sweep rules and the single events

17 rules, for every repeat count and any tape around. Sweeps: A going right moves every 0 one cell right, D going left rewrites pairs, over the words 1101, 110111, 11, the 20-cell word of the left zone (blocks 1 3 1 5 5) and the 60-cell word of the second zone (blocks 3 1 3 1 3 5 5 3 5 5 3 1 3 1 3). Events: a single 0 moving right, the head stepping over two 0s, the right turn at three 0s, and the five cases of the left sweep landing on a 0 (e_bounce, e_z2_pass, e_z2_bounce, e_end, e_prehalt).

The regular left sweep, for any tape of pairs

State E on the right cell of a pair, the pair being 01 or 11: after 2 or 4 steps the pair is 11 or 10 and E stands on the right cell of the next pair to the left. By induction the same holds for any list of such pairs, so on blocks of ones of odd length the left sweep is a fixed rewriting and never stops.

e_pair, sweep, lean/Sweep.lean

A halt comes from exactly one pattern

Three steps before any halt the machine is in state E on a 0 with 1 0 0 to its left (E0, B0, C0, then F reads the 1). So if the run from blank tape never shows 1 0 0 E>0, the machine never halts; and from that pattern it does halt.

Perturbed real tapes do not halt; small tapes of blocks do

Tapes of the real run at three moments (3.8·106, 4.5·107, 2.3·108 steps) with one cell flipped, one cell deleted, a 0 or a 1 inserted, two cells flipped, or a 1 cleared next to a 0: 3,600 tapes, none halts within 2·106 steps. Tapes made of blocks of ones separated by single 0s are not safe in general: blocks 3, 2 (111011, state A on the first cell) halt after 655 steps, and of 2,000 random tapes of n blocks of lengths 1 to 5 those that halt within 3·106 steps number 62, 14, 0, 4, 2, 0, 1, 1 for n = 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 20, 30 (odd lengths 1, 3, 5, 7 only: 0, 29, 0, 3, 3, 0, 0, 0). Random bit strings halt often and early (they contain three 0s in a row). So, as for the two sibling machines, tapes of the same shape can halt; a proof has to use more of the real tape than its shape.

No closed set found (four searches)

(1) The closed-set method of the sibling machines needs a tape zone w^N T u^M tail with a bounded zone; here the number of zones grows, so there is no finite list of nodes. (2) Finite-automata reduction with a DFA that tracks parity and the last k ≤ 6 cells, on either side: the blank start is accepted as possibly halting. (3) A closed tape language whose states are windows of K runs (lengths exact up to a cap, then parity): K = 2, 3, 4 reach a halting abstract configuration, K = 5 exceeds 3·106 abstract configurations. Reason in both: a window forgets what lies beyond it, and windows from the left end get glued to inner ones (the real run visits 4,821 abstract configurations where the closure has 406,469). (4) The same with the far end of the tape, the number of even blocks and a parity added to the state: small sizes reach a halting abstract configuration, larger ones exceed 3·106 configurations; the abstraction cannot tell the long real tape from short block tapes, which do halt.

Regular-language deciders tried (F11)

None of these closes: each reaches a halting configuration in its abstraction, which says nothing about whether the machine halts. n-gram CPS (tools/c/ngramcps.c), equal widths on both sides: a halting local configuration is reached with plain up to width 16, history 1 up to width 16, history 2 up to width 16, history 3 up to width 18, LRU history up to width 14 (larger widths ran out of the 150 s or 60 million configuration budget). n-gram CPS with every cell tagged by its position mod p: fails for p = 2 up to width 16, p = 3 up to width 16, p = 4 up to width 12, p = 6 up to width 12. n-gram CPS where each side also carries the state of a small automaton that has read that side from the far end (every automaton with up to 3 states on both sides, or 4 states on one side), with position mod 1..4 and widths 4, 6, 8: no closed set without a halt in all 120 parameter sets. Run-length windows (the K nearest runs of equal macro cells on each side, run lengths kept exactly below T and mod M above): fails for all 72 parameter sets (cells of 1 to 4 bits, K = 2, 4, 6, T = 2, 4, M = 1, 2, 4). Two-automaton closed tape language by SAT (a deterministic automaton reading each side inward, n states each, CaDiCaL): no solution for n = 2..8; n = 9 not finished in 150 s.

Growth shape: sqrt/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.405). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.479).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Content drifts right; defects start inner bounces; the tape is nested

Right sweep and regular left sweep together complement the pairs and shift them by one pair, so the content moves two cells right per round trip against a right end that stands still and consumes it. A defect (the left sweep landing on a 0: an even block or two 0s) is an inner left wall: the head bounces between it and the right end, the defect moves left one block per bounce, and what is to its right becomes new content with longer runs of blocks of 3. Defects nest, so at 109 steps the tape is a sequence of zones: (1 3 1 5 5)^a, the 15-block word 254 times, a 45-block word (the three words have 20, 60 and 180 cells), then runs (3)^k separated by a 1 or a 5 with k growing to the right. Composition of the proved rules, checked against traces; not formalised.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. What is missing is an invariant that excludes 1 0 0 E>0: two 0s in a row arise inside the tape only when the left sweep lands on the 0 after a block of length 1, and they are removed again by the next bounce unless a third 0 is adjacent. A proof has to control where the grid of pairs (fixed by the right end) stands relative to such places; a regular language with windows does not do it.
