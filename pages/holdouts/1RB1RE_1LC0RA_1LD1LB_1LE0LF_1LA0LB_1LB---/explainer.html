The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in F reading 1. F is entered only from D1 (writes 0, moves L): 1 D>1. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

What it does

A conveyor belt

At a right turn the tape reads like (100)^7 10000 (100)^5 10000 (100)^15 … 10000 (100)^104: runs of 100 separated by markers 10000, plus 10s near the left end. The belt moves 9 cells right per round trip. The rightmost run shrinks by two 100s per round trip.

A block 1^5 0 at a left turn is (100)^2 at a right turn: b_block converts one way, b_unblock the other.

Can it halt?

Where a halt would come from

It halts from 1 D>1; D reads a 1 about 53 million times in 109 steps, always with a 0 on its left. Both the engine and the reader would have to be understood to rule it out.

Try it

Play the block law

Seen from far enough away, this machine never handles single cells. Behind the head the tape is blocks: runs of 1s separated by single 0s. Ahead of it the tape is gaps: runs of 0s separated by single 1s. The head goes out over the gaps, turning them into blocks, turns, and comes back over the blocks, turning them into gaps. For this machine the blocks are on the right and the gaps on the left. It is the same law as for 1RB0RC_1LC1LA_1RD0LB_1RE1RC_1RA0RF_1RC---, mirrored and with this machine's state names and its own start. Every move in that game is one of a short list of rules about whole blocks and gaps, and each rule is a theorem of lean/Law.lean for every size at once.

Step through it below. The numbers are block lengths and gap lengths, and the only way to halt is state F arriving on a gap of length 0, two adjacent 1s. From blank tape that has not happened; the two small tapes in the menu are built from the gap words that make up almost all of the run (1, the pair 2 2, and the pair 4 2), and they do halt, which is why the law alone does not decide this machine.

The mirrored table of 1RB0RC_1LC1LA, a different start

Mirroring left and right and renaming the states A, B, C, D, E, F of this machine to B, C, D, E, A, F gives the transition table of 1RB0RC_1LC1LA_1RD0LB_1RE1RC_1RA0RF_1RC--- exactly (proved in lean/Twin.lean: the renaming commutes with every step, and halts_iff says this machine halts from blank tape exactly when that table halts from the other start). So this machine started on blank tape runs like the mirror image of that one started in state B instead of A. The shared table does not make the halting questions the same. The two machines start from different configurations, and their runs from blank tape never reach a common configuration (checked for the first 200,000 steps, up to the renaming). So one could halt and the other not; each is its own open question.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The right-end reader looks like a simple finite process on the runs it eats; the left engine is a counter-like zone whose output is not understood. A proof would describe both and show that the reader never meets a word that halts.
