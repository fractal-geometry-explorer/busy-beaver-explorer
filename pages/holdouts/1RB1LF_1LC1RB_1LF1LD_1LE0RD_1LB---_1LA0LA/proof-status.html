What is known about 1RB1LF_1LC1RB_1LF1LD_1LE0RD_1LB---_1LA0LA

1RB1LF_1LC1RB_1LF1LD_1LE0RD_1LB---_1LA0LA

OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape. Seen in pairs of cells, each visit of the growing left end applies one recursive map to the tape; that map runs a counter in base 4 whose levels are long blocks of the word 111010. The counter itself can never halt; what the head does on the rare occasions it reaches the right end is not understood.

Status as of 23 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The map from one wall visit to the next is known exactly as a recursion on words of pairs, with exact step counts, and the counter phases are proved in Lean. An accelerated simulator that finds its own rules, checked against direct simulation at every one of 1,425 wall visits (word and step number), runs through the seventh right-end event (wall visit 4.137·10148) to wall visit about 1025600 (step about 1.733·1051200) without a halt, and a level-stripping accelerator built on it shows that the next event cannot come before wall visit 108.73·10146. It is exact by construction but has not been re-derived independently. The right-end events do not stop, each one can in principle leave a halting word (such words exist, proved in Lean), and no invariant is known.

The arithmetic of a descent

For every value: under law A the fuel after j phases satisfies 21j(23F0 + c) ≤ 44j(23Fj + c) and 44j·23Fj ≤ 21j·23F0 + 44j·4hi, under law B the same with a factor of 4, and a stack of phases whose maps grow at least like aKj needs at least aΣKj times the hits below it. The arithmetic only: that the machine's phases follow the laws is the log above.

Law A from the factory rule

For every value, with the factory rule as a hypothesis: q cycles of 11 hits add q rungs, burn 6q units of fuel and pass 2q hits down; after t hits the rung count nt satisfies t + 11lo ≤ 11nt + 10 and 11nt ≤ t + 11hi; and a phase of k super-cycles whose hits H (21k − s0 ≤ H ≤ 21k + s1) all reach the factory writes a fuel that obeys law A with offsets 10 + s0 and s1 + 11hi.

The recursion with step counts, checked against the machine

On digit words: G(a T) = 0^a h(T) in 16 + 4a steps, h(0 T) = 3 + T, h(k T) = 1 + G^k(2 + T) in 2k steps plus the sub-visits, a unit 1 0 passes a hit in 26 steps. Accelerated run: no halt up to wall visit about 1025600 (step about 1.733·1051200)

The digit engine finds cycle, run and recursion rules by symbolic execution, and the macro simulator does the right-end events on cells. Rules added on 16 November 2026: frozen words emitted by cycle rules, and nested-frame rules for G12(2 (1 0 0 3 0)n X), which opens itself one level down. With them the run passes the seventh event (after wall visit 4.137·10148), where the old run stalled. The carries through the levels used memory quadratic in the depth, because each open level kept its own old copy of the rest of the word. Deep levels now drop their input and redo the call if the carry stops early, and each frame counts its steps locally. With that change the run reaches wall visit about 1025600 (57,907 levels, descent 41,800 deep in 1.1 GB) with no further event and no halt. It was then stopped to free the core; the next jump would need about 4 GB. The run is exact by construction. It was validated against direct simulation (1,422 wall visits, words and step numbers, also with dropping forced on) and against the plain recursion on 400 random cases, but not re-derived independently.

No right-end event before wall visit 108.73·10146

At wall visit 103200 the word has 5,316 levels and then an eater on a fuel (1 0 0)m, m = 6.46·10147. With the fuel symbolic, 3,633 hits burn 27 units, pass 5 hits down and leave a block of four base-4 levels (×256); it runs K = 2.394·10146 such rounds, which alone gives 256K wall visits. Then come descents: eaters of the ×64 kind on runs of a 20-digit rung, each fuel an affine function of the round count before it, shrinking by about 2.1 per phase. 42,000 phases in 233 descents, with no event, need 22.899·10147 top hits. The bottom word does not run down (259 fuels (1 0 0)F before, 269 after): the fuels that start the descents shrink by the same factor of about 2.1 from one descent to the next, a hierarchy of the same shape at every level, so the event needs a rule for the levels. Checks: the phase jumps reproduce the twin's seventh event exactly and agree with plain batches on 6 words with a ×64 phase; the cached transitions between phases agree with the plain steps over the first 700 phases.

The laws between phases

Logged over 12,482 phases from the word at wall visit 103200: after a ×64 phase (slack F − 4K of 5 to 9) the next fuel is fresh by law A, 11F′ = 21K + e with −913 ≤ e ≤ 224 (6,936 times), fresh by law B, F′ = K + δ with 2 ≤ δ ≤ 12 (1,401 times), or the rest of the same run (4,063 times). The rung is a frozen word, so the hits a phase passes down cross every run of rungs. The bottom word does not grow: its long runs, stored by the engine as many pieces in different rotations, are 279 runs (24,236 digits) at the start and 306 (27,474 digits) after 14,000 phases, about 270 of them fuels (1 0 0)M. With the pieces merged, 1,294 of the first 1,404 phases follow law A. The logged phases give the same bound as before (22.899·10147 top hits), all of it from the first phases. Three fixes made the accelerator run past phase 30 again.

The factory rule: 11 hits make one rung

Behind a ×64 eater the word reads (10) 4 06 (10) 4 04 3 [100]M 3 4 03 4 3 4 03 (10) X, and the hits the eater passes down all land there. Run with symbolic counts from each of the 11 states of its cycle, the digit recursion gives the same rule, for every n ≥ 1 and M ≥ 7: 11 hits turn [rung]n into [rung]n+1 and [100]M into [100]M−6, and pass 2 hits on to X. So an eater phase of K super-cycles (21K hits) writes a fuel of about 21K/11 rungs: law A.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The counter cannot halt; a right-end event could leave a halting word, and no rule for these events is known.

README

The right-end events

A right-end event is a hit that reaches the last digit of the word. Some are resets (the levels are crushed into single digits), some only turn the youngest ladder rungs back into fuel. They are simulated on cells; a rule for them on digits (a second recursion for the return sweep) is not known, and they keep happening: every event is a new chance for a halting word. The list of runs below the eater grows as the phases go on (see above), so the next event needs that list used up, and no rule for it is known.
