OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 24 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. At each left record the tape is 0^inf ell B>c (10)^D1 x1 (10)^D2 x2 … with single cells x = 1 or 0. The blocks form a binary counter, incremented once per long sweep while the first block counts down by 2. An exact block macro machine (py/blockmm.py) maps each record to the next by cached symbolic crossings; it agrees with direct simulation at all 15,144 records of the first 3·108 steps. Its 387 crossing rules used in the first 300,000 records are proved in Lean (kernel) for every block size and tape, and so is the even long sweep over any list of blocks (carry_even). The composition of rules is trusted Python, not Lean. The macro run: no halt through 16,409,053 left records and 338,536,834,271,065 steps (3.39·1014), across 28 resets. At the resets the countdown block follows X → 3X/2 + 1 (X even) or (3X + 5)/2 (X odd); this reset map is proved in Lean for the head of the tape, for this machine and its twin, given that every carry into the tail comes back (lean/ResetMap.lean); every carry of the first 130 phases of both runs is checked in Lean, by a proved Hashlife over the carries (lean/TailHashLong.lean, X ≈ 3.3·1024, about 6·1049 steps), so there the map holds with no assumption, so the long run is a Collatz-like question about the tail; random block lists that look like records can halt, so no simple invariant is available.

The reset map, for the head of the tape

From a reset 0^inf 1 B>1 0 (10)^X T with X ≥ 36 the run reaches the reset with X′ = ⌊3X/2⌋ + 1 (X even) or + 3 (X odd), for every tail T whose carries come back with the signatures listed in Good (reset_map); k phases in a row by induction (orbit). The run from blank tape is at Reset 40 T0own after 8,577 steps (reach_own) and the twin at Reset 52 T0twin after 14,638 (reach_twin); if every phase's carries come back, neither halts (never_halts_own, never_halts_twin). The runs between carries are 19 theorems for all parameters and tails.

lean/ResetMap.lean, lean/ResetMapRuns.lean (py/gen_resetmap.py)

Excursions, checked for every tape on the left

A run-length simulator whose left side ends in an arbitrary tape: no move reads past the end of the left side, and every move (a crossing of a run, or one step) is proved for every tape on the left in a number of steps that does not depend on it, so a successful run proves a tail excursion exactly as Good states it (exc_spec). A checker runs the excursions of each phase in order on the real tail; its success for k phases proves GoodN k (cOrbit_spec), so the reset map then holds with no hypothesis (reach_of_check).

lean/ExcSim.lean, lean/ResetExc.lean

An excursion simulator with symbolic counts (linear forms, with the conditions its decisions used), every move proved for every valuation satisfying them; transforms of many carries for a whole family of tails, and their composition; a memoized phase checker whose success proves GoodN k.

No halt in the first 9.48×10134 steps (region-memo accelerator)

The region-memo accelerator tools/py/bbtools/regaccel.py ran with blocks of 4 cells, from blank tape for 602 seconds and reached step 9.48×10134 without a halt. It keeps the tape run-length coded and, for each stretch of tape from the left end, a macro (the run until the head first leaves that stretch) computed by a symbolic run of the machine with the long runs as variables; a macro is used only when the linear inequalities its symbolic run assumed hold, otherwise the stretch is run again by the same recursion. Shape of the records

In the first 105 records: no block (0, 1) after the first; after a 0-block the next block has D + x even; the first block has D + x odd (or, right after a reset, 1 B>1 then (0, 0) then a block with D + x odd). 99.99% of macro steps are a single sweep: crossings right, one turn, crossings left.

py/reset_map.py

The carries come back

Every carry into the tail seen on the direct runs has the signature reset_map assumes: 1,888 phase double steps of both machines in their first 4,000 records, and 11 resets and 12 phase ends with X from 40 to 727. The signatures never depend on X or on the tail in the instances seen. For the first 36 phases of both runs this is now proved (lean/ResetExcLong.lean).

py/gen_resetmap.py

The tail is not safe by itself

Steered runs of the real machine, with the countdown X replaced at each reset and the tail kept, halt when the phase lengths are irregular: 21 of 200 runs with X uniform in [36, 400] over 30 resets (20 of 100 for the twin), 25 of 100 with X in [2000, 4000], 4 of 40 with noise in [−3, 3] on the true X. Constant X, a fixed growth ratio, and 400 single changes to the true orbit never halted. So the safety of the carries depends on the 3/2 orbit's phase lengths, not only on the head part. Every steered halt is at a sweep that turns in the rough part of the tail. The true run turns there at 0.7% of its records, against 14.4% in the steered runs, and its 1,864 rough turns so far would give about 0.2 halts at the steered rate.

c/perturb.c, c/branch.c

What the tail sees

Right of the countdown and the growing block Y, the tail changes at each increment by one of four operators fixed by Y mod 2 and Y's cell, independent of the countdown (checked on constructed tapes). Inside every phase they alternate E0, E1 (1,467 of 1,467 increments here, 2,965 of 2,965 in the twin), and a reset applies E1 O0 O1 or E1 as X is even or odd (13 of 13 resets here, 13 of 14 in the twin). So the tail's future is one word of operators spelled out by the orbit of the 3/2 map; they do not commute.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The reset map is proved for the head of the tape (reset_map), so a halt can only happen inside a carry into the tail; missing is a proof that every carry comes back (∀ k, GoodN k 40 T0own gives never_halts_own), or an invariant of the reachable tails that excludes 1 A>1.
