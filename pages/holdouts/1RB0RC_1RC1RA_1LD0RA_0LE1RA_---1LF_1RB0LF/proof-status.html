What is known about 1RB0RC_1RC1RA_1LD0RA_0LE1RA_---1LF_1RB0LF

1RB0RC_1RC1RA_1LD0RA_0LE1RA_---1LF_1RB0LF

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The head sweeps between the ends and each sweep adds about one cell at an end, so time is about quadratic in the width. Lean proves the shift rules and that C meeting 1^(2n+2) 0 halts at once; the run has never met it in 1010 steps, but the right end is not decoded.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Bouncer + Chaotic Edge. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

Halting family

C going right over 1^(2n+2) 0 halts within 2n + 5 steps, for every n and every tape around: it erases the ones (C1 = 0RA, A1 = 0RC), writes a 1 on the 0 and turns into D going left, D reads the 0 just written and turns into E, and E reads another 0.

halts_C_even, lean/Rules.lean

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.504). Right of the start it grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.077).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Three parts of the tape

At the steps where F reaches a new cell on the left: the left end runs through a cycle of about ten prefixes (one new cell each), the middle is (1 0 1 0^8)^k with k growing by about one per cycle (16 blocks at step 19,164, 104 at 356,532, 497 at 6,664,000), and the right end, written with a = 10 and b = 110, is a string such as 0a000abbbbbbbbbbbbbbbbbbbbbbba0aa000abbbbbbbba00000aa000ab… whose runs of b grow and collapse with no pattern decoded. Width 5,692 at step 6,664,000: about 0.2·w2 steps.

Does it halt?

Not known. What is left is the right end: whether a sweep ever brings C onto an even run of 1s followed by a 0 (or another E-on-0 pattern).
