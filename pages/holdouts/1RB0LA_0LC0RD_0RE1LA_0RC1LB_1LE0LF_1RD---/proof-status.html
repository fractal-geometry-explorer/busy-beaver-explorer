1RB0LA_0LC0RD_0RE1LA_0RC1LB_1LE0LF_1RD---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. At the left end the tape is a list of blocks (10)^x separated by single 0s, alternating big and small; in long stretches every pair (x, y) becomes (x − δ, y + δ) with the same δ between two left visits. The collisions when a block runs out are not understood. Halting needs F on a 1, entered only from E on a 1: never one step away in the first 3·106 steps.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: power/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.512). Right of the start it grows like a power of the step count other than 1/2 (fitted exponent 0.238).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

The tape is a list of pairs of blocks

Whenever the visited part grows on the left (in A), the tape reads A>0 0^3 (10)^x1 0 (10)^y1 0 (10)^x2 0 (10)^y2 0 … with x big and y small, e.g. at step 140,133,553: 442 61 407 61 407 64 381 62 340 56 303 52 …, and a short irregular end near the right edge.

tools/c/tokrun.c MACHINE N a=10 L

The drift: (x, y) → (x − δ, y + δ)

Between two left extensions every pair loses δ from its big block and gives it to its small one, the same δ for the whole stretch: step 140,133,553 → 140,405,051 has δ = 6 for all of 442 61 407 61 407 64 381 62 340 → 436 67 401 67 401 70 375 68 334. So x + y is kept and the separator inside each pair moves left by δ while the separators between pairs stay. δ varies from visit to visit (2, 4, 6, …); in some visits the list is re-indexed from the left end, and when a block runs out the pair is rebuilt.

the left-extension samples, 226 transitions to 3·109 steps

Never one step from a halt

The machine halts when F reads a 1, and F is entered only from E reading a 1 (E1 → 0LF). In the first 3·106 steps E reads a 1 122,248 times, always with a 0 to its left.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The drift is measured, not proved, and the collisions (a small block reaching 0, a new pair at the left end) are not written as rules. This looks like a particle system whose collisions decide everything; it may be chaotic.
