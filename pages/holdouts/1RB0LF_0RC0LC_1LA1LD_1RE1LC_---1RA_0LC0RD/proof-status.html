What is known about 1RB0LF_0RC0LC_1LA1LD_1RE1LC_---1RA_0LC0RD

1RB0LF_0RC0LC_1LA1LD_1RE1LC_---1RA_0LC0RD

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Almost the whole tape is the seven-cell word 0101111 (five 1s) in one of its seven phases. Long runs of it are separated by a handful of short defects. Two crossings of a run are proved in Lean. The defects' dynamics, and with it halting (E reading a 0), is not understood.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Skelet 1-like Class 1. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: power/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like a power of the step count other than 1/2 (fitted exponent 0.148). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.553).

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The machine halts only when E reads a 0. E is entered from D on a 0 (writing 1, moving right), and the sweeps run the zigzag D, C, A, E through the defects. A halt would need a defect shape not seen so far. Ruling it out needs the defect dynamics. CTL (windows of 2 to 5 cells) and NGramCPS (widths 2 to 8, history, LRU) fail.
