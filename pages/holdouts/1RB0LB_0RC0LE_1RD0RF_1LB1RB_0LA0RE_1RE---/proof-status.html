This six-state machine is on the bbchallenge list of holdouts. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on. The headline: it halts from blank tape, proved in Lean.

Status as of 13 November 2026.

In short

It halts, proved in Lean (theorem halts_from_blank). The machine is a small rewriting system on the right half of its tape (the visit law, kernel-checked): blocks 000/100 are binary digits, blocks 1010 double the visits passing through, and a visit that meets 1011 halts. A checker for this system, proved correct in the kernel, finds that rounds 1 to 161,210 only rewrite and that round 161,211 meets 1011; that evaluation is by native_decide (compiled code), in ten segments. It halts after between 6.3 × 1024,266 and 1.08 × 1024,267 steps (computed, not proved).

lean/Events.lean (generated by py/events.py)

The visit law, in two forms

A visit (the head on a 0 with a 0 on its left and a stream s on its right, in state C or F; it returns in state A two cells further left) rewrites the stream by 0 s → 1 s, 100 s → 000 v(s), 110 s → 000 v(s), 1010 s → 1010 v(v(s)), and halts the machine on 1011 s; for every stream and every left tape. Form W obeys the rules for 0, 100, 1010, 1011; it has no such rule for 110 (W_1100: 1100 s → 1010 v(s)). The run is a sequence of rounds 0^inf A> 1 0 0 S → 0^inf A> 1 0 0 v(1 0 S), starting after 6 steps with S = 1 0^inf.

V_0, V_100, V_110, V_1010, V_1011_halts, W_*, round, round_halts, start, lean/Visit.lean (generated by py/visit2.py)

The abstract visit law and its count laws

The four rules and the halting pattern as inductive relations on streams, with no machine in them; the 1010 rule is guarded (the stream behind must not start with 11), and after two visits a stream never starts with 11. Under n visits, 000 r and 100 r are binary digits that pass every second visit on, and 1010 r passes 2n visits on; likewise for "one of the first n visits halts".

If the checker accepts, the machine halts

This machine satisfies the law, so for every k: if the checker runs k rounds from the word 1 and then finds a halting visit in the next round, the machine halts from blank tape. Axioms: propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound.

The same round as the PR59 machines

The three machines of busycoq PR59 (1RB0LB_0RC0LD_1LD1RE_0LA0RF_0RD1RF_0RC--- and two relatives) obey the same law with one form of visit, and PR59 proves in Rocq that they halt in round 161,211. Between 6.3 × 1024,266 and 1.08 × 1024,267 steps

Computed, not proved: the machine halts after between 6.3 × 1024,266 and 1.08 × 1024,267 steps (190.3·280605 to 323.1·280605). Every visit costs a fixed number of steps of its own: 4 or 8 steps for 0 → 1, 6 or 8 for 100, 10 for 110, 22 or 24 for 1010 (entered in C or F). A C program repeats the checker's pass for all 161,210 rounds and adds up the visits per rule (5.9519·280605 of each main kind in the completed rounds, at most 2.1164·280605 more in the halting round). Its final word agrees cell for cell with the word the Lean segments check; the sums are in floating point, and the bounds are widened by 0.1% for that.

Growth shape: exp/exp

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.075).
