What is known about 1RB0RE_1RC---_0RD0RC_1LE1LF_1LD1RA_1RC0LF

1RB0RE_1RC---_0RD0RC_1LE1LF_1LD1RA_1RC0LF

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The run is a sequence of phases; in each, rounds (proved in Lean) move two 10s from a stack into a growing gap of 0s, and at the end the gap is turned back into a stack about 3/2 times as large. The phase map, checked by simulation for every x ≤ 120 in the decoded cases, takes the blank-tape orbit through 63 phases, to P(1, 2,162,240,868,156,300); the next phase is in the case x ≡ 4 (mod 8) with the leading 1, which splits further by higher powers of 2 (e.g. 32i + 20 → 243i + 202) and was not decoded.

Rounds of a phase

With the stack (10)x+2 1 1 1 0, a gap of 2g + 2 zeros and D at the right end, one round removes two 10s and adds six zeros: (10)x+2 1110 (00)g 00 D> → (10)x 1110 (00)g+3 00 D> in 4g + 26 steps (and the first round, from no gap, in 22 steps). Plus the shift rules: C erases 1s going right, D and E fill 0s going left.

The phase map

P(pre, x) = [1] (10)x 1110 D> at the right end of blank tape. Checked by direct simulation for all 225 decoded cases with x ≤ 120: P(0, x) → P(x mod 2, 3⌊x/2⌋ + 6); P(1, 2i+1) → P(0, 3i + 6); P(1, 4i+2) → P(1, 9i + 15); P(1, 8i) → P(1, 27i + 18). The slopes 3/2, 9/4, 27/8 are powers of 3/2.

Where it can halt

Only B reading 1 halts; B is entered only from A reading 0, and A only from E reading 1, so the machine halts exactly on E> 1 0 1. Read off the table.

63 phases, then the undecoded case

From blank tape the machine reaches P(1, 3) at step 34; the map then gives P(0, 9), P(1, 18), P(1, 51), P(0, 81), P(1, 126), P(1, 294), P(1, 672), P(1, 2286), P(1, 5154), P(1, 11607), P(0, 17415), ... The map itself is only checked for small x, so the long orbit is a prediction, not a proof.

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.051). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.496).

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The decoded cases multiply x by (3/2)k; which case applies depends on the low binary digits of x, and each further level of 2-adic digits brings another factor 3/2. Deciding halting means understanding this map on all of its 2-adic levels (and whether some level produces E> 1 0 1), a Collatz-type problem.
