The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in B reading 1. B is entered only from A0 (writes 1, moves R): A>0 1. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

What it does

Sweeps that rewrite a periodic tape

The head sweeps right and left across the tape, but not always to the ends: it turns at the first wall it meets, so sweeps of every length are nested inside one another, and the tape grows by a few cells at each end now and then (the square-root growth on both sides).

The two sweeps are simple finite-state rewritings. Leftward, D runs over 1s; at a 0 with a 1 on its left it swaps them (1 0 becomes 0 1, via C) and carries on, so every isolated 0 moves one cell left. At 0 0 it writes 0 1 and C turns right into A. Rightward, the head reads the tape two cells at a time in states F and A (E, C and B only make one-cell back-steps).

Almost the whole tape is the word 1110 repeated. A rightward sweep in F turns each 1110 into 0111 (one cell to the right) and the leftward sweep moves every 0 back one cell to the left, so the background survives a round trip unchanged (each of the two crossings is proved in Lean for every number of copies). The background comes in four phases (where its 0s sit modulo 4), and the tape is a row of domains of different phases. Between two domains is a wall: a block 1k0 with k ≠ 3. Walls with a phase jump of 2 alternate between 1 1 1 1 1 and 1 0 1 0 1 and drift left; walls with a jump of 1 (0 1 1 0) are born at the left end and run right fast; walls meet and merge. The particles

The tape as a row of particles

Cut the tape into blocks 1k0 and write each block as its k. Almost every block is a 3, the background word 1110; a run of n of them is written (n), or . for one. The other blocks are particles:

blockparticlephase jump
1, 5a wall, in its two forms2
2a runner3
0runner fused with a 1-wall1
4runner fused with a 5-wall1
7, 9walls in contact (rare)0, 2

The leftmost block is the left gadget (4 when idle). Each cycle the head goes from the gadget to the first runner and back. Rightward (state F) it reads two cells at a time and crosses walls, flipping each one (1 ↔ 5); it turns at the first block with an odd phase jump, the first runner. Leftward (state D) it moves every 0 back one cell. So the tape is: the gadget, an active stretch of walls that flip every cycle and drift one block left every two cycles, the first runner, and a frozen stretch that nothing reads until a runner gets there: older runners at rest, the walls they left behind, and a periodic crystal of walls at the right end.

Read together: walls flow left through the active stretch and are turned into runners at the left end; each new runner becomes the first one and the old one stops; when a runner catches a resting runner the two make a wall again. When the last two runners have merged the head sweeps all the way to the right end, where the crystal grows and releases more walls.

The sweeps

Halting

The one pattern that halts, and what a proof still needs

The machine halts only when B reads a 1, and Lean proves that the only way there is F reading 0 0 0 1 in step with its pairs. In blocks that is two 0-blocks side by side inside the stretch the head crosses. A 0-block is a runner fused with a 1-wall and lasts one cycle, so a halt needs two fused runner-walls next to each other, or a fused one resting in the frozen part right next to another when a runner arrives.

In 1.2·1011 steps the rightward state never reads 0 0 0 inside the tape, and at none of the 448,994 sweep starts of the first 109 steps is there 0 0 0 inside the tape.

What a proof needs. A closed list of the forms the frozen part can take (runners resting at every stage of passing a wall, the walls between them, the crystal, the right end), mapped into itself by one cycle, with no two 0-blocks side by side. The eleven rules cover a runner meeting one wall or one resting runner across at least one background block; the contacts at gap 0 and the right end are not listed yet.

What did not work. Closed tape languages built from windows (of cells, of blocks, or of cells plus a summary of the far side) all reach a halting configuration in their abstract runs: a sweep shifts the tape by one cell, which a window cannot see, and a summary of the far side cannot stop two 0-blocks from being guessed next to each other. Random tapes built from the neighbouring particle pairs seen in the run also halt, so the invariant has to remember more than pairs.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. Whether it halts comes down to whether the particles can ever put two 0-blocks (runners fused with 1-walls) side by side where the rightward state reads them. The rules of single collisions are proved; what is missing is a closed description of the frozen part of the tape (see the next claim).

lean/Cycles.lean (generated by py/cycles.py)
Eleven one-cycle particle rules

In block notation (each block 1k0 written as k, Dn for n background blocks 1110): the runner steps right (4 Da 2 Db+1 ⟶ 4 Da+1 2 Db), walls left of it flip 1 ↔ 5 and drift left, the runner passes a 1-wall or a 5-wall in two cycles (through the fused blocks 0 and 4), two runners merge into a 1-wall, and the left end absorbs a wall in three cycles and emits a new runner. Each rule is one full cycle of the head, for every gap size and every tape right of the runner, which the cycle never reads.

The tape is a row of particles

Blocks other than the background are walls (1 and 5, phase jump 2), runners (2, jump 3), and runners fused with a wall (0 and 4). The head turns at the first runner; everything right of it is frozen until a runner arrives. The rules in lean/Cycles.lean are the cycles with one particle ahead of the head across at least one background block; contacts at gap 0, the right end and the whole-tape sweeps are not stated as rules.

A closed language for the frozen part

Non-halting would follow from a list of the forms the frozen part can take, closed under one cycle, with no two 0-blocks side by side. Windowed closed tape languages (cells, blocks, cells plus a summary of the far side) all fail: the abstraction cannot see the one-cell shift a sweep makes, or guesses two 0-blocks side by side.
