1RB0RD_1LC1LB_---1LD_1RA0RE_1RD0LF_1LE0LD

Nobody knows whether this six-state machine halts from blank tape. This page describes what its run looks like at the level of words and counters, what a halt would need, and what is still missing. The description comes from simulation (first look, about an hour of study); nothing beyond the bounded runs is proved.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves no halt in the first 107 steps, the C simulator none in 109. The macro structure described below is observed in the simulation, not proved.

A six-step counter at the left end

At each visit in state F to the left end, the tape reads F>0 0 a^n X Y W (a = 10, P = 110, Q = 100), and (X, Y) runs through (P,P), (Q,P), (P,Q), (Q,Q), (P,aP), (Q,aP), with n going up by 1 at each step and by 2 at the last. In the first 109 steps 981 transitions follow the cycle (164 full cycles) and 4 do not (all at the start, n ≤ 4); the last sample has n = 1151.

Growth shape: fixed/linear

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows in proportion to the step count (the head drifts steadily that way) (fitted exponent 0.92).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

A row of pair counters

At the left-end samples in state E the tape is a long run of 10 pairs, two markers 1^2 0, a second long run, and then shorter runs of pairs separated by markers 1^2 0 or 1 0^2 (observed up to 2·107 steps).

Proposed family: F11, defect gases and irregular zones

This machine stopped being a bouncer early in the run. The last new cell on the left (cell −1,973) was reached before step 6.4·107, and since then the head has stayed near the right end: in the eight successive doublings of the step count from 6.4·107 to 1.6·1010 its greatest distance from the rightmost visited cell was 19,277, 24,350, 25,882, 29,767, 35,564, 39,489, 41,342 and 37,190 cells (c/front.c), while the right end moved from cell 54,852 to cell 6,666,290, in proportion to the steps (about 0.4 cells per 1,000 steps). What is left behind is frozen and does not compress: 24,137, 96,613 and 387,336 level-2 tokens at 109, 4.1·109 and 1.6·1010 steps for 419,103, 1,661,759 and 6,668,265 cells (one token per 17 cells each time). It is runs (10)^k and (01)^k with k mostly below 40, separated by single cells. The first 4,400 cells ((10)^1153 111 (01)^1054 …) are the same at the three records. So the description is not bounded and the tape is not a list that is swept: it is an irregular sea written once by a zone of some tens of thousands of cells at the right end. F11. The zone itself is where a proof would have to work; its depth grew by a factor of about 2 while the tape grew by a factor of about 120.
