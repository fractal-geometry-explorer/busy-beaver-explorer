What is known about 1RB0LD_1RC1RB_1RD0RA_0LE1LF_---1LA_1LA0LC

1RB0LD_1RC1RB_1RD0RA_0LE1LF_---1LA_1LA0LC

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves the two sweeps over the (01) stretches and the situations that lead to a halt (kernel), and no halt in the first 107 steps (native_decide); the C simulator finds no halt in 109 steps.

What it does. At the left end the tape reads (01)k₁ 1 (01)k₂ 1 (01)k₃ …, a list of numbers k, most of them odd (3 is the most common), with rare defects such as 10100. At the right end the same list appears in the words 1110, 1101, 1011, 0111. The sweeps translate between the two forms; the numbers near the left end change irregularly from trip to trip (stretches merge and split), while the list further right is carried along almost unchanged.

Where a halt would come from. Only E reading a 0 halts; E is entered from D reading a 0, so 0 D>0 halts. In 2·108 steps E always reads a 1.

E never reads a 0 in 2·108 steps

In the first 2·108 steps the machine is in state E 17,398,306 times, every time on a 1: the halting transition E0 is never close to firing in a way the run shows.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: sqrt/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.499). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.502).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Proposed family: F11, defect gases and irregular zones

The number of pieces is proportional to the length of the tape at every scale: 389, 787, 1,572 and 3,217 level-2 tokens at the left-end records at 2.6·108, 109, 4.1·109 and 1.6·1010 steps, for 25,212, 50,578, 100,222 and 201,727 cells (one token per 63 or 64 cells each time). No block holds more than 4% of the tape. Seen from the left end the tape is stretches (10)^k separated by single 1s, and stretches of a second word (1101010)^k between them: two periodic backgrounds with domain walls and point defects, in number proportional to the tape. That is a defect sea (F11), not a list of counters (the stretches have no bounded number and no visible order) and not a bounded description.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The list of numbers k changes at its left end in a way nobody has described; NGramCPS with n ≤ 10 leaves it open.
