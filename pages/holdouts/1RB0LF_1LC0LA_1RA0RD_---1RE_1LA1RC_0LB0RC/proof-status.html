Decided: it never haltsProved in this repository, checked by Lean and by Coq. The ledger below says what each part rests on.

This six-state, two-symbol Turing machine was one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. It does not halt from blank tape: Lean proves it, with a symbolic checker that the kernel proves sound, the round trips evaluated by the kernel and the long runs by compiled code (native_decide). This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

It never halts. Lean proves nonhalting: from blank tape the machine reaches one of 62 tape templates and moves from template to template forever. The 62 round trips are checked by the kernel; the start and the 62 ends of an epoch (2.5 million symbolic moves) by native_decide.

What it does. Each time B reaches the left end the tape reads B> 00 (01)a 000 (01)b 000 …, a list of counts separated by short words. The rightward sweep turns 010101 into 011001 and the leftward sweep turns it back. Each round trip takes 3 from a, while the end of the list runs through one of four periodic patterns (periods 15, 22, 16 and 9 round trips) and b gains 99, 141, 99 or 57 per period. When a is used up, b takes its place and a new, longer epoch begins: the first counts of successive epochs are 4, 12, 20, 38, 68, 122, 240, 484, 1002, 2104, 4375, ….

Why it cannot halt. Only D reading a 0 halts, after C>1 0. Every round trip of every pattern and every way an epoch can end has been checked as a symbolic run with the counts as variables, and each lands on a template again; a potential on the counts shows that an epoch always ends with the second count large enough for those runs.

A second proof assistant checks the same result: H36670.nonhalt : ~ halts tm c0, stated on busycoq's own definitions of a Turing machine and of halting, in tools/coq/busycoq/H36670. Coq 8.18 compiles it and Print Assumptions reports no axioms; the build log is next to the files.

Shift rules

B with a 1 behind it turns each 010101 into 011001 going right (14 steps a copy); C with 110 ahead turns each 011001 back into 010101 going left (22 steps a copy).

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: sqrt/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.497). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.507).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Four regimes, growing epochs

How the templates were found: along the run, the list at the left end settles each epoch into one of four periodic patterns of period 15, 22, 16 or 9 round trips, in which the first count drops by 3 per trip and the second grows by 99, 141, 99 or 57 per period. Concrete simulations from every phase and residue return to a pattern.
