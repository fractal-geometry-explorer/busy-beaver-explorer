The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in F reading 0. F is entered only from C0 (writes 1, moves L): 0 C>0. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

The shape of the run

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.605).

After 109 steps the tape spans 58,407 cells (19 left of the start, 45,037 right), with a density of 1s of 0.733.

What it does

A counter beside a growing block

The tape is a row of blocks of 1s with single 0s between them. At the right end a block of words 111110 grows by two cells per round trip of the head. Left of it is a zone of about a hundred cells that every round trip rewrites.

A round trip starts at the right end. The pass runs left in state D: it crosses the words (each becomes 1110 10), and in the zone it cuts every block of three or more 1s into two, until it meets a block of length 1, where it stops. The head then sweeps back, restoring the words. How deep the passes get behaves like a counter: deeper passes are rarer and rarer.

The machine halts only on 0 0 1 D> 0: the pass finds a block of length 1 with two 0s on its left. Inside the tape the 0s are single (observed), so this needs a pass that gets through the whole zone and finds a lone 1 at the very left end of the tape.

Passes reached the left end at triggers 1, 4, 7, 12, 18, 24, 45, 2384 and 8520 (a trigger is a new cell on the right in state B). At the last one, step 143,858,957, the zone started again at the right end; the block of about 2,820 words it had built stayed behind, between the new zone and the old one. The old zone now moves only when the new one overflows.

Cutting the growing block short should not change the rest of the tape, because Lean proves that crossing it does not depend on its length. That reduction is checked on 20,000 triggers but not proved. The reduced run, followed to trigger 1,073,741,824, has no halt and no new arrival at the left end.

The halting pattern

The pass

Moving left from separator to separator, the pass cuts a block of length k+3 into k+1 and 1 and goes on (pass_split); a block of length 1 stops it (pass_stop). For all lengths and every surrounding tape.

Crossing the block of words does not depend on its length

The pass crosses n words 111110 in 6n steps, in the same state, turning each into 1110 10; the sweep back turns them into 111110 again. For every n and every surrounding tape.

c/reduced.c; README.md
The reduced system

With the growing block cut to 10 words at every trigger (which, by the two crossings, should not change the rest of the tape; checked against the real machine on 20,000 triggers, not proved), the run from trigger 20,000 to 1,073,741,824 has no halt and no arrival at the left end; the deepest pass came within 65 cells of it.

Heuristic
c/zone.c; README.md
The leftmost zone alone

The leftmost zone run by itself, one arriving pass at a time, from the real configuration of trigger 100, reproduces the real arrival of trigger 8520 (same new cells, same blocks afterwards). Continued, this model halts in its tick 4,581,101 after 19 more arrivals. That is not a prediction for the machine: after trigger 8520 the old zone gets a pass only when the new zone lets one through (32 of them by trigger 5,165,364, fewer than 64 by trigger 3·108; the model needs about 4.6 million), and the block between the two zones is being used up, which the model does not describe.

Halting needs the left end

At the snapshots taken the 0s inside the tape are single, so the halting pattern can only form at the leftmost block. Observed, not proved.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. It halts if a pass reaches the leftmost block while that block is a lone 1 with blank tape on its left; nothing known forces or forbids that.
