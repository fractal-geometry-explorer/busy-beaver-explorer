What is known about 1RB0LA_1RC1RA_1RD1RE_1LC---_0RB1LF_1RA0LE

1RB0LA_1RC1RA_1RD1RE_1LC---_0RB1LF_1RA0LE

OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Seen when the head reaches a new cell on the right, the tape is often a list of numbers m written as blocks 1^(4m+2) 0: two more than a multiple of four ones, each followed by a single 0. In the run of 2·1010 steps 57 of the 143 new right cells reached in state D have exactly this form. The numbers range from 0 to about 10 with no evident order, and the last block grows by one 1 at each new right cell (four new cells per visit, in states A, B, C, D). The left end is short and changes little.

The head works in sweeps. Moving right in states A and B (or E, B, C) it turns the alternation 1 0 1 0 1 back into solid blocks; moving left in E and F it turns a solid block into an alternation 1 0 1 0 ..., and A erases runs of 1s. Between sweeps the tape left of the head is a list of blocks 0 1^a of even length (mostly 2 mod 4) and right of the head a list of alternations 1 (0 1)^k separated by gaps 0^(4j+2), with a few irregular cells at the two ends. The halt needs a right-moving A or B to land at the start of a gap of the wrong length: a gap of length 3 in front of A, or 2 in front of B.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The machine halts exactly from C> 0 1 (both directions proved in Lean); the moves before it are fixed, so it halts exactly when, moving right, B meets 0 0 1 or A meets 0 0 0 1 (the runs from these to the halt are proved in Lean; that there is no other way in rests on the table: Lean lists every configuration two steps before a halt, B> 0 0 1 and 0 D> 0, and the second cannot occur because D is entered only from C, which writes a 1 and moves right). Counting every time the head moves right from a 1 onto a 0 over 3·109 steps: A always finds a single 0 or a run of 0s of even length, B a single 0 or a run of odd length (293 million and 145 million times). Halting is a break of this parity law, which nothing has broken; no proof of the law has been found.

The halting context

From C> 0 1 the machine reaches its halting transition (proved step by step, then step_none_iff). The halting transition is D reading a 1. D is entered only from C reading a 0 (C writes 1 and moves right); C is also entered from D reading a 0 and moving left, but D comes only from C, so the cell C then reads is the 1 it wrote two steps before, and it moves on to E. The converse is proved: a configuration halts one step later if and only if it is C> 0 1 (halt_iff_1), so a halting run from blank passes through it (halts_blank_pred_1); halt_pred_2 and halt_pred_3 list every configuration two and three steps before a halt.

Near misses

In the first 109 steps C never had 0 1 under and after it: its contexts are 0 0, 1 0 and 1 1, each about 3.2·107 times. There are no near misses at all at this window, which suggests a local reason (the blocks 1^(4m+2) 0 keep a 0 where C reads a 0).

The gap-parity law

In 3·109 steps, each time the head moved right from a 1 onto a 0 inside the tape: A found a single 0 290,346,873 times, a run of length 2 mod 4 96,765,873 times and 0 mod 4 1,283 times, never 1 or 3 mod 4 (beyond the single 0); B found a single 0 48,384,348 times, a run of length 1 mod 4 96,764,414 times and 3 mod 4 1,632 times, never an even run. A gap of 3 before A or 2 before B halts. (Runs of 63 or more cells are lumped and not in these counts.)

c/gaps.c: ./gaps M 3000000000

No small finite-automata certificate

A finite-automata-reduction search (a DFA of n states on the left part of the tape, the least co-closed NFA on the rest) finds no certificate for any DFA of at most 7 states, with the DFA on the left part or on the right part. A search with closed tape languages whose automata are a hand-written grammar for each side (the blocks and the gaps above, lengths mod 4) times a window of up to 8 cells did not close either: the irregular cells at the two ends of the tape escape the grammar.

c/far.c: ./far M 7, ./far M 7 m

Growth shape: exp/exp

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.116). Right of the start it grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.113).

The shape of the tape

Sampled at every 1000th turn of state E on a 0 in the first 3·108 steps, the tape left of the head is a list of blocks 0 1^a with a of length 2 mod 4 or (less often) 0 mod 4, the i-th block often of length exactly 4i (the first 40 kinds of exceptions to 2 mod 4 were all multiples of 4); right of the head a list of alternations 1 (0 1)^k separated by gaps 0^(4j+2), except near the head and at the right end (a lone 1 and a gap of any length).

Does it halt from blank tape?

Is the gap-parity law an invariant? A proof would be a closed language that keeps the gap lengths mod 4 and the arrival state together; it must also cover the two ends of the tape, which do not follow the pattern of the middle.
