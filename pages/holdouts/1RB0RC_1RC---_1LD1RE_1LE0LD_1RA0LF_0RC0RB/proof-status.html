What is known about 1RB0RC_1RC---_1LD1RE_1LE0LD_1RA0LF_0RC0RB

1RB0RC_1RC---_1LD1RE_1LE0LD_1RA0LF_0RC0RB

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Sampled with the head in E at the left end, the tape is 1^X c 0000 (10)^s1 110 (10)^s2 110 …: a number X and a stack. Two rules of the map are clean (observed without exception after the first few samples); the folding of a non-empty stack is not decoded. Shift rules are proved in Lean.

Other proofs and outside reports

An outside proof is waiting for review: it never halts

busycoq pull request 55 (Alk, 2026-11-03) contains a Rocq proof that this machine never halts (verify/M_1RB0RC_1RCHHH_1LD1RE_1LE0LD_1RA0LF_0RC0RB.v). As of 12 November 2026 it was open: not merged, not reviewed by the maintainer, and not compiled here unless the ledger below says so. The verdict on this page does not rely on it.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.072). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.496).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

The stack map

At the 69 samples to 2.9·1010 steps (head in E on a 0 at the left end, c = 1110, a = 10, b = 110) the tape is 1^X c, then either nothing (empty stack) or 0000 a^s1 b a^s2 b …. Empty stack: X ≡ 0 (mod 4) gives X' = 3X/2 + 2, empty (1424 → 2138, 4240 → 6362); X ≡ 2 gives X' = X/2 + 3 with stack [(X−2)/4] (76,954 → 38,480, [19,238]); 20 of 20 cases from X = 18 on. Non-empty stack, X ≡ 0 (mod 4): X' = X/2, push X/4, top minus 1 (1072 [534] → 536 [268, 533] → 268 [134, 267, 533] → 134 [67, 133, 267, 533]); every case. Non-empty stack, X ≡ 2: the stack is folded back into a larger X (for one entry, X' = X + 3s + 5: 12,118 [6,057] → 30,294), not decoded in general.

tools/c/tokrun.c

Where a halt would come from

The halt is B reading a 1. B is entered from F reading a 1 (about 125 million times in 109 steps) or from A reading a 0; in all of them B read a 0.
