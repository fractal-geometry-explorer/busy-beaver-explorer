It halts, proved in Coq (theorem halt : halts tm c0 on busycoq's definitions, no axioms). Mirrored and with its states renamed, its table is 1RB1RA_1LC0RD_1LD1LB_1RA0LE_0LB0LF_0RA--- started in state C, one transition away from the table DeepMind proved halting in busycoq PR 93. DeepMind's fast engine, changed for that transition as for 1RB0LC_1RC1RA_1LD0RE_1LA1LD_0RA0RF_0LD--- (the same table started in B), reaches a halting configuration from state C after 427,055 engine steps, on a tape of about 24360 cells.

What it does. Before the halt the machine runs a layered counter: a growing prefix (110)^k is turned, at each reset, into runs of groups of sizes 4, 7, 10, 13, … with large multiplicities, which the following sweeps use up. That description comes from simulation; the halt does not depend on it.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.5). Right of the start it grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.1).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Prefix, groups and resets

At each visit of the left end in state B the tape is B> 00 (110)^k (10)^a₁ 1 (10)^a₂ 1 …. Between resets k grows by 2 a visit and the sweeps merge the groups into runs of sizes 4, 7, 10, 13, …; at a reset the prefix becomes about k/2 groups of size 4. Large and small resets alternate (k = 909, 37, 1670, 220, 2591, 397, 3185 between steps 3.6·107 and 5.3·108). Observed in simulation, not proved.

c/sim.c, c/resets.py

No halt in the first 1.48×1014 steps of an accelerated run

Observed, not proved: the accelerated simulator rsim.py ran 1.48×1014 steps from blank tape without reaching a halting transition. Its tape, state and head position were equal to those of a plain C simulator at 6 checkpoints, the last at 1.00×1010 steps. Of these steps, the part after the last checkpoint rests on the simulator alone, including 12,476 trip-level jumps: a jump applies many identical round trips at once after seeing the same tape shape recur with constant count changes, which is validated against direct simulation but not proved.
