What is known about 1RB1LE_1LC0LF_1LA1RD_1LB0RD_0LC0LA_---1RA

1RB1LE_1LC0LF_1LA1RD_1LB0RD_0LC0LA_---1RA

OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

It is a binary counter in disguise. At the left end a solid block of 1s grows by four cells per overflow, and each overflow comes about twice as many steps after the previous one. The rest of the tape is a word of blocks 1^k separated by 110 and runs of 10, which changes slowly. Around step 3.8·108 the word was used up and the run restarted with a longer one.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves no halt in the first 10,000 steps by the kernel alone and in the first 107 with native_decide; the C simulator finds no halt in the first 1010 steps.

Where a halt would come from. The machine halts only in state F reading 0, and F is entered only from B reading 1 and stepping left (0LF). In the first 108 steps that happens 11,363,688 times and F always reads a 1.

What a proof needs. An exact description of one overflow and of the epoch change.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Shift-overflow counter. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

The halting configurations

The machine stops exactly when it reads the symbol of a halting transition (F0); the generated statement covers every configuration.

step_none_iff, lean/Blank.lean

No halt in the first 10,000 steps

The list simulator of the shared library, proved to agree with the machine's semantics, runs 10,000 steps from blank tape without a halt, evaluated by Lean's kernel.

blank_runs_kernel, lean/Blank.lean

A run-length frame map, every case proved

At every head turn write the tape as two lists of runs (blocks of equal cells), left and right of the head: a frame with list parameters. A table of 165 cases, each a pattern on the first few runs of both sides (lengths exact or at least 4, the rest of each side any list), sends each frame to the frame of the next head turn; every case is proved for all values of its parameters and all lists by one symbolic run (RLCase.sound), so the frame map next = rlNext cases is sound (rlNext_sound). Between two head turns this machine crosses only a few run boundaries, which is why a finite table covers a tape whose number of runs grows without bound.

An epoch change

Width: 222 cells at 107 steps, 238 at 108, 419 at 109, 435 at 1010. The jump comes from a restart between steps 3.78·108 and 5.14·108, when the left block is rebuilt and the tape grows by about 150 cells.

The case table covers the run

Every frame at a head turn in the first 3·106 steps (1,227,376 head turns) is matched by a case, and the case's next frame is the machine's (no mismatch, no unmatched frame); 161 cases at 106 steps, 165 at 3·106, so a longer run may need more.

py/frames.py fit

heuristic Evidence only

No finite frame map with scalar parameters

The frame-map method (stop at head turns, match the tape against finitely many templates with block-length parameters, fit affine cases) needs the tapes at head turns to fall into a bounded number of shapes. Here they do not: tokenizing the tape at every 7th head turn into maximal powers of words of length at most 4, the first 107 steps give 316,491 distinct shapes (exponents of 3 and more merged), 286,341 of them new in the last decade, and up to 52 tokens per tape; the number grows tenfold per decade of steps. For comparison the same count saturates at 32 shapes of at most 5 tokens for 1RB1LC_1LC1RD_1LA0LB_0RB1RE_1RF0RA_---0RC and at 89 for 1RB1RF_1LC1LF_0RE1LD_0LB1LD_---1RC_1RA0RD, where frame maps were proved. The cause is the counter itself: its digits form an arbitrary word, and the word of higher digits on the right grows too, so a frame needs a list parameter; the run-length frames above are such frames.

py/shapes.py

Growth shape: fixed/power

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like a power of the step count other than 1/2 (fitted exponent 0.133). Right of the start it stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half).
