What is known about 1RB1LD_1RC0LE_1LA1RE_0LF1LA_1RB0RB_---0LB

1RB1LD_1RC0LE_1LA1RE_0LF1LA_1RB0RB_---0LB

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. After 1.1·106 steps the left end of the tape never moves again. The tape is a fixed left part, a reserve block (011)^k, a counter, and a block of ones at the right end. Each tick a hole runs across the ones and back, and the counter changes near its right end. The counter grows only like the logarithm of the time, and it grows by eating the reserve from the right (k = 481, 476, 472, 468 at 7·106, 4·107, 2·108, 109 steps). The two sweeps are proved in Lean. What happens when the reserve runs out is far beyond simulation.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Shift-overflow bouncer counter. Its 3 November 2026 edition lists the machine as a cryptid: its halting is thought to come down to a Collatz-like question that no known method decides. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

The sweeps across the ones

Going right, a hole 00 moves across (111)^n, three cells every five steps (hole). Going left, the head runs across the ones in states D and A, one cell a step (run_left). Both hold for every n and every tape around.

The left end is fixed and the counter eats the reserve

In the first 109 steps, the leftmost cell visited is last reached at step 1,072,561. From then on, the 1,621 cells left of the block of ones read: a fixed part, the reserve (011)^k, then the counter. The reserve has k = 481, 476, 474, 472, 470 at steps 7.4·106, 3.7·107, 9.1·107, 1.7·108, 4.0·108, and 468 at 109. Over the same times the counter has 94, 109, 115, 121, 127 cells.

A counter that carries

Over 5,240 ticks in the first 4·108 steps, how far back from the ones a tick changes the tape falls off roughly geometrically. 2,618 ticks change at most 10 cells, 219 reach 30–39 cells back, 19 reach 80–89, and 2 reach 120–129.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: fixed/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.454).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

When the reserve runs out

The counter takes about 2 units of the reserve each time the time grows by a factor of about 2.4. At that rate the remaining 468 units last until about 1090 steps. Only then does the counter reach the fixed left part, where something new must happen. The machine halts only when D reads 0 and the cell to its left is also 0 (F reading 0 is its only undefined transition). Before then, the counter's digits would have to produce the pattern, and their rules are not worked out.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The open part is the counter's carry rules, and what happens when the reserve block is used up.
