The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in C reading 1. C is entered only from B0 (writes 1, moves R): B>0 1. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

What it does

A counter and a garden

At a left turn the tape reads 0 1^n 0 1^a 0 1^b 0 … with n = 3 mod 4 and a, b, … odd. The big block grows by 4 to 20 cells at a time and stays about half of the tape.

The sweeps

Rules proved in Lean

rulefromtowhat it does
f_onesL 1 F> (1)^n RL (1)^n 1 F> RRightward: F crosses a block of ones (3 steps a cell).
d_onesL (1)^n D> 1 RL D> 1 (1)^n RLeftward: D crosses a block of ones.
f_01L F> (01)^n RL (00)^n F> RRightward: F erases the ones of a run of 01.
d_1110L (1110)^n D> 1 RL D> 1 (1101)^n RLeftward: D turns each 1110 into 1101.
f_0111L 0 F> (0111)^n RL (1110)^n 0 F> RRightward: F, a 0 behind it, turns each 0111 into 1110.
Can it halt?

Where a halt would come from

It halts from B>0 1: B reads a 0 about 29 million times in 109 steps, always with a 0 on its right. No proof that this always holds is known.

Where a halt can come from

If the machine halts on its third step from a configuration, that configuration is one of: A with 0 under the head, 0 right, 1 two right. So a run can reach a halt only through one of these patterns, and a proof that the machine never halts only has to rule them out. The proof splits on the state and the five cells around the head.

The sweep rules

Shift rules of the two sweeps, for every repeat count and any tape around: F crosses ones going right (3 steps a cell) and D going left (1 step a cell), F erases the ones of (01)^n, D turns each 1110 into 1101 and F each 0111 into 1110.

The left block

At every left turn the tape starts 0 1^n 0 with n = 3 mod 4 (3, 7, 11, 15, 23, …, 1,231 at 2.9·106 steps); n grows by 4 to 20 at some round trips and stays the same at others.

Odd blocks

Right of the big block, the blocks of ones at a left turn have odd length (at 2.9·106 steps: 148 of length 1, 52 of 3, 38 of 5, 16 of 7, …, and one of 6, the only even one).

The halt needs B>0 1

The only halting transition C1 is reached only from B>0 1. In the first 109 steps B reads a 0 29,488,351 times, never with a 1 on its right.

Open
Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. How the right region feeds the counter (each growth of n by 4k), and whether its rewriting can put a 1 right of a B on a 0, are not understood.
