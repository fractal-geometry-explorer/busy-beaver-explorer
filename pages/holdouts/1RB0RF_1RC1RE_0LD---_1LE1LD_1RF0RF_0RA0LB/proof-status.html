OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. At a left turn the tape is 0 1^n 0 followed by blocks of ones of odd length (1, 3, 5, …) separated by single 0s. The block 1^n is always 3 mod 4 and grows by multiples of 4; it is half of the tape (1,231 of 2,299 cells at 2.9·106 steps). The odd blocks on the right are rewritten every round trip, without visible order. Five shift rules of the sweeps are proved in Lean.

Lean proves no halt in the first 107 steps (10,000 by the kernel alone); the C simulator finds none in 109.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Spaghetti. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

How it works

The left half of the tape is a single block of ones, a unary counter: its length is always 3 mod 4 and it grows by 4 to 20 cells at some round trips. The right half is blocks of ones of odd length separated by single 0s, rewritten every round trip.

The sweeps cross the counter quickly (rules proved in Lean); all the irregular work happens on the right. A halt needs B on a 0 with a 1 to its right, which has not happened in 109 steps.

The halting configurations

The machine stops exactly when it reads the symbol of a halting transition (C1); the generated statement covers every configuration.

Where a halt can come from

If the machine halts on its third step from a configuration, that configuration is one of: A with 0 under the head, 0 right, 1 two right. So a run can reach a halt only through one of these patterns, and a proof that the machine never halts only has to rule them out. The proof splits on the state and the five cells around the head.

halt_route, lean/Route.lean (written by py/route.py)

The sweep rules

Shift rules of the two sweeps, for every repeat count and any tape around: F crosses ones going right (3 steps a cell) and D going left (1 step a cell), F erases the ones of (01)^n, D turns each 1110 into 1101 and F each 0111 into 1110.

f_ones, d_ones, f_01, d_1110, f_0111, lean/Rules.lean (generated by py/rules.py)

The halt needs B>0 1

The only halting transition C1 is reached only from B>0 1. In the first 109 steps B reads a 0 29,488,351 times, never with a 1 on its right.

Regular-language deciders tried (F11)

None of these closes: each reaches a halting configuration in its abstraction, which says nothing about whether the machine halts. n-gram CPS (tools/c/ngramcps.c), equal widths on both sides: a halting local configuration is reached with plain up to width 16, history 1 up to width 15, history 2 up to width 15, history 3 up to width 15, LRU history up to width 7 (larger widths ran out of the 150 s or 60 million configuration budget). n-gram CPS with every cell tagged by its position mod p: fails for p = 2 up to width 12, p = 3 up to width 12, p = 4 up to width 12, p = 6 up to width 12. n-gram CPS where each side also carries the state of a small automaton that has read that side from the far end (every automaton with up to 3 states on both sides, or 4 states on one side), with position mod 1..4 and widths 4, 6, 8: no closed set without a halt in all 120 parameter sets. Run-length windows (the K nearest runs of equal macro cells on each side, run lengths kept exactly below T and mod M above): fails for all 72 parameter sets (cells of 1 to 4 bits, K = 2, 4, 6, T = 2, 4, M = 1, 2, 4). Two-automaton closed tape language by SAT (a deterministic automaton reading each side inward, n states each, CaDiCaL): no solution for n = 2..8; n = 9 not finished in 150 s.

sweeps of 13 November 2026 (family F11); the tagged and run-length deciders are not in the repository yet

Growth shape: sqrt/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.5). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.501).

The left block

At every left turn the tape starts 0 1^n 0 with n = 3 mod 4 (3, 7, 11, 15, 23, …, 1,231 at 2.9·106 steps); n grows by 4 to 20 at some round trips and stays the same at others.

Odd blocks

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. How the right region feeds the counter (each growth of n by 4k), and whether its rewriting can put a 1 right of a B on a 0, are not understood.
