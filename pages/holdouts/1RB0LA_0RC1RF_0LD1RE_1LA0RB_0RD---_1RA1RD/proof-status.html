1RB0LA_0RC1RF_0LD1RE_1LA0RB_0RD---_1RA1RD

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

The tape is a list of numbers written in two ways. When the head is at the right end, they are runs of 1s separated by single 0s (1^4 0 1^8 0 1^5 0 1^23 0 …); when it is at the left end, the same numbers are gaps of 0s between single 1s (1 0^4 1 0^8 1 0^5 1 0^23 …). State A, running left over a run of 1s, erases it (Lean: (1)^n A> 1 becomes A> 1 (0)^n), and the sweep right writes the runs back. The left end is a run A>0 0 (10)^n lengthened by one at each visit (45 to 48 between steps 7.4·108 and 1.5·109); the right end is one run of 1s lengthened by one at each new cell on the right (34 to 38 in the same stretch). In between, the list changes slowly and irregularly.

Status as of 12 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The machine can halt only from B> 0 1 1 (proved in Lean); in 20,000,000,000 steps that never happens. In the first 109 steps B had 1 0 1 ahead 25 times and 1 1 0 10 times (the last at step 740,088,686, just after a visit to the left end), and never 0 1 1.

The halting context

From B> 0 1 1 the machine reaches its halting transition (proved step by step, then step_none_iff). The halting transition is E reading a 1. E is entered only from C reading a 1 (C writes 1 and moves right), and C only from B reading a 0 (B writes 0 and moves right).

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/power

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.074). Right of the start it grows like a power of the step count other than 1/2 (fitted exponent 0.125).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Growth

The left run of 10 and the right run of 1s each gain one copy per visit of the head to that end, and the visits come at exponentially spaced times.
