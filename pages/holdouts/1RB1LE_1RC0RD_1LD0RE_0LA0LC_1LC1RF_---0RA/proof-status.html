What is known about 1RB1LE_1RC0RD_1LD0RE_0LA0LC_1LC1RF_---0RA

1RB1LE_1RC0RD_1LD0RE_0LA0LC_1LC1RF_---0RA

OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

This machine is 1RB0RC_1LC0RE_0LD0LB_1RA1LE_1LB1RF_---0RD with its states renamed (A here is D there, B is A, C is B, D is C) and so a different start state. Both runs settle into the same binary counter: digits written as 00 and 11, an overflow at the left end whose time doubles each time, and a marker on the right whose arrival at the end of its track restarts the counter in a new epoch. The twin's page has the full description.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves no halt in the first 10,000 steps by the kernel alone and in the first 107 with native_decide; the C simulator finds no halt in the first 109 steps.

Where a halt would come from. The machine halts only in state F reading 0; F is entered only from E reading 1 and stepping right. In the first 108 steps F is entered 9,523,774 times and always reads a 1.

What a proof needs. The same as for the twin: a symbolic description of a counter increment and of the change from one epoch to the next. A proof for one machine should carry over to the other.

No halt in the first 10,000 steps

The list simulator of the shared library, proved to agree with the machine's semantics, runs 10,000 steps from blank tape without a halt, evaluated by Lean's kernel.

blank_runs_kernel, lean/Blank.lean

A run-length frame map, every case proved

At every head turn write the tape as two lists of runs (blocks of equal cells), left and right of the head: a frame with list parameters. A table of 68 cases, each a pattern on the first few runs of both sides (lengths exact or at least 4, the rest of each side any list), sends each frame to the frame of the next head turn; every case is proved for all values of its parameters and all lists by one symbolic run (RLCase.sound), so the frame map next = rlNext cases is sound (rlNext_sound). Between two head turns this machine crosses only a few run boundaries, which is why a finite table covers a tape whose number of runs grows without bound.

tools/c/tmrun.c (at A L)

Overflows double, epochs

At the left-end events the tape starts A>0 0 1^(2n+1), n growing by one per event and the gaps between events doubling; the counter restarts (a block 1 at the left end) at steps 8, 37, 206, 809, 3,782, 17,297, 22,706,588 and 81,579,887 in the first 108 steps. Read as digit strings, the events follow the twin's (marker walking right, restart when it reaches the right end; the restart's rewriting of the string is not understood for either machine).

heuristic Evidence only

No finite frame map with scalar parameters

The frame-map method (stop at head turns, match the tape against finitely many templates with block-length parameters, fit affine cases) needs the tapes at head turns to fall into a bounded number of shapes. Here they do not: tokenizing the tape at every 7th head turn into maximal powers of words of length at most 4, the first 107 steps give 455,393 distinct shapes (exponents of 3 and more merged), 410,066 of them new in the last decade, and up to 36 tokens per tape; the number grows tenfold per decade of steps. For comparison the same count saturates at 32 shapes of at most 5 tokens for 1RB1LC_1LC1RD_1LA0LB_0RB1RE_1RF0RA_---0RC and at 89 for 1RB1RF_1LC1LF_0RE1LD_0LB1LD_---1RC_1RA0RD, where frame maps were proved. The cause is the counter itself: its digits form an arbitrary word, and the word of higher digits on the right grows too, so a frame needs a list parameter; the run-length frames above are such frames.

py/shapes.py

Growth shape: fixed/power

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like a power of the step count other than 1/2 (fitted exponent 0.155). Right of the start it stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half).
