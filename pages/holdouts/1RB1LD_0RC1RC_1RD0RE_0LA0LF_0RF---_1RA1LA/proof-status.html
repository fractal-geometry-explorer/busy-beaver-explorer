What is known about 1RB1LD_0RC1RC_1RD0RE_0LA0LF_0RF---_1RA1LA

1RB1LD_0RC1RC_1RD0RE_0LA0LF_0RF---_1RA1LA

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 13 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The machine's two bulk phases are proved in Lean for every length (growth_phase, stall_phase in lean/Phase.lean): they cannot halt. What is left is the short zone at the left end of the tape, which the head rewrites at the end of every phase; it is the only place a halt could happen, and its rule (as a function of the residue of the tape length) has not been worked out. Direct simulation follows 32 phases (1.5·1010 steps, length 104,205) without a halt.

A closed-set search with all remainders of the length allowed (13 November) found no halting class but did not close: the zone is not a finite engine.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Bell-like. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

Rounds: growth then stall, length × about 3/2

In a direct C simulation to 1.5·1010 steps the run is a strict alternation of growth phases and stall phases (32 phases); at the start of each round the tape is a zone of under 50 cells followed by (110)k, and the length goes 252, 372, 546, 817, 1221, 1824, 2731, 4087, 6114, 9162, 13727, 20592, 30881, 46319, 69474, 104203 at the stall-phase starts: L′ = 3L/2 minus a few cells taken by the zone.

No remainder class halts (sampled)

From the start of a growth phase, 0∞ Z (110)n 01 A>, the next zone and length depend on the zone Z and on n mod 8, with n' = (3n + k)/2. Following every residue from 1,513 zones, the C simulator found no halt. The set of zones did not close: zones contain inner segments (110)k that are rescaled every round, so they grow without bound once all residues are allowed.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.048). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.47).

The zone and the halting question

A halt needs the right-moving zigzag (states A, B, C, E, F) to find 1 1 where the 3-periodic tape has its 0; in the body the proved rules exclude it. The zone at the left end (a few walls such as 1110 and runs of ones, rewritten at each phase end, depending on how the front arrives, i.e. on the residue of the length) is not decoded, so whether it can ever present that pattern is open.
