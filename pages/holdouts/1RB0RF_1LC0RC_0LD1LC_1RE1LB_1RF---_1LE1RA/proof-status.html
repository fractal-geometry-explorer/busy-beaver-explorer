What is known about 1RB0RF_1LC0RC_0LD1LC_1RE1LB_1RF---_1LE1RA

1RB0RF_1LC0RC_0LD1LC_1RE1LB_1RF---_1LE1RA

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 12 November 2026.

In short

Open, partly decoded. At the left end of the tape the machine runs a doubling chain: a block of x ones becomes 2x+12 ones each time an item 1^7 0 follows it, by a transfer that moves the ones two at a time into copies of 10 (both proved in Lean). The chains make the giant blocks of ones and take nearly all the running time. What the sweeps do to the rest of the word, and so how long each chain gets, is not decoded; no halt in 7.3·1012 steps.

Other proofs and outside reports

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Chaotic Ack-like Bell. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

The transfer at the left end

Write G for the left end of the tape 0^inf 1 1 1 0 1 0 1 1 0 with the head in C on a 0. Then G (10)^(2j+1) 1^3 R becomes G (10)^(2j+3) 1 R in 28j+64 steps, for every j and every tape R to the right: two ones become two more copies of 10, and the tape grows by two cells on the left. Repeated n times it costs n(28j+14n+50) steps.

transfer (lean/Rules.lean, generated by lean/rules.py), transfer_n, cost_eq (lean/Transfer.lean)

The doubling chain

G 10 1^x (10)^3 1^5 0 1^7 0 R with x = 2n+2 becomes G 10 1^(2x+12) (10)^3 1^5 0 R: the transfer empties the block of ones, one more pass turns the 2n+1 copies of 10 into 4n+16 ones, and the item 1^7 0 is used up. With c items in a row the block grows to 2c(x+12)−12 ones.

double7 (lean/Rules.lean), chain7, chain, dbl_eq (lean/Transfer.lean)

Three giant blocks of ones, to 3·109 steps

Run from blank tape, the tape at a full sweep is a word over the tokens 1 and a = 10. Three times so far one token run dominates: a block of 102 ones at step 11,869 (tape 144 cells), 1,730 ones at step 3,472,160 (1,918 cells) and 49,142 ones at step 2,820,772,373 (52,484 cells). Each comes after a long stretch with no full sweep, and is followed by sweeps that rewrite it as a^k and then into periodic words ((1^2 a)^k, (1^4 a^2)^k, (1^6 a)^k, …). No halt.

Does it halt from blank tape?

The chain at the left end is understood, but its length is set by how many items 1^7 0 stand in a row after the block, and these are made by the sweeps over the rest of the word, which act like a transducer that has not been decoded. Halting needs the head in D on a 0 followed by 1 or by 0 0; whether the word can ever present that is not known.
