OpenNobody has proved whether this machine halts. Everything below is what is known so far.

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 20 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. At each new cell on the right the tape is 2 3^16 5 3 5 3^a1 5 3^a2 ... 5 3^am TAIL (lengths of runs of 1s), and the counts ai follow a closed-form law Pn → Qn → Rn → Pn+1, checked against direct simulation on every event to 109 steps. An exact model of the whole tape as one ternary counter follows the machine through the first collapse (2.5×1018 steps) and the middle collapse at 2.06×1035; a lean accelerator (17 November 2026) carries it on through a second middle collapse at 2.58×1054 steps and the collapse of the third regime's oldest segment (2.8×1054 to 3.0×1054; the end counter goes 126 → 127), and on (18 November) through about 87 more middle collapses and the end counter running out at 4.8×1054 (124 blocks, merged into the oldest segment (290 → 414)) to 6.1×10135 steps, with no halt. The oldest segment now holds 241 blocks.

16 shift rules

For every n, the head crosses n copies of a short word in a fixed number of steps per copy and leaves another word behind (for example A going left over 0111 leaves 0100, 8 steps a copy; these are the moves that cross the 111-blocks of the counter). Generated and proved by bbtools.symtm, checked by Lean's kernel.

The generic counter sweep, for every number of carry digits

A sweep whose carry passes any number of T digits and stops on a Z or an O digit, proved for every list of carry digits and all run lengths: the T digits become Z digits with the same runs, the top one loses 1 block (Z stop) or 2 (O stop), the stop digit goes up by one, the end run gains 2 blocks, in 12 steps per block and 16 per carry digit plus 12 per end-run block and 20 (Z) or 18 (O). The pieces (start, crossing one T digit, the stop, crossing back, end) are proved by the symbolic prover, and the chain by induction in Lean.

The general event law and the first collapse, reproduced (16 November 2026)

At right-end D/F records the list [g, y1..ys, c] follows birth, drain, drain: birth [g,y,c] → [2·3s+4−28, g−3s+1+1, yi−3j+1, c], drain [g+2·3s+3, yi−3j+1, c−1] (j = segments to the right). The collapse at P14, simulated exactly on reduced tapes with 4, 5 and 6 kept segments and spliced into the full list (newborn entries fitted as A·3s+B, hidden segments drained by 10 events), gives exactly the real list at step 16,989,897,072,297,977,885, and all later records of the exact run match the law continued from it (end counter within 2). The record simulator agrees with direct simulation on all 49 records to 2×108 steps.

The whole tape as one exact counter (16 November 2026)

Read at the left end, where the head bounces between right-end records, the tape is one ternary counter: runs of blocks 1000 (mirrored) separated by walls Z, O, T (digits 0, 1, 2) and a few other walls near births and collapses. Each bounce is a symbolic run of the machine on a window holding the carry (cdigits.py); every sample of the direct run to 107 steps (12,348 sweeps; 4,655 to 2×106 for the twin) equals the model. Counter macros (tacc.py) stand for exactly the sweeps they replace, step counts included: checked against plain sweeps on 5.07 million sweeps.

No halt in the first 3.09×1054 steps, through two more collapses

A lean accelerator (py/lean.py) treats the tape as one ternary counter: a carry turns the T digits it passes into Z and takes 1 or 2 blocks from the top one, and the low end of the tape below a big digit repeats exactly (14,976 sweeps per period in the third regime, 4,257,468 after 2.58×1054 steps). Whole stretches of periods are then added in closed form, step counts included; every other sweep is run exactly. It reproduces all 206 logged states of the counter-macro run to 8.2×1051 steps (and 482 of the twin's), and its jumps equal plain sweeps wherever compared. The run passes a middle collapse at 2.58×1054 steps and the collapse of the oldest segment (it reaches 0 at about 2.76×1054, its carry reaches the far end at 2.82×1054, and the empty digit is absorbed into the end counter at about 3.02×1054) without halting, and goes on to 3.09×1054 steps.

The sweep rules form a short table

Every exact sweep rule the long runs derive (about 295 over a cycle, the same table for both machines) is a stop rule plus a carry chain whose changes and step cost do not depend on its length. Besides the generic Z and O stops there are about 20 stop rules: 3 borrows when the top carry digit has 0 or 1 blocks (they start odd walls), the cascades of odd walls that merge back into the counter, and the end rule that adds a digit at the bottom.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.512). Right of the start it grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.028).

The law only looks at a segment's distance from the right end, so the right end of P13 was simulated beforehand from a shortened tape with 3, 4 or 5 kept segments. The same shortened runs say that in the second regime the oldest segment (321 blocks) loses about 9 per cycle, so the next collapse would be some 36 generations later, near 1046 steps. Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. Halting needs the head in state D on a 0 with 1 1 to its right (D0 → 1RE, E1 → 1RF, F1 halts). The law never produces it, and neither did the first collapse, the middle collapses at 2.06×1035 and 2.58×1054 steps, or the oldest-segment collapse at 2.8–3.0×1054 (exact runs). Nor did the end counter running out at 4.8×1054 or about 87 further middle collapses to 6.1×10135 steps. The oldest segment holds 241 blocks and loses 2 per collapse, the second 873 and loses 9; at the current rate the second runs out first, near 10228 steps (a fit).
