Does this machine ever halt?

1RB0RC_1LC1RE_1RA1LD_0LC0LA_1RF1RD_---0RD

Nobody knows. Its tape is an alternating block and a solid block whose lengths trade back and forth; each pass rewrites one block into the other.

The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in F reading 0. F is entered only from E0 (writes 1, moves R): E>0 0. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

The diagram stacks the tape at many moments. Sampling at each new cell shows the shape of the run as the visited region grows; sampling evenly in time shows where the head spends its time. The records list every step at which the head reaches a cell no earlier step reached, with the side, the width so far and the whole tape: the first place to look for a repeating macro structure.

Macro structure

What the machine does

Stop the machine whenever its head first reaches a new cell. With a for 10, around step 9.8·107:

  • step 97,915,409: D>0 a^2 1 a^802 1^338 (left end)
  • step 97,921,865: A>0^2 a^806 1^336 (left end)
  • step 98,376,215: 1^2 a 1^604 a^672 C>0 (right end)
  • step 98,837,298: A>0^2 a^540 1^874 (left end)
  • step 99,362,025: C>0^2 a^722 1^513 (left end)

The tape is two long blocks, one alternating and one solid, and at successive moments their lengths trade: 806 copies of 10 and 336 ones, then 604 ones and 672 copies of 10, then 540 and 874, then 722 and 513. Rewriting a block of length x costs about x passes of length x, so the width grows like the cube root of the step count. The shape is the one of the three machines 1RB1RD_0LC1RA_…_1RF0LD_---0RE, where one block doubles at the other's expense; the exact rules here have not been worked out.

The halting transition

Where a halt would have to come from

The halting transition is F reading a 0. F is entered only from E reading a 0 (1RF), so a halt needs E>0 0. In the first 108 steps F was entered 83,257 times, every time on a 1, and 82,807 of those times at least 7 cells from the left end.

Whether the trading ever leaves E in front of two 0s is open.

The map was found by fitting the machine's runs (py/framemap.py) and collapsing the fit into cases (py/pieces.py); Lean then checks every case for all sizes. It is undefined only at .z 1 1 1, .w l 0 1, .w l 1 0, .W l 0 1, .W l 1 0, and those halt. So the open question is exactly whether the orbit of Ph.z 0 0 2 reaches one of them; the first 108 frames do not.

Kernel
lean/Phases.lean, lean/Pieces.lean
The whole frame map

next in lean/Phases.lean sends each of the 8 frames to the next one the machine reaches, in 78 cases; next_sound proves every case for all parameters, each from a symbolic run in lean/Pieces.lean (generated by py/pieces.py) glued by tape_eq. Where next is undefined (.z 1 1 1, .w l 0 1, .w l 1 0, .W l 0 1, .W l 1 0) the machine halts (none_halts).
