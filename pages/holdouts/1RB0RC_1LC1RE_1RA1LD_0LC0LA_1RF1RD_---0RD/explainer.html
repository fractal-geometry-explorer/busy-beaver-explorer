The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in F reading 0. F is entered only from E0 (writes 1, moves R): E>0 0. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

Macro structure

What the machine does

Stop the machine whenever its head first reaches a new cell. With a for 10, around step 9.8·107:

  • step 97,915,409: D>0 a^2 1 a^802 1^338 (left end)
  • step 97,921,865: A>0^2 a^806 1^336 (left end)
  • step 98,376,215: 1^2 a 1^604 a^672 C>0 (right end)
  • step 98,837,298: A>0^2 a^540 1^874 (left end)
  • step 99,362,025: C>0^2 a^722 1^513 (left end)

The tape is two long blocks, one alternating and one solid, and at successive moments their lengths trade: 806 copies of 10 and 336 ones, then 604 ones and 672 copies of 10, then 540 and 874, then 722 and 513. Rewriting a block of length x costs about x passes of length x, so the width grows like the cube root of the step count. The shape is the one of the three machines 1RB1RD_0LC1RA_…_1RF0LD_---0RE, where one block doubles at the other's expense. The exact rules are the frame map below; the epochs they make, a growth phase where one block grows by 4/3 a pass and a drain phase where the other grows by 4, are in Whole sweeps as one jump.

The halting transition

Where a halt would have to come from

The halting transition is F reading a 0. F is entered only from E reading a 0 (1RF), so a halt needs E>0 0. In the first 108 steps F was entered 83,257 times, every time on a 1, and 82,807 of those times at least 7 cells from the left end.

At the level of the frame map, a halt needs a zigzag to start exactly on one of five rows; see The halting rows and the close calls. Whether the run ever lands on one is open.

The recursion, rule by rule

Exact tapes, exact step counts

The same frame map written the way the bbchallenge wiki writes Antihydra's: name the tapes exactly, then list the rules, each with the exact number of machine steps it takes. The Lean map works frame by frame, so the families are its eight frames (the head is on the cell after >; Z, Y, W, U are z, y, w, u with a bracket 1110 at the left end):

Most of the time the head zigzags: a zig and a zag turn three 1s of the left block and one of the right block into two more 10 pairs. Every row except the k-round rows is one case of next, or a few cases glued together (the zig is .z (l + 2) 0 (r + 1) and .z (l + 2) (j + 1) (r + 1)), and each is a theorem: next_sound in lean/Phases.lean for the moves, none_halts for the halts, init_start for the start. The step count of each case is in Lean too: it is the step number in the statement of the case's piece theorem in lean/Pieces.lean (the zig for j ≥ 1 is pc_z13b, steps (2 * j + 4)), although next_sound itself only says that some number of steps exists. A halting row counts the steps of its piece up to F on a 0, plus the halting step. The k-round rows are sums of k such cases and are not stated in Lean. With halts_iff, the machine halts iff these rules, started at z(0, 0, 2), reach a halt row.

The table leaves out the remaining cases of next (78 in all; the table uses 29): the small values of l, j and r listed in the frame map section above, with step counts from 2 to 26, or 2j + 8, 6j + 12 and 6j + 18. In the first 107 steps from blank tape they are used 14 times out of 77,045 moves. python3 py/rules.py reads the cases and their step counts from the two Lean files and checks them against direct simulation: all 78 cases on 5,832 frames (every variable below 9), every row of the table with its bracket variants on 25,957 instances (k up to 6), and every frame of the first 107 steps from blank tape, where the tapes agree and the step counts add up.

Macro structure

Whole sweeps as one jump

Almost every frame of the map repeats one of a few cases, and the repeats add up in closed form, proved for all sizes in lean/Jump.lean:

w(l, j + 2k, r + 1)      k frames, 6k steps              w(l + 4k, j, r + 1)     w_sweep (also W)
u(l, j + k, r)           k frames, 4k steps              u(l, j, r + 2k)         u_sweep (also U)
y(a + 3k, j, r + 1 + k)  2k frames, k(4j+9) + 4k(k-1)    y(a, j + 2k, r + 1)     y_zigzag (also Y)

A jump takes a whole sweep or zigzag at once and is one case of the map otherwise (jump, jump_sound). The run from blank tape is then about 108 jumps per 1.5·1014 frames, and the shape of the run becomes visible. Write the tape as a solid block 1^l (blue below), an alternating block (10)^j (purple) and a solid block 1^r (amber). An epoch has two phases:

  • Growth. A zigzag turns the left block into alternating cells (each round eats 3 of the left block and 1 of the right block), and a w sweep turns them back into a left block 4/3 as long. The left block grows by 4/3 a pass and the right block pays a third of it, until the right block runs out in the middle of a zigzag.
  • Drain. A u sweep moves the alternating block into the right block, and the zigzags now stop when the right block runs out, each pass multiplying it by about 4 and eating three times as much of the left block, until the left block is gone and a new epoch starts.

Inside a phase the ratios are fixed. What is chaotic is where the phases end: the remainders at the crossovers. The tape grows by a few cells a pass, so the sizes grow about linearly in the number of jumps and the step count like its cube.

Halting

The halting rows and the close calls

A zigzag stops when the left block is below 3 or the right block is down to 1. When both run out together, three more frames and a sweep decide, and five rows end in a frame where the map is undefined (halt_y, halt_Y, halt_y0, halt_y2, halt_Y0 in lean/Jump.lean):

From every frame with all three numbers below 60, every halt within 400 jumps comes before the first zigzag or right after a zigzag start in one of these rows (frun rows 60; not proved for all frames). In the run from blank tape every zigzag start so far has an even alternating block, so only the first two rows can be met: the machine halts if a crossover lands on y(a, 2m, R) with a = 3R − 5 or Y(a, 2m, R) with a = 3R − 8. Call d the distance to the row (d = R − ⌊a/3⌋ − 2, or −3 for Y, at starts with a ≡ 1 mod 3).

Macro structure

Passes: the whole run as a map of one number

A pass runs from one zigzag start to the next: a zigzag, a few single frames and a sweep. Away from the line where both blocks run out together it is one affine map, proved for all sizes in lean/Pass.lean (13 theorems, each a chain of the jump lemmas, with its exact frame count). Write the zigzag start t(a, j, r) with t = y or Y and a = 3q + e:

drain   r ≤ q − 1:  t(a, j, r)  →  t(a − 3r + 1, 0, 4r + 2j + 2)       drain_y, drain_Y
growth  r ≥ q + 7:  t(a, j, r)  →  t'(4q + 2j + c, 0, r − q − d)       grow_*

        t e  j even: t' c d   j odd: t' c d
        y 0          y  2 1          y  5 4

        Y 0          y  5 1          y  8 4
        Y 1          y 11 5          y  8 2

Only within 6 of the line r = q does the run go back to jumps, and that is where all five halting rows lie (d = 0 means r − q = 2 for y and 3 for Y). In the run from blank tape that happens about once in 108 passes. pass is the 13 cases where they apply and a jump elsewhere; pass_sound proves each pass is that many frames of the frame map, and native_decide runs 108 passes (passes_frames): no halt in the first 3.9·1015 frames (no_halt_passes, 1.1·1023 machine steps), against 1.5·1014 by 108 jumps.

One number. In both cases the total N = a + j + r (j is 0 at almost every start) changes by a few cells only, so the state is essentially the fraction x = a/N, and a pass is the map x → 4x/3 (growth, x < 3/4) or x → 4x − 3 (drain): two full branches of slopes 4/3 and 4, whose invariant density is uniform. The epochs of the section above are runs of the first branch ended by one step of the second. A halt needs x to land exactly on the branch point 3/4, to the cell. How likely is a halt. If x were spread uniformly at the scale of one cell, a pass would land on a given row with chance about 1/(4N), and N grows by about 2 a pass, so the expected number of hits grows like the logarithm of the pass count (not like ln ln J: an epoch lasts a bounded number of passes on average). The run follows this model: the first 1011 passes have 285 zigzag starts within 16 of the boundary, 0.98 of the uniform count, and 84 within 16 of a row, 0.87 of it (py/near.py; N = a + j + r). That gives a halting hazard of 0.22 per unit of Σ1/N. Random orbits of the same map (prun rate, 4,307 halts, every one right after a start with d = 0) give between 0.08 and 0.47 depending on the size, as orbits of the map merge and are far from independent. For the run from blank tape Σ1/N is 9.1 from pass 1,000 to 1011, so about 2.0 hits were expected there (chance of none 14%); each further factor 10 of passes adds about 0.24. If that holds the machine halts with probability 1, with a median around 1014 passes and 1041 steps, which the pass map reaches in about 21 days of one core (the earlier estimate was 1053 steps).

Macro structure

The closed pass map

The 13 cases of the section above leave out the passes within 6 of the line r = q. lean/Close.lean closes the map: for each tag (y or Y), each e = a mod 3, each parity of j and each δ = r − q from 0 to 6 the pass is again one chain of zigzags, sweeps and single frames, the same for all sizes (one exception splits off the sizes q = 1). That is 89 more theorems, generated by py/gen_close.py from the jumps of c/prun.c and checked by Lean for all q and j. Exactly five classes do not reach a next zigzag start: they are the five halting rows.

So cpass, the pass map with these cases, is defined on every zigzag start and gives either the next zigzag start with its frame count or a halt (cpass_total, cpass_go, cpass_halt). Every case maps an even alternating block to an even one (cpass_even), and the run from blank tape reaches its first zigzag start y(3, 0, 5) after 6 frames, so the three rows with odd j can never be met. Lean then proves the reduction:

halts_iff_row. The machine halts from blank tape if and only if the orbit of y(3, 0, 5) under the closed pass map reaches y(a, j, r) with a ≡ 1 (mod 3) and r = ⌊a/3⌋ + 2, or Y(a, j, r) with a ≡ 1 (mod 3) and r = ⌊a/3⌋ + 3.

This replaces the frame map, the jumps and the near-boundary fallback with one total map on three numbers and a target set of two lines. orb_frames runs 100,000,000 closed passes by native_decide (ending at Y(131,674,078, 0, 76,026,925), 3,893,689,985,794,413 frames after the first zigzag start) and no_row follows: none of them is on a row.

Growth runs in one step. A growth pass sends a = 3q + e to 4q + c with c fixed by the tag and e, so k growth passes in a row depend only on a mod 3k: with a = 3kQ + ρ the end state, the frames and the steps are polynomials in Q (degrees 1, 1 and 2), and the k passes are all growth passes iff r is at least a linear function of Q (the margin r − q − 7 shrinks every pass, so the last one decides). c/crun.c builds these tables for k ≤ 7 at start-up from the single-pass formulas, by exact interpolation checked on further points and at the edge of the condition, and takes each growth run with the deepest table that applies; a drain is one step and the rare near passes go by jumps. It agrees with the pass-by-pass iteration (state, frames and steps) at every one of the 8.6·109 table steps of the first 2·1010 passes (crun check), and runs about 8.5·107 passes a second, 2.6 times prun on the same core. A halt can only come from a near pass, which is never inside a table, and every start with |d| ≤ 3 is a drain or a near pass, so the close calls below are complete.

Heuristic
prun rate, py/near.py, explainer section on passes
A halt is expected, around 1014 passes

A pass is the map x → 4x/3 or 4x − 3 on x = a/(a + j + r), and a halt needs x to land on 3/4 to the cell. Near the boundary the run matches a uniform spread at the scale of one cell (285 starts within 16 of it in 1011 passes, 290 expected), which gives a hazard of about 0.22 hits per unit of Σ1/N, N growing by about 2 a pass (random orbits of the map: 0.08 to 0.47). So the expected number of hits grows like ln of the pass count, 0.24 per factor 10; about 2 were expected from pass 1,000 to 1011 and none came (chance about 14%). Median halt if this holds: about 1014 passes, 1041 steps, weeks of one core. (The earlier ln ln J estimate, median 1053 steps, took epochs to lengthen; they do not.)
