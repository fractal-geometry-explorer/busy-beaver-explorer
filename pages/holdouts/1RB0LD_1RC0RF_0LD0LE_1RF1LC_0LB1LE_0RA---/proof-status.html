1RB0LD_1RC0RF_0LD0LE_1RF1LC_0LB1LE_0RA---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The tape is a list of blocks (010)^n separated by short walls (10)^s (s = 4, 5, 6 or 8) and, near the right end, by single 1s. Each time the head comes back to the left end in state C, the first block has lost 4 copies and the i-th block 3·4i−1 copies: a counter in base 4 whose digits are whole blocks. When the first block is down to 5 copies, a wall phase of s−1 visits eats 3 copies per visit from the second block, which then becomes the new first block (seen: 48 → 17; predicted next: 792 → 773). The tail at the right end runs a cycle of four visits that adds one block of 4k-sized length. Shift rules are proved in Lean; the list rule is checked against the run. The halting condition at the level of the list is not identified yet.

The list at the left-end visits

All 51 visits of the head to the left end in state C up to 1.54·1010 steps were read off the direct run. Between consecutive visits of the main phase the first block loses 4 copies and block i loses 3·4i−1 (every comparison agrees); a wall phase (96, 93, 90) reloads the first block with 89, which runs 89, 85, …, 5; a second wall phase (36, 33, …, 18) reloads it with 17, which runs 17, 13, 9, 5.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: exp/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the logarithm of the step count: each new cell costs a roughly constant factor more time, the signature of a counter or of a Collatz-like map whose values are written on the tape (fitted exponent 0.051). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.483).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

A deterministic counter, no sign of a halt

The run is a nested counter whose digits are block lengths of the form 3·4km (plus 1 at the tail); nothing random is involved, so the probviously argument does not apply. Halting needs F to read 1; hand-made tapes with other walls in front of the second block (s = 6 instead of 4 or 8) also ran 1.5·1010 steps without a halt.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The list rule is deterministic but has more cases than are written down here (a second form with (001)^n blocks, the walls of 5 and 6 near the tail); the halting condition at the level of the list is not identified.
