1RB0LF_1RC0RB_1LD0RA_0LE0LC_1LB1LF_1LD---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. Its tape is a list of counters, each a run of 01 or 10, which the head sweeps over and updates. This page lists what has been established, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 13 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves the halting configurations and no halt in the first 107 steps. The counter structure described below is observed in simulations, not proved. No halt in the first 109 steps.

A first run 4 cells longer changes nothing else, for every way of entering it

In the table of 32883 the head can step onto the last cell of the first run of the tape in five states, on a 0 or a 1. For each of these ten entries the outcome for a run of n + 4 cells is the outcome for n cells with one more copy of a 4-cell word: same exit state, same cells left behind. Proved for every bounce with any tape further left, and for every sweep with n = 4a + 5 to 4a + 8, all a. It holds for the three sister machines after renaming the states (and mirroring).

No halt in the first 109 steps

The C simulator tools/c/triage.c runs 109 steps without a halt; at the end 76,681 cells have been visited and 38,338 of them hold a 1.

holdouts/triage.json

Halting needs 1 A>1 or 1 E>1

The only halting entry is F reading a 1. F is entered from A>1 (write 0, move left) and from E>1 (write 1, move left), so the machine halts exactly when A or E reads a 1 with a 1 to its left. In the first 108 steps F is entered 27,487 times, always onto a 0; the window around the head is 101[0]101 in 22,690 of them.

Four machines, one run shifted (checked to 2·1012 steps)

The holdouts 32883, 40600, 103746 and 131976 are one table started in four states. Compared at the starts of their trips, the four runs from blank tape have the same list of run lengths except for the first run, which differs by a constant (r, r + 4, r + 8, r + 12), at every one of the first 527,004 trips, up to 2·1012 steps. This is a computation; it does not show that the four halt together.

Growth shape: sqrt/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.554). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.389).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

A list of counters

At the left end the tape reads E>0 (01)^a (10)^b (01)^c (10)^d … (the boundaries between runs are single defects, 0 or 1, which change from visit to visit). a grows by one every three left-end visits, b at half that rate; c shrinks while d grows. For example (a, b, c, d) = (284, 167, 242, 19) at step 404,490 and (651, 349, 180, 53) at step 1,315,510.

py/snapshots.py

Proposed family: F06, block-list counters

The description grows slowly: 20 to 27 level-2 tokens at every record from 2.6·108 to 1.7·1010 steps, and 11 to 46 runs in the macro simulator (blocks of 4 cells) at the sampled records up to 2·1016 steps. The machine shares its table with 1RB1RF_1LC0LB_1RE0LD_1LB0RF_0RA0RC_1RE--- (states renamed, some of the four mirrored; related.json), started in another state. On the screen the four runs show the same list of blocks at the same moments. For example near step 6.4·107 all four tapes read (mirrored) blocks of 3, 1,546, 39, 1,753, 117, 1,467 and 997 pairs 01 and then a last block, of 4,405 pairs in 1RB1RF_1LC0LB… and of 4,411 pairs here. The four last blocks differ by 2, 4 and 6 pairs and the records come at slightly different steps. This is an observation at the sampled records: it is not a proof that the runs correspond, and nothing about halting is carried over. For 1RB1RF_1LC0LB… the right end is proved in Lean to be a base-3 counter on the separators of such a list (bb6-1RB1RF_1LC0LB/lean/Counter.lean). Those theorems are about that machine's table with its own state names; they apply here only after the renaming is carried through, which has not been done. A list of blocks with a base-3 counter is F06.

the README, section “Family screen”

The four machines would halt together if the first run never gets short

From the Lean theorems, the four runs stay shifted copies, and the four machines halt together or not at all, provided the first run of the run of 40600 has at least 3 cells every time the head enters it. That is observed (at least 5 cells at each of the 57,701 entries in the first 2·1010 steps, and growing) but not proved: a bounce removes 1 or 2 cells and nothing local bounds the number of bounces between two sweeps. So halting together is not established.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The rates suggest a counter system (the first runs as unary counters that pass amounts down the list), but the defects between the runs and the words at the right end change from visit to visit, and what happens when a shrinking counter reaches 0 (a run disappears and the list is rebuilt) has not been described; both are where a halt could come from.
