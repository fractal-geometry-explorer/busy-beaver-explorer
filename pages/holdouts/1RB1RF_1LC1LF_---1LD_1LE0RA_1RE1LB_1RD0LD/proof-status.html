What is known about 1RB1RF_1LC1LF_---1LD_1LE0RA_1RE1LB_1RD0LD

1RB1RF_1LC1LF_---1LD_1LE0RA_1RE1LB_1RD0LD

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Read as a list of digits, the tape is rewritten by markers that crawl through long blocks of 2s (proved in Lean), merge and decay, and by rare epochs that rebuild a long block at the left end; the rule for the epochs is not known yet.

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: fixed/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.512). Right of the start it stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Tape structure

Read as runs of 1s separated by single 0s, the tape is a list of blocks of 2-runs ((110)^n) separated by marker runs: at 109 steps 1 2^10415 4 2^975 3 2^3848 6 6 2^653 3 2^244 3 2^30 6 2^116 3 2^18 6 2^66 3 2^34 6 2^42 1. The right part from 2^30 on is identical at 108 steps; only the blocks near the growing left end change.

The rewriting rules

Read as a list of digits (runs of 1s between single 0s), every change between consecutive visits of the left end in the first 3·108 steps is one of a few local rules that keep the number of cells: 3 2 → 6 → 2 3 (the marker), 3 3 → 7 → 2 4 → 2 1 (merging and decay), 3 6 → 10 → 2 7, 3 1 → 5 → 2 2, plus fast signals (a 1 crossing a block of 2s in one sweep) and rare epochs where a long block at the left end is rebuilt (N = 2, 8, 214, 1220, 2528, 7645).
