The machine

6 states, two symbols, 1 halting transition

Each entry says what to write, which way to move and which state to enter. The machine starts in A on a blank tape (all 0s).

The machine stops when it is in F reading 1. F is entered only from D1 (writes 1, moves R): D>1 1. So a halt is the moment this pattern appears, with the head on the cell after >.

The tape

Regions of 100 and 101

Cut the tape into words 10 followed by a 0 or a 1: 100 and 101. On the way right, B turns 100 into 101; on the way left, it turns them back (b_right and b_left in Lean). The head moves back and forth between two neighbouring regions, and the boundary between them creeps along one word at a time.

The shape of the run

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape stopped growing in the second half of the run (bounded so far, or growing so slowly that no record fell in the last half). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.419).

After 109 steps the tape spans 36,984 cells (46 left of the start, 27,523 right), with a density of 1s of 0.654.

Kernel
lean/Rules.lean (from lean/rules.py)
Shift rules

b_right: going right, B turns each 100 into 101. b_left: going left, B turns 101 words back into 100 words. a_right: going right, A shifts a block of 100 words by one cell. All three hold for every n and every surrounding tape.

Rare full sweeps

The head reaches the left end after having been at the right end at steps 38, 110, 416, 660, 1,070, 1,904, 3,712, 8,212, 25,912, 827,042 and 1,021,956,316. At those times the tape is 12, 19, 37, 44, 55, 71, 102, 149, 238, 1,499 and 37,210 cells wide. No halt in 3·109 steps.

Heuristic
Why it is hard

The gaps between full sweeps grow faster than geometrically: 25,912, then 827,042, then about 109. This is the mark of nested counters, where each region of the tape is used up one unit at a time by the region to its left. To decode it, one has to find the counting rule at each kind of boundary, and there are several kinds.
