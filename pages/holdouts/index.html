The BB(6) holdout map

bbchallenge has decided almost every six-state, two-symbol Turing machine. These are the ones left: the list of 608 holdouts of 30 October 2026. Each one was run for a billion steps from a blank tape and sorted by how the visited tape grows on each side; each links to an explainer that runs the machine in your browser and to a ledger of what is known about it, every result marked by what it rests on (a Lean proof, a computation, or only evidence).

Growth exponents

Each dot is a machine: how fast the tape grows left of the start (across) and right of it (up), as an exponent of the step count. Near 0 a side grows logarithmically (counters, Collatz-like maps); at 1/2 the head sweeps back and forth (bouncer-like); at 1 it drifts. Click a dot to open its explainer.
