1RB0LC_0RC0RF_1RD0LE_1LA1RA_1LC1RB_0RB---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 13 November 2026.

In short

Open; analysis only begun. Sampled when the head is in E at the left end, the tape is a list of blocks of the words 0100, 0010 and 0001, as in E> (10)^2 (0100)^189 (10)^3 (0010)^633 (01)^2 (0001)^n plus a short tail. All blocks but the last are frozen. The last one grows by a factor of about 1.4 per event: n = 13, 159, 363, …, 28,645, 40,230 up to step 1.9·1010. From time to time a new level begins: 189 copies of 0100 froze at step 0.43·106, and 633 copies of 0010 at step 9.1·106. The exact map and the halting condition are further work.

Third pass. No halt in 1018 steps (macro simulation); the third level froze at 40,231 copies and a fourth grows. The growth factor 1.40 is 7/5 (community tracker: a recursive counter).

The shape of the run from blank tape

The E-events at the left end up to 1.93·1010 steps have the form described: a frozen prefix (10)^2 (0100)^189 (10)^3 (0010)^633 (01)^2 since step 9.1·106, then (0001)^n with n = 13, 159, 363, 649, 1050, 1612, 2398, 3499, 5039, 7197, 10215, 14443, 20361, 28645, 40230, then a short tail (0^5 1^2, (001)^2 1, 0^2 1^2, …). At the last event the tail is 0^2 (10)^2 (0010)^7 0^2 1^2, which looks like the start of a new level.

Proposed family: F01, periodic blocks driven by a bounded engine

The description is the smallest of the family: 9 to 14 level-2 tokens at every record from 3.8·108 to 1.9·1010 steps. At the left-end records the tape is (0010)^190 (01)^3 (0001)^633 001 (0100)^n and a tail of at most 40 cells (n = 5,040, 10,216, 20,361, 40,231). The macro simulator (blocks of 4 cells) at its last left-end record before 1.45·1018 steps shows eleven blocks: 189, 633, 40,231, 8,119, 92,808, 5,890,878, 1,188,870, 19,851,057, 48,010,873, 11,795,335 and 284,041,139 copies. So a level ends with its block frozen and a new block started. Within a level this is the F01 shape: one growing periodic block, a short tail, and frozen blocks that the sweeps cross. The list of frozen blocks is not bounded, so a closed set has to treat the frozen part as an arbitrary list; whether the sweeps allow that is the first thing to check.

the README, section “Family screen”

Ten blocks have stopped growing by 1.45·1018 steps, and some of them drift

Observed: by step 1.45·1018 ten blocks have stopped growing, at 189, 633, 40,231, 8,119, 92,784, 5,890,876, 1,188,875, 19,851,057, 48,010,873 and 11,795,336 copies, in that order. Each grew by a factor of at least 7/5 per lap for 1 to about 15 laps before it stopped; what stops a block is not known. The stopped blocks are not all frozen: the fifth gains one copy every second lap (92,784 to 92,808) and the seventh loses copies slowly (1,188,875 to 1,188,870).

The map and the halting condition

The growth ratio of the last block is not constant (1.44 falling to 1.40), and the tail changes from event to event, so the event map has more structure than a single number. During a sweep 14 copies of the middle block move at a time. No halting configuration of a high-level map has been identified yet.
