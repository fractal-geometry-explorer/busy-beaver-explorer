Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

Open. The run is a bouncer inside a bouncer: an inner tape 1x(10)y11 is swept linearly, its resets double it, and each inner reset takes a slowly growing amount (12, 13, 14, 15, 16, …) from an outer block (10)C with C = 32,751 at 5·109 steps. The outer counter will last about 250 inner resets, so the next outer event is about 2250 steps away. Lean proves that any halt happens at the right edge of the tape (state D reading blank tape).

Other proofs and outside reports

An outside proof is waiting for review: it never halts

busycoq pull request 67 (Alex Havrilla, 2026-11-07) contains a Rocq proof that this machine never halts (verify/M_1RB0RE_1LC0RA_1RF0RD_HHH1LE_0LB1LE_1LE1RC.v). As of 12 November 2026 it was open: not merged, not reviewed by the maintainer, and not compiled here unless the ledger below says so. The verdict on this page does not rely on it.

Community label

In the community's BB(6) tracker the machine's class is Quadratic Mess. Its 3 November 2026 edition lists the machine as a cryptid: its halting is thought to come down to a Collatz-like question that no known method decides. These are labels read off the run by others, not results.

No halt in the first 10,000 steps

The list simulator of the shared library, proved to agree with the machine's semantics, runs 10,000 steps from blank tape without a halt, evaluated by Lean's kernel.

blank_runs_kernel, lean/Blank.lean

Halting only at the right edge

A closed tape language of windows of 4 cells covers the whole run from blank tape; in it the halting transition D0 fires only with blank tape from the head onward. So the machine halts iff state D reaches the blank region to the right.

halt_at_edge, halts_iff_edge, lean/Edge.lean

The sweep rules

Three one-sweep rules found and proved by bbtools.cycles: one sweep turns one 11 pair into two 10 pairs, for all sizes (with and without the outer context).

rule0–rule2, lean/Rules.lean

The doubling rounds

With P(x, y) = 0 1x 0 (10)y 1 B> and D(m, c) = C> 0 (10)m 1c at the left end: the sweep P(x+2, y) → P(x, y+2) whatever lies to the left; a top-level empty tank P(1, y+2) (blank tape to the left) reaches D(1, 2y+12); and one round D(m, c+2m+6) → D(2m+3, c+2) for all m, c and tapes to the right (left block 1, 5, 13, 29, 61, …), any number of rounds by induction. Leaf rules by bbtools.symtm.prove.

p_sweeps, top_reset, round, rounds, lean/Top.lean, lean/Leaves.lean

The halting resets, for every j

A top-level reset from (10)y+1 1 B> (the tank (10)y 11 two steps later) on blank tape halts whenever y = 2j − j − 5, j ≥ 4: after j − 2 rounds exactly two 1s are left, and D reaches blank tape.

reset_halts, rUse_closed, d_halt, lean/Top.lean

How a top-level reset ends

With K(C, y) = (10)C 0 (10)y 1 B> (an inner empty tank): a doubling round D(q+j+1, 2j+6) on blank tape, too short for a full round, leaves 2q+1 ones on the left as the outer stock and reaches K(2j, 7) to their right, the outer block being (10)2j (round_end, through top_end: 1^(p+1) B> 1 0 (10)^(i+2) 1 1 → 1^(p+1) 0 (10)^i 0 (10)^7 1 B>). This is how the second level starts.

round_end, top_end, lean/Level2.lean

The inner reset map

For every C, y and every tape on the left: an inner tank K(C+2, y) turns into 2y+7 ones and uses two outer pairs (g_entry); then inner rounds run, each using one more outer pair and doubling its block, k → 2k+6 (6, 18, 42, 90, …; g_step, g_end); when the rounds run out with one 1 on the right a new inner bouncer starts and sweeps to the next tank (g_term, p_sweeps). inner_reset: innerMap C y = some (C′, y′) implies K(C, y) reaches K(C′, y′), with innerMap an explicit Lean function. It is none only when the outer block is too short (an outer event) or a round starts with exactly three 1s on the right (further nesting); those cases are not covered.

inner_reset, lean/Level2.lean (leaves in lean/Level2Leaves.lean, by py/gen_level2.py)

Which resets halt

Started on otherwise blank tape from (10)y 11 (head in B on the last cell), the machine halts for y = 2j − j − 5 (j = 4..11: 7, 22, 53, 116, 243, 498, 1009, 2032) and y = 12, and for no other y < 2500 within 3y² + 105 steps. (That the whole family 2j − j − 5 halts is now a Lean theorem, below.)

c/hscan.c

heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: power/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like a power of the step count other than 1/2 (fitted exponent 0.266). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.415).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

The two levels

At the moments B is at the right end the tape is 110(10)C1x(10)y11 (after 5·109 steps). Sweeps move x → x−2, y → y+2; inner resets roughly double x+y and lower C by 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, … (one more each time). Read off the run up to 2·1010 steps.

tools/c simulator; README

The near misses

By the same count, the tanks y = 2j − 5 of the run from blank tape leave 2j + 2 ones after j − 2 rounds instead of 2; the leftover becomes the second-level structure. Why the run keeps landing on 2j − 5 is not explained.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known; the next outer event is about 2250 steps away. The end of a top-level reset and the inner reset of the second level are now theorems (lean/Level2.lean); not yet in Lean: the outer event (an inner round that finds the outer block used up, which hands the run back to a top-level bouncer), the rare inner round that starts with exactly three 1s, and hence the full two-level map.
