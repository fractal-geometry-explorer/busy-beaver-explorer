What is known about 1RB0RA_1LC0RE_0LD0LB_1LA1LF_1RA0LF_1LC---

1RB0RA_1LC0RE_0LD0LB_1LA1LF_1RA0LF_1LC---

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 13 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. The tape is an exact ternary counter: its digits are pairs of runs of alternating cells, valued by their lengths mod 4, and each trip of the head adds one. That increment is proved in Lean (kernel) for every carry depth, and it covers 99.86% of the trips in the first 3·1012 steps. The counter keeps shrinking one run of each digit; when such a run runs out, a transfer moves a long run to the right end of the tape, two cells per shuttle of the head. The shuttle itself is now proved in Lean for every list of runs in between and any number of shuttles, and so are the events at a run boundary, including the one pattern that halts.

What is missing is the transfer as a whole (the shuttles before the tail is “saturated”, transfers nested inside transfers, the pairing of the digits afterwards) and an invariant. The invariant cannot be about residues alone: tapes with exactly the residues of a real configuration but several short runs do halt (for example 6 6 4 6 5 5 5 5 6 7 at step 5,943). In the real run every transfer of the first 1014 steps has long runs to spare, but that is an observation, not a proof.

Sweep moves

L A>(0101)^n R → L (1010)^n A> R (4n steps: going right A flips the phase of a block), L (01)^n B>0 R → L B>0 (01)^n R and L (10)^n C>1 R → L C>1 (10)^n R (2n steps: going left blocks are crossed unchanged), for every n, L, R.

a_right, b_left, c_left, lean/Rules.lean

The pieces of a trip

Eight rules for all run lengths and every surrounding tape: digit 0 going from 0 to 1 and from 1 to 2 (whole trips), the carry leaving digit 0, the carry passing a digit at 2 (a bounce), the carry stopping at a digit at 1 or at 0, and going home over a passed digit and over digit 0, with exact step counts.

inc0, inc1, out0, carry, absorb1, absorb0, home, home0, lean/Counter.lean

The increment, for every carry depth

By induction over the list of digits a carry passes: from the start of a trip with digit 0 at 2, any number of digits at 2, then a digit at 1 (or at 0), the machine reaches the start of the next trip with the exact tape and step count stated. With inc0 and inc1 this is every kind of trip while the runs involved are long enough (r0 ≥ 7, r1 ≥ 5, a passed digit x ≥ 9, y ≥ 5, the stopping digit x ≥ 4, y ≥ 6 or x ≥ 6, y ≥ 7).

carry_chain, home_chain, trip_stop1, trip_stop0, lean/Counter.lean

The events at a run boundary

For every surrounding tape: going right, A passes a boundary (run length 0 or 3 mod 4, counted from A on a 0) or turns (1 or 2 mod 4); going left, the head crosses a 00 boundary and moves it two cells, bounces at a 11 boundary, or, on a run 1 0 of exactly two cells between 11 and 00, halts four steps later. Both passes flip every cell they cross.

loc_pass0, loc_pass3, loc_turn1, loc_turn2, loc_cross, loc_bounce, loc_halt, loc_halt_stops, lean/Transfer.lean

The shuttle of a transfer, for every tail and any number of shuttles

L (10)^m 0 A> 1(0101)^(a1+1) … 1(0101)^(an+1) 1(0101)^z 0^inf becomes the same tape with (10)^m removed and the last run 1(0101)^(z+m), in exactly m(2S + 8z + 8) + 4m(m − 1) steps (S = total length of the n runs in between): 2m cells leave the moved run, 4m arrive at the right end, nothing in between changes. By induction over the list of runs and over m.

shuttle, shuttles, scst_eq, right_chain, left_chain, lean/Transfer.lean

Tapes with the same residues and short runs halt

Real trip starts with every long run replaced by the smallest length of the same residue mod 4 halt in 20 of 2,692 cases (threshold 4): 6 6 4 6 5 5 5 5 6 7 halts at step 5,943 and 6 6 2 7 9 6 6 7 7 7 5 at step 1,005, both confirmed on a plain cell-by-cell simulator. With every long run cut to its shortest length of 8 or more cells none halted (0 of 9,155 shortened tapes at threshold 8, and none at 10, 12, 16; 3 of 7,119 at threshold 6). One run changed by ±1 or ±2 halts in about 4% of the cases; long runs replaced by random lengths of the same residue never did (0 of 3,829). So an invariant must fix the residues and bound lengths from below.

Growth shape: sqrt/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.554). tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

99.86% of trips are the proved kinds

Of the 726,270 trips in the first 3·1012 steps, 725,254 have one of the four proved shapes with the runs long enough. Of the other 1,016, the carry reaches the right end in 98, a run is too short in 269, and a digit has residues outside the table in 648 (and one is the first trip). They come, as far as we have looked, with transfers (a run xk has run out and the run yk−1 is shuttled, two cells at a time, to the right end of the tape, after which the digits past it are paired anew) or with the right end, where new digits are made from short runs.

py/classify.py, README

How close it comes to halting

The machine halts exactly when D reads a 1 whose left neighbour is a 1. Of the 6,032,756 times D read a 1 in the first 3·1012 steps, the alternating stretch ending at D's cell had length 2 158 times and length 3 17 times (a halt needs length 1); those we looked at were near the right end or in transfers.

py/classify.py, README

Transfers nest, and the right end is the same counter one level deeper

A shuttle of a transfer can become a whole inner transfer (trip 2,225,395: an inner transfer of 10,807 shuttles inside an outer one of about 55). The runs behind a freshly deposited run go through exactly the states the whole tape went through from the start (125, 69 and 35 states compared, all equal), three times slower per level; after a transfer the digit pairs take residues outside the table for a few carries (14 of the 16 classes occur).

py/runs.py, py/monitor.py, README

Every transfer of the first 1014 steps ends saturated

In 3,153,668 trips there are 168 transfers or nested levels with at least 3 shuttles; each ends with at least 6 saturated shuttles (the proved kind), after at most 20 unsaturated ones (60 in one level containing two inner transfers). D read a 1 63,281,275 times; the alternating stretch ending there was 2 cells long 218 times and 3 cells 21 times, never 1 (a halt). A candidate regular invariant (windows of 3 runs, lengths below 8 exact, else mod 4) did not stabilise: 263 windows, new ones still at 3.9·1013 steps.

Proposed family: F06, block-list counters

The description grows slowly: 20 to 27 level-2 tokens at every record from 2.6·108 to 1.7·1010 steps, and 12 to 47 runs in the macro simulator (blocks of 4 cells) at the sampled records up to 2·1016 steps. The machine shares its table with 1RB1RF_1LC0LB_1RE0LD_1LB0RF_0RA0RC_1RE--- (states renamed, some of the four mirrored; related.json), started in another state. On the screen the four runs show the same list of blocks at the same moments. For example near step 6.4·107 all four tapes read (mirrored) blocks of 3, 1,546, 39, 1,753, 117, 1,467 and 997 pairs 01 and then a last block, of 4,405 pairs in 1RB1RF_1LC0LB… and of 4,407 pairs here. The four last blocks differ by 2, 4 and 6 pairs and the records come at slightly different steps. This is an observation at the sampled records: it is not a proof that the runs correspond, and nothing about halting is carried over. For 1RB1RF_1LC0LB… the right end is proved in Lean to be a base-3 counter on the separators of such a list (bb6-1RB1RF_1LC0LB/lean/Counter.lean). Those theorems are about that machine's table with its own state names; they apply here only after the renaming is carried through, which has not been done. A list of blocks with a base-3 counter is F06.

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. A proof needs the transfer as a rule (saturation, nesting, merges, entry and exit), the regular trips for the residue classes that follow a transfer, and an invariant that bounds each moved run from below by the size of the tail behind it; a halt would need several long runs to be short at the same time.
