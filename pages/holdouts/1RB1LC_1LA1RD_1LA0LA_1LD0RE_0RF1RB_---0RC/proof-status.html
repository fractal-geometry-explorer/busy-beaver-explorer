What is known about 1RB1LC_1LA1RD_1LA0LA_1LD0RE_0RF1RB_---0RC

1RB1LC_1LA1RD_1LA0LA_1LD0RE_0RF1RB_---0RC

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Its run is a counter of the Fibonacci kind on the left, whose overflows come at times growing by the golden ratio, and a word of binary digits on the right that the counter's carries rewrite; at rare restructures the word is rebuilt longer. Lean proves that a halt can only happen at the right edge.

Status as of 8 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Lean proves no halt in the first 107 steps (native_decide) and that a halt can only happen at the right edge (kernel); the C simulator finds no halt in the first 1011 steps.

What it does. At each overflow of its counter the tape is C>0 (1 0)^z W; z grows by one per overflow and the step counts grow by a factor tending to the golden ratio. Eleven times in 1011 steps the word W is rebuilt (a restructure), the last at step 75,515,153,373.

Why it is hard. A halt can only come out of a restructure, they are rare and come at growing intervals, and the rule that maps one epoch to the next has not been decoded.

The halting configurations

The machine stops exactly when it reads the symbol of a halting transition (F0); the generated statement covers every configuration.

step_none_iff, lean/Blank.lean

A halt can only happen at the right edge

A closed tape language from windows of 4 cells covers every configuration of the run from blank tape; in all of its abstract configurations where F can read a 0 (the only halting transition), the tape right of the head is blank. So the machine halts exactly when F steps onto the blank region right of everything written.

halt_at_edge, halts_iff_edge, lean/Edge.lean, lean/EdgeCert.lean

The overflow configurations of the run

Every time C is at the left end on 0 1 0, the tape from the head on is (1 0)^z followed by odd blocks of 1s separated by single 0s (a string of binary digits read in pairs). In the first 1011 steps z rises by one from each such moment to the next, except at 11 restructures where it falls back to 1 (the first such moment is at step 40): steps 105, 184, 529, 660, 3,423, 13,944, 149,177, 370,694, 1,993,257, 442,705,820 and 75,515,153,373.

A Fibonacci counter

Within an epoch the ratio of successive overflow times tends to 1.618 (1.534, 1.583, 1.604, 1.609 at z = 37, 39, 41, 42): the counter has Fibonacci-many states per digit count.
