What is known about 1RB0RE_1LC0LB_1RD0LB_1RF0LA_1RC---_0RA0LD

1RB0RE_1LC0LB_1RD0LB_1RF0LA_1RC---_0RA0LD

Nobody knows whether this six-state, two-symbol Turing machine halts when started on a blank tape: it is one of the holdouts left open by the bbchallenge project. This page lists what has been established about it, sorted by what each result rests on.

Status as of 9 November 2026.

In short

The question is open. Each epoch is now understood exactly: the engine eats the stack of 20-cell half-units from the left in one of three exact cycles (1,744 round trips per 100 half-units, 704 per 40, 2,336 per 132), each round trip adds one word to the block, and the block becomes the next stack, 8.72, 8.80 or 8.85 times larger. Following the epochs with exact symbolic round trips (Python) gives 30 epochs and about 7·1061 steps without a halt. Which cycle and which junction the next epoch gets depends on residues of the stack size, a Collatz-like map; the search for a finite closed set of epoch classes, which would prove non-halting, was started but did not close. Lean proves the sweep rules, the shift rules of the epoch transition, one engine lemma and the right part of a round trip.

The sweep rules

5 rules for all values of the repeat counts and every tape around them: B erasing ones and crossing the block (1101110101)^n, C crossing the stack of units (10101010101010101011)^n (20 steps a half-unit), the rightward sweep restoring the stack (116 steps a half-unit, with a 45-cell carried word) and the block (30 steps a word).

The halting state is never close

direct simulation

Exact round trips: three engine cycles

The symbolic simulator py/decide/fs.py (blocks with linear-form counts, shift rules found by one-copy simulation) splits each round trip into an engine lemma (engine and stack, symbolic in the stack size, re-run at its threshold) and a right-part lemma (symbolic in every block count). Within an epoch the engine falls into one of three cycles: 1,744 round trips eating 100 half-units 10101010101010101110, 704 eating 40, or 2,336 eating 132; every round trip adds exactly one word 1011101110 to the block.

No halt in 30 epochs, about 7·1061 steps

py/decide/epochsim.py starts from the real configuration at step 449,234,410 (c/dump.c), chains the lemmas, jumps over repeated periods while every count stays above the lemmas' thresholds, and runs each epoch transition in full symbolically. heuristic Evidence only

Growth shape: sqrt/sqrt

Left of the start, the visited part of the tape grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.51). Right of the start it grows like the square root of the step count, the signature of a head sweeping back and forth across the whole tape and adding a cell or a few at the end of each sweep (a bouncer-like shape) (fitted exponent 0.497).

tools/py/bbtools/triage.py

Three parts

At every visit to the right end (after the first 84) the tape is P U^N 1101110 V^m 1101110010110, with U = (10101010101010101011)^2 (40 cells), V = 1101110101, and an engine P of 71 to 222 cells in the samples up to 4·1010 steps (during the conversions the stack is being built and the form differs).

c/snap.c, py/epochs.py

Epochs

Within an epoch one unit disappears about every 23.5 round trips (the units are in fact eaten at the left end of the stack; see the exact cycles) and m grows by about 1.5 per round trip, so a unit yields about 35 copies of V. When N reaches 0 the block of V's is turned into the next stack, 4 V's (40 cells) per unit, over about a hundred round trips. Epoch sizes: 21 units (from step 4.5·105), 165 (from about 2.3·107), about 1,490 (from about 1.3·109); at 4·1010 steps 595 units are left. Each epoch is about 8.8 times the last; the fourth should start near 1011 steps with about 13,000 units.

py/epochs.py

The engine does not repeat at visits to the right end

The engine P took 2,322 different values at the first 2,521 visits to the right end. It stays short, so it is a finite system, but its period (if it is periodic between unit consumptions) is not small. Between the checkpoints after each left crossing of the stack it is periodic: see the exact cycles.

direct simulation

The epoch map is Collatz-like

n' = (R/2C)·n + O(1), where R/C is the cycle of the epoch; the cycle of the next epoch and its junction (01000, 0001000101000 or 1010101010001000101000, then (1010001000)^j with j from 4 to 631) depend on residues of n modulo the consumption of a period and on parities. In epochs 3 to 29 the cycles go A A B B A A B B A A A B B B A A B A A C A B A B B A (A = 1,744/100, B = 704/40, C = 2,336/132) with no visible pattern.

py/decide/epochsim30.log

A closure search did not close

py/decide/bfs.py follows epoch classes symbolically (every residue of the stack size, the block size up to parity). A partial run handled 121 transitions and 118 classes without a halt, but the queue grew faster than it was processed, and it used an earlier, unsound threshold for the engine lemmas, so it proves nothing.

open Open

Does it halt from blank tape?

Not known. The epochs are followed exactly, but a proof needs every epoch class: show that the classes (engine content at the stack end, residue of the stack size, junction, parity of the block) form a finite closed set under the epoch map with no halting transition. The map is affine per residue class and the next class depends on residues, so this may be a Collatz-type problem; the first search did not close.
