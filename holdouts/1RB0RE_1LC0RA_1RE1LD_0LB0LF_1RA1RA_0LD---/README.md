## The picture

At the moments the head is in A on the first blank cell right of everything written: The tape is then a word of blocks of 1s and gaps of one or two 0s (`(1 2 /2)^4` is four copies of `1 0 11 00`, `…` an unchanged stretch, `1^17` seventeen single 1s). The fast counter is the run of single 1s (`1 0 1 0 … 1`) at the right end: it grows by one 1 per round, and each round takes about twice as long as the one before (T′ = 2T + 14 and 2T − 10 alternately in the regular stretches).

| step | tape | |
|---|---|---|
| 40,506,598 | `3 /2 (1 2 /2)^4 1 /2 1 /2 (1 2 /2)^7 1 1 1 2 /2 … 1 1 1` | after a rebuild |
| 52,537,788 | `3 /2 (1 2 /2)^4 4 /2 (2 1 /2)^5 4 /2 … 1^17` | the walker 4 moves left |
| 810,682,800 | `3 /2 1 2 /2 4 /2 (1 2 /2)^3 (2 1 /2)^5 4 /2 … 1^23` | two pieces before the front |
| 22,818,010,861 | `3 /2 (1 2 /2)^2 (2 1 /2)^4 (1 2 /2)^3 1 /2 1 /2 … 4 /2 1 4 /2 1 4 /2 …` | after the third rebuild |

Left of it the slow list is a word in a few pieces, written here with `/2` for a double 0: mostly `1 2 /2` (that is `1 0 11 00`) and `2 1 /2` (`11 0 1 00`), behind a leading `3 /2`. In each round a walker `4 /2` moves one piece to the left, turning the `1 2 /2` it passes into `2 1 /2`; when it reaches the leading 3 the list is rebuilt longer and a new walk starts.

Rebuilds seen at about 2.3·10^4, 4.05·10^7 and 1.2·10^10 steps (the walker reached the front at step 6.2·10^9).

## What is established

**Proved in Lean, kernel only** — The halting configurations: The machine stops exactly when it reads the symbol of a halting transition (F1); the generated statement covers every configuration. (`step_none_iff`, `lean/Blank.lean`)

**Proved in Lean, kernel only** — No halt in the first 10,000 steps: The list simulator of the shared library, proved to agree with the machine's semantics, runs 10,000 steps from blank tape without a halt, evaluated by Lean's kernel. (`blank_runs_kernel`, `lean/Blank.lean`)

**Proved in Lean, kernel only** — Three rounds of the run, for any tape further left: Three consecutive rounds of the run from blank tape (between moments the head is in A on the first 0 right of everything written), each proved for every tape left of the cells it visits, with its exact step count. (`lean/Rounds.lean` (generated by `py/gen_rounds.py`))

**Proved in Lean with `native_decide`** — No halt in the first 10^7 steps: The same run, evaluated by compiled code. (`blank_runs_native`, `lean/Blank.lean`)

**Computed (trusted C/Python)** — No halt in the first 10^9 steps: The C simulator `tools/c/triage.c` runs 10^9 steps without a halt; at the end 182 cells have been visited and 104 of them hold a 1. (`holdouts/triage.json`)

**Computed (trusted C/Python)** — No halt in 3·10^10 steps; rebuilds: Direct simulation. The fast counter is the run of single 1s (`1 0 1 0 … 1`) at the right end: it grows by one 1 per round, and each round takes about twice as long as the one before (T′ = 2T + 14 and 2T − 10 alternately in the regular stretches). The slow list was rebuilt at about 2.3·10^4, 4.05·10^7 and 1.2·10^10 steps (the walker reached the front at step 6.2·10^9); between rebuilds each round makes one local change in it. (`tools/c/tmrun.c` (`at A R`))

**Heuristic / observed** — Two-level counter: Left of it the slow list is a word in a few pieces, written here with `/2` for a double 0: mostly `1 2 /2` (that is `1 0 11 00`) and `2 1 /2` (`11 0 1 00`), behind a leading `3 /2`. In each round a walker `4 /2` moves one piece to the left, turning the `1 2 /2` it passes into `2 1 /2`; when it reaches the leading 3 the list is rebuilt longer and a new walk starts. Not shown for all sizes. (`README.md`)

**Open** — Does it halt from blank tape?: Not known. See the heuristic claims above for what a halt would need.
