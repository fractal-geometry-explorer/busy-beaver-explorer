## What it does

Right of the start the tape is a row of blocks `u^n` of the word `u = 101011`, separated by short markers (with
`a = 10`: `a^4 u 1^4`, `a^7`, `a 1^6 a^4`, `a 1^6 a u 1^4`, ...). The left side stays put. For example, at step
299,927,588:

    1^3 a^9 u a^2 u 1^4 a 1^6 a u 1^4 a^2 u 1^4 a u 1^4 a^5 u 1^4 a^21 u 1^4 a 1^6 a^4 u^3 a^4 u 1^4 u^12
    a 1^6 a^4 u^51 a^7 u^204 a 1^6 a u 1^4 u^817 a 1^6 a u a 1^8 u a 1^6 a 1^8 a^2 u 1^6 a^4 u a u 1^4 u^3281 a 1^8 D>0

The long blocks are about four times apart and grow at rates four times apart: (`c/rec.c`, records on the right,
sampled every 3,000) [5, 20, 83, 345] at step 3.5*10^6, [16, 67, 271, 1095] at 3.4*10^7, [5, 22, 91, 364, 1470] at
6.1*10^7, [11, 46, 184, 739, 2970] at 2.5*10^8. Between samples the largest block gains 375 words and each smaller
one about a quarter of what the next gains; from time to time the small blocks are rebuilt and a new block appears.
The markers cycle through a few shapes and look like base-4 digits (heuristic).

Lean (`lean/Rules.lean`, generated by `lean/rules.py`): `cross_u`: `L D> (101011)^n R` -> `L (111110)^n D> R` in
6n steps, and `cross_u'`: `L D> (111110)^n R` -> `L (101011)^n D> R`, for all n, L, R.

## Status

Open. Lean: `lean/Blank.lean` (halting configurations, no halt in 10^4 steps by the kernel, 10^7 by
`native_decide`), `lean/Rules.lean`. No halt in 3*10^8 steps. The only halting transition is F reading 1.
