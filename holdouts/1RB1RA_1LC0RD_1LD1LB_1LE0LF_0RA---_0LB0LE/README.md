# 1RB1RA_1LC0RD_1LD1LB_1LE0LF_0RA---_0LB0LE

bbchallenge id 181655. One of the six-state holdouts whose halting from blank tape is open. Family: list of blocks 1^(6m+1) whose counts halve each round (exp/sqrt), not solved.

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 1RA |
| B | 1LC | 0RD |
| C | 1LD | 1LB |
| D | 1LE | 0LF |
| E | 0RA | --- |
| F | 0LB | 0LE |

## Status

Open. Lean (`lean/Blank.lean`): the halting configurations (`step_none_iff`), no halt in 10,000 steps by
the kernel, in 10,000,000 by `native_decide`. Lean (`lean/Rules.lean`, kernel only): 8 sweep rules for all n.
C (`tools/c/triage.c`): no halt in 1,000,000,000 steps.

## Structure

Each time the head extends the tape on the left in state E (a round end), the tape is a list of blocks of 1s
separated by `00`. Almost all blocks have length 6m + 1 and equal blocks stand together, longest on the left.
`c/blocks.c` prints these lists; the last round ends of a 10^9-step run, with the number of blocks of each
length (round ends at least 10^6 steps apart, 28 of them):

| step | 1 | 7 | 13 | 19 | 25 | 31 | 37 | 43 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 207,427,257 | 234 | 896 | 40 | 192 | 3 | 37 | 0 | 8 |
| 266,253,684 | 2260 | 117 | 448 | 20 | 95 | 1 | 18 | 0 |
| 326,503,961 | 286 | 1130 | 58 | 224 | 10 | 47 | 1 | 9 |
| 417,668,259 | 2814 | 143 | 564 | 29 | 112 | 5 | 23 | 1 |
| 511,016,052 | 356 | 1407 | 71 | 281 | 14 | 56 | 3 | 11 |
| 653,806,000 | 3526 | 180 | 702 | 35 | 140 | 7 | 28 | 2 |
| 800,860,207 | 453 | 1764 | 89 | 350 | 18 | 69 | 4 | 14 |

Read down a diagonal: the count of blocks of length 6m + 1 is about half (within a few blocks) the count of blocks of
length 6(m - 1) + 1 one round earlier. Each round pairs up the blocks of every run and turns each pair into
one block six cells longer, and a new run of blocks `1` appears on the right, alternately long and short. A
few irregular blocks sit at the boundaries of the runs. This is observed, not proved.

Inside a round the head goes back and forth across most of the tape, turning at a point that moves left a
few cells per trip; each trip adds a few cells on the right. So the right side grows like the square root of
the step count and the left side, which grows only at round ends, like its logarithm.

Lean (`lean/Rules.lean`, generated by `py/gen_rules.py`) proves 8 sweep rules for all n, for example
`L (110)^n 1 1 B> 0 R` to `L B> 1 1 (100)^n 1 R` in 5n + 2 steps.

Halting is E1. State E is entered by D0 (write 1, move left) and by F1 (write 0, move left), so the
machine halts when one of these moves lands on a 1. Nothing seen in the run does that. The sister machine
1RB1RA_1LC0RD_1LD1LB_1LE0LF_0RA---_0LB0LF has the same structure.

## For later

This needs more than a day of work.

- Write the round map on block lists exactly, including odd counts and the irregular boundary blocks, and
  prove it in Lean from the sweep rules (one lemma per kind of boundary).
- Find what the irregular blocks encode; the halving suggests a binary-counter invariant.
- Then decide whether E can ever land on a 1.
