# 1RB1RA_1LC0RD_1LD1LB_1LE0LF_0RA---_0LB0LF

bbchallenge id 123029. One of the six-state holdouts whose halting from blank tape is open. Family: list of blocks 1^(6m+1) whose counts halve each round (exp/sqrt), not solved.

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 1RA |
| B | 1LC | 0RD |
| C | 1LD | 1LB |
| D | 1LE | 0LF |
| E | 0RA | --- |
| F | 0LB | 0LF |

## Status

Open. Lean (`lean/Blank.lean`): the halting configurations (`step_none_iff`), no halt in 10,000 steps by
the kernel, in 10,000,000 by `native_decide`. Lean (`lean/Rules.lean`, kernel only): 10 sweep rules for all n.
C (`tools/c/triage.c`): no halt in 1,000,000,000 steps.
Lean (`lean/Halt.lean`, kernel only): a halt needs state B reading 0 with 0, 0, 1 to its left
(`halt_needs_pattern`). Coq (`coq/`): a halting proof built from busycoq pull request 93 that compiles with one
assumption, that its accelerated engine stops; the engine has not stopped in more than 10^5363 steps (see below).

## Structure

Each time the head extends the tape on the left in state E (a round end), the tape is a list of blocks of 1s
separated by `00`. Almost all blocks have length 6m + 1 and equal blocks stand together, longest on the left.
`c/blocks.c` prints these lists; the last round ends of a 10^9-step run, with the number of blocks of each
length (round ends at least 10^6 steps apart, 29 of them):

| step | 1 | 7 | 13 | 19 | 25 | 31 | 37 | 43 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 234,913,178 | 119 | 991 | 45 | 187 | 12 | 36 | 2 | 7 |
| 302,079,143 | 2521 | 70 | 495 | 22 | 93 | 5 | 18 | 0 |
| 368,553,233 | 190 | 1261 | 34 | 247 | 10 | 46 | 2 | 8 |
| 473,757,674 | 3146 | 102 | 629 | 16 | 123 | 5 | 22 | 0 |
| 577,347,151 | 224 | 1572 | 48 | 314 | 8 | 61 | 2 | 10 |
| 739,133,414 | 3947 | 110 | 784 | 24 | 156 | 4 | 30 | 1 |
| 900,835,194 | 268 | 1969 | 54 | 392 | 12 | 78 | 1 | 15 |

Read down a diagonal: the count of blocks of length 6m + 1 is about half (within a few blocks) the count of blocks of
length 6(m - 1) + 1 one round earlier. Each round pairs up the blocks of every run and turns each pair into
one block six cells longer, and a new run of blocks `1` appears on the right, alternately long and short. A
few irregular blocks sit at the boundaries of the runs. This is observed, not proved.

Inside a round the head goes back and forth across most of the tape, turning at a point that moves left a
few cells per trip; each trip adds a few cells on the right. So the right side grows like the square root of
the step count and the left side, which grows only at round ends, like its logarithm.

Lean (`lean/Rules.lean`, generated by `py/gen_rules.py`) proves 10 sweep rules for all n, for example
`L (110)^n 1 1 B> 0 R` to `L B> 1 1 (100)^n 1 R` in 5n + 2 steps.

Halting is E1. State E is entered by D0 (write 1, move left), so the
machine halts when one of these moves lands on a 1. Nothing seen in the run does that. The sister machine
1RB1RA_1LC0RD_1LD1LB_1LE0LF_0RA---_0LB0LE has the same structure.

## For later

This needs more than a day of work.

- Write the round map on block lists exactly, including odd counts and the irregular boundary blocks, and
  prove it in Lean from the sweep rules (one lemma per kind of boundary).
- Find what the irregular blocks encode; the halving suggests a binary-counter invariant.
- Then decide whether E can ever land on a 1.

## The DeepMind sibling and the engine run (20 November 2026)

Up to swapping E and F this machine is U = `1RB1RA_1LC0RD_1LD1LB_1LF0LE_0LB0LE_0RA---`, which is the table T3 of the
DeepMind proof in busycoq pull request 93 (`1RB1RA_1LC0RD_1LD1LB_1RA0LE_0LB0LF_0LB---`) with two changes: T3's halting
transition F1 becomes a step (E keeps erasing to the left), and T3's D0 `1RA` becomes the detour D0 `1LF`, F0 `0RA`,
A1 `1RA`, which halts exactly when D reads 0 with a 1 on its left. Lean proves the matching fact for this machine: a
halt needs B reading 0 with the cells 0, 0, 1 to its left.

`coq/mkU.py` turns PR 93's file into a proof that this machine halts, complete except for `run_ok`, the claim that
PR 93's accelerated engine (run on T3 without its halt) stops. The engine, extracted from that file, ran 18,449,229
fast steps from blank tape without stopping; the tape then holds more than 2^4439 cells, so the machine does not halt
in its first 10^5363 steps (computed with extracted code, not by the kernel).

Where the sibling would halt: T3 halts (from A at fast step 4,452, from C at fast step 2,452,241, PR 93's halt) just after C reads 0 with
`1 1 1` on its left at the left end of the written tape. In this machine's run that configuration recurs once per lap
of the counter, 10 times so far, always with the same nearest cells; each time the machine erases three 1s and goes
back to the macro rules. A halt can only happen where the engine leaves its macro rules, and in 18.4 million fast steps
it left them in only 14 kinds of local situation besides the start, none of which can lead to the halting pattern.
So the sibling's halting point gives no halting point here; a halt would need a kind of exit never seen. Observed,
not proved. Details and tools: [`coq/README.md`](coq/README.md).
