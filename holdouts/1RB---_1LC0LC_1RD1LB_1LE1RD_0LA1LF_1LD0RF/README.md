## Status

**Open; block rules and the halting pattern proved (Lean, kernel), strong evidence that it never halts.** The
counter itself (a chain of base-4 units that gains new top digits) is described from the run, not proved.

## What it does

Read the tape as blocks of 1s separated by runs of 0s. The head alternates between two passes
(`lean/Rules.lean`, each rule for all block lengths):

- **Pass to the right**, state D on the second cell of a block, `1 D> 1 1^a`. With the next two blocks `1^b` and
  `1^c` (single 0s between):

  | next block | effect | rule |
  |---|---|---|
  | b ≥ 3 | `1^a 0^(b-2) 1 D> 1 1^(c+4)`: the block is erased, the pass goes on from the block after it | `r_step`, `r_step_3`, `r_step_4`, `r_step1`, `r_step1_3` |
  | b = 2 | merged: `1^(a+1) D> 1 1^(c+4)` | `r_merge` |
  | b = 1 | the head turns: `1^(a-1) C> 1 1^5` followed by `1^c`, and the sweep to the left starts | `r_bounce`, `r_bounce1`, `r_bounce2` |

- **Sweep to the left**, C and B on alternate cells: over 1s every other cell is erased (`l_sweep`); a single 0 is
  filled and the sweep goes on (`l_gap1`); at a gap of two or more 0s the head turns and a new pass to the right
  starts two cells further left (`l_turn`, C on the leftmost 1 of the block; `b_turn7`, B on it and seven or more
  0s).

- **The wall.** Blocks 3, 1, 3, 1, ... (the word `110101`). The pass to the right turns it into blocks of 5 with
  single 0s (`r_step_3` with a = 3), the sweep to the left turns those back into the wall (`wall_l`).

**Where it can halt.** A1 is reached only through `1 0 0 0 0 B> 1`: the sweep to the left arriving in state B on
the leftmost 1 of a block with exactly four 0s on its left (`halt4`; that it is the only way is read off the table:
A is entered only by E0, E0 only after D0 with a 1 on the left of D, and D0 there only after C0, B1). The gaps are
made by `r_step`, b − 2 zeros from a block of b cells, and in the run every erased block is odd.

**The counter.** At the moments the head is at the left end, the tape is a fixed prefix followed by a
chain of units, each a wall `(110101)^n` and a digit with four phases. For the first unit, after the wall:

    (01)^7 1^8  ->  1^6 (01)^6 1^8  ->  W (01)^4 1^6 0 1^9  ->  W 1^6 (01)^3 1^6 0 1^9  ->  W W (01)^7 1^8

(one phase per round; the wall gains two words per cycle and the next unit advances one phase). At 3·10^9 steps
the walls hold 9989, 2486, 614, 113, 27 and 4 words. Most rounds turn at the first digit; a round reaches unit k
about every 4^k rounds. The right end of the tape was last reached at step 193,765,310 (a carry out of the last
unit, which rebuilt the end of the chain); since then new units appear at the end of the chain, and the last
~250 cells are the same at steps 7.4·10^8, 1.5·10^9, 2.2·10^9 and 3·10^9 (`py/view.py 3e9 1`).

## Results

- `lean/Rules.lean` (generated by `py/rules.py`): 14 block rules and `halt4` (kernel).
- `py/stats.py` (`c/stats.c`), 2·10^10 steps: of 714,283,559 passes to the right, 712,600,950 erase a block of 3,
  421,262 a block of 5 or more (always odd: none of 2 or 4), 1,261,179 turn at a block of 1; 123 start with D on a
  0 and 45 touch the right end (last at step 193,765,310). B stands on the leftmost 1 of a block 123 times, with
  1, 3, 5, 7, 11, 17, 55 or ≥ 60 zeros on its left (4 times blank tape), never 4. After step 193,799,086 C and A
  are always on even cells, B on odd ones, and the head stays at least 182 cells from the right end.
- Lean (`lean/Blank.lean`): no halt in 10^7 steps; `py/stats.py`: none in 2·10^10.
- **Missing for a decision:** (1) the round rules for a chain of units of any length: the four phases of a digit,
  the carry through the stretch between two units, and the birth of a new unit at the end of the chain; (2) an
  invariant closed under them (a regular description of the chain with its cell parity should do: block lengths
  seen after step 6·10^4 are bounded) that excludes `1 0^4 B> 1`; (3) the start: the run up to step 193,799,086,
  with its rebuilds of the whole tape (the head in state B on a 1 at the left end: steps 80, 377, 39,402, 58,217) and the carry at 1.94·10^8, has to be
  reached by computation. An outside tracker reports an accelerated run to 1.1·10^17 steps without a halt
  ("nested shift-overflow counters ... the first overflow freezes the old tape"), not reproduced here.
