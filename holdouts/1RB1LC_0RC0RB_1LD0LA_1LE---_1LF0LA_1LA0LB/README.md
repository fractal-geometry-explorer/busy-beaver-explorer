Family: **Up/down two counters with resets (G12 family 3b)**, with [`1RB1LC_0RC0RB_1LD0LA_1LE---_1LF1RA_1LA1LC`](../1RB1LC_0RC0RB_1LD0LA_1LE---_1LF1RA_1LA1LC), [`1RB1LD_1RC0RB_1LA1RC_1LE0LA_1LF---_1LC---`](../1RB1LD_1RC0RB_1LA1RC_1LE0LA_1LF---_1LC---), [`1RB1LE_1RC0RB_1LD1RC_1LA1LE_1LF0LA_1LC---`](../1RB1LE_1RC0RB_1LD1RC_1LA1LE_1LF0LA_1LC---), [`1RB1LE_1RC0RB_1RD1LF_1LA0LC_1LD0LA_---0RE`](../1RB1LE_1RC0RB_1RD1LF_1LA0LC_1LD0LA_---0RE). The family's scripts are here: `py/counters.py` (accelerated simulator of the ticks), `py/rebuild.py` (the rebuild map and the macro simulator on (H, n, N)), `py/rebuild_stats.py` (exhaustive checks of the rebuild map, statistics of the macro run), `py/gen_rebuild_rules.py` (writes `lean/Rebuild.lean`), `py/gen_rules.py` (writes `lean/Rules.lean`), `py/run_counters.py`, `py/validate.py`, `c/fastdirect.c`, `c/fromtape.c` (direct simulation from a given tape), `c/ticks.c`, `c/pat.c`.

## What it does

Tick configuration `1 U 00 D>H V` (H = `0110101` or `0110111`): U in 5-cell digits (`00000` = 0, `00001` = 1, a sentinel 1 on top), V in pairs (`01` = 1, `11` = 0, least significant first); a tick is U + 1, V − 1 (proved, `lean/Rules.lean`). At V = 0 the tape is clean, `0^inf 1 U 00 D>H (11)^n 1 0^inf`, and the macro state is (H, n, N) with N = sentinel + U as one binary number. The **rebuild** reads the lowest j digits of N (j odd in practice, long-tailed), and returns to a clean tick configuration with up-counter N' = N >> j and V' = 2^(n+c) − δ, c = (5j + 1)/2; the phase then runs V' ticks, so the next state is (H', n + c, 2^(n+c) + N' − δ). Below 2^16 the rebuild halts only for N = all ones (with a parity condition); so the machine halts iff some rebuild of the run has δ = N' + 1 (new header `0110101`, n' odd). That happens for N = 5889, n = 13 (a halting configuration of the family, 545,221,856 steps), but in the run from blank tape, from the seventh rebuild on, N' stays hundreds of digits longer than δ. Macro run: 847 rebuilds, ~10^17730 steps, no halt. The rebuild's stopping rule is a 4-state walk over pairs of digits of N (checked, `py/rebuild_rule.py`), and the rebuilds of the family N ≡ 2^(2k+1) − 1 + b·2^(2k+2) (mod 2^(2k+3)) are one Lean theorem for every k (`lean/FamilyRule.lean`, with `lean/Phase.lean` for any run of ticks).

## For later

The rebuild is now a closed integer map (`py/map.py`: a walk in modes O and Q with a pending borrow, δ a sum of terms; checked, not proved). Next: prove it in Lean, cutting the rebuild along its walk into local passes and tick phases as `lean/FamilyRule.lean` does for the path O, ..., O, stop; the Q steps run smaller rebuilds inside. Then the real question: an invariant that keeps every walk below a fifth of the counter. The run is self-similar with factor 3.5 (a walk of j digits leaves a gap of (7j &minus; 1)/2 zeros, a later long δ turns it into a block of ones, one walk reads the block), and the largest walks stay at about 9% of the counter; between the blocks the counter is isolated ones with gaps 7k + 3, where a walk stops at the first or second 1 in the upper place of a pair. A proof would have to control which gaps become blocks and how far a walk goes past its block. The fast run in `py/map.py` slows down because the low part of the counter gets millions of digits long; a representation by runs would go further.

## What is established

**Proved in Lean, kernel only** (`step_none_iff`, `lean/Blank.lean`): The halting configurations. The machine stops exactly when it reads the symbol of a halting transition (D1); the generated statement covers every configuration.

**Proved in Lean, kernel only** (`blank_runs_kernel`, `lean/Blank.lean`): No halt in the first 10,000 steps. The list simulator of the shared library, proved to agree with the machine's semantics, runs 10,000 steps from blank tape without a halt, evaluated by Lean's kernel.

**Proved in Lean, kernel only** (`lean/Rules.lean` (4 theorems)): The tick rules of the two counters. For each of the headers `0110101`, `0110111` the run uses, two theorems: with V odd, `L 00000 (00001)^j 00 D>0110101 01 R` becomes `L 00001 (00000)^j 00 D>0110101 11 R` in 10 * j + 34 steps; with V having i + 1 trailing zeros, `L 00000 (00001)^j 00 D>0110101 (11)^(i+1) 01 R` becomes `L 00001 (00000)^j 00 D>0110101 (01)^(i+1) 11 R` in 8 * i + 10 * j + 42 steps. That is U + 1 and V − 1 for every number j of carries and i + 1 of borrows and every tape L, R beyond (proved along the symbolic run of `tools/py/bbtools/symtm.py`).

**Proved in Lean, kernel only** (`lean/Rebuild.lean` (38 theorems, written by `holdouts/1RB1LC_0RC0RB_1LD0LA_1LE---_1LF0LA_1LA0LB/py/gen_rebuild_rules.py`)): 38 rebuild rules. For 38 residue classes of the up-counter N modulo 2^j (j in [3, 5, 7], headers `0110101`, `0110111`) the rebuild after V = 0 is proved for every length n of V and every tape L left of the digits it reads: `L d_j-1..d_0 00 D> H (11)^(n+k) 1 0^inf` becomes `L 00 D> H' w (01)^(n+e) 1 0^inf`, that is: the up-counter loses its lowest j digits (N becomes N >> j) and the new down-counter is 2^(n+k+c) − δ with c and δ fixed by the class. For example, header `0110101` and N = 1 (mod 2^3): new header `0110101`, 8 more digits, δ = 737. The classes were found by sampling; each theorem is then proved along the symbolic run of `tools/py/bbtools/symtm.py`.

**Proved in Lean, kernel only** (`phase`, `lean/Phase.lean`): Any run of ticks. For both headers, every up-counter U (of any width w), down-counter V and every k ≤ V with U + k < 2^w: k ticks take the tick configuration to the one with U + k and V − k, the widths unchanged and no cell beyond the counters read. Proved by induction on k from the tick rules.

**Proved in Lean, kernel only** (`rebuild_family`, `val_Vfam`, `length_Vfam`, `lean/FamilyRule.lean`): The rebuild for a whole family, every k. For every k ≥ 1, b in {0, 1}, n ≥ 2k + 3 and every tape beyond: when V = 0 has n digits, the header is `0110101` and the lowest 2k + 3 digits of N are, from the bottom, 2k + 1 ones, a 0 and b, the rebuild ends at the clean tick configuration with up-counter N >> (2k + 3), header `0110101` (b = 0) or `0110111` (b = 1), and a down-counter of n + 5k + 8 digits with value 2^(n+5k+8) − δ + b, δ = 1 + 32^(k+1) (3 · 2^(2k+3) − 1). One theorem for the whole family, not one per class: four local passes proved along symbolic runs (`lean/Family.lean`, written by `py/gen_family.py`), four phases of 2^(2k) − 1 or 2^(2k+1) − 1 ticks by the phase lemma, and two carries into the top word. For k = 1 it is the class N = 7 or 23 (mod 2^5) of `lean/Rebuild.lean` (δ = 97281).

**Proved in Lean with `native_decide`** (`blank_runs_native`, `lean/Blank.lean`): No halt in the first 10^7 steps. The same run, evaluated by compiled code.

**Computed (trusted C/Python, tested)** (`holdouts/triage.json`): No halt in the first 10^9 steps. The C simulator `tools/c/triage.c` runs 10^9 steps without a halt; at the end 195 cells have been visited and 61 of them hold a 1.

**Computed (trusted C/Python, tested)** (`tools/c/tmrun.c`): No halt in the first 10^11 steps (direct). `tools/c/tmrun.c` runs 100,000,000,000 steps without a halt (224 cells reached on one side, 104 on the other).

**Computed (trusted C/Python, tested)** (`holdouts/1RB1LC_0RC0RB_1LD0LA_1LE---_1LF0LA_1LA0LB/py/rebuild.py` (`validate`)): The macro map and its simulator. At each moment V reaches 0 the tape is the clean configuration `0^inf F U 00 D> H (11)^n 1 0^inf` (F the counter's top word), so the state is (H, n, N) with N the up-counter read as one number. `py/rebuild.py` writes that tape, runs the rebuild (tick runs of its sub-phases jumped by the proved tick rules, the rest cell by cell in C) to the next clean tick configuration (H', n', N', V'), and then adds the V' ticks of the phase by arithmetic: next state (H', n', N' + V'), steps by the closed formula of the tick costs. Tested: its first 7 states and step counts, up to about 10^161 steps, equal those of `py/counters.py` (itself tested against `tools/c/tmrun.c`).

**Computed (trusted C/Python, tested)** (`holdouts/1RB1LC_0RC0RB_1LD0LA_1LE---_1LF0LA_1LA0LB/py/rebuild_stats.py 1RB1LC_0RC0R... run`): No halt in the first 10^17730 steps (macro run). The macro run from blank tape goes through 847 rebuilds without a halt; at the end V has 58,893 digits and the step count is about 10^17730.

**Computed (trusted C/Python, tested)** (`py/rebuild_stats.py 1RB1LC_0RC0R... halting 16`): The rebuilds that halt, N < 2^16. For every N < 2^16, both headers, and n − len(N) + 1 in {0, 1} (the only values the run produces, len(N) the number of binary digits of N with the sentinel), the rebuild halts in exactly 22 cases, all with N = 2^(L+1) − 1 (every digit 1): header `0110101` with L even (n = L or L + 1), header `0110111` with L odd and n = L. The halting step counts grow by a factor 4 per two digits (1727, 6769, 26787, ... for header `0110101`, n = L + 1). Every other rebuild returns to a clean tick configuration.

**Computed (trusted C/Python, tested)** (`py/rebuild_stats.py ... shape 14`, `c/fromtape.c`): A halting configuration two rebuilds away. For N < 2^14 (same inputs) the rebuild always gives N' = N >> j for some j, or N' = 2, and N' − δ = −1 (the next counter N' + V' all ones) only for N = 5885 and 5889, n = 12, 13, header `0110101`. For N = 5889, n = 13 that all-ones counter has the halting parity: the tape `1 [5889 in 5-cell digits] 00 D>0110101 (11)^13 1`, run directly by `c/fromtape.c`, halts after 545,221,856 steps (a rebuild, 2^21 − 737 ticks, a halting rebuild), the same count as the macro simulator's. So the machine can halt from configurations of its own family; the question is whether the run from blank tape reaches one.

**Computed (trusted C/Python, tested)** (`py/rebuild_rule.py` (`check 16`, `random 2000 200`, `run 2000 1700`)): Where every rebuild stops: a walk over pairs of digits. The rebuild reads N's digits from the bottom in pairs b_2i+1b_2i and moves a 4-state automaton (table on the explainer page); header `0110101` starts it in O, `0110111` in Q. If it stops on its k-th pair, j = 2k + 1 digits are read, N' = N >> j, the new header is `0110101` or `0110111` by the last digit read, and V gains c = (5j + 1)/2 digits. Compared with the exact rebuild of `py/rebuild.py`: for every N < 2^16, both headers and n − len(N) + 1 in {0, 1}, 151,368 rebuilds follow it and none contradict it; 110,768 are not covered (the walk does not stop below the top digit of N). For 2,000 random 200-digit N, both headers, all 4,000 follow it (longest walk j = 127). In the macro run from blank tape, 940 of the first 941 rebuilds follow it (longest walk j = 5,015); the other one, rebuild 5, is not covered: its walk reaches the top of N (it read 80 of the 82 digits and left N' = 2). The automaton was read off the data; it is not proved.

**Computed (trusted C/Python, tested)** (`py/map.py` (`check 14`, `random 1500 300`, `blank 300`)): The rebuild in closed form. The rebuild written out completely as a function of the macro state: (h, n, N) goes to (b, n + c, 2^(n+c) + (N >> j) − δ), c = (5j + 1)/2, where j, b and δ come from one pass over the pairs of digits of N from the bottom, in mode O (header `0110101`) or Q (`0110111`). Pairs are written upper digit first, and k counts the pairs `11` skipped since the last term. O, pair `01`: stop, b is the next digit, last term 1 − b + 2^(5k+5)(3·2^(2k+3) − 1). O, `00`: term (3·2^(2k+2) − 1) 2^(5k+5), subtract 1 from the rest of the counter, stay in O. O, `10`: the same term, go to Q. Q, `00`: term A, subtract 1 from the rest, stay in Q. Q, `10`: term A + 10, subtract 1 from the rest, go to O. Q, `01`: term B, subtract 1 from the rest, go to O. Here A = 2^(7k+9) − 3·2^(5k+6) and B = 2^(7k+10) − 3·2^(5k+6) − 2, and directly after a Q-`00` step A = 2^(7k+10) − 2^(5k+7), B = 2^(7k+11) − 2^(5k+7) − 2. δ is the sum of the terms, each shifted left by 5 places per pair read after it. So the states P and R of the 4-state walk are O and Q with a borrow pending on the counter. The terms were fitted to computed rebuilds and then compared with the exact rebuild: all 1,582 classes of low digits with j ≤ 14 (both headers), each with two values of n; 1,500 random 300-digit counters (longest walk j = 75); rebuilds 8 to 300 of the run from blank tape. No difference. The formulas are not proved.

**Observed only / heuristic** (the two checks above and `lean/Rebuild.lean`): The reduction: halting iff δ = N' + 1 once. If the halting rebuilds are the all-ones ones for every N (checked below 2^16), then, since the next counter is N' + V' = 2^(n') + N' − δ, the machine halts if and only if some rebuild of the run gives δ = N' + 1 with new header `0110101` and n' odd. A rebuild of a proved class has a fixed δ (at most 24 digits for j ≤ 7) and N' = N >> j, so once N has more than 31 digits (from the second rebuild on) only the classes not yet in Lean (j ≥ 9) can do it.

**Observed only / heuristic** (`py/rebuild_stats.py 1RB1LC_0RC0R... run 1500`): What the rebuild map looks like in the run. All 847 rebuilds of the macro run have N' = N >> j. j is odd except once (rebuild 5 read 80 of the 82 digits) and then the new V has c = (5j + 1)/2 more digits than the old. The number of digits read has a long tail (84 with j = 3, 108 with j = 5, 83 with j = 7, 77 with j = 9, 61 with j = 11, 54 with j = 13; up to j = 2481), like the stopping time of a random walk on the digits. From rebuild 7 on, N' always has at least 301 more digits than δ (the gap grows with n), so δ = N' + 1 never came close.

**Observed only / heuristic**: Probably never halts. δ has about 2.5j to 3.5j digits and N' about len(N) − j, so δ = N' + 1 needs a rebuild that reads about a quarter of the counter and then an exact match of the remaining three quarters, digit for digit. Treating those digits as random, that has probability about 2^(−0.7 n) at a rebuild with n digits; n is over 45 from the third rebuild on and grows by about 70 per rebuild, so the sum over the whole run is tiny. A heuristic, not a proof: the digits of N are produced by the machine, not drawn at random.

**Observed only / heuristic** (`py/map.py` (`run 320500`)): 320,500 rebuilds of the map without a halt. If the closed map is the rebuild for every counter (checked on the cases listed there, not proved), the run from blank tape goes through 320,500 more rebuilds after the seventh without a walk that reaches the top of the counter. At the end V has 41,132,351 digits, so the machine has run more than 10^12,000,000 steps. A halt needs δ = (N >> j) + 1; after the first 1,000 rebuilds δ was never longer than 0.34 of the length of N >> j, and no rebuild had δ > N >> j. This run is much shorter than those of the two sibling machines (10^8 rebuilds) because the long walks below make the low part of the counter millions of digits long.

**Observed only / heuristic** (`py/map.py` (`run 320500`)): Long walks come back 3.5 times longer. Unlike its two open siblings this machine keeps making walks that read a fixed share of the counter. Observed in that run: a walk of j digits leaves a gap of (7j − 1)/2 zeros below the new top digit; when the δ of a later long walk ends inside such a gap, the borrow turns the rest of the gap into a block of ones; when the block reaches the low end one walk reads all of it and a little more. Meanwhile the counter has grown 3.5 times as well, because every digit read adds 2.5 digits on top. So the long walks form chains: 5,015, 19,303, 69,007, 242,225, 848,077, 2,968,733 digits, each about 3.5 times the one before and each reading 8.7% to 8.9% of the counter at that moment; a second chain runs 7,237, 24,695, 86,021, 302,021. Between the blocks the counter is isolated ones with gaps of 7k + 3 zeros, and a walk crossing them stops at the first or second 1 that sits in the upper place of a pair, so it goes only a few gaps beyond its block (in the cases looked at). A halt would need a walk that reads more than a fifth of the counter (δ has up to 3.5j digits and must be as long as the rest) and then an exact match of the rest. None of this is proved; it explains why the share stays constant but does not bound it.

**Open**: Does it halt from blank tape? Not known. Lean proves the ticks, any phase of ticks, 38 short rebuild classes and one family of rebuilds for every k. The whole rebuild is now written in closed form (checked against the exact rebuild, not proved in Lean). What is missing is a proof of that map and, above all, an invariant of the counter that keeps every walk away from its top: the long walks keep reading about 9% of the counter, generation after generation, and nothing proved prevents a longer one.
