# 1RB1RF_1LC0RF_1LE0LD_1RC0LB_---1RA_1LB1RA

bbchallenge id 144461. One of the six-state holdouts whose halting from blank tape is open (group G09, visited tape growing like the square root of the step count on both sides).

**In one sentence.** Sweeps back and forth over a period-10 background (1011011011 and its rightward form 0111101101); a clean zone of it at the left end grows, the rest is a soup of 011, 100, 1 1 0 0 pieces; a halt needs B to read the third of three 0s.

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 1RF |
| B | 1LC | 0RF |
| C | 1LE | 0LD |
| D | 1RC | 0LB |
| E | --- | 1RA |
| F | 1LB | 1RA |

## What it does

Rightward, A, B and F cross the period-10 word whole (12 steps a copy): A turns `1011011011` into `0111101101`; on period 3 A turns `011` into `101` and F turns `101` into `110`. Leftward, C and D take them back (about 50 steps a copy), and B, C, D turn `011`, `101`, `110` into `001`, `100`, `010`.

At the left end there is a clean zone of the period-10 word that grows steadily (`(1011011011)^173` after 7·10^6 steps, `(1110110110)^296` after 2·10^7); the rest of the tape is a soup of `(011)^k`, `(100)^k`, runs `1^3`, `1 1 0 0` and `0 0 1 0 0`.

## Halting

The machine halts only when E reads a 0, and Lean proves that the only way there is B reading a 0 with `0 0` just left of it: three 0s in a row with B on the right one (B and C write 1s and step left, and E meets the last 0; `halt_needs_pattern`, `lean/Halt.lean`). In 10^9 steps B read a 0 with `1 0` on its left (one 0 short) 38,739 times, all inside the tape.

Pairs of 0s are common in the soup; a third one under B never happened in 10^9 steps, and no invariant forbidding it is known.

## Status

Open. Proved in Lean, kernel only: the halting configurations (`step_none_iff`, `lean/Blank.lean`), no halt in 10,000 steps, the 13 sweep rules (`lean/Rules.lean`), and that every halt from blank tape passes through the pattern above (`halt_needs_pattern`, `lean/Halt.lean`). With `native_decide`: no halt in 10,000,000 steps. C: no halt in 1,000,000,000 steps.
