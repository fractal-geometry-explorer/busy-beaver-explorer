# 1RB1LD_1RC0LA_1LA0RE_1LB1LA_1RF0RB_---0LB

A bouncer with a moving inner wall, interrupted by zigzag phases through the left part of the tape; the left end grows in bursts of multiples of 30 cells. Halting is open.

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 1LD |
| B | 1RC | 0LA |
| C | 1LA | 0RE |
| D | 1LB | 1LA |
| E | 1RF | 0RB |
| F | --- | 0LB |

## What it does

**The tape.** At a left turn: blocks of ones of lengths 1, 2, 4 (mostly) separated by single 0s; at a right turn
`1^3 0 1^2 0 1^2 0 1^2 0^2 1^2 0^2 1 0 1^3 …`.

**The sweeps (proved, `lean/Rules.lean`).**

| rule | from | to | what it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `b_011` | `L B> (011)^n R` | `L (100)^n B> R` | Rightward: B turns each 011 into 100. |
| `c_111` | `L C> (111)^n R` | `L (011)^n C> R` | Rightward: C turns each 111 into 011. |
| `a_110` | `L (110)^n A> 1 R` | `L A> 1 (011)^n R` | Leftward: A turns each 110 into 011. |
| `d_001` | `L (001)^n D> 1101 R` | `L D> 1101 (111)^n R` | Leftward: D, carrying 1101, turns each 001 into 111. |

**Emergent behaviour (observed).** Head trajectory from 2.0·10^6 steps (`py/observe.py path 2000000 2034000`): turns at the right end (cells 566, 567, 568,
569, 570, 571: one new cell per bounce) and at an inner wall (−263, −259, −255, −254, −253): a bouncer between the wall
and the right end, the wall moving right. At 2.033·10^6 steps the head starts a zigzag going left through the rest of
the tape. The left end grows in bursts (`c/bursts.c`; from 8.4·10^8 to 9.9·10^8 steps: 30, 30, 90, 30, 60, 60, 60, 60, 30, 30, 60,
60 cells). Compared with the previous left turn, the stretch 450..1,278 of the tape (1,419 cells at 2.7·10^6 steps)
is the old tape moved 42 cells left.

**Halting.** F0 halts; F is entered only from `E>0 0`. In 10^9 steps E reads a 0 28,543,358 times, never with a 0 on
its right (`c/prehalt.c`).
