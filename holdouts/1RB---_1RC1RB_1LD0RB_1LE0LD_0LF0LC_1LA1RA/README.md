# 1RB---_1RC1RB_1LD0RB_1LE0LD_0LF0LC_1LA1RA

bbchallenge id 144929, group G10 (one side grows like √t, the other like a smaller power). Family: **bouncer over a 10 background with a defect gas**. Halting from blank tape: **open**.

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | --- |
| B | 1RC | 1RB |
| C | 1LD | 0RB |
| D | 1LE | 0LD |
| E | 0LF | 0LC |
| F | 1LA | 1RA |

## What it does

Almost all of the tape is the two-cell word `10` repeated. The head sweeps right across it in states C and B (each copy of `10` becomes `01` or back: the whole background slides by one cell per pass) and returns left in D/E. The right end grows like the square root of the step count, as in any bouncer.

What makes it non-trivial is the left part of the tape: read as words `a=10`, it is a list of runs of `a` separated by *defects*, a lone `0`, a lone `1` or `000`, e.g. `C>0 0 a^4 1 a^6 1 a^5 1 a^9 0 a^8 1 a^4 0 a^9 1 a^11 0 …` at step 16,379,475. Each sweep moves and rewrites these defects; new ones are born at the left wall and old ones travel right and dissolve into the background. The left edge advances by about 4–5 cells each time the head reaches it, and those visits become rarer (left extent ~ t^0.18).

We did not find a finite rule set for the defect dynamics: the run lengths between defects look irregular (no period, no linear growth). This is the signature of a chaotic machine.

## Halting

The machine halts on A reading 1. The closed tape language with windows of 5 cells (bbtools.ctl) allows that only with `11010` or `11011` to the right of the head; nothing tighter was found.

Closed tape language with 5-cell windows (`python3 -m bbtools.ctl M 5`, trusted Python check): a halt (A reading 1) needs `11010` or `11011` to the right of the head; any left side.

## What is proved

- `lean/Blank.lean` (generated): the halting configurations (`step_none_iff`); no halt in 10,000 steps (kernel) and 10^7 steps (`native_decide`).
- `lean/Sweeps.lean`: 8 shift rules, kernel-checked: C crosses `10` rightward writing `01`, B crosses `01` writing `10`; D, E and C cross `1010`/`0101` leftward unchanged (`sweep_rC_10`, `sweep_rB_01`, `sweep_rC_1010`, `sweep_lD_1010_1`, `sweep_lE_1010_1`, `sweep_rB_0101`, `sweep_lC_0101_0`, `sweep_lD_0101_0`). Found by trying every state and direction on one copy of each background word with `bbtools.symtm` (`_one_copy_right`, `_one_copy_left`), then `symtm.prove`.

Everything else here is observation of the run (heuristic) or a trusted computation, as marked in `status.json`. Nothing proved here decides halting.

## Evidence and how to reproduce

```sh
./tokrun 1RB---_1RC1RB_1LD0RB_1LE0LD_0LF0LC_1LA1RA 1000000000 a=10,b=110 L 100000 # the tape as words at the left end (R for the right)

```
