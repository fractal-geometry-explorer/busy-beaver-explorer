# 1RB1LC_1LA0RD_1LA0LA_---1RE_1RB0RF_1RC0RA

A periodic sea of blocks `1^5 0` with defect blocks drifting through it at several speeds. Halting is open.

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 1LC |
| B | 1LA | 0RD |
| C | 1LA | 0LA |
| D | --- | 1RE |
| E | 1RB | 0RF |
| F | 1RC | 0RA |

## What it does

**The tape.** At a left turn: blocks of ones separated by single 0s, mostly 5 (374 of 461 blocks at 2.97·10^6 steps;
also 13, 4, 7, 21). At a right turn the sea reads `(1^2 0)^k` with defects `1 0^2 1 0`.

**The sweeps (proved, `lean/Rules.lean`).**

| rule | from | to | what it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `a_block` | `L (101101)^n A> 1 R` | `L A> 1 (110111)^n R` | Leftward: A turns each 101101 back into 110111 (a block of five ones). |
| `d_block` | `L D> (111101)^n R` | `L (110110)^n D> R` | Rightward: D turns each 111101 (a block of five ones) into 110110. |
| `e_111` | `L E> (111)^n R` | `L (101)^n E> R` | Rightward: E turns each 111 into 101. |
| `a_10` | `L (10)^n A> 1 R` | `L A> 1 (11)^n R` | Leftward: A fills each 10 into 11. |

**Emergent behaviour (observed, `py/observe.py shift 3000000 L`).** Compared with the previous left turn, stretches of
the tape equal the old tape moved right by 36 (cells 392..783), 39, 229, 42 (cells 1,174..1,607), 120 and 24 (cells
1,881..3,370): different parts drift at different speeds, so the defects meet. The explainer's picture shows the
slanted tracks.

**Halting.** D0 halts; D is entered only from `B>1 0`. In 10^9 steps B reads a 1 102,606,472 times, never with a 0 on
its right (`c/prehalt.c`).

## For later

List the defect words and their speeds, and the outcomes of collisions; then this is a particle system and the question
is whether some collision makes `B>1 0`.

## Status

Open. Lean (`lean/Blank.lean`): the halting configurations (`step_none_iff`), no halt in 10,000 steps by
the kernel, in 10,000,000 by `native_decide`; `lean/Rules.lean`: the sweep rules, kernel only. C (`tools/c/triage.c`): no halt in 1,000,000,000 steps.
