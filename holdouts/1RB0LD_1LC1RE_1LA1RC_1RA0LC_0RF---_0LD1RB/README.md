# 1RB0LD_1LC1RE_1LA1RC_1RA0LC_0RF---_0LD1RB

bbchallenge id 168919, a six-state holdout of group G05 (growth class fixed/sqrt). **Open.** The machine translates
its tape back and forth between a block code and a pair code (halving one way, grouping by threes the other); blocks
of eight 1s are a fixed point of the round trip, and a few defects drift left through a background of them.

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 0LD |
| B | 1LC | 1RE |
| C | 1LA | 1RC |
| D | 1RA | 0LC |
| E | 0RF | --- |
| F | 0LD | 1RB |

## What it does

**Block form.** Each time the head reaches the right end (state C on the blank after the tape), the tape right of
the start cell is a row of blocks of 1s separated by single 0s, and every block except the first (cut at the start
cell) and a last block of 3 has **3d + 2** ones (2,890,717 blocks checked at the 5,852 right-end moments of the first
3·10^7 steps; only the first and last differ). Writing each block by its digit d, the tape after 23,657,027 steps is
`[6] 1 2^31 1 2^122 1 2^199 4 2^46 6 2^502 0` (`2^n`: n blocks of `1^8`).

**Pair form.** An even block and the 0 after it, `1^(2k+2) 0`, has the same length as the segment `0 0 (1 0)^k 1`.

**Leftward pass** (state C): even blocks are rewritten into pair form one after the other (`left_even`); the first
odd block is rewritten too, two 1s are glued onto the block left of it, and the head turns right in state B
(`left_odd`). This is halving, and like a carry it stops at the first odd block.

**Rightward pass** (B, C, D): every three pairs become `1^5 0` (`right_group`); a segment end `1 0 0` met in phase
adds three 1s (`right_seg0`). So `1^8` (three pairs and a segment end) comes back as `1^8`: it is a fixed point, and
the tape fills with 8s. A segment end met out of phase (one pair into a group) turns the head left again in the middle
of the tape, starting a new leftward pass there.

**Emergent behaviour: drifting defects.** Between long runs of 8s sit a few small groups of other digits (`4 1`, `7`,
`1 5`, `3 1`, `6`, `1 2 1`, ...). Each group cycles through a few shapes; while it does, the run of 8s left of it
shrinks by one and the run right of it grows by one, so the defects drift left; the rightmost run grows by about one
block per pass (the square-root growth). Snapshots:

| step | tape (digits) |
|---|---|
| 19,545,086 | `7 2^30 1 5 2^122 5 2^202 4 1 2^68 4 1 2^415 0` |
| 22,406,297 | `7 2^30 3 1 2^121 6 2^200 4 2^52 1 5 2^477 0` |
| 26,319,497 | `[6] 1 2^31 4 2^119 1 5 2^195 5 2^34 5 2^552 0` |
| 29,948,960 | `[6] 4 2^30 5 2^118 4 1 2^191 3 1 2^18 1 2 1 2^615 0` |

At the right end, 2,896 of the 5,851 transitions between consecutive right-end moments are the cheap `... 5 3` ->
`... 8 2`; the others rewrite the tail `X Y 8^k 2` in a few fixed ways, e.g. `5 5 8^k 2 -> 14 8^(k-1) 5 3`,
`8 5 8^k 2 -> 17 8^(k-1) 5 3`, `5 17 8^k 2 -> 11 5 8^k 5 3` (block sizes).

**Where it can halt.** Only E reading 1, and E is entered only from B reading 1. In the rightward pass B reads the 1
of a pair and E then reads its 0; a halt needs B to meet `1 1`, a rightward pass running into block form.

## Status and evidence

**Lean, kernel only** (`lean/Passes.lean`, generated by `py/gen_rules.py` with `bbtools.symtm.prove`):
`left_even`, `left_odd`, `right_group`, `right_seg0`, each for every k and every surrounding tape. `lean/Blank.lean`:
the halting configurations (`step_none_iff`) and no halt in 10,000 steps.

**Lean, `native_decide`**: no halt in the first 10^7 steps (`blank_runs_native`).

**Computed (trusted)**: `tools/c/tmrun.c` runs 10^11 steps from blank tape without a halt (522,424 cells visited; 9 minutes).

**Observed only**: the 3d+2 structure, the fixed point 8, the drifting defects and the tail rules above
(records of the first 3·10^7 steps, `tools/c/tmrun.c MACHINE 30000000 records`).

**Open**: the rightward pass out of phase, the motion and collisions of defects, and the question itself. The
dynamics is a recoding between halving and grouping by threes, of the Collatz-like kind.

## Reproduce

```sh
. /etc/profile.d/lean.sh && holdouts/1RB0LD_1LC1RE_1LA1RC_1RA0LC_0RF---_0LD1RB/lean/check.sh
python3 holdouts/1RB0LD_1LC1RE_1LA1RC_1RA0LC_0RF---_0LD1RB/py/gen_rules.py   # rewrites lean/Passes.lean
gcc -O2 -o tmrun tools/c/tmrun.c && ./tmrun 1RB0LD_1LC1RE_1LA1RC_1RA0LC_0RF---_0LD1RB 30000000 records
```

## For later

The next step is the full rightward transducer: the configurations `B>` in each of the three phases meeting a
segment end, proved with `symtm` like `right_seg0`, and what the out-of-phase turn does. With those, a macro
machine on the digit form (runs of 8s run-length coded) would let the defects be followed for far longer, and a
description of each defect's cycle (how many passes, how far it moves) would give the rule for their drift and their
meetings, which is where a halt would have to come from.
