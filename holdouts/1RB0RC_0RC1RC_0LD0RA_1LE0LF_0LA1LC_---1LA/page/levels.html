The question "does this machine halt?" can be asked at four levels of description. Each level keeps less detail than the one above it, and each step down is a theorem saying that the answer is the same. The page walks down the ladder: the six-state machine on its tape; the tape read as blocks of repeated words at the moments state E reaches the left end; whole cycles of the long phases, each a rule that holds for every size of the blocks; and finally one number r, the length of the last block, which grows by 321,042,928 every period and never leads to a halt.
