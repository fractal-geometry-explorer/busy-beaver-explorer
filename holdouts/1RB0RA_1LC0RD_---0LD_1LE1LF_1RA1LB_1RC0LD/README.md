# 1RB0RA_1LC0RD_---0LD_1LE1LF_1RA1LB_1RC0LD

bbchallenge id 75665. One of the six-state holdouts whose halting from blank tape is open (group G09: the visited tape grows like the square root of the step count on both sides).

**What it does.** At every visit to the left end the tape is a uniform block `(001)^n`, a steady 77% of the tape, followed by a binary word: single 1s separated by one or three 0s (`01`, `0001`). The head crosses the block with proved shift rules; the binary word is rewritten every round trip like a cellular automaton, with no known rule. The halt (C0) needs E on a 1 with two 0s to its left, which no run has shown. Decision: open.

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 0RA |
| B | 1LC | 0RD |
| C | --- | 0LD |
| D | 1LE | 1LF |
| E | 1RA | 1LB |
| F | 1RC | 0LD |

## The sweeps

A visit to the right end adds 3 cells; a visit to the left end adds 10 to 64 (the head bounces several times near the left end before leaving). The
tape grows by about 36 cells per round trip.

## The tape as words

At the first new cell of a visit to the left end (`py/sweeps.py STEPS L`):

| step | width | `(001)^n` block | rest | block share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 19,827,463 | 5,560 | 4,281 | 1,279 | 0.770 |
| 295,055,255 | 21,496 | 16,566 | 4,930 | 0.771 |
| 885,320,577 | 37,248 | 28,698 | 8,550 | 0.770 |
| 1,789,936,717 | 52,972 | 40,812 | 12,160 | 0.770 |

The rest is a binary word: single 1s (24,768 of 24,769 runs of 1s in the samples up to 3·10^8 steps) separated by one or three 0s (16,366 and 8,368
runs; 35 others, near the ends of the zone). After a rightward sweep the same zone reads as blocks `011` and `0111` (runs of two or three 1s, runs of
one or two 0s). The block is crossed by shift rules (`block`, `left_D`, `left_F`, `left_D2`); the zone is rewritten every round trip in a way that looks
like a one-dimensional cellular automaton, and the boundary moves so that the shares stay fixed.

## Halting

C0 is the only halting transition and C is entered only by B0 (write 1, move left). B is entered by A0 (1RB) and E1 (1LB). After A0 the cell C reads is
the 1 that A wrote, so a halt needs the pattern `0 0 E>1`: E on a 1 with two 0s to its left. In the first 10^9 steps C is entered 100,516,612 times and
reads 1 every time.

## Sweep rules (`lean/Rules.lean`)

Generated by `py/rules.py` with `tools/py/bbtools/symtm.py`; every rule holds for all values of `n` and every tape `L`, `R`, with the exact step count. `S>` is the head in state S on the next cell, `(w)^n` is n copies of w, `0^inf` is blank tape.

| rule | from | to | steps |
|---|---|---|---|
| `block` | `0^inf D> (001)^n+2 R` | `0^inf 1 0 (110)^n+1 1 0 D> R` | 5 · n + 10 |
| `right_A` | `L A> (010)^n R` | `L (110)^n A> R` | 5 · n |
| `left_D` | `L (011)^n D> 1 R` | `L D> 1 (001)^n R` | 5 · n |
| `left_F` | `L (101)^n F> 1 R` | `L F> 1 (100)^n R` | 5 · n |
| `left_D2` | `L (110)^n D> 1 R` | `L D> 1 (010)^n R` | 5 · n |
| `ones` | `L A> 1^n R` | `L (0)^n A> R` | n |

## Status

Open. Lean, kernel only: the halting configurations, no halt in 10,000 steps (`lean/Blank.lean`), the sweep rules (`lean/Rules.lean`). Lean with `native_decide`: no halt in 10^7 steps. C: no halt in 10^10 steps. Observed: the 77% block and the binary zone.

**For later.** The binary zone is where the question lives: find the rule by which a round trip rewrites the word over {`01`, `0001`}, and an invariant of it that excludes `0 0 E>1`.

## Deciders tried (F11, 13 November 2026)

None closes. Each reaches a halting configuration in its abstraction, which says nothing about halting.

- n-gram CPS (`tools/c/ngramcps.c`), equal widths on both sides: a halting local configuration is reached with plain up to width 15, history 1 up to width 15, history 2 up to width 13, history 3 up to width 12, LRU history up to width 7 (larger widths ran out of the 150 s or 60 million configuration budget).
- n-gram CPS with every cell tagged by its position mod p: fails for p = 2 up to width 12, p = 3 up to width 12, p = 4 up to width 12, p = 6 up to width 12.
- n-gram CPS where each side also carries the state of a small automaton that has read that side from the far end (every automaton with up to 3 states on both sides, or 4 states on one side), with position mod 1..4 and widths 4, 6, 8: no closed set without a halt in all 120 parameter sets.
- Run-length windows (the K nearest runs of equal macro cells on each side, run lengths kept exactly below T and mod M above): fails for all 72 parameter sets (cells of 1 to 4 bits, K = 2, 4, 6, T = 2, 4, M = 1, 2, 4).
- Two-automaton closed tape language by SAT (a deterministic automaton reading each side inward, n states each, CaDiCaL): no solution for n = 2..9; n = 10 not finished in 150 s.
