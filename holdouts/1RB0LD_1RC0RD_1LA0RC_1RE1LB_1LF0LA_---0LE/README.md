## What it does (observed)

- **Words.** At the moments the head is at the left end (state B), the tape cut into words `1^i 0^j` uses only
  a = `110`, b = `10`, c = `100`, d = `1100` (plus a short piece at the start). After 7,187 steps:
  `b a3 b d b a d b a d b a d b a d b a d b a12 b`; after 3·10^7 steps it starts
  `b a3 b d b a d b a d b a d b a d b a d b a4 b d ...` and goes on for thousands of words in an irregular mix.
- **Zigzag sweeps.** The head moves in short zigzags (no run of more than 9 cells in one direction after 20,000
  steps). Each sweep goes from the left end to a turning point inside the tape and back; the left end grows by 5
  cells per sweep; the turning point wanders from sweep to sweep; the right end stays put (228 cells right of the
  start after 10^9 steps).
- **Where it can halt.** F reading 0. F is entered only from E reading 0, E from D reading 0 or F reading 1; going
  left, E and F alternate across `0 1` pairs, so a halt needs that zigzag to meet two 0s in the wrong phase.
