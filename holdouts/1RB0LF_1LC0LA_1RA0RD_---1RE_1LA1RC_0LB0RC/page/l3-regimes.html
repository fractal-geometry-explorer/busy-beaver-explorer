Follow the templates from one round trip to the next (nxt in lean/Tables.lean) and they go round in cycles. There are four cycles, called regimes R0 to R3, with 15, 22, 16 and 9 templates. After one full lap of a regime the tail is exactly as it was, a has dropped by 45, 66, 48 or 27 (3 per trip), and b has grown by 99, 141, 99 or 57. Click a template in the rings to see its shape; the button runs the selected regime's whole lap on the machine.
