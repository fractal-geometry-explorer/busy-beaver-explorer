# 1RB1RF_1LC0LF_1RD0LB_0RA1RE_1RD---_0RC0LA

bbchallenge id 89727. One of the six-state holdouts whose halting from blank tape is open.

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 1RF |
| B | 1LC | 0LF |
| C | 1RD | 0LB |
| D | 0RA | 1RE |
| E | 1RD | --- |
| F | 0RC | 0LA |

## What it does: two periodic regions that turn into each other

The tape is made of two periodic regions and a few dozen defects. One region repeats the 8-cell word `00100101`
(blocks of single 1s), the other `11111010` (blocks of five 1s); the defects are blocks of 3, 7, 9, 11 or 13 ones.
Counting the blocks of each length (`tools/c/tmrun ... every`, `py/regions.py`):

| steps | tape length | single 1s | blocks of 5 | defects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4·10^8 | 32,011 | 4,483 | 3,727 | 21 |
| 8·10^8 | 45,179 | 8,150 | 4,366 | 21 |
| 1.2·10^9 | 50,497 | 8,191 | 5,309 | 48 |
| 1.6·10^9 | 62,321 | 14,968 | 4,141 | 48 |

The head sweeps back and forth across the tape. Moving right it turns the region of single 1s into the other one (the results are rotations of `11111010`) a whole word at
a time: in state E every `00100101` becomes `10101111`, in state D every `10100100` becomes `11110101` (`sweepE`,
`sweepD`, proved in Lean for any number of copies, 8 steps a word). The sizes of the two regions go up and down
while the whole tape grows like the square root of the step count; most of the tape is right of the start, the
left end barely moves.

## Does it halt?

The only halt is E reading 1, and E is entered only from D reading 1 (D1 = 1RE): the machine stops exactly when the
head in state D reads `1 1` (`halt11`, `halt11_stop`). The sweep rules never do that, so a halt would have to come
from a defect or from the turn at an end. The C simulator runs **10^11 steps** without a halt.

No accelerator used here gets far: the sweeps change the tape everywhere, so `bbtools.cycles` (blocks of 4 or 8
cells) finds no repeated cycle at the tape ends. What the defects do, and whether the oscillation of the two regions
is regular (a counter) or chaotic, is not known.

## For later

Stopped at: the two regions, the sweep rules and the halting condition in Lean, the plain run to 10^11 steps. Next:
(1) follow the defects from sweep to sweep (they are few: 21 to 48) and write their motion as rules; (2) find the
law of the region sizes (a sampled series every 10^8 steps is cheap with `tmrun every`); (3) an accelerator that
applies whole sweeps symbolically, so that the run can go beyond 10^11.
