# 1RB0RF_1LC---_1LF1LD_1RE0LD_0RC0RE_1LC1RA

bbchallenge id 134470, a BB(6) holdout (group G06). **Family: Collatz-like ×3/2 map with a memory word, the word at the left end; probviously
halting.**

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 0RF |
| B | 1LC | --- |
| C | 1LF | 1LD |
| D | 1RE | 0LD |
| E | 0RC | 0RE |
| F | 1LC | 1RA |

## What it does

The left end barely moves (33 cells left of the start); the tape grows to the right. At the end of each
half-phase the tape is `w (10)^k` or `w (01)^k`, the head at the right end, w a short word (≤ 6 cells so far).

* **Zigzag:** `L (10)^(x+2) 1 1 E> 0 1 1^y 0^inf -> L (10)^x 1 1 E> 0 1 1^(y+6) 0^inf` (y odd): the copies of `10`
  are eaten two per round at the junction while the block of ones at the right end grows by six; k copies give
  about 3k ones.
* **Second sweep:** the block of ones becomes about 3k/2 copies of `01` (or `10`), and the roles swap.
* **The map:** a half-phase end is `w (10)^k F>` (or `w (01)^k A>`); the next is `w' (10)^k'` with
  **k' = (3k + c)/2**, where w' and c depend only on w and k mod 2 (`py/wordmap.py`, each entry read off direct
  simulation, `c/halfphase.c`, for 12 values of k of that parity and identical for all). From w = '' an even k
  gives ('1', 8), an odd k ('', 11); from '1' even ('', 8), odd ('1000', 5); and so on.
* **Halting entries:** the half-phase halts (B reads 1) only from words with `1111` in them and one parity of k:
  ('1111', odd), ('111101', odd), ('11111', even), ... (45 among the 2,958 word states reachable under any parities
  with |w| ≤ 18). From the start, the shortest way there needs 41 prescribed parities.

k at the 34 half-phase ends to step 7.6·10¹³: 4, 10, 19, 35, 55, 86, 133, 203, 307, 464, 700, 1055, 1585, 2381,
3578, 5371, 8063, 12097, 18149, 27226, 40843, 61267, 91906, 137863, 206801, 310204, 465311, 697969, 1046957,
1570438, 2355661, 3533494, 5300245, 7950370 (words w: '', '11', '01', '0100', '0101', '1100', '10', '1000',
'1011', '011000').

## Status

Open; **probviously halting**. Given the table, the machine halts exactly when the orbit of (w = '', k = 4) meets a
halting entry. With the table the orbit makes 2,000,005 half-phases without one (k then has 352,184 digits, about
10^704,000 machine steps), visiting 42 word states and never one where the other parity of k would have halted. If
the parities of k were fair coins, a halt would come after about 2^40 half-phases (probability 0.62 within 2^40,
0.9996 within 2^43; `py/wordmap.py markov 18`). Finding the halt by running the map is out of reach: k has 0.585
bits per half-phase, so 2^40 half-phases mean numbers of 10^12 bits and about 10^24 bit operations.

* Lean (kernel), `lean/Zig.lean`: `zig` — `Z (x+2j) y L` reaches `Z x (y+3j) L` for all j, x, y, L, where
  `Z x y L` is `L (10)^x 1 1 E> 0 1 1 (11)^y 0^inf`; by induction on `e_round` of `lean/Rules.lean` (generated by
  `lean/rules.py` with `tools/py/bbtools/symtm.py`).
* Trusted (`py/phases.py`, macro simulator): 34 half-phases, no halt in 7.6·10¹³ steps.
* Trusted (`py/wordmap.py`): the table; it reproduces the 21 half-phases simulated directly (k ≤ 61,267) and the
  34 of the macro simulator; 2,000,005 half-phases of the orbit without a halt.
* Heuristic: the Markov estimate above.
* Lean (`lean/Blank.lean`): no halt in 10⁷ steps; C: no halt in 10⁹ steps.
