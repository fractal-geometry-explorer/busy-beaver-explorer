# 1RB0RC_1LC0RB_1RE1LD_0LB0LF_---0RA_1LB1LD

bbchallenge id 120990. One of the six-state holdouts whose halting from blank tape is open (group G09, visited tape growing like the square root of the step count on both sides).

**In one sentence.** Sweeps back and forth over a period-7 background (0101101 and its rightward form 1010100); a clean zone of it at the left end grows steadily, the rest is a soup of (10)^k, 010, 1 1 0 pieces; a halt needs E to read 1100.

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 0RC |
| B | 1LC | 0RB |
| C | 1RE | 1LD |
| D | 0LB | 0LF |
| E | --- | 0RA |
| F | 1LB | 1LD |

## What it does

Rightward, C, E, A and B cross the period-7 word whole (9 steps a copy): C turns `0101101` into `1010100`, E turns `1011010` into `0101001`, and so on; on period 3 B turns `100` into `101` and A turns `010` into `110`. Leftward, B, C and D take them back (23 or 25 steps a copy).

At the left end there is a clean zone of the period-7 word that grows steadily (`(1010110)^362` after 2·10^7 steps); the rest of the tape is a soup of `(10)^k`, `1 1 0 1`, `(010)^k` and short pieces.

## Halting

The machine halts only when E reads a 0, and Lean proves that the only way there is E reading `1 1 0 0` (E, A and C step right and E meets the last 0; `halt_needs_pattern`, `lean/Halt.lean`). In 10^9 steps E read `1 1 0` followed by a 1 about 4·10^7 times, so the safety rests on the cell after `1 1 0`.

No invariant forbidding `1 1 0 0` under E is known.

## Status

Open. Proved in Lean, kernel only: the halting configurations (`step_none_iff`, `lean/Blank.lean`), no halt in 10,000 steps, the 16 sweep rules (`lean/Rules.lean`), and that every halt from blank tape passes through the pattern above (`halt_needs_pattern`, `lean/Halt.lean`). With `native_decide`: no halt in 10,000,000 steps. C: no halt in 1,000,000,000 steps.
