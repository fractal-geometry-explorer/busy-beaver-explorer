## Status

**Open.** A stack of counters that works at the right end of the tape: the top block is converted at a steady
rate, and when it is used up the next block takes over at about 2.5 times the size, while the blocks below change
by a few cells per cycle. Lean proves (kernel) the two working modes for all block lengths.

## What it does

The left part of the tape is history; the work happens at the right end. The tape is a stack of blocks
`1^u (01)^v` separated by `00`, and every time the head, in state F, is at the right end (reading blank) the top of
the stack is in one of two modes:

| mode | tape end | next visit |
|---|---|---|
| A | `1^X (01)^Y 0 F>` | `1^(X-2) (01)^(Y+2) 0 F>` |
| B | `1^X (01)^Y 0010 (01)^Z 0 F>` | `1^(X-4) (01)^(Y+2) 0010 (01)^(Z+1) 0 F>` |

So the 1s of the top block are turned into `01` pairs, two (mode A) or four (mode B) per visit. When they are used
up the head goes down the stack (a *pop*): the pairs of the top become the 1s of a new top block, about twice as
many, and the blocks just below change by a few cells. A mode-A top of X ones ends with about X + Y pairs; a mode-B
top ends with about X/2 pairs plus about X/4 after the marker. One A pop and one B pop multiply the top by about
2.5 each:

    1^1227 (B) -> 1^1225 (01)^310 (A) -> (01)^1535 -> 1^3069 (B) -> (01)^1537 0010 (01)^766
               -> 1^3069 (01)^771 (A) -> (01)^3840 -> 1^7679 (B) -> 1^7677 (01)^1923 (A) -> ... -> 1^19199 (B)

Meanwhile the block two levels down, `1^W (01)^V`, loses 4 ones and gains 2 pairs every two pops (W = 975, 971,
967, 963, ... between 2.1·10^8 and 2.4·10^8 steps, 955 at 1.1·10^9), and the block one level down cycles through a
few small shapes. So the stack is a hierarchy of counters, each level ticking once per cycle of the level above:
the tops reach 1^12933 at 7·10^7 steps, then a lower level changes and the next tops halve (6451, 3211, 1591, 781)
before growing by 2.5 again (19183 at 3.6·10^8, 47999 at 1.1·10^9).

The level-two block will take about 240 more pops to run out, by which time the top is about 2.5^240 cells long:
whether the machine halts depends on what the deep levels do when they empty, far beyond simulation. Nothing is
known about that; it has the look of a Collatz-type question.

  left (kernel).
- `py/modes.py 1e9`: at the 38,033 right-end visits of the first 10^9 steps, mode A predicts 19,511 and mode B
  18,284 exactly (no wrong prediction); the other 238 are pops and small cases. It also lists the top blocks.
- Next step: the exact rules of a pop (they involve the two blocks below the top) and the induced map on the
  stack.
