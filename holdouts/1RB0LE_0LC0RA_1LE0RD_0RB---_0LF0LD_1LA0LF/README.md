# 1RB0LE_0LC0RA_1LE0RD_0RB---_0LF0LD_1LA0LF

bbchallenge id 36869. One of the six-state holdouts whose halting from blank tape is open (group G11, family *gap counter*). It grows to the left; its tape is single 1s with gaps of one to three 0s.

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 0LE |
| B | 0LC | 0RA |
| C | 1LE | 0RD |
| D | 0RB | --- |
| E | 0LF | 0LD |
| F | 1LA | 0LF |

## What it does

**Visits to the left end.** At the visits of E to the left end on blank (`tmrun M 1000000000 at E L`) the tape is
`E>0 0 1 (0 0 1)^k 0 1 ...` followed by single 1s with gaps of one, two or three 0s. k grows by 1 per visit while
the time between visits roughly doubles (from step 109,450,476: k = 2, 3, 4, 5 at steps 109,450,476,
123,935,687, 152,906,122, 210,847,005: gaps 14.5, 29.0, 57.9 million), and drops now and then (14 to 8 at step
4,121,436; 11 to 9; 12 to 11; 18 to 2 at step 109,450,476). The signature of a binary counter whose digits are
the gaps.

**Sweep rules** proved in Lean: `a_right_01`, `a_right_001`, `b_right_10`, `b_right_100`, `c_right_10`,
`d_right_01`.

**Where it can halt.** `python3 -m bbtools.ctl M 6` closes (not in Lean); it allows a halt only with D reading a 1
at the left end of the tape, with `100100`, `100101`, `101001` or `101010` from the head on.

**Larger windows (worker 2, second round).** `python3 -m bbtools.ctl 1RB0LE_0LC0RA_1LE0RD_0RB---_0LF0LD_1LA0LF K` for larger K: K = 7: closes, 2112 abstract configurations where a halt can fire; K = 8: closes, 6144 abstract configurations where a halt can fire; K = 9: no closed set within the default 100 rounds. So larger windows do not remove the halting contexts (their number grows with K) and soon stop closing: this route gave no certificate. These machines are counters, whose halting depends on a global count, which a language of K-cell windows cannot see; a certificate will have to use the macro structure.

## Events (worker 2, third round)

At an *event* the head is in state A just right of the 14-cell word `ENGINE = 00101001010100`:

    0^inf [left counter] ENGINE A> 0 PREFIX [M] rest

- left counter: binary, 3-cell digits `000` = 0, `100` = 1, most significant first (the "gaps" of the first look);
- `PREFIX`: the 46-cell word `1001010100100101010010001001000100100010101010` (in this stretch of the run); inside an
  event the head turns at several places inside it, rewriting and restoring it, and it comes back unchanged;
- M: two 4-cell digits `1000` = 0, `1010` = 1, lowest first.

**Event rule (Lean, every k).** For M = 0, 1, 2: the left counter gets 1 (carry over k ones: `0 0 0 (1 0 0)^k` ->
`1 0 0 (0 0 0)^k`, also when the ones reach the left end of the tape) and M gets 1, in 8k + 608 (M = 1: 8k + 616) steps,
for every tape left of the counter's changing digits and right of M. `lean/Events.lean` (6 theorems, generated by
`python3 py/events.py H36869 > lean/Events.lean`).

**In the run** (`python3 py/track.py 3000000`): events of this shape start at step 78,415 (left counter 32, M = 1); 110
consecutive events add 1 to both counters (27 of them wraps of M) up to left counter 142; then the PREFIX is rebuilt.
Example: at steps 100,465, 101,089, 101,705, 102,321, 102,979 the left counter reads 1000011, 1000100, 1000101,
1000110, 1000111 and M = 0, 1, 2, 3, 0.

**Open.** When M wraps, a slower counter right of M changes (4-cell words `0101`, `0100` and others; not decoded),
and now and then the PREFIX is rebuilt (the drops of the left-end count above). A halt (D reading 1) can only come in
those steps, never inside a non-wrapping event. Next steps: decode the word right of M and the wrap of M as a rule,
then the rebuilding of PREFIX.

## Status

- Lean (kernel): `Blank.lean` and `Rules.lean` (six sweep rules).
- Trusted: no halt in 1e9 steps (`tmrun`); CTL with K=6 closed (`bbtools.ctl`).
- Lean (kernel): `Events.lean`, the non-wrapping events for every carry length.
- Trusted: 110 consecutive events of that shape in the run (`py/track.py`).
