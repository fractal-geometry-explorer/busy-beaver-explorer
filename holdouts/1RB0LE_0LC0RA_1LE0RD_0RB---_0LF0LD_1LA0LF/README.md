# 1RB0LE_0LC0RA_1LE0RD_0RB---_0LF0LD_1LA0LF

bbchallenge id 36869. One of the six-state holdouts whose halting from blank tape is open (group G11, family *gap counter*). It grows to the left; its tape is single 1s with gaps of one to three 0s.

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 0LE |
| B | 0LC | 0RA |
| C | 1LE | 0RD |
| D | 0RB | --- |
| E | 0LF | 0LD |
| F | 1LA | 0LF |

## What it does

**Visits to the left end.** At the visits of E to the left end on blank (`tmrun M 1000000000 at E L`) the tape is
`E>0 0 1 (0 0 1)^k 0 1 ...` followed by single 1s with gaps of one, two or three 0s. k grows by 1 per visit while
the time between visits roughly doubles (from step 109,450,476: k = 2, 3, 4, 5 at steps 109,450,476,
123,935,687, 152,906,122, 210,847,005: gaps 14.5, 29.0, 57.9 million), and drops now and then (14 to 8 at step
4,121,436; 11 to 9; 12 to 11; 18 to 2 at step 109,450,476). The signature of a binary counter whose digits are
the gaps.

**Sweep rules** proved in Lean: `a_right_01`, `a_right_001`, `b_right_10`, `b_right_100`, `c_right_10`,
`d_right_01`.

**Where it can halt.** `python3 -m bbtools.ctl M 6` closes (not in Lean); it allows a halt only with D reading a 1
at the left end of the tape, with `100100`, `100101`, `101001` or `101010` from the head on.

**Larger windows (worker 2, second round).** `python3 -m bbtools.ctl 1RB0LE_0LC0RA_1LE0RD_0RB---_0LF0LD_1LA0LF K` for larger K: K = 7: closes, 2112 abstract configurations where a halt can fire; K = 8: closes, 6144 abstract configurations where a halt can fire; K = 9: no closed set within the default 100 rounds. So larger windows do not remove the halting contexts (their number grows with K) and soon stop closing: this route gave no certificate. These machines are counters, whose halting depends on a global count, which a language of K-cell windows cannot see; a certificate will have to use the macro structure.

## Events (worker 2, third round)

At an *event* the head is in state A just right of the 14-cell word `ENGINE = 00101001010100`:

    0^inf [left counter] ENGINE A> 0 PREFIX [M] rest

- left counter: binary, 3-cell digits `000` = 0, `100` = 1, most significant first (the "gaps" of the first look);
- `PREFIX`: the 46-cell word `1001010100100101010010001001000100100010101010` (in this stretch of the run); inside an
  event the head turns at several places inside it, rewriting and restoring it, and it comes back unchanged;
- M: two 4-cell digits `1000` = 0, `1010` = 1, lowest first.

**Event rule (Lean, every k).** For M = 0, 1, 2: the left counter gets 1 (carry over k ones: `0 0 0 (1 0 0)^k` ->
`1 0 0 (0 0 0)^k`, also when the ones reach the left end of the tape) and M gets 1, in 8k + 608 (M = 1: 8k + 616) steps,
for every tape left of the counter's changing digits and right of M. `lean/Events.lean` (6 theorems, generated by
`python3 py/events.py H36869 > lean/Events.lean`).

**In the run** (`python3 py/track.py 3000000`): events of this shape start at step 78,415 (left counter 32, M = 1); 110
consecutive events add 1 to both counters (27 of them wraps of M) up to left counter 142; then the PREFIX is rebuilt.
Example: at steps 100,465, 101,089, 101,705, 102,321, 102,979 the left counter reads 1000011, 1000100, 1000101,
1000110, 1000111 and M = 0, 1, 2, 3, 0.

**Open.** When M wraps, a slower counter right of M changes (4-cell words `0101`, `0100` and others; not decoded),
and now and then the PREFIX is rebuilt (the drops of the left-end count above). A halt (D reading 1) can only come in
those steps, never inside a non-wrapping event. Next steps: decode the word right of M and the wrap of M as a rule,
then the rebuilding of PREFIX.

## The whole run as one counter system (14 November 2026)

This replaces the event picture above by rules that cover the whole run, and reduces the halting question to one
statement about a travelling defect. Everything here is on the **mirrored** tape (read the machine's tape from right to
left; the mirrored table is A: 1LB 0RE, B: 0RC 0LA, C: 1RE 0LD, D: 0LB ---, E: 0RF 0RD, F: 1RA 0RF).

**Visits.** A *visit* of state X at cell q is the stretch of the run from the head arriving at q in state X until it
first reaches q-1. A visit reads and rewrites only cells from q on, so it is a function on the half tape from q on. For
this machine the visits of A and E always end in B, and four functions close up (S, T, U, V any half tape):

    A(0 S)   = 1 S            A(1 S)   = P(1 E(S))
    E(0 0 T) = 1 0 A(T)       E(1 0 T) = 1 0 E(T)       E(0 1 T) = P(0 F(1 T))      E(1 1 T): the machine halts
    F(1 0 U) = 1 0 A(U)       F(1 1 U) = P(1 F(1 U))
    P(c 0 V) = 1 0 E(V)       P(c 1 V) = 0 0 V

**Rounds.** From the tape `0^inf [A] 0 0 S` the machine comes back to `0^inf [A] 0 0 S'` one cell further out, with
`S' = 1 0 A(S)`; S_0 is blank. So the run is the sequence S_{k+1} = 1 0 A(S_k), and the machine halts if and only if
some A(S_k) meets `E(1 1 ...)`. Found and checked against the machine by `c/zvis.cpp` (it derives the rules by local
simulation and compares the tape with direct simulation); `py/zeval.py` evaluates the functions exactly.

**Tokens.** Read S in the words `L = 1010`, `O = 000`, `I = 100`. On a tape made of these words A is a counter:

    A(L W) = L A(A(W))        A(O W) = I W        A(I W) = O A(W)

O and I are the binary digits 0 and 1 (lowest first), and L is a *doubler*: every carry that crosses it becomes two.
On such a tape A never halts and never changes where the L's are.

**The defect.** Each round puts `1 0` in front, which does not fit the words: `x = 10`, or a single `1` or `0` (`z`),
is a *defect*. A defect travels away from the head, rewriting the words it crosses (Ê is E without the `1 0` it always
writes first, E0 is E after a 0):

    A(z W) = 1 W       A(x O W) = O x W      A(x I W) = L O Ê(W)      A(x L W) = L A(A(x W))      A(1 W) = O Ê(W)
    Ê(L W) = L Ê(W)    Ê(O W) = 1 W          Ê(I W) = E0(W)
    E0(O W) = L W      E0(L W) = O A(x W)    E0(I W) = O 1 W          x at the blank end = I,  1 at the blank end = I

These rules follow from the four functions by hand. None of them is a halting case: **a tape with one defect cannot
halt**, and stays a tape with at most one defect. The defect disappears at `1 I O -> O L` (two digits become a doubler)
or when it reaches the blank end. A halt needs `E(1 1 ...)`, which takes two defects next to each other.

**What the run does** (`py/tokz.py`, the token rules run exactly, equal cell for cell to `py/zeval.py` up to round 24):
at the start of every round S is `L^m`, one defect, and then words only. During the round the defect runs through the
tape and disappears at `1 I O -> O L` about 40% of the way into the part behind the two leading runs of L, far from the
blank end; the next round starts with one defect again. The tape gains exactly 4 words per round. This
held in every round from 13 to 4,500 (at round 4,500 the defect ends at word 9,725 of 17,983; the first twelve rounds
are irregular). The part of the tape the defect leaves behind settles: about the first 40% of the tape behind the
leading L's is the same in round k and round k+2.

**Long run.** `c/zvis.cpp` (visit counts kept as sums of powers of two) ran 100,000 rounds without a halt; the visit
counts reach 187,512 bits, and only 7 window rules of A and E are ever used (A: `0`, `100`, `110`, `1010`; E: `10`,
`00`, `010`), never A on `111` or `1011` and never E on `11` or `011`.

**Open.** The machine does not halt if in every round the defect disappears before the round ends. Where it disappears
depends on the digits of the counter behind the settled part, which look irregular, so this is a statement about the
digits of a doubling counter, not proved. If a defect survived a round the next one could catch up with it; only then
is a halt possible.

The twin 1RB0LF_1LC1RC_0RA0LD_0LE1RE_0RF0LC_1RC--- has the same words: at every second visit of its state E to a new
leftmost cell its tape is `0 0 0 1 0 S_k` (`py/twinchk.py`, checked up to k = 18), so it halts or not together with
this machine as far as the common rules go (the equivalence itself is observed, not proved).

    g++ -O2 -o zvis c/zvis.cpp && ./zvis m:1RB0LE_0LC0RA_1LE0RD_0RB---_0LF0LD_1LA0LF A1B,E1B - run 20000 600
    python3 py/zeval.py 24 > words.txt && python3 py/tokz.py 4500 words.txt && python3 py/twinchk.py 30000000 words.txt

## A conserved value, and what is left to prove (14 November 2026, second pass)

**Value.** Give a tape of words the value v with `v(L W) = v(W)/2`, `v(O W) = 2 v(W)`, `v(I W) = 1 + 2 v(W)`, blank = 0
(a digit counts 2^-b, b = the number of L's minus the number of digits before it: 2^b visits reach it for each
visit at the front). With a defect: `v(x W) = F(W)`, `v(1 W) = G(W)`, `v(z W) = G(W) - 1`, where

    F(O W) = 2 F(W) - 1     F(L W) = F(W)/2         F(I W) = (G(W) - 1)/2                       F(blank) = 1
    G(O W) = 2 G(W) - 1     G(L W) = (G(W) - 1)/2   G(I O W) = v(W) - 1    G(I I W) = 4 G(W) - 1    G(I L W) = 4 F(W) + 3    G(blank) = 1

**Every visit of A adds exactly 1 to v**, with or without a defect (each token rule above, by hand; `py/tokv.py` checks
it with exact fractions on every round, no violation). At the start of round k the value is 1/15 up to about
2^(-k/2) (0.0666666666666 at round 100): the far end of the tape is the binary expansion of 1/15, the word
`I O O O` repeated k/8 times (measured), and each round appends one period.

**Where the defect goes** (measured, rounds 100 to 1,500). At the start of a round the tape is `L^m`, the defect,
`L^(m-1)`, a part rich in L's, and then digits only up to the end (about 1.8 k digits). The defect always disappears
at the first `I O` of the digit part or a few tens of words around it. There a digit has b of about 0.45 times the
length of the tape, so 2^b visits arrive: the defect is pushed one word further by every visit and cannot stay.

**The argument, and its gap.** (1) If the defect were still there at the end of a round, with the rewritten words P
behind it, conservation gives 1 = v(P) minus the constants of its steps, at most the sum of 2^-b over the digits it
passed; so it would have to be among the last log2(length) words of the tape. (2) It cannot pass two periods
`I O O O I O O O` alive (`1 I O` ends it; `x I O O O` becomes `L O 1 O O` and the next `I O` ends it). So a tail of
a few more than log2(k)/4 periods, with every digit before the tail having b larger than the length of the tail, forces
the defect to disappear in every round. (3) The tail of the next round is the old one plus the visits that reach
it, and it stays periodic as long as the digits before it have large b. Not proved: **that every digit in the part
rich in L's keeps b above a few times log2(k)**. Measured: b is at least 2m - 5 there (the minimum is 10 words in).
Bookkeeping of the worst case only gives that b does not decrease over two rounds. That is the one missing lemma
for a proof that this machine and its twin never halt; nothing of this second pass is in Lean.

## Status

- Lean (kernel): `Blank.lean` and `Rules.lean` (six sweep rules).
- Trusted: no halt in 1e9 steps (`tmrun`); CTL with K=6 closed (`bbtools.ctl`).
- Lean (kernel): `Events.lean`, the non-wrapping events for every carry length.
- Trusted: 110 consecutive events of that shape in the run (`py/track.py`).
- Trusted (14 November 2026): the visit functions and the round law S' = 1 0 A(S) (`c/zvis.cpp`, checked against direct
  simulation); no halt in 100,000 rounds; the token rules agree with the exact evaluation and every round from 13 to
  4,500 ends with a single defect (`py/tokz.py`).
- By hand, not in Lean: the token rules, and that a tape with one defect cannot halt.
- By hand and checked numerically (`py/tokv.py`): the conserved value. Open: a lower bound on b in the part rich in
  L's, which would give that every round's defect disappears; halting.
