# 1RB0LE_0LC0RA_1LE0RD_0RB---_0LF0LD_1LA0LF

bbchallenge id 36869. One of the six-state holdouts whose halting from blank tape is open (group G11, family *gap counter*). It grows to the left; its tape is single 1s with gaps of one to three 0s.

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 0LE |
| B | 0LC | 0RA |
| C | 1LE | 0RD |
| D | 0RB | --- |
| E | 0LF | 0LD |
| F | 1LA | 0LF |

## What it does

**Visits to the left end.** At the visits of E to the left end on blank (`tmrun M 1000000000 at E L`) the tape is
`E>0 0 1 (0 0 1)^k 0 1 ...` followed by single 1s with gaps of one, two or three 0s. k grows by 1 per visit while
the time between visits roughly doubles (from step 109,450,476: k = 2, 3, 4, 5 at steps 109,450,476,
123,935,687, 152,906,122, 210,847,005: gaps 14.5, 29.0, 57.9 million), and drops now and then (14 to 8 at step
4,121,436; 11 to 9; 12 to 11; 18 to 2 at step 109,450,476). The signature of a binary counter whose digits are
the gaps.

**Sweep rules** proved in Lean: `a_right_01`, `a_right_001`, `b_right_10`, `b_right_100`, `c_right_10`,
`d_right_01`.

**Where it can halt.** `python3 -m bbtools.ctl M 6` closes (not in Lean); it allows a halt only with D reading a 1
at the left end of the tape, with `100100`, `100101`, `101001` or `101010` from the head on.

**Larger windows (worker 2, second round).** `python3 -m bbtools.ctl 1RB0LE_0LC0RA_1LE0RD_0RB---_0LF0LD_1LA0LF K` for larger K: K = 7: closes, 2112 abstract configurations where a halt can fire; K = 8: closes, 6144 abstract configurations where a halt can fire; K = 9: no closed set within the default 100 rounds. So larger windows do not remove the halting contexts (their number grows with K) and soon stop closing: this route gave no certificate. These machines are counters, whose halting depends on a global count, which a language of K-cell windows cannot see; a certificate will have to use the macro structure.

## Events (worker 2, third round)

At an *event* the head is in state A just right of the 14-cell word `ENGINE = 00101001010100`:

    0^inf [left counter] ENGINE A> 0 PREFIX [M] rest

- left counter: binary, 3-cell digits `000` = 0, `100` = 1, most significant first (the "gaps" of the first look);
- `PREFIX`: the 46-cell word `1001010100100101010010001001000100100010101010` (in this stretch of the run); inside an
  event the head turns at several places inside it, rewriting and restoring it, and it comes back unchanged;
- M: two 4-cell digits `1000` = 0, `1010` = 1, lowest first.

**Event rule (Lean, every k).** For M = 0, 1, 2: the left counter gets 1 (carry over k ones: `0 0 0 (1 0 0)^k` ->
`1 0 0 (0 0 0)^k`, also when the ones reach the left end of the tape) and M gets 1, in 8k + 608 (M = 1: 8k + 616) steps,
for every tape left of the counter's changing digits and right of M. `lean/Events.lean` (6 theorems, generated by
`python3 py/events.py H36869 > lean/Events.lean`).

**In the run** (`python3 py/track.py 3000000`): events of this shape start at step 78,415 (left counter 32, M = 1); 110
consecutive events add 1 to both counters (27 of them wraps of M) up to left counter 142; then the PREFIX is rebuilt.
Example: at steps 100,465, 101,089, 101,705, 102,321, 102,979 the left counter reads 1000011, 1000100, 1000101,
1000110, 1000111 and M = 0, 1, 2, 3, 0.

**Open.** When M wraps, a slower counter right of M changes (4-cell words `0101`, `0100` and others; not decoded),
and now and then the PREFIX is rebuilt (the drops of the left-end count above). A halt (D reading 1) can only come in
those steps, never inside a non-wrapping event. Next steps: decode the word right of M and the wrap of M as a rule,
then the rebuilding of PREFIX.

## The whole run as one counter system (14 November 2026)

This replaces the event picture above by rules that cover the whole run, and reduces the halting question to one
statement about a travelling defect. Everything here is on the **mirrored** tape (read the machine's tape from right to
left; the mirrored table is A: 1LB 0RE, B: 0RC 0LA, C: 1RE 0LD, D: 0LB ---, E: 0RF 0RD, F: 1RA 0RF).

**Visits.** A *visit* of state X at cell q is the stretch of the run from the head arriving at q in state X until it
first reaches q-1. A visit reads and rewrites only cells from q on, so it is a function on the half tape from q on. For
this machine the visits of A and E always end in B, and four functions close up (S, T, U, V any half tape):

    A(0 S)   = 1 S            A(1 S)   = P(1 E(S))
    E(0 0 T) = 1 0 A(T)       E(1 0 T) = 1 0 E(T)       E(0 1 T) = P(0 F(1 T))      E(1 1 T): the machine halts
    F(1 0 U) = 1 0 A(U)       F(1 1 U) = P(1 F(1 U))
    P(c 0 V) = 1 0 E(V)       P(c 1 V) = 0 0 V

**Rounds.** From the tape `0^inf [A] 0 0 S` the machine comes back to `0^inf [A] 0 0 S'` one cell further out, with
`S' = 1 0 A(S)`; S_0 is blank. So the run is the sequence S_{k+1} = 1 0 A(S_k), and the machine halts if and only if
some A(S_k) meets `E(1 1 ...)`. Found and checked against the machine by `c/zvis.cpp` (it derives the rules by local
simulation and compares the tape with direct simulation); `py/zeval.py` evaluates the functions exactly.

**Tokens.** Read S in the words `L = 1010`, `O = 000`, `I = 100`. On a tape made of these words A is a counter:

    A(L W) = L A(A(W))        A(O W) = I W        A(I W) = O A(W)

O and I are the binary digits 0 and 1 (lowest first), and L is a *doubler*: every carry that crosses it becomes two.
On such a tape A never halts and never changes where the L's are.

**The defect.** Each round puts `1 0` in front, which does not fit the words: `x = 10`, or a single `1` or `0` (`z`),
is a *defect*. A defect travels away from the head, rewriting the words it crosses (Ê is E without the `1 0` it always
writes first, E0 is E after a 0):

    A(z W) = 1 W       A(x O W) = O x W      A(x I W) = L O Ê(W)      A(x L W) = L A(A(x W))      A(1 W) = O Ê(W)
    Ê(L W) = L Ê(W)    Ê(O W) = 1 W          Ê(I W) = E0(W)
    E0(O W) = L W      E0(L W) = O A(x W)    E0(I W) = O 1 W          x at the blank end = I,  1 at the blank end = I

These rules follow from the four functions by hand. None of them is a halting case: **a tape with one defect cannot
halt**, and stays a tape with at most one defect. The defect disappears at `1 I O -> O L` (two digits become a doubler)
or when it reaches the blank end. A halt needs `E(1 1 ...)`, which takes two defects next to each other.

**What the run does** (`py/tokz.py`, the token rules run exactly, equal cell for cell to `py/zeval.py` up to round 24):
at the start of every round S is `L^m`, one defect, and then words only. During the round the defect runs through the
tape and disappears at `1 I O -> O L` about 40% of the way into the part behind the two leading runs of L, far from the
blank end; the next round starts with one defect again. The tape gains exactly 4 words per round. This
held in every round from 13 to 4,500 (at round 4,500 the defect ends at word 9,725 of 17,983; the first twelve rounds
are irregular). The part of the tape the defect leaves behind settles: about the first 40% of the tape behind the
leading L's is the same in round k and round k+2.

**Long run.** `c/zvis.cpp` (visit counts kept as sums of powers of two) ran 100,000 rounds without a halt; the visit
counts reach 187,512 bits, and only 7 window rules of A and E are ever used (A: `0`, `100`, `110`, `1010`; E: `10`,
`00`, `010`), never A on `111` or `1011` and never E on `11` or `011`.

**Open.** The machine does not halt if in every round the defect disappears before the round ends. Where it disappears
depends on the digits of the counter behind the settled part, which look irregular, so this is a statement about the
digits of a doubling counter, not proved. If a defect survived a round the next one could catch up with it; only then
is a halt possible.

The twin 1RB0LF_1LC1RC_0RA0LD_0LE1RE_0RF0LC_1RC--- has the same words: at every second visit of its state E to a new
leftmost cell its tape is `0 0 0 1 0 S_k` (`py/twinchk.py`, checked up to k = 18), so it halts or not together with
this machine as far as the common rules go (the equivalence itself is observed, not proved).

    g++ -O2 -o zvis c/zvis.cpp && ./zvis m:1RB0LE_0LC0RA_1LE0RD_0RB---_0LF0LD_1LA0LF A1B,E1B - run 20000 600
    python3 py/zeval.py 24 > words.txt && python3 py/tokz.py 4500 words.txt && python3 py/twinchk.py 30000000 words.txt

## A conserved value, and what is left to prove (14 November 2026, second pass)

**Value.** Give a tape of words the value v with `v(L W) = v(W)/2`, `v(O W) = 2 v(W)`, `v(I W) = 1 + 2 v(W)`, blank = 0
(a digit counts 2^-b, b = the number of L's minus the number of digits before it: 2^b visits reach it for each
visit at the front). With a defect: `v(x W) = F(W)`, `v(1 W) = G(W)`, `v(z W) = G(W) - 1`, where

    F(O W) = 2 F(W) - 1     F(L W) = F(W)/2         F(I W) = (G(W) - 1)/2                       F(blank) = 1
    G(O W) = 2 G(W) - 1     G(L W) = (G(W) - 1)/2   G(I O W) = v(W) - 1    G(I I W) = 4 G(W) - 1    G(I L W) = 4 F(W) + 3    G(blank) = 1

**Every visit of A adds exactly 1 to v**, with or without a defect (each token rule above, by hand; `py/tokv.py` checks
it with exact fractions on every round, no violation). At the start of round k the value is 1/15 up to about
2^(-k/2) (0.0666666666666 at round 100): the far end of the tape is the binary expansion of 1/15, the word
`I O O O` repeated k/8 times (measured), and each round appends one period.

**Where the defect goes** (measured, rounds 100 to 1,500). At the start of a round the tape is `L^m`, the defect,
`L^(m-1)`, a part rich in L's, and then digits only up to the end (about 1.8 k digits). The defect always disappears
at the first `I O` of the digit part or a few tens of words around it. There a digit has b of about 0.45 times the
length of the tape, so 2^b visits arrive: the defect is pushed one word further by every visit and cannot stay.

**The argument, and its gap.** (1) If the defect were still there at the end of a round, with the rewritten words P
behind it, conservation gives 1 = v(P) minus the constants of its steps, at most the sum of 2^-b over the digits it
passed; so it would have to be among the last log2(length) words of the tape. (2) It cannot pass two periods
`I O O O I O O O` alive (`1 I O` ends it; `x I O O O` becomes `L O 1 O O` and the next `I O` ends it). So a tail of
a few more than log2(k)/4 periods, with every digit before the tail having b larger than the length of the tail, forces
the defect to disappear in every round. (3) The tail of the next round is the old one plus the visits that reach
it, and it stays periodic as long as the digits before it have large b. Not proved: **that every digit in the part
rich in L's keeps b above a few times log2(k)**. Measured: b is at least 2m - 5 there (the minimum is 10 words in).
Bookkeeping of the worst case only gives that b does not decrease over two rounds. That is the one missing lemma
for a proof that this machine and its twin never halt; nothing of this second pass is in Lean.

**Third pass: where the lemma stops.** Two more facts hold by hand: a pass of the defect keeps the number of words of
any stretch it crosses, and changes (digits minus L's) of the stretch by 0 or 2 either way. With them the lemma
follows from one inequality: the digit-only end of the tape (Z, about 1.8 k digits) must keep more than half of all
digits, |Z| >= k + 2 log2 k + 20 (the tape has 2k - 6 digits). Z gains 4 digits per round at its far end and loses
the digits the defect crosses in Z before it disappears. Measured in rounds 40 to 4,000: none in 2,711 rounds,
otherwise 2 to 39, on average 2.2 (largest: 39 in round 1,808). The defect crosses `O` and pairs `I I` and stops at
the first other pattern, so the loss is decided by the low digits of Z, which are irregular; in the worst case it
is not bounded, and on the regular `I O O O` pattern alone it is exactly 4 per round, equal to the gain. A proof
therefore needs a statement about the average of irregular digits (2.2 against 4), which I do not know how to prove;
the induction through the regular tail loses log2 k at every step. The halting question of this machine and its twin
stays open, with this as the precise obstacle.

## Fourth pass (18 November 2026): what the missing lemma really asks

**The digits of Z behave like random digits; the margin comes from elsewhere.** `py/reach.py 7500` follows the
defect of every round from 100 to 7,500. Z is the digit-only end of the round's starting tape, and e the word where
the defect disappears minus the first word of Z (e >= 0: it got into Z and ate e words; Z then starts e + 2 words
further on).

- The defect gets into Z in only 32% of the rounds. Whether it does looks like an independent coin: the share is
  31% right after a round in which it did, 32% right after one in which it did not, 32% after three in which it
  did not.
- When it gets into Z it eats 4.8 words on average (largest 37), with a tail that falls by a factor of 0.82
  per word. That is what random digits give: the defect crosses `O` (chance 1/2) and `I I` (chance 1/4) and stops
  at `I O` (chance 1/4), so it survives n words with chance about 0.809^n (0.809 = (1 + sqrt 5)/4), mean 4.
- Z loses 2.15 words per round on average and gains 4, so |Z| is about 1.85 k (smallest |Z|/k after round 100:
  1.24).

So the statement to prove is not that the digits at the bottom of Z are biased: by this statistic they are not. It is that
the defect gets through the far end of the part rich in doublers in fewer than about half of the rounds: the lemma
|Z_k| >= k + 2 log2 k + 20 holds as long as the loss averages below 3 words per round over every stretch of rounds
from the start, against 2.15 measured. In the worst case one round's loss is unbounded (a bottom of Z made of `O`s
and pairs `I I` lets the defect run on), so the lemma cannot come from the token rules alone; it is a statement about
pseudo-random digits of the same kind as for Antihydra. No proof here, and no counterexample: in every round from 13
to 7,500 the defect disappeared by `1 I O -> O L`, never at the blank end (and `c/zvis.cpp` saw no halt in 100,000
rounds).

**Odds, under the random-digit model.** A defect alive at the end of a round would have to be among the last
log2(length) words of the tape (the conserved value), so it would have crossed all of Z first, about 1.3 k words of
irregular digits: chance about 0.809^(1.3 k) = 2^(-0.4 k), below 2^(-40,000) for every round after 100,000. A halt
needs that and then a second defect catching up with the first. For Z to shrink below k the loss would have to
average 3 instead of 2.15 over a stretch of about k rounds, which for independent rounds has a chance falling
exponentially in k. Heuristically the machine and its twin do not halt.

**Lean (kernel), `lean/Tokens.lean`.** It takes the four visit functions A, E, F, P as the definition (half tapes are
infinite 0/1 sequences, `BV`) and proves:

- `sound`: every token rule of "The defect" above (the counter rules for `L`, `O`, `I`, the rules of the defect
  `x`, `1`, `z`, and the re-readings at the blank end; `TV`) is a computation of the four functions. None of them uses
  the halting case `E(1 1 T)`.
- `conserve`: every visit of A derived by the token rules adds exactly 1 to the value v, on any token tape; the
  visits Ê and E0 have values (G(W) + 1)/2 and (G(I W) + 1)/2. This replaces the hand proof and the numerical check
  of the second pass.

Not in Lean: that the machine's visits are these four functions (found and checked by `c/zvis.cpp`), and that the
token rules apply to every tape with one defect (the hand argument that such a tape cannot halt).

## Fifth pass (22 November 2026): one defect in Lean, a fast engine, and the layers of the counter

**A tape with at most one defect cannot halt (Lean, kernel), `lean/Total.lean`.** On top of `Tokens.lean`:

- `det`: the token rules are deterministic.
- `total`: on every token tape with at most one defect (`Good`: words `L`, `O`, `I` and at most one of `x`, `1`, `z`,
  anywhere), any number of visits of A in a row are derived by the token rules, and every tape in between has at
  most one defect again. The proof is an induction on the length of the tape behind the defect, with the counter
  rules lifted over the words in front of it (`run_L`, `run_O`, `fine_cons`).
- `total_bits`: the same at the bit level: each of these visits is a computation of the four visit functions,
  none of which is the halting case `E(1 1 T)`.

This replaces the hand argument of the 14 November notes. Within a round a halt therefore needs two defects, that
is a defect that survived its own round. Still not in Lean: the link between the four functions and the transition
table (checked by `c/zvis.cpp`), and that every defect dies in its round.

**The fast engine, `c/toksim.cpp`.** The same token rules as `py/tokz.py`, with three changes that make a round
cost about the length of the tape: each carry (a number of visits) is a run-length coded bit string, lowest bit
first, so a doubler, a digit or a defect step is O(1) whatever its size, and a run of L's is skipped at once; the
digit-only end Z is one big number to which an arriving carry is added in one addition; and once the defect has
died, the carries still on their way are walked to the end together (on a tape without defects carries commute).
It gives the same tape as `py/tokz.py` after every round (compared token for token at round 3,000, and with the
plain version of the engine at rounds 8,000 and 30,000), about a thousand times faster: 100,000 rounds in two
minutes.

    g++ -O2 -o toksim c/toksim.cpp && ./toksim ROUNDS 10000 -1 rec.bin && python3 py/rounds_stats.py rec.bin

two defects ever met. The largest carry of the last round has 944,371 bits, so the run covers more than

**The value, term by term (`py/vasym.py`, exact fractions to round 420).**

with |r_k| < 64 * 2^-k from round 80 on. r_k 2^k settles to -33.68959699240... (even k) and -42.12560163496...
(odd k), which are not small fractions, and still moves by about 2^(-k/2) from one round to the next: the next term
is of size 2^(-3k/2), and there the value stops being regular.

**Four layers of the digit end (`py/layers.py`).** The far end of the tape holds the largest digits of v, so it is
the binary expansion of these terms. Read lowest digit first, Z is four layers (round 50,000; round 8,000 alike):

| layer (from the top) | digits | ratio to k |
|---|---|---|
| period 4: the expansion of 1/15 | 24,996 | 0.50 |
| period 28: 1/15 minus a multiple of 1/127 | 24,997 | 0.50 |
| frozen: the same digits in round 50,056 (and from round 8,000 to 16,008) | 29,782 | 0.60 |
| irregular | 13,402 | 0.27 |

The defect only ever eats the irregular layer at the bottom. The three regular layers are about 1.6 k digits, far
more than the k + 2 log2 k + 20 the third pass needs, so the missing lemma would follow from two statements:

1. the regular layers are always as observed (they should follow from the first two terms of the value, which
   would need the value to 2^(-3k/2), that is control of the irregular digits' contribution to v), and
2. no defect crosses the whole irregular layer, about 0.27 k words, in a single round.

Statement 2 is still about pseudo-random digits (the defect crosses `O` and pairs `I I` and stops at the first odd
run of `I` followed by `O`; on random digits it survives n words with chance about 0.809^n, and the most it ever
ate is 60 words). Neither statement is proved, so the machine and its twin stay open.

## Sixth pass (22 November 2026, later): the machine itself, in Lean

**The four visit functions are the machine (Lean, kernel), `lean/Link.lean`.** Until now `BV` was a definition,
checked against the transition table only by `c/zvis.cpp`. `Link.lean` proves the link on the generated table
`H36869.trans`:

- `link`: every computation of the four visit functions is a run of the machine. A visit of `X` on the half tape
  `S` (the tape from the head leftwards, head cell first) starts with the head on `S 0` in state `X` and ends with
  the head one cell to the right of `S` in state B, for any tape to the right, which it never reads. P is the visit
  of B entered with the cell under the head already 0. Each of the nine equations is a short run (one to six steps)
  around the visits it calls.
- `round`: from `0 S` with blank tape to the right, in state A, the machine reaches `0 1 0 A(S)`, again with blank
  tape to the right, in state A (six steps, the visit E(0 0 S), one step). From blank tape `S = []`.
- `nonhalt_of_clean`: if in every round from blank tape the visit of A leaves a tape without defect, the machine
  never halts from blank tape. The rounds are `Reach` (`S_0 = []`, `S_(k+1) = x A(S_k)`); the step from a round to
  the next uses `total` of `Total.lean`.

So everything except one statement is now in the kernel: that the defect `x` put in front by each round is gone
when the round's visit of A ends. That is the inequality of the earlier passes, and it is still about pseudo-random
digits. **[Correction, seventh pass: as stated, counting rounds from blank tape, this hypothesis is false (round 2's
defect survives), so `nonhalt_of_clean` is vacuous. `lean/Fuel.lean` proves that and gives the reduction from round 9
on, `nonhalt_of_need`.]**

**The twin's round law on any tape (trusted).** The correspondence with the twin 1RB0LF_1LC1RC_0RA0LD_0LE1RE_0RF0LC_1RC---
is not tied to the run: started in E on `0 0 0 1 0 S` with blank tape to the left, the twin reaches `0 0 0 1 0 S'`
with `S' = 1 0 A(S)` at its second visit of E to a new leftmost cell, for every one of 2,000 random tapes S of up to
30 cells on which A(S) is defined, and it halts on exactly the 483 tapes on which A(S) reaches `E(1 1 ...)`. Its own
visits close up on the token words with five entry forms (state and the two cells to the left): B after `1 0`
computes A, C after `1 0` computes E, B after `0 0` computes E on `0 S`, C after `1 1` gives E with `0 0` in front
instead of `1 0`, and A after `0 1` gives E on `0 S` without its first cell. With these every token rule of this
machine is a run of the twin (forms checked on random tapes, rules run symbolically: `py/twinforms.py`; not in
Lean). The twin
works the defect rule `x L` differently (through E on the tail), so a Lean proof needs its own token rules or a
bridge between the two derivations. **Update (later on 22 November 2026):** done, without new rules. The twin's
`lean/Link.lean` (`../1RB0LF_1LC1RC_0RA0LD_0LE1RE_0RF0LC_1RC---/lean/Link.lean`) proves every token rule `TV` of this folder as a run of the twin's own table from those entry forms;
the `x L` rule runs this machine's way after all (the twin meets B after `1 0` on `x W` four cells in). With
`Total.lean` the twin never halts under the same hypothesis as `nonhalt_of_clean` here
(`K83189.nonhalt_of_clean`).

**Checkpoints for the engine.** `c/toksim.cpp` now writes its state every `TOKSIM_CKPT_EVERY` rounds (default 50,000)
to the file named by `TOKSIM_CKPT`, and `TOKSIM_RESUME=FILE` continues from such a file, appending to the record file. A run to
2,000,000 rounds is under way.

## Seventh pass (22 November 2026, evening): what a defect needs, and a correction

**Correction: the old reduction was vacuous.** `nonhalt_of_clean` (sixth pass) assumes that in every round counted
from blank tape the defect is gone when the round's visit of A ends. That is false in round 2: `S_2 = x L O I` and
`A(x L O I) = L I x I`, so round 3 starts with two defects. `lean/Fuel.lean` proves this (`old_hyp_false`). The
early rounds are not one-defect tapes (round 8's defect survives too); the token picture of the earlier passes
starts at round 9, where `py/tokz.py` and the machine agree. The twin's `K83189.nonhalt_of_clean` (pull request 416) assumes the same
hypothesis and is vacuous for the same reason; it needs the same correction from a later round.

**The corrected reduction (Lean, kernel), `lean/Fuel.lean`.**

- `round9`: from blank tape the machine reaches the start of round 9, `0 x a9` with blank tape to the right in state
  A, at step 5,848; `a9 = L L L L O L L L I L I L I L O O I O I I` is the clean tape round 8 leaves (the list
  simulator of `tools/lean/TM/Fin.lean`, evaluated by the kernel).
- `nx`, `ne`, `n0`, and `fuel`: **the fuel a defect needs.** `nx W` is a number of visits of A after which `x W` (W
  clean) has no defect left; `ne W` and `n0 W` are the same for the tapes `Eh W` and `E0 W` leave. They are a plain
  recursion over the words of W, read from the token rules:

      nx [] = 1      nx (O W) = 2 nx W + 1      nx (L W) = ceil(nx W / 2)      nx (I W) = ne W + 1
      ne [] = 0      ne (O W) = 2 ne W + 1      ne (L W) = ceil(ne W / 2)      ne (I W) = n0 W
      n0 [] = 0      n0 (O W) = 0               n0 (L W) = 2 (nx W - 1)         n0 (I W) = 2 (2 ne W + 1)

  `fuel` proves (induction on W, using that n visits on `L r` are 2n visits on r and n visits on a digit in front of
  r are floor(n/2) or ceil(n/2) visits on r) that this many visits always leave a clean tape, for `x W`, `1 W`
  (`2 ne W + 1` visits), `Eh W` and `E0 W`.
- `dies`: if `nx W <= 1`, the round's single visit kills the defect of `x W`. `nonhalt_of_need`: **if `nx W <= 1` for
  the round tape `x W` of every round from 9 on, the machine never halts from blank tape.** `need_a9`: round 9 is
  fine.

So the open statement is now a property of each round's starting tape alone, decided by one pass of a recursion
over its words, without running any visit.

**The margin.** Normalized, a round tape is `L^m x U` (or `L^m z U`), so `nx (L^m U) = ceil(nx U / 2^m)` and the
defect dies as soon as `nx U <= 2^m`. Measured (`py/fuel.py`): the need is 3 or 4 visits in every round from 13 to
3,000 (26 in round 12, the smallest margin: m = 6), and 3 at rounds 20,000, 50,000 and 300,000 (dumps of
`c/toksim.cpp`), while the defect gets 2^m visits with m = k/2. The defect is pushed about 2^(k/2) times harder than
it needs to be.

**Why the irregular layer is not the question.** The defect's path is fixed by the words ahead of it: the fuel only
decides when it stops, never where it turns or which words it rewrites. Among digits it crosses `O` and pairs `I I`
and dies at the first odd run of `I` followed by `O`, or at the blank end. It can only stop for lack of fuel after
crossing words of small height b (b = L's minus digits before the word): the potential fuel·2^(-b) starts at 1 and
drops by at most 2·2^(-b) per step of the defect (`py/potential.py`, exact fractions, all 329,061 defect steps of
rounds 6 to 1,500), so it reaches 0 only after the sum of 2^(-b) over the crossed words is at least 1/2. Up to where it dies
every height is at least m (the smallest is the word right after the defect), and the words of Z have heights from
|Z| down to 0 at the far end. A defect that crossed the whole irregular layer (about 0.27 k words) would still carry
about 2^(1.5 k) visits and would die in the frozen or period-4 layers above it, which contain `I O`. So item 9 of the
lead list ("no defect crosses the irregular layer in one round") is not needed for the defect to die: crossing it
only costs words of Z. What a proof needs is that the heights in front of the death point never collapse.

**Heights never drop over two rounds (observed, not proved).** Let h_k(p) be the height at token index p at the
start of round k. In every round k from 40 to 3,000, h_(k+2)(p) >= h_k(p) for every index p up to where round k's
defect died, and the same holds for 19,991 random tapes `L^m x W` (`py/fuel.py`). One pass alone moves the height
at every index it crosses by exactly 1, up or down. If this monotonicity is a theorem, the words near the front keep
their heights forever, and what is left is how high new words enter the part the defect crosses, that is the depth
of Z where the defect dies. A rough count then asks only that Z keeps a fixed fraction of the tape (loss of Z below
4 − ε words per round on average, against 2.11 measured), weaker than the third pass's |Z| >= k + 2 log2 k + 20
(loss below 3). That count is a sketch, and Z's loss is still a statement about the irregular digits, so the
machine stays open.

    python3 py/fuel.py 3000 20000          # margins, and the two-round height check (about 40 s)
    python3 py/fuel.py --dump dump.txt     # the margin of one round-start tape written by c/toksim.cpp
    python3 py/potential.py 800            # fuel against height, every defect step
