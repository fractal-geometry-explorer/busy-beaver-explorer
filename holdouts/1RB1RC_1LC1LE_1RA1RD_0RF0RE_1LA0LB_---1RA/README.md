# 1RB1RC_1LC1LE_1RA1RD_0RF0RE_1LA0LB_---1RA

bbchallenge id 174634, called a Hydra/Antihydra variant by mxdys (July 2024). **It halts iff an Antihydra-like
map — a number multiplied by about 3/2 each round, driving a counter up by 2 on even values and down by 1 on odd
ones — ever drives the counter below zero; this reduction is proved in Lean (kernel).** The counter has never gone
below 1 and is at 50,008 after 10⁵ rounds; a probability argument says it never returns.

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 1RC |
| B | 1LC | 1LE |
| C | 1RA | 1RD |
| D | 0RF | 0RE |
| E | 1LA | 0LB |
| F | --- | 1RA |

## What it does

Every *round* starts with the head in state A on the blank cell at the left end of the tape. The tape is

    R n m  =  A> 0 11 (01)^n 0 11 (01)^m        (a number n, then a counter m)
    Z k    =  A> 0 11 (01)^k                    (the counter used up)

Within a round the head sweeps back and forth between the left end and the first `11`: each sweep removes two
copies of `01` right of the `11` and writes three copies left of it, so the `11` walks right through the number
while the number left of it grows by 3/2. When fewer than two copies are left, the round ends:

| round start | n even | n odd, m ≥ 1 | n odd, m = 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `R n m` | `R (3n/2 + 2) (m + 2)` | `R ((3n+7)/2) (m − 1)` | `Z ((3n+9)/2)` |

and `Z k` (no counter block) goes to `R ((3k+1)/2) 1` for odd `k` and **halts** for even `k` (the head runs off the
right end in state F, which has no transition on a blank). Writing `Z k` as `R (k − 1) (−1)`, the whole rule is
one line: **`n ↦ 3n/2 + 2` and `m ↦ m + 2` for even `n`, `n ↦ (3n + 7)/2` and `m ↦ m − 1` for odd `n`; the machine
halts when an odd `n` meets `m = −1`.** With `a = n + 4` the number follows `a ↦ 3a/2` (even) and `a ↦ 3(a + 1)/2`
(odd), the Antihydra map up to the odd branch's constant.

From blank tape the machine reaches `Z 1` (`n = 0`, `m = −1`) in 10 steps and the orbit is

    (0,−1) → (2,1) → (5,3) → (11,2) → (20,1) → (32,3) → (50,5) → (77,7) → (119,6) → (182,5) → (275,7) → …

The number `n` is written on the left part of the tape and grows by a factor 3/2 a round, while a round costs
about `n²` steps (a sweep crosses the number, and there are `n/2` sweeps): the left side of the tape grows like the
square root of the step count. The counter on the right moves by +2 or −1 a round, about +1/2 on average: the right
side grows like the logarithm of the step count. That is the exp/sqrt shape of the triage, mirrored.

## Does it halt?

It halts iff the counter, which goes up by 2 for every even `n` and down by 1 for every odd `n`, ever gets below
zero, that is iff the odd values of the orbit ever outnumber twice the even ones (plus the start offset).

* **Proved (Lean, kernel only):** the whole reduction, `halts_iff'` in `lean/Reduction.lean`: the machine halts from
  blank tape iff the orbit of `next` from `Z 1` reaches `Z k` with `k` even. Behind it, `round` and `round_halt` in
  `lean/Macro.lean` (every round, for all `n`, `m`), built from 8 symbolic rules in `lean/Rules.lean` (generated by
  `lean/rules.py` with `tools/py/bbtools/symtm.py`) and an induction on the number of sweeps (`pass_run`).
  Axioms: `propext`, `Quot.sound`, `Classical.choice`.
* **Proved (Lean, kernel):** the first 10,000 rounds do not halt (`first_rounds`, `decide +kernel` on the map; `n`
  then has about 5,800 bits).
* **Computed:** 10⁵ rounds of the map (`py/orbit.py 1e5`): the counter is never below 1 (its lowest values are 1,
  at rounds 1 and 4) and stands at 50,008, with 49,997 odd rounds against 50,003 even ones. The first 19 round
  starts of a direct 10⁸-step simulation agree with the map (`py/orbit.py 1000 --check 1e8`), and the rule was
  checked against direct simulation for all `n < 40`, `m < 6`.
* **Heuristic:** if the parities of `n` behave like fair coin flips (as for Antihydra, Hydra and the Collatz map),
  the counter is a random walk with steps +2 and −1, drift +1/2, and the chance that it ever falls from `m` to −2 is
  `φ^(−(m+2))` with `φ = 1.618…` (`1/φ` is the root below one of `q³ − 2q + 1 = 0`, the chance of ever falling by one). From the counter's position
  after round 100 (50) that is about 10⁻¹¹, from 50,008 about 10⁻¹⁰⁴⁵⁰. Probviously non-halting, but a proof would
  need control of the parity sequence of an Antihydra-like orbit, which is out of reach of known methods.

## Files

- `lean/Rules.lean` (generated by `lean/rules.py`): the symbolic rules of a round, for all values of the counts.
- `lean/Macro.lean`: the sweeps by induction, `next`, `round`, `round_halt`.
- `lean/Reduction.lean`: the orbit from blank tape, `halts_iff`, `halts_iff'`, `first_rounds`.
- `py/orbit.py`: the map, its long iteration, and the check against direct simulation.
