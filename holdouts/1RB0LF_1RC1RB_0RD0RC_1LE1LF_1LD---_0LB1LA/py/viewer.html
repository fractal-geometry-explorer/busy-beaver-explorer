One row per phase start (phase 1 at the top), the counter word right-aligned so that its right end, where it meets the block, is at the right edge; one pixel a cell (scroll left for the rest). Zeros are dark, ones light; the orange row is the phase chosen above (click a row to choose it). Each dark diagonal is a separator 0^3 between blocks: the tail of the word moves one cell to the right every phase (its last block loses a 1), so the separators drift right, and where one reaches the right edge an event (c = 10) follows and neighbouring blocks merge (the lines that stop). The ragged top edge is the part on the left that changes in every phase.
