## What it does

A list of counters with two alternating regimes. Both ends of the tape grow like the square root of the step count.

**Where it can halt.** Only in F reading a 1. F is entered only from `E>1` (1LF), so the machine halts exactly when
`1 E>1` occurs. In the first 10^8 steps F is entered 687,974 times, always onto a 0, all but 38 times inside
`101[0]110` (`c/haltwatch.c`).

**The right-end regime.** At the right end in state A the tape is

    T a ks m  =  (10)^(a+2) 1 (10)^(k_n+2) ... 1 (10)^(k_1+2) (1101010)^(m+1) A>0

(`ks = [k_1, ..., k_n]` from the right). One round trip: going left, every block `(10)^(k+2) 1` becomes
`1 (10)^k 0 1 1 0 0` (`crossL`); at the left end one new cell (`turnL`); going right, every block comes back as
`1 (10)^(k+2)` (`crossR`), one cell further left; at the right end the tail gains a word (`rightEnd`, `rightEnd2`).
So `T (a+1) ks m -> T a ks (m+1)`, in `4a + 24m + 57 + sum (4k + 20)` steps.

**The left-end phase: a local rewriting system.** At a *left-A record* (the head on a new leftmost cell in state A,
blank to the left), read the tape from the head as units `(01)^b 0 0110`, then a maximal `(01)^a`, then a frontier
word `w`, then a rest `S`. One round trip goes right over the units, turns at the frontier and comes back. If the
first unit has `b >= 2` and so do the others, the first unit loses one `01`, the other units and `S` are unchanged,
and `(01)^a w` becomes

| `w` | after a unit | no unit in front |
|---|---|---|
| `1` (G1) | `(01)^(a+2) 0` | `(01)^(a+1) 0` |
| `00101` (G2) | `(01)^(a+4) 1` | `(01)^(a+3) 1` |
| `001001` (K) | `(01)^(a+5)` | `(01)^(a+4)` |
| `0001` (H) | `(01)^a 00110011` | `(01)^(a-1) 00110011` |
| `0000` (X0) | `(01)^a 00110010` | `(01)^(a-1) 00110010` |
| `001000` (X1) | `(01)^(a+4) 00` | `(01)^(a+3) 00` |

(For `a >= 3`. The cases `a = 0, 1, 2` are separate rules. Seven of them right after a unit start a new unit and do
not have this form; see below.) After a maximal `(01)^a` the next cells are `1` or `00`, so these words, a unit
`00110`, and `00111` (never seen in the run) are all the cases. The earlier pump/cursor picture is this system at work.
The cursor `(01)^c (10)^d 0110` is the frontier `1` in front of `(01)^(d-1) 0 0110`, and two trips take `c` to `c+6`
and `d` to `d-1`. When `d` is used up, H or K turns the cursor into a new unit and the next block becomes the cursor.
With no unit in front, the same trips are the pump.

**Phases** (`py/phases.py 3e8`): the right-end regime runs at steps 2,889-7,790 (a = 13), 35,911-263,899 (a = 108,
2 blocks), 7,677,126-21,453,756 (a = 714, 125 blocks), 36,507,005-48,506,720 (a = 295, 123 blocks) and from
250,823,121 (a = 7299, 121 blocks; still running at 3·10^8). The left-end phases lie in between. The tail survives
them (m = 714, 717, 1012, 1015, ...).

  `a` and every rest `R`, after a unit (`tu*`, `su*`: the turn) and with no unit (`te*`, `se*`: the whole trip).
- `lean/Frontier.lean`: `tripGen`: if the head, just right of a unit, turns at `In` and leaves `1 1 Out`, then for

  `A> Un (x+1) (UL bs (0 1 0 1 0 1 Out))`. The 17 `trip_tu*`/`trip_su*` theorems apply it to each turn rule.

## Computed / observed

- No halt in 10^9 steps (`tools/c/triage.c`).
- Coverage (`py/coverage.py 3e7`): of the 5,490 left-A records before step 3·10^7, 2,105 trips are a `te*`/`se*`
  rule, 2,509 a `trip_tu*`/`trip_su*` theorem, 835 lie in a right-end regime (`trip`), and 41 are not covered.
  Of these, 32 have the first unit used up (`b <= 1`; the next unit then takes over), 7 are the small cases
  `G1, a = 0` and `X0, a = 1` right after a unit, which start a new unit, and 2 are early trips with a unit `b <= 1`.
- `c/leftrec.c run 300000000`: none of the 17,518 left-A records in 3·10^8 steps has a factor `1111` on the tape.
  `c/leftrec.c all 30`: from every tape of at most 30 cells without `1111` (387,559,437 tapes) the trip reaches the
  next left-A record without halting, in at most 138 steps; every one-trip halt from a tape of at most 16 cells needs
  a `1111`. About 4% of those trips create a `1111`, so this set of tapes is not closed by itself.
- The phase list above (`py/phases.py`), halt-entry counts (`c/haltwatch.c`), snapshots (`py/snapshots.py`).

## Open: what remains

A non-halting proof needs:

1. **Boundary trips.** Rules for the trips the theorems do not cover: the first unit at `b <= 1` (x-exhaustion: the
   next unit becomes the first, or with no unit left the head works at the frontier directly); the seven small
   frontier cases right after a unit (`G1` at `a = 0`, `H` and `X0` at `a = 0, 1, 2`); units with `b <= 1` outside
   the right-end regime; and the switch from the left-end phase into a right-end regime and back (the right-end
   regime's trips are right-end records; its first and last trips are not covered).
2. **An invariant on the tapes that arise.** It must be strong enough that every trip is a covered rule or a boundary
   trip. It is not enough to rewrite the frontier: the frontier moves into the rest `S`, which was written by earlier
   phases, so the invariant must describe the whole list. A sufficient conjecture: *every left-A record has no factor
   `1111`, and from every tape without `1111` the head reaches the next left-A record*. Both halves are checked as
   above, and neither is proved.

The map from one right-end regime to the next (first counters 13, 108, 714, 295, 7299, ...) comes from this
rewriting acting on a list it keeps changing. It looks open-ended, so a proof will most likely need a regular
invariant, of the kind closed-tape-language deciders search for, rather than a closed-form regime map.
