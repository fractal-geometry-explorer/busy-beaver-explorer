# 1RB1LC_1LA0RE_0LD---_1LB1LD_1RB1RF_0LA0RB

bbchallenge id 107251. One of the six-state holdouts whose halting from blank tape is open (group G09, visited tape growing like the square root of the step count on both sides).

**In one sentence.** Sweeps back and forth over a period-11 background (01010111101 and its rightward form 10101010010) with a soup of (01)^k, 1^3, 1^4 pieces; the clean zone at the left end grows; a halt needs B or F to read 0 just right of 1 1.

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 1LC |
| B | 1LA | 0RE |
| C | 0LD | --- |
| D | 1LB | 1LD |
| E | 1RB | 1RF |
| F | 0LA | 0RB |

## What it does

Rightward, B and E (with F) cross the period-11 word whole: E turns `01010111101` into `10101010010`, B turns `10101111010` into `01010100101`; they also swap `01` and `10` and turn `11101` into `10010`. Leftward, A, B, C and D take the rightward forms back (37 steps per copy), and C crosses `10` unchanged.

At the left end there is a clean zone of the period-11 word (`(10100101010)^91` at the right end of a sweep after 4·10^6 steps) that grows; the rest of the tape is a soup of `(01)^k` with k = 4, 7, 10, 13, runs `1^3 0 1^4` and short copies of the period-11 word.

## Sweep rules (Lean, kernel)

`lean/Rules.lean`, generated by `py/rules.py` with `tools/py/bbtools/symtm.py`; each holds for every n and every tape L, R.

|---|---|---|---|

## Halting

The machine halts only when C reads a 1, and Lean proves that the only ways there are B or F reading a 0 with `1 1` just left of it (B writes 1 or F writes 0, steps left, A reads the first 1 and steps left again, and C meets the second 1; `halt_needs_pattern`, `lean/Halt.lean`).

In 10^9 steps B read a 0 with a 1 on its left about 4.2·10^7 times, and every time the cell before that 1 was a 0; nothing known rules out a second 1 there.

## Status

Open. Proved in Lean, kernel only: the halting configurations (`step_none_iff`, `lean/Blank.lean`), no halt in 10,000 steps, the 12 sweep rules (`lean/Rules.lean`), and that every halt from blank tape passes through the pattern above (`halt_needs_pattern`, `lean/Halt.lean`). With `native_decide`: no halt in 10,000,000 steps. C: no halt in 1,000,000,000 steps.
