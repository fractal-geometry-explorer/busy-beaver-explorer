## What it does

The right end of the tape stops growing early (21 cells right of the start); everything happens on the left. The
tape is `1 0^X T`: a lone 1 at the left end, a **gap** of X zeros, and a short **tail** T of blocks of ones.

* **Phase start:** `0^inf B> 0 1 0^X T`.
* **Rounds:** `0^inf 1 C> (11)^m 000 1111 0^(g+4) T -> 0^inf 1 C> (11)^(m+3) 000 1111 0^g T` in 6m+44 steps: the head
  runs right over the block of ones, pushes the marker `000 1111` four cells into the gap, and returns to the left
  end, where the tape grows by two cells. A gap X = 4k+r+7 is used up in k+1 rounds, leaving
  `0^inf 1 C> (11)^(3k+3) 000 1111 0^r T` with r = 1 or 3.
* **Phase end:** the head meets the tail. The block of about 3X/2 ones is erased again except for a lone 1 (F and D
  erase it, three steps per cell) and becomes the next gap; the first blocks of the tail are rewritten. Observed:
  **X' = 3X/2 + d**, with d ∈ {+6, −6} when X ≡ 0 (mod 4) and d ∈ {+3, −3, −9} when X ≡ 2 (mod 4), d chosen by
  the first blocks of the tail.

## Status

Open. The halting transition (F reads 0) can only be met during a phase end, while the head is in the tail.
Whether it ever is depends on the joint evolution of the gap parity X mod 4 and the tail, a Collatz-like iteration
with a growing memory.

* Lean (kernel), `lean/Phase.lean`: `phase` — from `0^inf B> 0 1 0^(4k+r+7) T` (gap ≥ 24, any T) the machine
  reaches `0^inf 1 C> (11)^(3k+3) 000 1111 0^r T`; by induction (`rounds`) on the rules `start` and `round` of
  `lean/Rules.lean` (generated by `lean/rules.py` with `tools/py/bbtools/symtm.py`).
* Trusted (`c/phase.c`, `py/tailmap.py`): one phase simulated from `0^inf B> 0 1 0^X T` gives X' = (3X+d)/2 and T',
  and (d, T') depends only on (X mod 4, T) (four gaps per residue class for every tail met). Iterating this table
  from X = 6, T = `1^8` reproduces the 35 phases below and goes on for 2,009 phases without a halt (gap of 1,179
  bits, tail of 12,469 cells in 1169 blocks). X stays even. Trying the three other residues at each phase: 0 halting
  outcomes in 6,000; no halting entry of the table has been seen at all.
* Computed (`py/phases.py`, macro simulator): 35 phases, no halt in 3.9·10¹³ steps; the gap is then 9,304,044.
* Lean (`lean/Blank.lean`): no halt in 10⁷ steps; C: no halt in 10⁹ steps.

## For later

* The tail is rewritten at its front by rules with parameters, read off the orbit (not proved), for instance
  `1^a 0^b -> 1^(a+12) 0^(b-6)` (X = 2 mod 4) and `-> 1^11 0^(a-3) 1^4 0^(b-4)` (X = 0 mod 4) for odd a >= 11;
  `1^4 0^b -> 1^4 0^3 1^4 0^(b-7)` (X = 0) and `-> 1^11 0^3 1^4 0^(b-4)` (X = 2). With b >= 8 they cover 1,440
  of the first 2,000 table phases without exception; other rules apply when the block of zeros is shorter. Prove them with symtm (the first part of a phase is already `phase` in `lean/Phase.lean`),
  find a set of tails closed under both parities, and check whether any rule in it reaches F reading 0. Since no
  halting entry has been seen even off the orbit, a proof of non-halting by such a closed set is the plausible way.
