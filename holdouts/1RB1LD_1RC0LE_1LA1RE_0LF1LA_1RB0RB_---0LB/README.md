# 1RB1LD_1RC0LE_1LA1RE_0LF1LA_1RB0RB_---0LB

bbchallenge id 6741. One of the six-state holdouts whose halting from blank tape is open.

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 1LD |
| B | 1RC | 0LE |
| C | 1LA | 1RE |
| D | 0LF | 1LA |
| E | 1RB | 0RB |
| F | --- | 0LB |

## What it does

**A counter growing into a reserve, beside a conveyor.** In the first 10^9 steps the leftmost cell is last reached
at step 1,072,561. From then on, whenever the head turns in state B just left of the block of ones, the tape reads

    0 a 1^6 a 1^6 a^2 1^12 a^14 1^3 a^k [counter] B> 1^N        (a = 011)

The block of ones grows by 3 at each visit. A tick is a round trip across it: a hole `00` moves right, three cells
every five steps (`hole`), and the head runs back left in states D and A (`run_left`). Then it rewrites the
*counter*, about a hundred cells of ones-runs separated by single 0s. How far back a tick reaches falls off
geometrically, as for a counter's carries (over 5,240 ticks: 2,618 change at most 10 cells, 219 reach 30-39 cells
back, 2 reach 120-129). The counter grows like the logarithm of the time, by eating the reserve `(011)^k` from its
right:

| step | reserve k | counter cells | ones N |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7,374,569 | 481 | 94 | 3,749 |
| 37,090,113 | 476 | 109 | 8,987 |
| 91,194,593 | 474 | 115 | 14,225 |
| 169,686,245 | 472 | 121 | 19,463 |
| 399,835,097 | 470 | 127 | 29,939 |

At about 2 units per factor 2.4 in time, the remaining reserve lasts until about 10^90 steps.

## Rules proved in Lean (`lean/Rules.lean`, generated by `lean/rules.py`)

- `hole`: `L E> 0 0 (111)^n R` reaches `L (111)^n 1 1 C> R` in 5n + 2 steps.
- `run_left`: `L (11)^n D> 1 R` reaches `L D> 1 (11)^n R` in 2n steps.

## Status

**Open.** Proved: the halting configurations, no halt in 10^7 steps (`lean/Blank.lean`), and the two sweeps. The
machine halts only when D reads 0 and the cell to its left is 0 (F reading 0). Deciding it needs the counter's carry
rules: can a carry create that pattern, and what happens when the reserve is gone?

## For later

- The counter's digits and carry rules, as a map on the list of ones-runs.
- Whole ticks in Lean from a real tape with the block of ones of any length. The block's length is 2 mod 3 at one visit and 1 mod 3 at the next,
  so a tick needs the period-6 word `111111` and both phases.
