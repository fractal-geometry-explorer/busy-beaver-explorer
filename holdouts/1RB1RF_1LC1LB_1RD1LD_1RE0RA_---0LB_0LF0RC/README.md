# 1RB1RF_1LC1LB_1RD1LD_1RE0RA_---0LB_0LF0RC

bbchallenge id 279. One of the six-state holdouts whose halting from blank tape is open. Its run is a sequence of
**epochs; in each, two blocks at the growing left end, (011)^n and (1011)^p, grow while they eat the tape to their
right in two proved phases**; the rule that sets up each epoch is not known, halting is open.

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 1RF |
| B | 1LC | 1LB |
| C | 1RD | 1LD |
| D | 1RE | 0RA |
| E | --- | 0LB |
| F | 0LF | 0RC |


## What it does

Write `b = 011`, `c = 1011`, `d = 1110`, `e = 10`, `a = 100`. The right end of the tape has not moved since early in
the run; the left end grows like the square root of the step count. Seen at the moments the head is at the left
end in state D, the tape is

    Y n p R = 0^inf D> 0111011 (011)^n (1011)^p R

and the two blocks grow by eating R, in two phases, both proved in Lean for all n, p:

    phase 1:  Y n p (1111 R)            ->  Y (n+1) (p+1) R                 10n+10p+35 steps   (grow)
    phase 2:  Y n p ((1110)^(k+2) 1 R)  ->  Y (n+2) (p+1) ((1110)^(k+1) 1 R)  20n+20p+86 steps   (eat)

(lean/Rules.lean, generated by py/rules.py with bbtools.symtm; restated and iterated in lean/Phase.lean as `grow'`,
`grows`, `eat'`, `eats`). So a run of 4j ones is eaten in j cycles and a block `(1110)^k` in k cycles, while the
blocks on the left grow and the head's round trips get longer.

**Epochs.** When what is in front of the blocks is used up, the tape is reorganized and a new epoch starts from
`0^inf D> 0111 0 ...` with a short left part and a long periodic middle; at the epoch starts the middle reads
`(1011 10)^K` followed by a block `(1011)^X`:

| step | K | X | right end |
|---|---|---|---|
| 47,656 | 24 | 3, 39 | `100 d^20 1 d^5 1` |
| 303,250 | 75 | 3, 113 | `100 d^19 1 d^5 1` |
| 891,193 | 102 | 319 | `100 d^19 1 d^5 1` |
| 6,201,331 | 395 | 475 | `100 d^18 1 d^5 1` |
| 11,855,003 | 223 | 1517 | `100 d^18 1 d^5 1` |
| 102,712,121 | 1685 | 1855 | `100 d^17 1 d^5 1` |
| 166,939,733 | 1264 | 4386 | `c^16 100 d^4 1` |

(with intermediate epochs of other shapes). The short block `d^k` at the right end counts down slowly (20, 19, 18,
17, ...), like a second, slow counter. At 10^9 steps the run is in phase 2 of an epoch: `b^9979 c^5940 e c^344 b
c^16 a d^4 1`.

## Where it could halt

The machine halts only on E reading 0; E is entered from D reading 0 moving right (D0 = 1RE). The CTL search of
`bbtools.ctl` (K = 6) allows it only with the cells left of the head reading `110101` and blank tape after the head
(or `01`, `011` before the blank): at the right end of the tape. So a halt needs the slow structure at the right
end to run out (for instance the counter `d^k` reaching a small value with the right parity).

Tape samples (periodic notation, `w^k` is the word w repeated k times; `X>b` is the head in state X on symbol b; a long middle is cut with `...`):

- step 10,000,000: `100^133 10 B>1 011^10 1011^143 111010^203 1110^1070 1101^18 110 0111^4 01`
- step 100,000,000: `100^2044 10 D>0 1^3 011^1277 1011^1830 1110^25 0111^19 1011^4 101`
- step 1,000,000,000: `100^3733 10 A>1 011^6248 1011^5940 10^2 1110^343 1101^17 110 0111^4 01`

## Status

Open. Proved in Lean: the halting configurations, no halt in 10^7 steps (`lean/Blank.lean`), both growth phases and
their iterations (`lean/Rules.lean`, `lean/Phase.lean`). Observed (not proved): the epochs and the slowly
decreasing block at the right end. Not understood: the rule that maps one epoch to the next (K, X), so there is no
macro map yet.

### Next steps

- Find and prove the epoch transition: from the end of phase 2 (when the block in front is used up) to the next
  epoch start, as a function of n, p and what lies beyond; then iterate the epoch map.
- Track the counter `d^k` at the right end across epochs: if it decreases by one every epoch or two, it bounds the
  number of epochs before the right end changes, and that is where a halt would have to come from.
