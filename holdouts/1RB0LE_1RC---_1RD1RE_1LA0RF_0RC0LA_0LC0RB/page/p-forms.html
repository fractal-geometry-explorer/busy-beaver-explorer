Watch the machine for a while and its tape turns out to be made of long runs of a few short words: 1000, 10, a 14-cell word such as 00001000001000, and a few others, with short fixed pieces between them. So the tape can be written as a form: a pattern with the run lengths as numbers. That is level 2. For example

is the form O a b c: the head in state A at the left end, then seven 0s, a copies of 1000, b copies of 10, c copies of the 14-cell word, and 101. The proof uses ten forms. Seven are seen with the head at the left end, in state A or E (the "round-trip" forms, counting O a 0 c separately), and three with the head at the right end, in state E (the "right-phase" forms). Pick one to see its shape:

A form names a whole tape, so a sequence of forms with step counts is a description of the run as faithful as the machine itself, as long as every form is on the tape at exactly the stated step. The first time the run is in one of these forms is at step 29,301, in the form O 14 37 11. Lean proves this by running the machine (blank_O in lean/Table.lean).
