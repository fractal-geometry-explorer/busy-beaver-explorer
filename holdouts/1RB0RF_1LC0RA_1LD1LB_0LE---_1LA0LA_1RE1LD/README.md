# 1RB0RF_1LC0RA_1LD1LB_0LE---_1LA0LA_1RE1LD

bbchallenge id 10442, a BB(6) holdout (group G06). **Family: nested counter, a block of ones turned into copies of
`01` and back, with a tripling inner counter (proved) and a recursive E-phase (not decoded).**

## What it does

Write `C(n)` for `0^inf C> 1^n 0^inf`: the whole tape one block of n ones, head in C at its left end. The run is a
sequence of cycles from one `C(n)` to the next, in three parts:

1. **Ones to copies of `01`** (Lean): `C(2x+2)` reaches `0^inf D> (01)^(2x+3) 1 0^inf`. Each round of the sweep runs
   right over the copies and back, turns two ones into a copy of `01` and adds one more copy at the left end.
2. **Inner counter** (Lean): `D> (01)^(n+1) 1` becomes `1 (01)^N 0 0 F>` (N = n+1, `y_run`) and a head block
   starts at the right end: `X(p, q, r)` = `0^inf 1 (01)^p 1 B> 1 (01)^q (11)^r 0^inf`, from X(N, 1, 0)
   (`x_start`).
   * round (`x_round`): X(p+2, q, r+1) -> X(p, q+3, r), in 6q+18 steps (any tape on both sides);
   * restart (`x_restart`): X(p+1, q, 0) -> X(p, 1, q+1);
   * end (`x_end0`, `x_end1`, `x_end00`): with p = 0 or 1 the head runs to the left end,
     `0^inf A> 0 x (01)^m (11)^s 0^inf`, (x, m, s) = g(N, 1, 0) (`g`, `xrun`, `fphase` in `lean/Counter.lean`).
   So r goes 2, 8, 26, 80, ... (r+1 triples) while the copies on the left are eaten.
3. **At the left end** (checked on the run, `py/counter.py`): with k = 2s ones, x = 0 and m even restart the
   counter with N = m+k+3; x = 0 and m odd start an **E-phase** from `1 (01)^(m+2) 0^(k+2) 01 E>`; x = 1 ends the
   cycle: `1 (01)^(m+k) 0 A>`, which becomes C(2(m+k)+4) (`fin`, `to_c`). After an E-phase the head is back at the
   left end with `(0 1)? (01)^m 1^k`: k even ends the cycle, k odd restarts the counter with N = m+k−1.
4. **E-phase** (not decoded): the head block runs through the zeros, two zeros per round (`z_round`); when its ones
   run out it plants a block of copies of `01` in the zeros, on which a counter of the kind above runs, then the
   zero rounds go on: a recursive counter.
5. **Copies of `01` to ones** (Lean): `A> 1 1 (01)^m` reaches `C(2m+6)` in 4m+14 steps.
3. **Copies of `01` to ones** (Lean): `A> 1 1 (01)^m` reaches `C(2m+6)` in 4m+14 steps.

## Status

Open. The outer parts of a cycle and the inner counter are proved for every size and cannot halt; the transitions
at the left end are checked on the run; the E-phase is not decoded and is where a halt (D reads 1) could happen.

* Lean (kernel), `lean/Sweep.lean`: `ones_to_pairs` (`C(2x+2)` reaches `Y (2x+3) 0`), by induction (`sweep`,
  `sweep_e`) on the rounds `d_round`, `d_round_e`, with `c_start`, `d_first`; `pairs_to_ones` from `fill`. The rounds
  are in `lean/Rules.lean`, generated by `lean/rules.py` with `tools/py/bbtools/symtm.py`.
* Lean (kernel), `lean/Counter.lean`: `fphase` (C(2x+2) reaches `Lev (g (2x+3) 1 0)`), by recursion on p
  (`xrun`) on the rules `x_round`, `x_restart`, `x_end*`; `to_c`. Also `z_round`, `b_round` in `lean/Rules.lean`.
* Trusted (`c/cycles.c`, direct simulation): the cycles above, no halt to step 52,281,652,034.
* Trusted (`c/wrsim.cpp`, accelerated: runs of a word crossed at once, shift rules `u^n w -> w v^n` found and
  applied automatically; exact: its edge events agree cell for cell with `c/plainev.c` for all 56 events to 3·10⁹
  steps, `py/wrcheck.py`): 14 cycles, n up to 71,144,918, no halt to step 2.8·10¹⁶. `py/counter.py` checks g and
  the transitions at the left end on all its events.
* Lean (`lean/Blank.lean`): no halt in 10⁷ steps.

## For later

* The transitions at the left end need an inductive proof (a zigzag, not a single shift rule): symtm stops with
  NeedSplit on them.
* The E-phase as a recursive counter on a list of blocks (`0^k`, `(01)^p`) with the rules `z_round`, `x_round`,
  the zero restart `(0^k, q, 0) -> (0^(k-5), 1, 2q+5)` (k ≥ 5, seen by simulation) and the plant rule
  `(0^k, q, 1) -> (0^(k-1) (01)^(q+1), 1, 0)`; then the cycle map n -> n' without simulation.
