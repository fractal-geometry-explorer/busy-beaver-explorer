# 1RB1RE_1LC0RA_1LD1LB_1LE0LF_1LA0LB_1LB---

A conveyor: a counter-like engine at the left end writes words that ride to the right, 9 cells per round trip, and the right end eats them. Halting is open.

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 1RE |
| B | 1LC | 0RA |
| C | 1LD | 1LB |
| D | 1LE | 0LF |
| E | 1LA | 0LB |
| F | 1LB | --- |

## What it does

**The tape.** At a right turn: a word over `100`, `10` and `10000` (gaps of 2, 1, 4 zeros between ones), e.g.
`… 10000 (100)^29 (10000 (100)^5)^3 (100)^34 10000 (100)^9 … 10000 (100)^104` at 1.88·10^6 steps; `10` occurs only
near the left end. At a left turn the same tape reads as blocks of ones: 5 (= `(100)^2`), 7, the group `2 6 9`, and a
final 4.

**The sweeps (proved, `lean/Rules.lean`).**

| rule | from | to | what it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `a_111` | `L A> (111)^n R` | `L (010)^n A> R` | Rightward: A turns each 111 into 010. |
| `b_block` | `L B> (110111)^n R` | `L (001001)^n B> R` | Rightward: B turns each 110111 (a block of five ones and its 0) into 001001. |
| `b_unblock` | `L (010010)^n B> 0 R` | `L B> 0 (111011)^n R` | Leftward: B turns each 010010 back into 111011. |

**Emergent behaviour (observed).**

- *Conveyor:* compared with the previous left turn, all the tape after a zone at the left end is the old tape moved 9
  cells right (cells 47..3,187 of 3,187 at 2.4·10^6 steps, `py/observe.py shift 3000000 L`).
- *Engine:* the changing zone at the left end is usually a few hundred cells but varies from 31 to 6,431 cells over
  3·10^9 steps (`c/engine.c`, `engine M 3000000000 L -9 40`): long rewrites like carries in a counter. The words it
  leaves on the belt are runs of `100` separated by markers `10000`.
- *Reader:* at a right turn the tape ends with `(100)^k`, and k drops by 1 per right turn (`py/observe.py turns
  30000000 R`); when it is used up the marker and the run before it are absorbed and a new last run starts. There are
  long stretches with no right turn (2.41·10^6 to 3.16·10^6 steps) while this happens.

**Halting.** F1 halts; F is entered only from `1 D>1` (D from `C>0`: the pattern is `1 1 C>0`). In 10^9 steps D reads
a 1 53,174,798 times, never with a 1 on its left (`c/prehalt.c`).

## For later

1. The reader: state as rules what the right end does with `(100)^k 10000 (100)^m` (a shrink rule and an absorption
   rule), prove them with `symtm`. 2. The engine: find its digits (the left zone reads `7^a 5 …` with the group
   `2 6 9`) and the counter it implements. With both, the run would be a counter feeding a reader.

## Status

Open. Lean (`lean/Blank.lean`): the halting configurations (`step_none_iff`), no halt in 10,000 steps by
the kernel, in 10,000,000 by `native_decide`; `lean/Rules.lean`: the sweep rules, kernel only. C (`tools/c/triage.c`): no halt in 1,000,000,000 steps.
