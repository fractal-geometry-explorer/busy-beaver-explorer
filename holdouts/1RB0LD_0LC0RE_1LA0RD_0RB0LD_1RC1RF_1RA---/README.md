## What it does

An **event** is the head in state A on cell 65 (cells counted from the start cell; the busiest place of the tape
from step ~10^7 to 10^11). At an event the tape is

    0^inf 1 H marker low | phase R 0 1 0^inf          (| = the head, on the first cell of `phase`)

- `H`: a binary counter, 4-cell digits `0100` = 0, `0101` = 1, most significant digit first;
- `low`: a binary counter, 4-cell digits `1000` = 0, `1010` = 1, least significant digit next to the head;
- `phase`: `111` or `110`, alternating from event to event;
- `R`: a base-3 counter, 3-cell digits `001` = 0, `011` = 1, `010` = 2, least significant digit first.

**The two-counter rule (first regime, proved for all sizes).** A 111-event adds 1 to `low`; a 110-event adds 2 to
`low` and 1 to `R`. When `low` wraps around, `H` gets 3. So per pair of events `low` gets 3 and `R` gets 1: after n
pairs the left side holds 3n in binary (`low` the last bits, `H` = 3 x the number of wraps) and the right side holds n
in base 3. When `R` is all 2s it gets one more digit (the right end moves 3 cells).

- `lean/Events.lean` (35 theorems, kernel, generated by `py/events.py`): every event of this shape, for every number
  of carried ones in `low` (n), of carried 2s in `R` (k) and of carried ones in `H` (j), with its exact step count
  (12n+12 for a 111-event; 12k+12n+40, +44 or +52 for a 110-event; wraps of `low` in all four cases of H mod 4 and
  all three of `R`).
- `py/phase1.py`: the two counters as an exact simulator built from these rules. Started from the tape of event
  13,316 (step 9,021,183) it reproduces the run cell for cell and step for step at all 466,024 events up to event
  479,339 (step 31,040,523), where `H` overflows.

**Where the shape changes: left-end visits.** When `H` overflows, the carry runs to the left end, the tape grows by
2 cells there, and a sweep to the right resets `H` (to `0...0 1 0` + 2 cells) and rewrites the start of `R`. Each
such visit moves the boundary between `H` and `low` one 2-cell symbol to the right: `low` and its marker take 9 symbols
in the period 9.0M-31.0M, 8 in the next, and so on down to 0 at step 9.33·10^9; and digits written into the start of `R` (`110`, `101`, `100`, here A, B, C) pile up
as junk. Inside one period the rules are again two counters (period 31.0M-114M: `low` 3 bits `0010`/`1010` +1 per
pair, `R` after one junk digit B +4 per pair; period 114M-298M: `low` 3 bits +1 per event, a two-digit junk counter
B2..B running through 9 states in 9 events, `R` +8 per 9 events), each period with its own rule.

- Left-end visits (first event after each): steps 1,069,921, 2,904,863, 9,021,183, 31,040,871, 114,227,597,
  297,999,547, 549,553,929, 994,667,787, 1,600,933,283, 2,744,729,159, 4,901,725,731, 9,327,411,783, and the
  visit after event 87,161,601 (step 106,434,757,217); events per period from event 1,877,435 on: 1,398,096 twice, 2,796,200 twice, 5,592,404 twice,
  11,184,810 twice, then 44,739,242 (about 4/3·2^k, each twice: a clockwork).
- At step 9,327,411,783 `low` is used up: `H` sits next to the engine and each event costs ~2,200 steps instead
  of ~400 (the head crosses the junk of `R`). At step 106,434,757,217 the next left-end visit changes the picture
  again: no event (A on cell 65) happens between then and step 2·10^11; at step 1.065·10^11 the head works around
  cell 140 and the 4-cell digits `1000`/`1010` reach far into the old right part. The engine has moved right: a new
  epoch. Not decoded.

**Where it can halt.** Only F1, and F is entered only from E1 (1RF): it halts exactly when B meets `1 1 1`
(`B1` = 0RE, `E1` = 1RF; both directions in Lean: `halt_iff_2` in `lean/HaltPred.lean`). In 2·10^11 steps E1 is used 24 times, every time followed by a 0 (`c/visits.c`): at steps
148, 282, 18,776, 76,076, 152,484, then always within ~600 steps after a left-end visit, in the sweep that follows it
(1,069,597, 2,904,564, 2,904,609, 9,021,072, 114,227,470, 297,999,449, 549,553,826, 994,667,603, 1,600,933,218,
1,600,933,263, 1,600,933,304, 1,600,933,461, 1,600,933,517, 2,744,729,135, 4,901,724,914, 4,901,725,133,
9,327,411,892, 106,434,757,552, 106,434,757,712), at cells from 44 (in `H`) to 127 (inside the junk of `R`). The
event rules of the first regime never meet `1 1 1` (no E1 in them), so halting is decided by what the left-end
sweeps find in the junk of `R`. A digit `111` does appear in `R` for two events right after the visit at step
114,227,597 (events 1,877,438-9), not where B met it.

## The new epoch, third round

Started from the tape at step 1.065·10^11 (`s1065`: head on cell 142 in state D, tape from cell −44 to 192) and
run 2·10^9 more steps (`c/busyl.c`: visit counts per cell and state): the head never leaves cells
−44..192, and the busiest point is A on cell 138, 32,383,797 visits, one every 61.8 steps on average. Cell 138 is
the new engine (73 cells right of the old one at 65). At A on cell 138 the tape reads

    … 0110 1000 1 0 A> p r r r …

with the old 4-cell words on the left (`0100`/`0101` and, next to the engine, `1000`/`1010`) and a phase cell `p`
followed by 3-cell digits on the right. The events alternate exactly (1,618 + 1,618 in the first 200,000 steps):
with p = 0 only the right part changes (`0100` → `1101`, `01011` → `11010`, a carry `01010011` → `11001010`), with
p = 1 the lowest left digit goes `1000` → `1010` and the right part changes too (`11011` → `01010`), or the left carry
runs further (`0010101000101011…` → `1010101010100011…`, 392 steps). That is the first epoch's pair of events (an
event on the left counter, one on both) one engine further right, but not with the same words: the old rules of
`lean/Events.lean` (phase triples `111`/`110`) do not match these tapes as they stand. Whether the new words are a
recoding of the old ones (so that the epoch repeats the first at a larger scale) is the next thing to check: find the
digit words of the new right part from the change counts per cell, then run `py/events.py`'s rule shapes with them.

## Status

Open. Lean, kernel only: the halting configurations and 10^4 steps (`lean/Blank.lean`), the sweeps of the run
(`lean/Rules.lean`), the event rules of the first regime for all sizes (`lean/Events.lean`), and where it can halt: exactly from `B> 1 1 1`, two steps before (`lean/HaltPred.lean`). `native_decide`: 10^7
steps. C: no halt in 2·10^11 steps (`c/visits.c`).

## Next steps

Write the rules of the later periods as one family with the period as a parameter (the boundary moves one symbol per
visit); decode what each left-end sweep writes into `R`; decode the epoch change at step 1.064·10^11 (where the
engine goes, and whether the new epoch repeats the old one at a larger scale). Then the E1 moments become a
statement about the junk, and halting asks whether a sweep ever finds `1 1 1`.
