## Status

**Open; a Collatz-type problem** (a 3/2 counter whose parities drive a word; a halting word is reachable). Lean proves
the rounds of a phase; the word dynamics is measured by simulation (trusted).

## What it does

At every phase start the head, in state C, is at the left end of the tape and the tape reads `C>0 0^4 111 (01)^n P`
(blank beyond). During a phase the head bounces between the block `111` and the far end, taking two pairs per round and
coming back six cells further; when one or no pair is left it edits the word P and turns the visited zeros into the
next run of pairs. So n goes to about 3n/2 (3n/2 + d for even n, (3n + d)/2 for odd n, with d from 3 to 17), and the
parity of n edits P (from the empty word: odd writes `1`, then even appends `000`, ...): an Antihydra-like 3/2 map
driving a word counter.

Exploring the word dynamics for every parity sequence (`py/phases.py bfs`): halting words exist (`1111` with n even,
and others), the shortest route from the empty word is 42 phases with parities `1 0 1 0 ...` in a precise order:

`'':1 '1':0 '1000':1 '101':0 '11000':1 '1101':0 '1000000':1 '100001':0 '1001000':1 '100101':0 '1011000':1 '101101':0 '11000000':1 '1100001':0 '11001000':1 '1100101':0 '11011000':1 '1101101':0 '1000000000':1 '100000001':0 '1000001000':1 '100000101':0 '1000011000':1 '100001101':0 '1001000000':1 '100100001':0 '1001001000':1 '100100101':0 '1001011000':1 '100101101':0 '1011000000':1 '101100001':0 '1011001000':0 '1011001':0 '10111000':1 '1011101':0 '110001000':0 '110001':0 '1101000':1 '110101':0 '1111000':0 '1111':0`

(words written as for the first machine, reading outward from the pairs; here P is to the right, read left to right it is reversed)

The real orbit (exact big integers, the word dynamics from simulation): 20,000 phases, no halt, the word never longer
than 8 cells. Random parities: no halt in 400,000 phases. Heuristically the machine halts with probability 1 but after
about 2^40 phases.

<b>Related machines.</b> 1RB---_0RC0RB_1LD1LE_1LC1RF_1RB0LE_1RA0RD, 1RB---_1LC0RB_0LD0LC_1RE1RB_1RD1LF_1LA0LE and 1RB0LA_0RC0RB_1LD1LA_1LC1RE_1RF0RD_1LA--- share this core. The second is the third mirrored and relabelled (the same table, started in another state); the first differs from the third in one transition that is used only at a few phase ends. Their word maps agree on all 400 words compared (both parities), except two transitions of the first (from <code>11111</code> and <code>1111011</code>), and all three reach a halt by the same 42-phase route; their orbits differ because they start differently.
