# 1RB1LD_1LC0RE_1LA1RC_0LC0LA_0RF0RC_---0RB

bbchallenge id 97431. One of the six-state holdouts whose halting from blank tape is open. Family: **binary counter driving a moving window through a word**.

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 1LD |
| B | 1LC | 0RE |
| C | 1LA | 1RC |
| D | 0LC | 0LA |
| E | 0RF | 0RC |
| F | --- | 0RB |

## What it does

Each time the head reaches a new cell at the left end in state A followed by `1 0`, the tape reads
`A>0 1 0 (1^2 0)^m 1^j 0 W`, with j = 4 except near restructures (35 of the 48 such moments from step 373 on). m grows
by one per such overflow and the gap between overflows doubles (within 0.5% at 20 of the 24 overflows from step 100,161 on; 23,622,320,125 steps before the last one in the run, at 66,670,838,854,
m = 29). Each overflow rewrites only a window of about ten blocks of W, which drifts right during an epoch. When it
reaches the right end, W is rebuilt (a restructure): at about 55,000 steps, and between 2.5·10^10 and 3.1·10^10
steps. The machine halts only in state F reading a 0, and Lean proves that this can only happen at the right edge.

## What is established

**Proved in Lean, kernel only**: the halting configurations (`step_none_iff`, `lean/Blank.lean`); no halt in
10,000 steps (`blank_runs_kernel`); a halt can only happen with the head in state F on the blank region right of
everything written (`halt_at_edge`, `halts_iff_edge`, `lean/Edge.lean`, from the closed tape language with windows of
3 cells in `lean/EdgeCert.lean`, generated by `python3 -m bbtools.ctl 1RB1LD_1LC0RE_1LA1RC_0LC0LA_0RF0RC_---0RB 3 lean H97431`).

**Proved in Lean with `native_decide`**: no halt in 10^7 steps (`blank_runs_native`).

**Computed** (trusted C): no halt in 10^11 steps (`tools/c/tmrun.c`; 178 cells, 162 left and 16 right of the start,
90 ones). All 48 overflow moments from step 373 on have the shape above.

**Heuristic**: the doubling gaps; the moving window.

**Open**: the per-overflow rule for W, the restructure rule, and the question.

## For later

* Decode the window: compare W before and after one overflow, started from constructed tapes, and look for a
  finite transducer (the window may be the carry of a multiplication).
* Prove the counter step `(1^2 0)^m -> (1^2 0)^(m+1)` in Lean as a macro rule with W as a black box, if the window
  rule can be stated.
* Watch the run past 10^11 for the next restructure (the next overflow is due near 1.1·10^11 steps).
