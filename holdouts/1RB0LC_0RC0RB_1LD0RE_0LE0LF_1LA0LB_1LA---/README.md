## Status

**Open.** One of sixteen holdouts that run the same *marker counter*: a base-5 counter written on the tape as markers between long blocks of `0010`, whose markers drift and occasionally collide. The family is described in full in the family lead [`1RB0LC_0RC0RB_1LD0RE_0LE0LF_1LA0LB_0LC---`](../1RB0LC_0RC0RB_1LD0RE_0LE0LF_1LA0LB_0LC---/README.md). Lean proves the pieces of a sweep and whole sweeps for all block lengths (kernel); an exact accelerated simulator runs the machine to 10^16 steps without a halt.

## What it does

The tape between two sweeps is `1 (0010)^b0 m1 (0010)^b1 ... mk (0010)^bk 000`: blocks of the word `0010` separated by markers, each in one of the phases `1` (0), `0` (0'), `0011` (1), `011` (2), `11` (3), `00111` (4). Each sweep adds one to the counter whose digits are the phases: the last marker goes up one phase, a marker in phase 4 carries into the one before it, and when every marker is in phase 4 a new marker appears at the end. The blocks around a marker change with its phase, so the markers drift left, the later ones faster, until one uses up the block before it and *merges* with the marker there. See the family lead for the rules.

D1 is 0LF and F0 is 1LA: at a merge where D reads a 1, F reads the next cell to the left; a 1 there would halt the machine, a 0 lets F0 finish the merge.

## Results

- `lean/Rules.lean` (generated by `py/rules.py`): 24 rules for all block lengths (kernel): the pieces of a sweep (leftward wave, turns, rightward waves, overflow) and nine whole sweeps.
- `py/check.py` runs the family checks for this machine: `check_rl.py` (the run-length simulator `c/rl.c` of the family lead agrees with the plain simulation at every sweep end of the first 10^8 steps), `model.py` (to 10^11 steps: 305,522 sweeps, 305,471 regular turns and 17 overflows exactly as the rules say, 34 merges, 4 entries into F), `merges.py` (none of the 48 merges of two markers with long blocks around them halts, for any block lengths).
- `far.py` (family lead): no halt in 10,000,000,116,642,110 steps; F entered 9 times, always reading a 0.
- `clusters.py`: 37 of 2,000 random short tapes of the same shape halt within 20,000 steps, while no merge with long blocks around it halts: halting needs short blocks close together, that is two merges close together.
- Lean (`lean/Blank.lean`): no halt in 10^7 steps.
- To rerun: `python3 py/check.py 1e11` (the `model.py` count above; the default 1e10 is quicker).
