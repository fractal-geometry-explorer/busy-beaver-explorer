# 1RB0RE_0RC0RF_1LD1RA_1RE0LD_0LB1RC_1LE---

bbchallenge id 4320, group G12. Whether it halts from blank tape is **open**. Family: two-level block counter (fast tail, walker in a list of 2s, rebuilds).

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 0RE |
| B | 0RC | 0RF |
| C | 1LD | 1RA |
| D | 1RE | 0LD |
| E | 0LB | 1RC |
| F | 1LE | --- |


## The picture

At the moments the head is in C on a 0 just right of a 1 the tape is

    1^b0 0 1^b1 0 ... 0 1^bk 0 1^l  C>  0^g1 1 0^g2 1 ... 0^gr 1

a list of blocks (the interior), a last block l and a zone of single 1s. In a *regular* configuration b0 is a multiple
of 3, the other b_i are 2 mod 3, l is 1 mod 4, g1 is a positive multiple of 4 and the other gaps are 3 mod 4. One
*sub-round* (to the next such moment) does two independent things:

- **W** on the interior: `(..., x, 3a+2) -> (..., x+3, 2, ..., 2)` (a blocks of 2). In digits a_i = (b_i - 2)/3: drop the
  last digit a, add 1 to the one before, append a zeros.
- **the counter**: l and the zone are a binary counter; l + segments = 4m + 1 during the 2^m sub-rounds of round m
  (`enc`/`dec` in `py/model.py`).

A tail `(..., q, a)` becomes `(..., q+a+1)` after exactly 2^a steps of W, so a list with digits a_1..a_k is a single
block after exactly T = sum_i 2^(a_i + ... + a_k + k - i) steps: the list is the binary expansion of the time it has
left. With a single block left the sub-rounds are irregular for a while (the *transient* of a rebuild): the block is cut
into `3 2 2 2 ...`, cells of l and the zone move into the interior, and the configuration becomes regular again with a
longer list. No rule is known for the transient. All of this is observed and checked against the machine, not proved.

rebuilds, more than 2^35,353 sub-rounds, no halt. Tapes of the same shape do halt: `1^(3k) 0 1^(4k-3) C>0`, k = 3..9.

## What is established

**Proved in Lean, kernel only** — The halting configurations: The machine stops exactly when it reads the symbol of a halting transition (F1); the generated statement covers every configuration. (`step_none_iff`, `lean/Blank.lean`)

**Proved in Lean, kernel only** — No halt in the first 10,000 steps: The list simulator of the shared library, proved to agree with the machine's semantics, runs 10,000 steps from blank tape without a halt, evaluated by Lean's kernel. (`blank_runs_kernel`, `lean/Blank.lean`)

**Proved in Lean with `native_decide`** — No halt in the first 107 steps: The same run, evaluated by compiled code. (`blank_runs_native`, `lean/Blank.lean`)

**Computed (trusted C/Python)** — No halt in the first 109 steps: The C simulator `tools/c/triage.c` runs 10^9 steps without a halt; at the end 189 cells have been visited and 86 of them hold a 1. (`holdouts/triage.json`)

**Proved in Lean, kernel only** — Three rounds of the run, for any tape further left: Three consecutive rounds of the run from blank tape (between moments the head is in C on the first 0 right of everything written), each proved for every tape left of the cells it visits, with its exact step count. (`lean/Rounds.lean` (generated by `py/gen_rounds.py`))

**Computed (trusted C/Python)** — No halt in 3·1010 steps; three rebuilds: Direct simulation for 3·10^10 steps. At the moments the head is in C on the first 0 right of everything written the tape is a list of blocks of 1s separated by single 0s; the last block grows by 4 per round; in walking phases the rounds satisfy T′ = 2T + 4 exactly. Rebuilds of the list at steps 72,083, 11,004,261 and 22,558,329,283 (the table). (`tools/c/tmrun.c` (`at C R`))

**Computed (trusted C/Python)** — Between rebuilds: a binary counter that applies one list operation per tick: At the moments the head is in C on a 0 just right of a 1, the tape is a list of blocks of 1s separated by single 0s (the interior), a last block l, and to the right of the head a zone of single 1s. Call it regular if the first block is a multiple of 3, the other interior blocks are 2 mod 3, l is 1 mod 4, and the gaps of the zone are 0 mod 4 (the first, counted from the head) and 3 mod 4 (the others). From one such moment to the next (a sub-round) a regular configuration changes in two independent ways. The interior takes one step W: (..., x, 3a + 2) becomes (..., x + 3, 2, ..., 2) with a blocks of 2. The last block and the zone are a binary counter: with segments s_1 = g_1, s_i = g_i + 1, a last block l ≥ 5 becomes a new first segment l − 1 and l = 1; with l = 1 the n leading segments equal to 4 are removed, the next segment loses 4 cells (blank tape supplies them if there is none) and l = 4n + 5. So l plus the segments is 4m + 1 for 2^m sub-rounds (one round, what earlier notes called the fast counter), then m grows by 1. Checked against the machine one sub-round at a time: of the 300,000 sub-rounds after step 2,918 (12.6 million steps) the 299,954 regular ones all follow this; the other 46 are the transients of the first two rebuilds. Observed and checked, not proved. (`holdouts/1RB0RE_0RC0RF_1LD1RA_1RE0LD_0LB1RC_1LE---/py/model.py` (`check 300000`))

**Computed (trusted C/Python)** — The list is the binary expansion of the time it has left: With digits a_i = (b_i − 2)/3, W removes the last digit a, adds 1 to the digit before it and appends a zeros; the sum of all digits plus their number never changes. A tail (..., q, a) becomes (..., q + a + 1) after exactly 2^a steps, so an interior with digits a_1, ..., a_k is a single block after exactly T = Σ_i 2^(ai + ... + ak + k − i) steps of W: the positions of the 1s in the binary expansion of T are the partial sums of the list read from the right. (This is arithmetic on the rule W, not on the machine.) Checked on the machine: the model predicts single blocks at sub-rounds 1,616 and 261,907 of that run and both are there; W steps and counter steps taken many at once agree with the machine in 28 test jumps; and after the third rebuild the lists at seven ends of rounds, up to step 2.8·10^10, are those of a direct run of the machine (`3 2^25 14 2 8 2 14 | 85` at step 22,558,329,286, ..., `3 2^12 20 2^6 14 2 8 2 14 | 109` at step 28,107,393,382). (`holdouts/1RB0RE_0RC0RF_1LD1RA_1RE0LD_0LB1RC_1LE---/py/model.py` (`check 300000`, `partial`, `rounds`))

**Computed (trusted C/Python)** — Tapes of the same shape that halt: A rebuild starts when the interior is a single block. The tape 1^3k 0 1^(4k−3) with the head in C on the 0 right of it, a single block of 3k cells meeting the counter exactly at the start of round k − 1, halts for every k from 3 to 9 (after 311, 659, 1,343, 2,699, 5,399, 10,787 and 21,551 steps; direct simulation). So no property of the shape alone can show that the machine never halts: it depends on which single blocks and counter values the run from blank tape produces. (`holdouts/1RB0RE_0RC0RF_1LD1RA_1RE0LD_0LB1RC_1LE---/py/model.py` (`halting 9`), `c/trans.c`)

**Heuristic / observed** — 243 rebuilds without a halt: A run that takes the regular sub-rounds by arithmetic (any number at once, with the two rules above) and gives everything else to the machine itself, cell by cell: the transient after each single block, and the sub-rounds where the counter reaches an irregular gap of the zone. It passes 243 rebuilds; by then the counter has made more than 2^35,353 sub-rounds, so the machine more than 10^10,642 steps, and the single block has 106,062 cells. A halt could only be found by the direct part and none was. The arithmetic part rests on the two rules above for all sizes, which are checked, not proved. (`holdouts/1RB0RE_0RC0RF_1LD1RA_1RE0LD_0LB1RC_1LE---/py/model.py` (`run 243`), `c/trans.c`)

**Open** — Does it halt from blank tape?: Not known. Between rebuilds the machine cannot halt if the model is right; a rebuild's transient can (tapes of the same shape do). What the transient does to the block and the counter has no rule yet, so the question is whether the single blocks and counter values of this run ever meet in a halting way. The halting family above needs the single block to arrive exactly when a round of the counter starts, a coincidence of two binary numbers that the run has avoided so far.

## For later

1. The transient: find its rule (it is itself made of sub-rounds, with irregular gaps in the zone that are used up one
   after the other), so that the whole run becomes arithmetic and the halting coincidences can be described.
2. Lean: W and the counter step as rules for all sizes (the sub-round is a few sweeps over the last blocks; the tools
   are `tools/py/bbtools/symtm.py` and `seqtm.py`), then T by induction.
3. The same skeleton appears in `1RB0RF_1RC1LB_1LD1RE_1LB0LD_1RF0RC_1RA---` (blocks 2a + 1, last block a multiple
   of 5) and probably in `1RB0RE_1LC0RA_1RE1LD_0LB0LF_1RA1RA_0LD---`.
