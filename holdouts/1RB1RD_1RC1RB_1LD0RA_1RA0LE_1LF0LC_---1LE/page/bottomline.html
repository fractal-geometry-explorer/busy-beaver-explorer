The whole run, in your browser

The program of the proof, written again in JavaScript with exact big integers: the compressed tape of level 2, and at every visit the jump of level 4 when its conditions hold (the same choices as lean/Run.lean: counts of at least 7, a margin of 6, at least 8 visits). It counts steps as it goes, a jump with the closed form of the Coq proof. The second choice adds the two block rules of the Coq proof, which cross a whole counter level in one round. Either way it takes several seconds; the picture shows the odometer growing, one dot per jump.

rounds 0jumps 0visits 0steps 0counters 0time 0 s
Across: the number of visits, on a log scale up to 1066. Up: the number of counters in the zone the jump read; a zone is read only as far as its counts are at least 7, so many jumps see part of the odometer, and the upper edge is the whole of it. Every transition, run crossed, copy unpacked, block rule and jump counts as one round.
