# 1RB1RF_1LC1RD_1RA1LD_1RE0LA_---0RC_0LC1RB

bbchallenge id 110862. One of the six-state holdouts whose halting from blank tape is open.

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 1RF |
| B | 1LC | 1RD |
| C | 1RA | 1LD |
| D | 1RE | 0LA |
| E | --- | 0RC |
| F | 0LC | 1RB |

## What it does: two periodic regions that turn into each other

The tape is made of two periodic regions, the 6-cell words `111010` and `111110` (each seen at any phase), and only
one to three defects (blocks of 2, 4, 6, 7 or 8 ones). Counting the blocks (`tools/c/tmrun ... every`,
`py/regions.py`), the number of `111010` words and of `111110` words swing back and forth while the tape grows:

| steps | tape length | words `111010` | words `111110` |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10^9 | 57,981 | 4,413 | 5,245 |
| 2·10^9 | 78,391 | 10,705 | 2,356 |
| 2.9·10^9 | 89,373 | 14,630 | 260 |
| 3·10^9 | 90,429 | 7,094 | 7,972 |
| 4·10^9 | 99,895 | 7,333 | 9,314 |

Sweeping right, in every state, the head turns each `111010` into a `111110` (at the matching phase), 6 steps a word:
`sweepA` ... `sweepF` in `lean/Rules.lean`, proved for any number of words. How words turn back the other way has not
been worked out. The tape length grows like the square root of the step count (length^2 / steps stays near 2.5); most of the
tape is left of the start, the right end barely moves.

This is the same picture as its relative `1RB1RF_1LC0LF_1RD0LB_0RA1RE_1RD---_0RC0LA` (two regions converting into
each other), with even fewer defects.

## Does it halt?

The only halt is E reading 0, entered only from D reading 0 (D0 = 1RE): the machine stops exactly when the head in
state D reads `0 0` (`halt00`, `halt00_stop`). The proved sweeps end without halting, so a
halt would have to come from a defect or a turn at an end. The C simulator runs **10^11 steps** without a halt.

`bbtools.cycles` finds no repeated cycle at the tape ends, because the sweeps rewrite the whole tape, so no
accelerated run is available; whether the swinging of the two regions is regular is not known.

## For later

Stopped at: the two regions, the sweep rules and the halting condition in Lean, the plain run to 10^11 steps. Next:
(1) follow the one to three defects from sweep to sweep and write their motion as rules; (2) find the law behind the
swing of the region sizes; (3) with those, an accelerator that applies whole sweeps symbolically.

Transitions not used in these steps: this machine never uses E0 and the other never uses F0. A halt of either machine is itself a break, and the halting transitions of the two do not correspond, so the two are not known to halt together.

These are different runs: in the first 10^7 steps of each, no configuration of this machine's run occurs in the other, even up to a shift of the tape. So each of these holdouts is its own halting question, and settling one does not settle another.
