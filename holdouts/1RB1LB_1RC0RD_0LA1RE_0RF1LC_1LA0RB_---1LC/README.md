# 1RB1LB_1RC0RD_0LA1RE_0RF1LC_1LA0RB_---1LC

bbchallenge id 166286, a BB(6) holdout (group G06). Family: **binary counter beside a growing block**. Open; it can
only halt during one of the rare overflows of a binary counter into a stretch of leftover cells.

| state | read 0 | read 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1RB | 1LB |
| B | 1RC | 0RD |
| C | 0LA | 1RE |
| D | 0RF | 1LC |
| E | 1LA | 0RB |
| F | --- | 1LC |

## What it does

Everything happens left of the start; the right side stops at 11 cells. Write `x = 1011`. Each time the head
reaches a new cell on the left in state B the tape is

    0^inf B> 0 10 x^N J T          J = 1010 x  or  11 x  (in turn)

* **The block.** `N` grows by one every two rounds (a round: one trip of the head from the left end into `T` and
  back), so the left end moves by two cells a round, a round costs time proportional to the tape and the visited
  part grows like the square root of the time (about `5 R^2` steps after `R` rounds).
* **The counter.** `T` begins with a binary counter, about three cells a bit, least significant bit next to the
  block, incremented every second round. Over 2,000,000 rounds the depth (in cells) of the changes in `T` from one
  record to the next is 12, 14, 18, 20, 24, 26, 30, 32, ... with 499,998, 250,000, 124,999, 62,499, 31,249, 15,624,
  7,812, 3,906, ... rounds: each depth half as often as the one before.
* **The leftovers.** Beyond the counter is junk: a run of `1110` words and a second, slow counter. The main
  counter's carries reach it only when it overflows; the visited region widens by two cells at rounds 349,558,
  8,738,166 and 25,515,382.

### Overflows, epochs and restarts (`py/`, simulation)

Counter code: digit i is `1010`/`1111` (0/1) for even i and `10`/`11` for odd i, least significant first; a pair
of digits is `101010` or `111111`; D digits take 3D + (D mod 2) cells. At the records in B (odd rounds)

    0^inf B> 0 10 (1011)^N 1010 1011 COUNTER (1110)^a T

* **Overflow** (`py/macro.py`). From an all-ones counter of even width w, two rounds give width w+2 and value
  2^(w+1); a goes down by 1 and 2 in turn; every second overflow adds one to the slow counter T. Over 30 overflows
  in a row the map on (a, T) is the same for w = 0..8 and a = 6..9. Predicted overflow rounds 349,559, 8,738,167,
  25,515,383 (one after the observed widenings).
* **End of an epoch** (`py/epoch.py`, `py/closure.py`). When the `1110` words are gone the counter absorbs T. If a
  terminator is left over, the next overflow is a **restart**: within three rounds `(1011)^N` becomes `(1110)^N`
  and the counter of width W becomes the new slow counter `1111 1110 (1010)^(3W/4) 11` (`... 1011` if
  W = 2 mod 4), and a new empty counter starts. If nothing is left, the next overflow reads 0 in F: **halt**.
  `closure.py 40 4,6,8`: of 120 fresh epochs, 107 restart in exactly that form, 6 with a variant tail, 7 halt.
* **Halting starts exist.** For slow-counter width 6, fresh epochs halt for a = 15, 18, 39, 42, 99, 102, 105, 108,
  117, ...; width 8: a = 51, ..., 72, 87, 90, 147, 150, ... (all multiples of 3, a Cantor-like set set by the
  slow counter's digits; `py/shortcut.py`, which also checks the shortcut below against full runs).
* **The blank-tape run.** Epoch 1 restarts at round 25,515,382 with a = 12,757,674. Skipping the 2k+2 overflows
  that only count T up (`shortcut.py`, `gen.py`: exact at small sizes, extrapolated here), epoch 2 restarts at
  counter width 17,010,256 with a block N2 of 17,010,257 bits (N2 = 1 mod 3, = 42 mod 64) and the variant tail
  `1111 1110 (1010)^(3W/4+1) 1011 1011` (`epoch2.py`, 1 s). Epoch 3 (a = N2) also restarts: its last overflows
  absorb a slow counter of 17,010,257 digits and leave `11` (`epoch3.py`; `ep3real.py` runs the last two
  overflows at the real width, 17,010,270 and 17,010,272 digits, a minute). Its block, epoch 4's junk, has about
  (4/3) N2, roughly 2^17,010,257, bits.

### Rules proved in Lean (`lean/Rules.lean`, generated by `lean/rules.py` with `tools/py/bbtools/symtm.py`)

* `left_turn`: `0^inf 0 0 (1110)^(n+1) A> 1011 R` -> `0^inf 111 (0111)^(n+1) 111 E> R` in 20n+38 steps: the head
  crosses the block leftwards (12 steps a word), bounces off the blank tape and crosses it back (8 steps a word).
* `right_sweep`: `0^inf B> 0 10 (1011)^n R` -> `0^inf 111 (0111)^n 0 B> R` in 8n+11 steps: the turn after a
  record in B (it makes a second record in A, 7 steps later), then the crossing to the right.

* `inc_even`: `0^inf B> 0 10 (1011)^n 1010 1011 (111111)^m 1010 R` -> the same with `(1011)^(n+1)` and
  `(101010)^m 1111 R`, 26m+40n+122 steps; `inc_odd`: `(111111)^m 1111 10 R` -> `(101010)^m 1010 11 R`,
  26m+40n+128 steps. The two carries of the counter, R never read.

The first two are why the part right of the block does not depend on N. `lean/Counter.lean` puts the carries
together: `count m` says a counter of 2m digits goes from all zeros to all ones (the block gains 4^m - 1 words), for
every block length and every tape right of it, by induction on m.

## Status

Open.

* Lean (kernel): `lean/Blank.lean` (halting configurations, no halt in 10^4 steps; 10^7 by `native_decide`),
  `lean/Rules.lean`, `lean/Counter.lean` (above).
* `c/m3red.c` runs the machine with the block cut to 40 words; checked against the full machine for the first
  24,522 rounds and faithful until the first restart at round 25,515,382 (about 3*10^15 steps): no halt before it.
  **Correction:** the first version of this page claimed 10^8 rounds without a halt from this run; after the
  restart the cut block makes the run wrong, so that claim is withdrawn.
* `c/m3depth.c` is the same run with the carry-depth histogram above.
* `py/`: the overflow, epoch and restart computations above (trusted at small sizes, heuristic where extrapolated).

### Where a non-halting proof breaks

The lead's target was a family of configurations closed under round, carry and overflow with symbolic lengths. The
carries and full counts are proved (`inc_even`, `inc_odd`, `count`). What is missing, in order:

1. The overflow round: the head runs off the all-ones counter into `(1110)^a T` (two alignments, as a goes down by
   1 and 2), leaving width w+2 and value 2^(w+1). Not yet generated with symtm.
2. The slow counter's increment, a zigzag over T: symtm stops with `NeedSplit` on `(11)^k`; it needs the same
   digit-pair words as the main counter and counters given as arbitrary bit lists, not only all zeros/all ones.
3. Absorption and restart (the block rewritten as junk), each a fixed rule but with several leftover cases.
4. The real obstacle: **the family is not closed without its halting members.** Fresh epochs with the same tail
   halt for some junk lengths (multiples of 3, Cantor-like). Whether the blank-tape run ever hits one depends on
   residues and binary digits of the junk lengths 12,757,674, N2 (17,010,257 bits), then about 2^17,010,257 bits,
   growing like a tower. A proof would have to control that arithmetic; epochs 2 and 3 are settled only by
   computation (heuristic), epoch 4 is out of reach.

## For later

* Prove steps 1 to 3 in Lean, which would make the epoch 2 and 3 results conditional only on the arithmetic.
* Find the exact halting condition of a fresh epoch as a function of (a, slow counter), and whether it can be
  decided from a few residues of a; then N3 mod small numbers might decide epoch 4.
