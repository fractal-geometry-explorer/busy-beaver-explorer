# The six-state machine 1RB1RF_1LC0LB_1RE0LD_1LB0RF_0RA0RC_1RE---

Whether this machine halts from blank tape is open. This folder holds its work; the shared tools are in
[`../tools`](../tools), the proof-status page is [`../pages/1RB1RF_1LC0LB/proof-status.html`](../pages/1RB1RF_1LC0LB/proof-status.html)
(built from [`status.json`](status.json)), the interactive explainer is
[`../pages/1RB1RF_1LC0LB/explainer.html`](../pages/1RB1RF_1LC0LB/explainer.html), and the log is
[`notes/LAB_NOTES.md`](notes/LAB_NOTES.md).

```
      0    1
A   1RB  1RF
B   1LC  0LB
C   1RE  0LD
D   1LB  0RF
E   0RA  0RC
F   1RE  ---
```

## What is established

**Proved in Lean, kernel only** (`lean/Blank.lean`): the machine halts exactly when it is in F on a 1, and
`E>0 1 1` halts two steps later (`step_none_iff`, `halt_shape`); D always has a 0 to its right one step after any
configuration (`d_right_zero`), so in the run from blank tape every halt comes two ordinary steps after `E>0 1 1`
(`halt_from_e011`, `halts_imp_e011`); the tape after 18 steps (`blank_at_18`); no halt in the first 20,000 steps
(`blank_runs_20000`).

**Proved in Lean, kernel only** (`lean/Rules.lean`, generated by `lean/rules.py` with `tools/py/bbtools/symtm.py`):
E runs right over pairs `10`, shifting them (`e_run`); E marks a block boundary, `E>0 1 0 → 0 1 1 E>` (`e_mark`); B
runs left over pairs, two at a time (`b_run`).

**Proved in Lean, kernel only** (`lean/Counter.lean`): the right end of the tape is a base-3 counter. Each time
the head is back at the right end, the tape read from the right is a list of levels `s (10)^g s' (10)^W` with digit
2, 1, 0 for seps `1 1`, `0 1`, `0 0`; one trip from the right end and back subtracts one from the number the digits
spell, moving one pair from W to g at each level whose digit goes 2 → 1 or 0 → 2 (`counter_last2`, `counter_last1`,
`counter_borrow2`, `counter_borrow1`, for any lengths and any number of borrows). No such trip halts.

**Proved in Lean, kernel only** (`lean/Wall.lean`): the copy loop that builds a wall. At the start of each round
the head is in C at the left end with `0 (10)^(2a+1) 0 (10)^(2t1+2) … 0 (10)^(2tm+2) 0 0 (10)^X R` to its right; one
round gives a+1 and X−1 (`wall_round`), for every a, every list of blocks, every X ≥ 1 and any R, and by induction the
whole loop copies the level into the first block (`wall_loop`, `wall_jump`).

**Proved in Lean, kernel only** (`lean/Top.lean`, `lean/TopWall*.lean` and `lean/TopFam.lean`, generated by
`lean/top_gen.py`): the top of the counter, the trips the four counter theorems do not cover. Any piece of tape that
takes the head from the walk's phase back to the sweep's can end a borrow (`counter_borrow_any` in `Counter.lean`,
`counter_borrow_ex` in `Wall.lean`); 23 such pieces, each a map on block lengths for all values of its variables
(`top_0` to `top_22`), and 7 *families* of pieces with a list of blocks of any length (`fam_0` to `fam_6`, proved by
induction on the list with `seqL`/`seqR` of `lean/Seq.lean`; the ladders `0^inf (1010)^x [1 10 10 (1010)^a]^ts 1 10 10
10 ...`, for example, with any number of rungs), give the other kinds of whole trips, with any number of borrows.
2 of the pieces build a wall: their runs are symbolic stretches chained with `wall_jump`.

**Proved in Lean, kernel only** (`lean/ReworkCore.lean`, `lean/Rework.lean`, generated by `lean/rework_gen.py`): the
wall trips for any list of blocks in front of the copied level. Before each copy round the head converts the blocks
next to the level with zig-zags that carry a state from block to block; `py/rework.py` computes a round as the
machine walks (modes crossing blocks and pieces of tape), each of its 94 cases is a symbolic run (`rw_ok`), and
`Sys.go_ok` proves by induction that the machine does what the round function says, for any list; with the rounds by
induction (`iter_ok`), the start and the end, `wall_turn` (after a walk, through `counter_borrow_ex`) and
`wall_last` are the whole trip. They replace 32 of the 34 wall shapes.

**Proved in Lean with `native_decide`**: no halt in the first 10,000,000 steps (`blank_runs_10M`).

**Computed** (trusted C/Python, tested): no halt in the first 10¹⁰ steps (`tools/c/tmrun.c`); no conserved
a·ones + b·pos + W[state] modulo 2 to 12 (`tools/py/bbtools/invariants.py`); closed tape languages from windows of 2 to
7 cells all allow a halt (`tools/py/bbtools/ctl.py`); of the 284,506 trips from the right end in the first 10¹²
steps, 283,803 are covered by the counter theorems and end as they say, and the other 703 reach the irregular left
end or a short level among the leftmost six (`py/counter.py check`, with `tools/py/bbtools/macro.py`); all 703 go
through the 7 families of `TopFam.lean` (`py/family.py`), the wall trips of `Rework.lean` (`py/rework.py`, 75 trips)
or the 23 shapes of `Top.lean` and `TopWall*.lean` (the wall-building ones with the loop jumped over by
`py/wall.py`) and end as they say (`py/top.py check`), so every trip of the first 10¹² steps is an instance of a
theorem; no halt in
the first 10³³ steps, with the counter trips applied in closed form and cycles inside the other trips jumped over
(`py/accel.py`, `tools/py/bbtools/cycles.py`).

**Observed** (heuristic): the tape is blocks of pairs `10` separated by single 0s, and the width grows like 2.6√t; the
mark `110` occurs at the left-end moments only right after a block, never after a boundary; in front of a long block
(a wall) the left end replays the run from blank tape while the wall shrinks by a fixed sequence of amounts, and
walls nest (`py/replay.py`); a wall is made when the walk meets a level with a single pair to its left: the left end
then copies that level's X pairs into a new first block of about 2X, one round of the head per pair. See the log.

## One run for four machines (F09, 13 November 2026)

Four holdouts have the same table up to renaming the states and mirroring the tape (`holdouts/related.json`), started in
four different states. Written in the states of 32883 (`1RB0RA_1LC0RE_0LD0LB_1LA1LF_1RA0LF_1LC---`):

| machine | id | start state in the table of 32883 | mirrored | first aligned trip: step, runs | first run |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `1RB0RA_1LC0RE_0LD0LB_1LA1LF_1RA0LF_1LC---` | 32883 | A | no | 6, `[6]` | r + 4 |
| `1RB1RF_1LC0LB_1RE0LD_1LB0RF_0RA0RC_1RE---` | 40600 | D | yes | 1, `[2]` | r |
| `1RB0LE_0RC0RA_1RD1RF_1LA0LD_1LD0RF_1RB---` | 103746 | B | yes | 17, `[10]` | r + 8 |
| `1RB0LF_1RC0RB_1LD0RA_0LE0LC_1LB1LF_1LD---` | 131976 | E | no | 37, `[14]` | r + 12 |

A trip starts when the head, in state A of that table, steps onto a new leftmost cell; the tape is then a list of runs of
alternating cells, written as their lengths. `py/lockstep.py` of 32883 simulates two of the four runs and compares the
run lists trip by trip.

**Observed: the four runs are one run, shifted.** At every one of the first 527,004 trips, up to 2·10^12 steps, the four
tapes have the same run lengths except for the first run, and the first runs differ by constants: r for 40600, r + 4 for
32883, r + 8 for 103746 and r + 12 for 131976 (the last two need one trip more to get going, so their trip k + 1 is
compared with trip k of the others). No mismatch occurred. Output: `py/lockstep_2e12.txt` in the folder of 32883.

### What is proved, and what is missing

The four tapes differ only in the length of the first run, so the question is whether the machine can ever see that
length other than mod 4. The head touches the first run only by entering it from the right: it steps onto the last
cell of the run in one of the five states a left move leads to (A, B, C, D, F), and that cell is 0 or 1. That makes
ten entries. Each either bounces (the head looks at no more than the last 2 cells of the run and comes back out),
sweeps (the head goes to the left end, turns and comes back, and the outcome depends on the length mod 4), or halts
at once (F on a 1).

**Proved in Lean (kernel), `lean/Shift4.lean` in the folder of 32883, 24 theorems generated by `py/shift4.py`:** for every entry, the
outcome for a first run of n + 4 cells is the outcome for n cells with one more copy of a 4-cell word in the run:
same exit state, same cells left between the run and the head, same change of the run length. The bounces are
proved for any tape to the left of the cells they touch, the sweeps for every n of the form 4a + 5, 4a + 6, 4a + 7,
4a + 8. `py/shift4.py check` compares n with n + 4 by simulation for every n from 1 to 300: the outcomes agree for
every entry and every n of at least 3, and differ only for n = 1 and n = 2.

**What follows.** While the head is outside the first run, two tapes that differ only in that run make the same steps.
So the four runs stay shifted copies of each other, and the four machines halt together or not at all, **provided**
the first run of the shortest tape (the run of 40600) has at least 3 cells every time the head enters it (with at
least 5 cells every step of the argument is a Lean theorem; for 3 and 4 cells the sweeps rest on the script). That
proviso is the only thing missing. It is observed, not proved: `py/entries.py` finds that in the first 2·10^10 steps
the head enters the first run 57,701 times in the run of 40600, with at least 5 cells each time (5 at step 7, 7 at
step 16, 8 or more afterwards; `py/entries_2e10.txt`), and the first run gains 8 cells every three ordinary trips.

Why the proviso is not a local fact: a bounce takes 1 or 2 cells off the right end of the first run, and nothing in
the ten entries limits how many bounces can happen between two sweeps. In the real run there is at most one bounce per
ordinary trip (the carry at digit 1), so the run grows, but showing that for every trip needs the theory of the
transfers, which is not complete (section "Why the gap does not close yet" of the README of 32883). So the statement
"the four machines halt together" is not proved, and no page claims it.

The rules proved for one of the four tables hold for the other three after renaming, because rules do not depend on
the start.

Reproduce (from the folder of 32883, about 12 minutes each):

    python3 py/lockstep.py 0 3 2e12    # start A against start D
    python3 py/lockstep.py 4 1 2e12    # start E against start B
    python3 py/lockstep.py 0 4 2e12    # start A against start E
    python3 py/shift4.py check 300     # every entry, n against n + 4
    python3 py/entries.py 3 2e10       # first-run length at every entry, run of 40600 (5 minutes)

## Files

| path | what it is |
|---|---|
| `lean/Machine.lean` | the machine, generated by `tools/py/bbtools/leangen.py` (namespace `BB6e`) |
| `lean/Blank.lean` | the halting shape and the bounded runs |
| `lean/Rules.lean`, `lean/rules.py` | the sweep rules, and the script that writes and proves them |
| `lean/Counter.lean` | the right-end counter: the four counter theorems, chained from the pieces in `Rules.lean` |
| `lean/Wall.lean` | the copy loop that builds a wall: one round, and the loop by induction |
| `lean/Top.lean`, `lean/TopWall*.lean`, `lean/top_gen.py`, `lean/top_shapes.txt` | the top of the counter: one theorem per shape, the generator, and the shapes it proves |
| `lean/Seq.lean` | crossing a list of blocks of any length (`seqL`, `seqR`), by induction on the list |
| `lean/TopFam.lean`, `lean/top_families.txt` | the families of top shapes, one theorem each for every list of blocks (generated by `top_gen.py`) |
| `lean/ReworkCore.lean` | the rounds of a wall trip on a list of blocks: the induction, for any cases (hand-written) |
| `lean/Rework.lean`, `lean/rework_gen.py`, `lean/rework_cases.txt`, `lean/rework_shapes.txt` | the cases of the rounds as lemmas, and the wall trips `wall_turn`, `wall_last` (generated from the saved cases, found from the wall shapes) |
| `lean/Axioms.lean`, `lean/AxiomsWall.lean` | print what each theorem rests on (the second for the wall pieces, generated) |
| `lean/check.sh` | compiles the shared library and these files, except the wall pieces |
| `lean/check_wall.sh` | compiles the wall pieces `TopWall*.lean` (longer; its own CI workflow, `wall.yml`) |
| `py/blank.py` | the run from blank tape: summary, the tape as words at the left end, the tape at every turn, transition counts |
| `py/counter.py` | the level lists at the right end, the counter step, and its check against the run |
| `py/top.py` | the top trips: cut into walk, piece and sweep, the piece generalised to a shape, checked against the run |
| `py/family.py` | the families: finds them among the shapes, and matches a trip's piece with one |
| `py/rework.py` | the rounds of a wall trip as a function on lists of blocks: finds the cases, checks them on the plain simulator, predicts a wall trip |
| `py/wall.py` | the copy loop for the symbolic runs: finds the round form and jumps over the whole loop |
| `py/accel.py` | the run with counter trips applied in closed form (no halt through 10³³ steps) |
| `py/replay.py` | finds the walls and how far the left end replays the blank run |
| `c/growth.c` | width, ones and left-end extensions at 1-2-5 steps, fast |
| `runs/` | outputs of the long runs |
| `tests/test_machine.py` | checks of the generated files, the pages and the observations (CI) |
