# Lab notes: 1RB1RF_1LC0LB_1RE0LD_1LB0RF_0RA0RC_1RE---

```
      0    1
A   1RB  1RF
B   1LC  0LB
C   1RE  0LD
D   1LB  0RF
E   0RA  0RC
F   1RE  ---
```

The machine halts only on F1. Trust levels as everywhere in the repository: **kernel** (Lean, kernel only),
**native** (Lean with `native_decide`), **computed** (C or Python here, tested, not proved), **heuristic** (patterns).

### The halting shape (kernel, `lean/Blank.lean`)

F is entered from A1 (`1RF`) and D1 (`0RF`) and reads the cell to their right. D is entered only by `C1 → 0LD`, a
left move that writes a 0 on the cell that is then D's right neighbour, so D never has a 1 to its right
(`d_right_zero`) and the D route never halts. A is entered only by `E0 → 0RA`. Hence `halt_from_e011`: in the run
from blank tape every halt comes two ordinary steps after a configuration `E>0 1 1`.

### Invariants and closed languages (computed)

* `bbtools.invariants`: no conserved a·ones + b·pos + W[state] (mod m) for m = 2..12.
* `bbtools.ctl` with windows of K = 2..7 cells: always closed, always allows a halt (K = 5, 6, 7: 3,285, 10,865,
  37,117 abstract configurations). The run's protection is the pair alignment below, which a window of raw cells
  does not see. A closed language over the words `10`, `110`, `0` (or windows that remember the cell's parity) is the
  natural next try.

### The tape: pairs, blocks, marks

Reading the tape with the words a = `10`, b = `110` (`python3 py/blank.py left`, or `tools/c/tokrun MACHINE N
a=10,b=110 L K`), every moment the head extends the tape at the left looks like

```
0^2 a^n1 0 a^n2 0 ... 0 a^nk 1          (D or B at the left end)
```

or the same with an occasional b right after a block (`a^10759 b a^3`) or a `0^2` boundary. The first ones:

```
32    0^2 a^3 0 a 1
275   0^2 a 0 a^7 0 a^8 1
1681  0^2 a^13 0 a^13 0 a^18 1
2184  0^2 a^3 0 a^12 0 a^15 0 a^22 1
5757  0^2 a 0 a^7 0 a^9 0 a^25 0 a^42 1
277787  0^2 a^257 0 a^123 0 a^192 1
```

The sweeps (kernel, `lean/Rules.lean`): E runs right over pairs shifting each by one cell, `E>(10)^n → (01)^n E>`
(`e_run`); at a boundary it marks it, `E>0 1 0 → 0 1 1 E>` (`e_mark`); B runs left over pairs, two at a time,
`1 (0101)^n B>0 → 1 B>0 (0101)^n` (`b_run`), through D in between, so the phase in which a left sweep reaches a
boundary depends on the parity of the block. The halting shape `E>0 1 1` is a mark that E meets **in step with its
own pairs**.

`py/blank.py turns FROM TO` prints the tape at every turn of the head. A typical stretch (steps 1681 to 2190): the
head sweeps right in E from the left end, marking every boundary (`a^3 b a^12 b a^12 b a^18 [C] 1^2`, read in the
shifted frame); excursions from the right end run left to a boundary, turn there (`0 [F] 0`), and return, the right
end growing by a pair or two each time; when the excursions reach the left end the next sweep starts. Positions of
the turns (`bbtools.explore turns`) show inner loops: between steps 277,787 and 1,942,279 the left turns come in
groups `D p`, `D q+4k`, `C p-1` with p fixed and q moving by 4 per group, so a block is counted up in unary while a
boundary near the left stays put.

At the 207 left-end moments up to 10^10 steps (`runs/left_1e10.txt`) the word b occurs 67 times, **always right after
a block of pairs, never right after a boundary 0**. After a pair, b is out of step with E (E arrives on its first 1,
`E1 → 0RC`, `C1 → 0LD`, and turns); after a boundary it would be the halting shape. (Heuristic: only the left-end
moments were looked at.)

### Walls and replays (heuristic)

`python3 py/replay.py` (on `runs/left_1e10.txt`): when a left-end moment reads `0^2 a^W 0 ...` with W ≥ 50 (a *wall*),
the following moments repeat the moments of the run from blank tape: moment i of the blank run is `0^2 P a^x 1`, and
the i-th moment after the wall starts `0^2 P a^y 0 ...`, the blank run's last block replaced by the wall. The wall
shrinks by the same amounts every time, whatever W and whatever lies beyond it:

```
step 277787:      wall 257:   256 253 251 247 245 235 229 219 213 211 205 193 187 173 167 163 161 157 155 145 139 129 123 93 75 45 27 21 5  (29 moments)
step 63654325:    wall 1547:  1546 1543 1541 1537 1535 1525 1519 1509 1503 1501 1495 1483 1477 1463 1457 1453 1451 1447 1445 1435 1429 1419 1413 1383  (24)
step 3999846159:  wall 43801: 43800 43797 43795 43791 43789  (5)
step 8246041679:  wall 99:    98 95 93 89 87 77 71 61 55 53 47  (11)
```

The decrements are 1, 3, 2, 4, 2, 10, 6, 10, 6, 2, 6, 12, 6, 14, 6, 4, 2, 4, 2, 10, 6, 10, 6, 30, 18, 30, 18, 6, 16.
What the wall has lost after moment i is about half the blank run's last block at moment i, plus one (last block 8,
lost 4; 12, 6; 18, 10; 22, 12; 42, 22): the wall gives up the room that the blank run's growing right end takes. The wall is not
literally blank tape to the machine (a run from `D>0 0 (10)^257` follows the blank run's head for only 17 steps); it
is the left-end configurations that agree. The replay counts are lower bounds: a left-end moment is taken where the
head extends the left end after extending the right end, and during a replay the left process grows into the wall
without extending the right end, so moments can merge.

Walls nest: at step 1.78·10^9 the tape starts `0^2 a^1768 0 a^6 0 a^10762 ...`, and at 7.0·10^9
`0^2 a^16920 0 a^698 0 a^6 0 a^5 b a^18873 ...`. The run looks like a recursive counter: the left end restarts the
blank run in front of each new wall and eats the wall at the blank run's pace; what happens when a wall is used up
(as at step 7.67·10^6, where a block that had been shrinking by these same decrements reaches 0, and the next
left-end moment starts with a wall of 1,752) is where the structure changes.

### Leads

1. **One excursion as a map.** Prove, from `e_run`, `e_mark`, `b_run` and the boundary cases (B or D arriving at a
   boundary or a mark, by parity), the effect of one excursion from the right end on the block list, with
   `bbtools.symtm.prove`. Then one sweep from the left end, then one left-end cycle.
2. **The replay.** State and prove: from `0^2 P a^W 0 S` with W large, the left-end configurations follow those of
   the blank run, the wall losing d_i. This would make the run a recursion on walls.
3. **Wall exhaustion.** Catalogue what happens when a wall runs out (finitely many cases if the blank run's state at
   that moment is determined by W modulo something), and look there for a mark after a boundary.
4. **A language over words.** A closed tape language over `10`, `110`, `0` that keeps the pair alignment might rule
   out `0 11` in front of E, or show where it can arise.
5. **Search.** Once the excursion map exists, a parts search over block lists (`tools/c/parts.h`) can look for lists
   that halt; until then a raw search has nothing to enumerate.
